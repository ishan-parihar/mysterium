# lines/07 — Willpower Line

## 1. Purpose

Specify the Willpower line — the capacity to set a goal and follow
through on it across time. The line that turns *capacity* into *change*.
A high-cognitive, high-emotional, high-moral player who cannot finish
anything still operates at a low integrated stage. Willpower is the
*delivery mechanism* of every other line.

Mysterium's verbs that involve sustained effort, vow-keeping, fatigue
resistance, and goal-locking all live here.

## 2. Scientific basis

### 2.1 Construct definition

Synthesis:

- **Mischel (1972, marshmallow test; 2014, *The Marshmallow Test*)** —
  delay of gratification; later-life predictive validity.
- **Baumeister (1998, ego-depletion)** — willpower as a resource that
  fatigues; the resource framing is debated (Inzlicht 2014) but the
  *phenomenon* of effort-cost is robust.
- **Duckworth (2007, *Grit*)** — sustained pursuit of long-term goals.
- **Kuhl (action vs. state orientation)** — implementation-intention
  research.
- **Wilber** — willpower as a *line* of development, often neglected in
  modern psychology curricula.

Mysterium's working definition:

> Willpower is the capacity to (a) set an intentional goal, (b) sustain
> attention to it under competing demands, (c) resist short-term
> rewards that conflict with it, and (d) deliver the action that
> realises it.

### 2.2 Validated assessments

| Assessment | Measures |
|---|---|
| Delay-of-gratification tasks (e.g., Marshmallow paradigm; intertemporal choice) | Preference for larger-later vs. smaller-sooner |
| Stop-signal task | Action-cancellation; partial overlap with inhibitory control |
| GRIT-S scale (Duckworth) | Long-term grit / consistency of interest |
| Action-Control Scale (Kuhl) | Action vs. state orientation |
| Sustained-effort task (handgrip dynamometer; vigilance under fatigue) | Endurance |

Willpower partially overlaps with inhibitory control (cognitive line) but
*adds* time-extended commitment. Mysterium models this overlap by giving
willpower a +1 cognitive ceiling slack vs. other lines (`docs/lines/00-overview-multi-line.md`).

### 2.3 Brain regions

dlPFC (top-down control), ACC (conflict / effort signalling), striatum
(reward delay), ventromedial PFC (value integration). Effort-related
fatigue signals appear in dACC.

## 3. Stage trajectory

| Stage | Capacity | In-game expression |
|---|---|---|
| Infrared | Reflex only; no chosen goal | No willpower verbs |
| Magenta | Magical wishing; "I want" without follow-through | Wish-buffs; uncostly to break |
| Red | Power-will — short, intense pursuits | Burst goals; fatigue ends them |
| Amber | Duty-will — group goal commitment | Long-form vows tied to group |
| Orange | Achievement-will — rational long-arc pursuit | Multi-session goals; planning + execution |
| Green | Pluralistic-will — multiple legitimate goals held in tension | Goal-portfolio management; reflective revision |
| Teal | Integral-will — vision-logic alignment of all goals | Holonic goal architecture |
| Turquoise | Effortless will — alignment with the cosmos; "thy will be done" | Goal arises spontaneously, executes itself |

## 4. Game-design mapping

### 4.1 Assessment modality affinity

**Goal-locker, fatigue-resistor, finisher.** Willpower-leaning builds
gain power from *committed sequences* — once the player declares "I will
take this boss without retreating," the build's damage scales; breaking
the commitment penalises. The build's identity is *I do what I said I
would*.

### 4.2 Assessment tasks / defences / skills

| Verb | Task vehicle | Mechanic | Stage gating |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lock Goal | Pre-fight goal declaration | Choose a goal-shape (e.g., "no-flee," "no-spell," "ally-first"); aligned actions × 1.15 | Red+ |
| Resist Fatigue | Sustained effort under decline | Stamina decay slowed when willpower is high; vigilance-task at decay points | Red+ |
| Finish | Damage scaling at end of long fights | Last-30%-HP damage × 1.25 if goal still locked | Amber+ |
| Vow-Strike | Locked-vow attack | Action restricted by vow gains huge bonus on the constraint-respecting strike | Amber+ |
| Multi-Goal Hold | Two simultaneous locked goals | Only at Green+; cost is heavy if either breaks | Green+ |
| Holonic Goal Tree | Sub-goals nested under super-goal | Vision-logic: completing sub-goals buffs super-goal | Teal+ |
| Effortless Pursuit | Goal completes itself once initiated | Highest tier; rare scripted moments | Turquoise |

### 4.3 Module parameters(s) backed

- **Resolve / Endurance** — fatigue resistance
- **Commitment** — vow integrity; gates Vow-Strike
- **Finish** — late-fight damage scaling

### 4.4 Cognitive task vehicles

- Delay-of-gratification choices (mid-fight: take the easy hit, or wait
  for the bigger window?)
- Sustained-effort vigilance (long-form fights with declining stimuli)
- Implementation-intention prompts ("If X happens, I will Y")
- Goal-revision checks (when the situation changes, do you adapt or
  rigidly stick?)

### 4.5 Depth progression

Willpower altitude is **gradual but durable** — once gained, regression
is slow. The line moves with *consistent practice patterns*: setting
goals, hitting them, revising honestly when missed. A player who
*always* succeeds at chosen goals is gaming the goal-setter; a player
who fails 100% is over-reaching. Health is a 60–80% goal-completion
rate over a season.

The DDA on willpower goals operates at the goal-difficulty axis: too
easy → easier goals get less credit; too hard → smaller goals offered.
Mirrors the cognitive staircase.

## 5. Enemy / encounter structure

### 5.1 Module-level encounters archetypes

| Stage | Side-character | Encoded test |
|---|---|---|
| Infrared | "Inertia" — passive resistance | Action vs. inaction |
| Magenta | "Wish-Phantom" — collapses if the player doubts | Belief sustain |
| Red | "Test-Strider" — rewards short bursts | Burst pursuit |
| Amber | "Oath-Watcher" — penalises broken vows | Vow keeping |
| Orange | "Long-Path" — multi-stage objective | Sustained pursuit |
| Green | "Conflict-Goal" — two valid goals demanding incompatible action | Multi-goal arbitration |
| Teal | "Holonic-Path" — nested sub-goals; misordering costs | Nested commitment |
| Turquoise | "Spontaneous Other" — appears when not pursued, vanishes when chased | Effortless will |

### 5.2 Cross-line encounterses

- **Willpower × Spiritual:** "The Tempter" — a value coherence + vow
  integrity challenge.
- **Willpower × Cognitive:** "The Long Vigil" — sustained n-back across
  3+ minutes; willpower stabilises decay.
- **Willpower × Somatic:** "The Endurance Test" — sustained posture
  with willpower-rate of stamina drain.

### 5.3 Main-boss role

Willpower phases appear as **the long phase** in a synthesis exam — a
sustained 90-second to 3-minute phase where the player must hold a
declared commitment across multiple attack patterns. Players who
declared a strong commitment pre-fight gain damage; players who broke
the commitment lose buffs.

### 5.4 Shadow encounters

**"The Quitter"** — at Red stage. The shadow of bursts without
follow-through. The fight is a long-form encounter that *cannot be won*
in a single burst. Defeated by sustained effort across 3+ minutes
without retreating. Lesson: willpower is not power; it is *return*.

**"The Iron Will"** — at Orange stage. The shadow of willpower without
flexibility. The boss adapts and the player's locked-in plan fails. The
win condition is to *consciously revise* the locked goal mid-fight —
demonstrating that willpower includes the will to update, not just to
persist.

## 6. Architectural contract

```
const WILLPOWER_VERBS = {
  lockGoal:        { task: 'goal_declaration',     altitudeMin: 'Red' },
  resistFatigue:   { task: 'sustained_vigilance',  altitudeMin: 'Red' },
  finish:          { task: 'late_arc_damage',      altitudeMin: 'Amber' },
  vowStrike:       { task: 'constraint_alignment', altitudeMin: 'Amber' },
  multiGoalHold:   { task: 'parallel_goals',       altitudeMin: 'Green' },
  holonicGoalTree: { task: 'nested_pursuit',       altitudeMin: 'Turquoise' },
  effortlessPursuit:{ task: 'spontaneous_will',    altitudeMin: 'White' },
} as const;

interface WillpowerProfile {
  altitude:          Stage;
  activeGoals:       ReadonlyArray<Goal>;
  goalCompletionRate: number; // 0..1, rolling
  vowIntegrity:      number;  // 0..1
  fatigueResistance: number;  // multiplier 0.8..1.5
}
```

Goal-completion rate is reported at season boundaries with a soft
suggestion: "your completion rate is X%, consistent with [over- /
under-reaching]; suggested goal scaling: …"

## 7. Open questions

- **Ego-depletion debate.** The empirical status of "willpower as
  finite resource" is contested. Mysterium models it as a *budget* that
  recovers between encounters but does not claim physiological truth.
- **Goal forgiveness.** A locked goal that breaks due to outside factors
  (network drop, real-life interrupt) should not penalise. Default:
  pause-during-interrupt with grace period.
- **Willpower vs. addiction.** Variable-ratio rewards that exploit
  willpower lapses are exactly what we said we wouldn't do. Mysterium
  *measures* willpower; it does not *exploit* its lapses.

## 8. Principles served

Principles **1, 3, 6** — willpower as a first-class developmental
line, ground for the staircase's "you came back tomorrow" loop,
non-exploitative design.
