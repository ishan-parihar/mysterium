# stages/07 — Teal / Integral

> **Cross-references:** [[docs/foundations/00-integral-theory|00 — Integral Theory]] · [[docs/foundations/02-eight-stages-overview|02 — Eight Stages Overview]]

## 1. Purpose

The world bible for the seventh stage — the integral, vision-logic,
holistic world. The first stage of **second tier** consciousness — the
first time the player can see *all previous stages* as legitimate parts
of a larger developmental whole, rather than as competitors. The "tier
shift" — not merely a new stage, but a new *kind* of stage.

Roughly 12–18 hours of play. Late game. Currently emerging in ~5% of
the global adult population — the leading edge of mainstream
psychological development.

## 2. Stage definition

Teal is the **integral** structure of consciousness. The mind
operates with **vision-logic** — the capacity to hold multiple maps at
once, to see partial truths *as partial*, to integrate contradictions
into larger holons. *Each previous stage was important because it
became an ingredient of the higher.* "Transcend and include" stops
being a slogan and becomes a felt operating principle.

The tier shift: where every first-tier stage believed *its* worldview
was the correct one and others were wrong, second tier *embraces all
levels* as partial truths with appropriate contexts of validity. Wilber:
*"All first tier levels actually loathe each other. Second tier is very
embracing of all other levels."*

In real development this stage emerges with significant maturity,
typically late-adulthood with appropriate developmental work; it is
also accelerated by integral practices, deep contemplative training,
and exposure to integral theory itself.

Pathologies of Teal: *integral elitism* (subtle pride at "seeing
through" everyone), *integral arrogance* (using the meta-map to dismiss
people at lower altitudes), *bypass* of necessary first-tier work in
the rush to claim integral.

Energetic correspondence: **Indigo-Ray** — the brow / third-eye
centre. *"The gateway to intelligent infinity, worked upon only by the
adept."* The gateway *opens* at Teal.

The Teal world is **observatories, holonic architecture, integral
training centres, global commons, knowledge networks, cross-tradition
sanctuaries**. The player's relationship with this world is "I see the
whole, and I am part of it."

## 3. The eight lines AT Teal

| Line | What it looks like at Teal |
|---|---|
| Cognitive | Vision-logic — post-formal, integrative, dialectical thinking. n=4; meta-WCST (rules-about-rules); recursive Tower of London. The mind that thinks *in wholes*. |
| Emotional | Vision-logic affect — sees emotional patterns across systems. *Reading the room* — group-level emotional waves. Equanimity emerging as baseline. |
| Moral | Stage 7 — universal-systemic. *Holonic ethics* — what protects the most depth for the most span. Multi-level harm trade-off. |
| Intrapersonal | Vision-logic self — meta-self that sees the developmental process. Witness of own stages; integrates pluralism into a unified self that includes the multiplicity. |
| Spiritual | Integral spirituality — paths as facets of one architecture. Cross-tradition synthesis. The sacred is the *whole pattern*. |
| Somatic | Integrated body — body-mind-emotion as one. Compound stances; effort-rest-effort cycling at will. The body listens to and informs cognition equally. |
| Willpower | Integral will — vision-logic alignment of all goals. Holonic goal architecture: super-goals, sub-goals, evolutionary purpose. |
| Interpersonal | Integral others — multiple selves attuned at multiple levels. Holonic teams; cross-stage attunement; mentorship across altitude differences. |

The line that *carries the player* through Teal: **Intrapersonal**
and **Cognitive** in their meta-forms. The player must *see the whole
arc of their own development* to operate at Teal.

## 5. World aesthetic

### 5.1 Visual language

- **Palette anchor:** indigo / midnight (Indigo-Ray); secondary
  teal, silver, deep violet, starlight turquoise.
- **Motifs:** observatories, fractal architecture, holonic
  geometries (circles within circles), libraries-of-libraries, vast
  integral training campuses, planetary gardens, contemplative
  cells inside vibrant communities.
- **Lighting:** twilight, starlight, the *liminal hour*; not bright,
  not dark — *clear*.
- **Architecture:** holonic — buildings that are clearly composed of
  smaller wholes, themselves part of larger wholes. Sacred geometry
  unifying influences from every prior stage.
- **Camera:** wide, contemplative, *high-angle vistas* alternating
  with intimate close-ups — the world *and* the eye.

### 5.2 Sonic language

- **Mode:** harmonic minor, octatonic, spectralism; cross-mode
  modulations; microtonal layers.
- **Instrumentation:** synth pads, bowed metals, prepared piano,
  strings, processed voice; world ensembles cross-fading into modern
  electronic.
- **Cadence:** *spacious* — long phrases, sustained tones, the silence
  *between* notes is musical.
- **Theme motif:** all prior stage motifs return *layered* —
  audible as themselves *and* as parts of a larger whole.

### 5.3 Narrative tone

- **Voice:** spacious, considered, often paradoxical; humour
  appropriate to the depth. NPCs may speak in koans without being
  obscure.
- **Theme:** *every previous chapter is still alive in me; I am the
  one in whom they are gathered*.
- **Conflict:** integral elitism vs. genuine humility; meta-mapping
  vs. embodied action; the *temptation* to dismiss earlier stages
  one has personally exited.
- **Ally:** a fellow integral practitioner — peer-equal across many
  lines simultaneously, including ones the player is weaker at.

## 6. Encounter archetypes at Teal

### 6.1 Module-level encounters concepts

| Line | Concept |
|---|---|
| Cognitive | **Recursion-Wraith** — meta-rule changes mid-encounter. |
| Emotional | **Hollow Mirror** — reflects player's own affect; misreads cause self-damage. |
| Moral | **Holonic Judge** — depth-and-span trade-off; multi-level harm calculation. |
| Intrapersonal | **Process-Witness** — narrates the player's developmental arc; misnames trigger flag. |
| Spiritual | **Holonic Adept** — composes tests across traditions. |
| Somatic | **Breath-Wraith** — synchronised to player's breath; out-of-sync hurts. |
| Willpower | **Holonic-Path** — nested sub-goals; misordering costs. |
| Interpersonal | **Cross-Stage Mentor** — partner at different altitude; asymmetric attunement required. |

### 6.2 Cross-line encounters concepts

- **Cognitive × Intrapersonal:** *The Process-Strategist* — n-back
  where the stimuli include the player's own past actions.
- **Spiritual × Moral:** *The Holonic Tribunal* — depth-vs-span
  ethical dilemmas with cross-tradition framings.
- **Interpersonal × Cognitive:** *The Holonic Council* — multi-stage
  partners; the player must coordinate with NPCs at three different
  altitudes simultaneously.

### 6.3 Main-boss concept

**The Adept Above** — the apex Teal pathology figure. A genuine
high-altitude practitioner who has *fallen into integral elitism*.
Sees through everyone. Cannot meet anyone where they are. Subtly
contemptuous of "first tier" — even though they are entirely composed
of first-tier ingredients.

The fight has four phases:

- **UR phase:** the most cognitively demanding of the game; vision-logic
  tasks; the Adept's own integral toolkit deployed against the player.
- **UL phase:** the Adept *names* the player's stages — accurately —
  and uses the naming to belittle. The player must *receive* without
  defending; the Adept's strikes land less when the player is
  groundedly humble.
- **LL phase:** a community of NPCs at varied altitudes; the Adept
  treats them all as objects of their meta-map; the player must
  *honour* each NPC at their own altitude, not lift them up the
  ladder.
- **LR phase:** the integral institution itself — the Adept has built
  systems that *enforce* their meta-view; the player must restore
  the systems' *humility*, including its capacity to be wrong.

Outcomes: defeat (the Adept's integral arrogance pierced), partnership
(synthesis ending — the Adept is restored to genuine humble integral
practice), transformation (rare — the Adept advances to Turquoise-altitude
non-dual humility under the player's witnessing).

### 6.4 Shadow concept

**The Bypass** — Teal's signature shadow. The player who has
*claimed* integral capacity without doing the first-tier work — usually
detected as a player who has rushed through Red, Amber, Orange, or
Green without lingering and now flounders at Teal. The shadow is
*one of the bypassed stages* returning *as itself* — usually whichever
the player evaded most. Win condition: spend serious time *back at
that stage* doing the work; altitude on that line resets to a healthy
expression and gradually re-climbs.

**The Subtle Pride** — Teal's gentler shadow. A small, persistent
inflation of "I see what they don't see." Detected from microaggressions
in dialogue choices, cross-stage condescension. Defeated by a brief
encounter where the player is *seen through* by a Magenta-altitude
NPC who casually names the player's pride without theory — the pride
becomes embarrassing in a kindly way.

## 7. Stage advancement criteria

To pass Teal and unlock Turquoise:

1. All 8 lines ≥ Teal (floor) — extremely demanding gate
2. ≥ 2 lines ≥ Turquoise — typically Spiritual and Intrapersonal
3. All 4 quadrants demonstrated at Teal, with multiplayer
   demonstration of the LL quadrant required (cannot fully demonstrate
   integral collective interior in solo)
4. Defeat / partner / transform The Adept Above
5. Have done at least one *deep return* — voluntary regression to a
   prior stage to do unfinished work
6. Have demonstrated *the absence of meta-pride* in a moment when the
   player could legitimately have claimed superiority — a chosen
   humility

## 8. Open questions

- **The depiction of Teal.** Authentic Teal is rare; the
  game cannot stereotype it. Collaboration with integral practitioners
  is implied. The Adept Above must be charismatic, not a strawman.
- **The temporal-honesty problem at Teal.** Real-life Teal
  takes decades. Compressed to a game it risks being a *rumour* of
  Teal rather than the thing itself. The codex must be explicit:
  "in-game Teal is a *practice indicator*, not a clinical
  attainment."
- **Multiplayer demand.** Teal's interpersonal-line demands
  multiplayer. Single-player runs of Mysterium cap at high-Green by
  design, with a clear narrative that the *we-space* lives elsewhere.

## 9. Principles served

Principles **1, 4, 5, 6** — the integral stage modelled with care,
demanding genuine integration rather than meta-claims, with the
multiplayer-LL gate enforcing honesty.
