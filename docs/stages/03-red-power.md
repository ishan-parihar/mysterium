# stages/03 — Red / Power

> **Cross-references:** [[docs/foundations/05-drives-and-polarities|05 — Drives And Polarities]] · [[docs/foundations/02-eight-stages-overview|02 — Eight Stages Overview]]

## 1. Purpose

The world bible for the third stage — the egoic, power-driven,
warrior world. The first stage where the player has a *stable
separate self* and can act on the world *as a self*. The "great
stepping-stone ray" in the energetic correspondence — the foundation
of all later development is laid here, even when later stages
transcend it.

Roughly 4–8 hours of play. The first major *gameplay world* — combat
becomes serious, decisions have consequence, the protagonist becomes
a *named character*.

## 2. Stage definition

Red is the **egocentric** consciousness — the first-person view
crystallised. The self has differentiated from environment and from
fantasy; it now perceives itself as *separate*, with *boundaries*,
and *capable of will*. Power becomes the organising drive — not in a
purely violent sense, but as the *capacity to make happen*. "I want"
sharpens into "I will." Healthy boundaries form here for the first
time.

In real development this is the toddler-power phase (~3–7 years) and,
historically, the warlord-feudal cultures, the matrifocal horticultural
shift, the "dog-eat-dog" power-driven worlds.

Pathologies of Red: domination, manipulation, cruelty, tribal
violence. *But* — Red without health (no stable Red, no power-self)
produces adults who cannot defend themselves, cannot set boundaries,
cannot pursue. Red is *necessary*. The work is to **transcend and
include**, not to *bypass*.

Energetic correspondence: **Yellow-Ray** — solar plexus, ego, personal
identity. "The great stepping-stone ray where mind/body potentiates
to its fullest balance."

The Red world is **fortresses, weapon-walls, throne rooms, war-camps**.
Hierarchies of strength. Loyalties of fear and admiration. The
player's relationship with this world is "I assert, therefore I am."

## 3. The eight lines AT Red

| Line | What it looks like at Red |
|---|---|
| Cognitive | Late pre-op / early concrete operations. Egocentric reasoning — "what works for me, here, now." n-back at n=2; first multi-step plans (combo length 2). The mind is *strategic but short-horizon*. |
| Emotional | Self-other emotion split. Egocentric anger and pride dominate. The player *knows* they are angry; they can use it as a buff. First emotional self-management beats — *I will not let my fear stop me*. |
| Moral | Pre-conventional. Stage 1–2 (Kohlberg): punishment-obedience and naïve hedonism. "Will I get in trouble? Will I get the prize?" Self-interest is the moral horizon. |
| Intrapersonal | Egocentric self-identification — "I am the one who fights." First witness pauses possible. The self is *grasped*, not yet *examined*. |
| Spiritual | Power-deities, transactional faith. *Pray for victory*. The sacred is on *my side* if I serve it. |
| Somatic | Power-body — strength, speed, dominance. The body is a weapon. |
| Willpower | Power-will — short, intense pursuits. Burst goals. *I will take this hill*. Fatigue ends the burst, then the will resets. |
| Interpersonal | Egocentric others — others as instruments. Allies as tools. Betrayal logic operates: "they will betray me if it serves them, so I will betray first if it serves me." |

The line that *carries the player* through Red: **Willpower**, fed
by **Somatic** and **Cognitive** in their early-strategic forms.

## 4. AQAL quadrant snapshots

| Quadrant | At Red |
|---|---|
| **UL** Individual Interior | Power-feeling, pride, anger, the thrill of dominance, the chill of vulnerability. The interior is sharp, charged, *alive*. |
| **UR** Individual Exterior | Combat skills, weapon mastery, raw physical assertion. The body as instrument. |
| **LL** Collective Interior | War-band loyalty, honour codes, leader-follower bonds. Tribal "us" against opaque "them." |
| **LR** Collective Exterior | Warlord systems, tribute economies, fortified holdings. Power as organising principle of the polity. |

## 5. World aesthetic

### 5.1 Visual language

- **Palette anchor:** saffron / amber-yellow (Yellow-Ray); secondary
  brick red, black, brass.
- **Motifs:** weapon-walls, throne rooms, war-banners, scarred
  champions, tributary processions, ritual combat circles, blood-on-stone.
- **Lighting:** strong contrast, golden-hour tones for triumphal
  scenes, deep shadow for menace.
- **Architecture:** fortresses, warlord-keeps, ziggurat forms,
  throne-platforms. Architecture *demonstrates power*.
- **Camera:** assertive, mid-range, from the player's eye-line —
  the world is *confronted*, not contemplated.

### 5.2 Sonic language

- **Mode:** Mixolydian, harmonic minor; war-cry intervals (perfect
  fourths, fifths).
- **Instrumentation:** brass, war drums, low strings, hand-percussion;
  voice as battle-cry rather than song.
- **Cadence:** driving, syncopated, body-moving.
- **Theme motif:** a five-note brass-call, often ironised in shadow
  encounters.

### 5.3 Narrative tone

- **Voice:** declarative. Short sentences. Power-rhetoric.
- **Theme:** *I will not be moved unless I move*.
- **Conflict:** dominance challenges, honour disputes, tributary
  rebellions, succession struggles.
- **Ally:** a fellow warrior — someone whose loyalty is *earned in
  combat*, not assumed.

## 6. Encounter archetypes at Red

### 6.1 Module-level encounters concepts

| Line | Concept |
|---|---|
| Cognitive | **Pyre-Knight** — feints / strikes patterned attacks; Go/No-Go + n=2 n-back. |
| Emotional | **Wroth-Champion** — visible rage telegraphs; misreading the affect costs HP. |
| Moral | **Bandit-Lord** — naked self-interest, transactional bargains. |
| Intrapersonal | **Echo-Warrior** — repeats the player's last action; defeated by *not* repeating. |
| Spiritual | **Power-Priest** — promises strength for tribute; transactional faith. |
| Somatic | **Striker** — power-trades; dodge-or-die. |
| Willpower | **Test-Strider** — rewards short bursts; punishes hesitation. |
| Interpersonal | **Power-Ally (NPC)** — useful while convenient; turns coat under pressure. |

### 6.2 Cross-line encounters concepts

- **Cognitive × Somatic:** *The Drumheart* — n-back stimuli matched
  on a war-drum beat.
- **Willpower × Somatic:** *The Long Ascent* — a sustained climbing
  combat through a fortress under siege.
- **Spiritual × Cognitive:** *The Oracle of Battles* — ritual
  combat with omen-reading; the right omen at the right beat *is*
  the spell.

### 6.3 Main-boss concept

**The Conqueror** — the apex Red figure. A warlord with a real claim
to the world (charisma, history, scars, allies). The fight has four
phases:

- **UR phase:** straight combat — the player's full Red toolkit on
  display.
- **UL phase:** the Conqueror *speaks* to the player — offers an
  alliance, names the player's pride, exposes the player's fear. The
  player must *answer* — pride, mercy, defiance, or refusal-to-engage,
  each opening different paths.
- **LL phase:** the Conqueror's war-band attacks together; the
  player's allies must hold; the fight is about *who breaks first* in
  the social fabric.
- **LR phase:** the fortress itself becomes the arena; siege engines,
  collapsing walls, the *system* as participant.

Defeat outcomes: the Conqueror can be slain (Red ending), spared
(seeds Amber moral material), or *recruited* (highest tier, requires
the player to demonstrate *the Conqueror's absent drive* — Communion;
see `foundations/05`).

### 6.4 Shadow concept

**The Cruelty** — Red's classic shadow. The player who has come to
*enjoy* dominance, who has slid from healthy assertion into
predation. Detected when the player has been killing rather than
sparing for several sessions, or has been ignoring ally welfare. The
shadow is a mirror-self at peak ferocity; defeated only by a moment
of *visible mercy* — a hard mechanical commitment (e.g., spending HP
to spare).

**The Coward** — Red's other shadow. The player who has retreated,
who has not asserted at all, who has let others' Red define them.
Detected from chronic flight / non-engagement patterns. Defeated by
*choosing* a confrontation — the encounter cannot be skipped.

## 7. Stage advancement criteria

To pass Red and unlock Amber:

1. All 8 lines ≥ Red altitude (floor)
2. ≥ 2 lines ≥ Amber — typically Somatic (full Red posture) and
   Willpower (vow capacity emerging)
3. All 4 quadrants demonstrated at Red
4. Defeat / spare / recruit The Conqueror
5. Have demonstrated *boundaries* (a refusal of an unjust offer that
   was tempting) — Red's healthy expression
6. Have honoured at least one *given word* across multiple sessions
   — proto-vow, the seam into Amber

## 8. Open questions

- **Power's portrayal.** Red is the most *seductive* stage — power is
  cinematic; mercy is harder to dramatise. The risk is that players
  *love* the Red world and resist leaving. Default: Red is glorious
  but *brittle*; the long-arc story makes Red's costs visible by
  late-Red.
- **Real-world parallels.** Red contains a lot of "warlord" content
  that maps to current and historical violence. Honest engagement
  beats sanitisation, but care is needed.
- **The Conqueror as character.** This boss is one of the game's
  signature figures. Their *charisma* must be real — they should be
  someone the player half-wants to follow. Hard writing.

## 9. Principles served

Principles **1, 4, 5, 6** — Red is the foundation of self-as-agent;
must be earned, must be seductive enough that the player understands
*why* people stay, must be exit-able with integrity.
