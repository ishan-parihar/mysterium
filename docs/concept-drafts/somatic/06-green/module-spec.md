# Somatic / Green — Module Specification

> **Line:** Somatic  **Stage:** Green / Pluralistic  **Vibration:** The listening body — attuned to subtle signals, feeling others through one's own flesh, body-as-bridge between self and world.

---

## 1. Capacity Definition

The Green somatic capacity is **relational embodiment** — the body that can feel others. Interoceptive sensitivity extends beyond personal boundaries into the interpersonal field. The body becomes a bridge rather than an instrument. Core tasks: somatic empathy (detecting another's body-state through own body), interoceptive discrimination (subtle signal detection), co-regulation (synchronising with another's nervous system), boundary awareness (distinguishing own from other's somatic signals), relational movement (moving WITH rather than performing FOR), and somatic listening (receiving body-information without acting on it).

Below (Orange): the skilled body — multi-domain mastery, rational training, body-as-instrument, performance metrics.
Above (Teal): the integral body — body as holonic field, somatic vision-logic, the body that holds all bodies.

## 2. Shadow Archetypes

### 2.1 Dark-Addiction: The Somatic Empath Overwhelm

Cannot distinguish own body signals from others'. Boundary dissolution — "I feel everything everyone feels." Somatic enmeshment produces compassion fatigue through the body. The player's interoceptive field has no membrane; all signals register as self-signals.

### 2.2 Dark-Allergy: The Performance Machine

Refuses somatic sensitivity. Stays locked in Orange body-as-instrument. "Feelings in the body are distractions." Dismisses subtle body-awareness as 'woo.' The body remains a tool to be optimised, never a relational organ.

### 2.3 Golden-Addiction: The Premature Energy Worker

Performs Teal somatic holism without genuine Green somatic empathy. Claims to "feel the field" while unable to genuinely attune to one other person's body-state. Bypasses the relational body for the cosmic body.

### 2.4 Golden-Allergy: The Sensitive Fortress

Somatic empathy is present but refuses integral embodiment. Terrified of "losing the body" in transpersonal states. "I can feel others but I won't go further." Clings to relational sensitivity as ceiling rather than floor.

## 4. Healing Vectors

### 4.1 Heal/Evolve (Agape + Agency → Dark Shadows)

DA: Agape grounds the overwhelmed empath back into their OWN body; Agency re-establishes somatic membrane. Practice: feel others AND know where you end.
DAll: Agape invites the performance machine to feel; Agency gives permission to be sensitive without weakness. Practice: notice one subtle signal without optimising it.

### 4.2 Evolve/Heal (Eros + Communion → Golden Shadows)

GA: Communion pulls the energy performer back to genuine one-on-one attunement; Eros redirects toward real relational depth. Practice: attune to ONE person fully before claiming the field.
GAll: Eros invites the sensitive fortress toward transpersonal opening; Communion dissolves the fear of losing self. Practice: let somatic empathy extend beyond the known.

### 4.3 Integration Criteria

Player demonstrates: (a) accurate somatic empathy with maintained boundaries, (b) capacity to co-regulate without enmeshment, (c) willingness to explore transpersonal somatic states, (d) body used as relational bridge without collapsing into instrument or dissolving into field.

## 6. Compound Shadows & Cross-Module Relationships

### 6.1 The Overwhelmed Empath (Somatic DA × Emotional DA)

Somatic enmeshment + empathic overwhelm = cannot function in groups. The body absorbs others' states AND the emotional system floods simultaneously. Intervention: somatic boundary work feeds emotional containment.

### 6.2 The Disembodied Pluralist (Somatic DAll × Cognitive DA)

Refuses body-sensitivity + paralysis-by-pluralism = all head, no body. Intellectualises multiple perspectives while the body remains a performance machine. Intervention: one embodied perspective grounds the cognitive plurality.

### 6.3 The Energy Performer (Somatic GA × Spiritual GA)

Premature energy work + premature interfaith = performed sensitivity. Claims cosmic somatic awareness AND universal spiritual attunement without genuine depth in either. Intervention: genuine one-person attunement anchors both lines.

### 6.4 The Sensitive Isolate (Somatic GAll × Interpersonal GAll)

Sensitive fortress + refuses authentic relating = feels others but won't connect. Somatic empathy is real but never becomes relational action. Intervention: one act of somatic connection breaks both fortresses.

## 7. Shadow Surfacing Sequence

### 7.1 Progressive Revelation

Sessions 1–3: Baseline somatic sensitivity without relational demand. Sessions 4–8: Introduce relational somatic tasks — co-regulation, empathy detection. Sessions 9–14: Increase relational complexity — multiple bodies, conflicting signals. Sessions 15+: Full shadow surfacing through compound scenarios.
