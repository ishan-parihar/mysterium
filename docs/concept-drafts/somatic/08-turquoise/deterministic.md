# Somatic / White — Deterministic Game

## 1. Core Mechanic

Objective measurement of effortless action — the game presents complex movement sequences and measures whether execution occurs without strain, excess tension, or deliberate correction. The deterministic modality captures what CAN be measured objectively: movement smoothness, stillness-in-motion metrics, and the absence of compensatory effort.

Input: touch/gesture trajectories requiring fluid, non-grasping execution.
Measurement: jerk minimization, velocity-profile smoothness, absence of corrective micro-adjustments, ratio of movement-to-stillness within action.

## 2. Shadow Detection

- **2.1 Grasping-at-Body:** Excess grip force, over-controlled trajectories, inability to release mid-movement — the hand that cannot let go of its own motion
- **2.2 Aversion-to-Body:** Avoidance of certain movement directions, gaps in the movement field, zones the body refuses to enter smoothly
- **2.3 Somatic Bypassing:** Movements that LOOK smooth but show micro-tension signatures — performing effortlessness while efforting beneath
- **2.4 Identity-with-Body:** Over-investment in movement quality itself; the score becomes a somatic identity marker rather than transparent expression

## 3. Drive-Residual Detection

- **Eros-residual:** Acceleration bias — always reaching toward the next movement before completing the current
- **Agape-residual:** Deceleration bias — lingering in completed movements, reluctance to release
- **Agency-residual:** Force excess — applying more energy than the movement requires
- **Communion-residual:** Force deficit — under-committing, letting movements dissolve before completion

## 4. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

**Catalyst:** Increasingly complex movement patterns that demand effortless execution — spirals, figure-eights, multi-axis flows. Complexity rises until strain appears.

**Experience:** The moment strain appears IS the diagnostic. The game holds the player at their effortlessness threshold — the edge where technique wants to reassert.

**Integration:** Adaptive difficulty finds the zone where effortless action is JUST possible. Repeated exposure at-threshold trains the nervous system to release grasping. Movement patterns that once required effort become transparent.

## 5. Progression & Infinite Checkpoint

- **Entry:** Simple single-axis movements — measure baseline smoothness
- **Mid:** Multi-axis compound movements — spirals, waves, figure-eights requiring coordination without control
- **Deep:** Unpredictable movement demands — the game generates novel patterns requiring immediate effortless response
- **Infinite:** Movement complexity is unbounded; the game always finds the threshold where grasping re-emerges
- **Checkpoint:** Every completed movement-set is a save point; session length is player-determined
- **Theta-decay:** 28-day half-life; neglected effortless-action capacity degrades toward the threshold where strain returns
