# Somatic / White — Immersive-RPG Game

## 1. Core Mechanic

Ecological luminous body — whether effortless action appears spontaneously in free-play without explicit somatic prompting. The RPG world presents situations that REQUIRE body-engagement but never NAMES them as somatic tasks. The game observes whether the player's embodied interaction with the world is transparent or grasping.

The player navigates a luminous environment — energy-fields, light-bodies, transparent landscapes. Every interaction requires physical input (touch, gesture, tilt). The game measures input QUALITY across all interactions — not just during "somatic moments" but continuously.

## 2. Shadow Detection

- **2.1 Grasping-at-Body:** Grip-force spikes during narrative tension, body bracing during challenge, inability to maintain light touch when stakes rise — the body that grasps when the world demands
- **2.2 Aversion-to-Body:** Minimal physical engagement with the world, avoiding gesture-heavy interactions, choosing menu-options over embodied-options — the body that withdraws from the game-world
- **2.3 Somatic Bypassing:** Smooth input during explicit "spiritual" moments but grasping during mundane interactions — performing luminosity selectively rather than being transparent throughout
- **2.4 Identity-with-Body:** Over-investment in avatar's physical form, customizing body-appearance excessively, treating the game-body as self — the body-identity projected into virtual space

## 3. Drive-Residual Detection

- **Eros-residual:** Always moving forward in the world — unable to rest in a location, body constantly seeking the next area
- **Agape-residual:** Lingering in safe zones — body settles into comfort, avoids areas requiring novel somatic engagement
- **Agency-residual:** Forceful world-interaction — pushing through obstacles rather than flowing around them, asserting body against environment
- **Communion-residual:** Passive world-interaction — letting the environment move the player rather than maintaining transparent agency, drifting

## 4. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

**Catalyst:** The RPG world itself is the catalyst. Luminous landscapes that respond to touch-quality. NPCs that are energy-bodies requiring attunement to communicate. Obstacles that dissolve under light touch but solidify under force. The entire ecology rewards effortless action.

**Experience:** The world mirrors the player's somatic quality back — grasping makes the world dense and resistant; transparency makes it luminous and responsive. The player LIVES the difference between grasping and release across hours of free-play.

**Integration:** Ecological — effortless action becomes the player's default mode of world-interaction because the world consistently rewards transparency. No explicit instruction; the body learns through environmental feedback across extended play.

## 5. Progression & Infinite Checkpoint

- **Entry:** Luminous tutorial zone — world responds obviously to touch-quality, teaching the body-world relationship
- **Mid:** Complex ecology — multiple simultaneous demands requiring sustained effortless engagement
- **Deep:** The harvest zone — environments so subtle that only genuine transparency allows navigation
- **Infinite:** World complexity is unbounded; new zones always find the edge of residual grasping
- **Checkpoint:** Save anywhere; the world persists; session length follows natural play-rhythms
- **Theta-decay:** 28-day half-life; ecological effortless-action capacity degrades, world becomes denser on return
