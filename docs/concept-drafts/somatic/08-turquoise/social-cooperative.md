# Somatic / White — Social-Cooperative Game

## 1. Core Mechanic

Shared luminous embodiment — communion in effortless movement and non-verbal somatic attunement. Two or more players move together, and the game measures whether coordination arises from effortless attunement or from deliberate synchronization (grasping).

The game pairs players in movement tasks requiring coordination. Measurement: does synchrony emerge spontaneously (transparent) or through effort (grasping)? At White, genuine somatic communion means bodies attune without trying — like birds in murmuration.

## 2. Shadow Detection

- **2.1 Grasping-at-Body:** Over-efforting to synchronize — watching the other's movement too closely, forcing the body to match, coordination through control rather than resonance
- **2.2 Aversion-to-Body:** Withdrawing from somatic contact — maintaining distance, avoiding the vulnerability of shared embodiment, subtle isolation within the pair
- **2.3 Somatic Bypassing:** Performing attunement — moving in ways that LOOK synchronized but lack genuine felt-resonance, mimicry without communion
- **2.4 Identity-with-Body:** Using the body as boundary — "my movement, your movement" maintained even in cooperative tasks, subtle separation through somatic identity

## 3. Drive-Residual Detection

- **Eros-residual:** Leading the pair — always initiating, pulling the shared movement toward novelty, unable to follow
- **Agape-residual:** Following exclusively — always matching, never initiating, losing somatic autonomy in communion
- **Agency-residual:** Maintaining separateness within coordination — moving together but asserting individual style, unable to dissolve into shared flow
- **Communion-residual:** Losing individual movement entirely — merging so completely that personal somatic intelligence disappears, enmeshment rather than communion

## 4. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

**Catalyst:** Paired movement tasks with increasing demand for spontaneous coordination. Mirror games, leader-follower with role-dissolution, shared rhythm without explicit beat. The task requires attunement but punishes effortful synchronization.

**Experience:** The game measures synchrony quality — phase-locked movement that emerges without lag (transparent) vs. synchrony with correction-signatures (grasping). Players feel the difference between forced and effortless coordination.

**Integration:** Repeated shared-movement practice trains the relational nervous system. Bodies learn to attune without trying. The boundary between self-movement and other-movement becomes transparent — not dissolved (that's communion-residual) but genuinely permeable.

## 5. Progression & Infinite Checkpoint

- **Entry:** Simple mirror tasks — baseline synchronization effort measurement
- **Mid:** Complex shared movement — multi-axis coordination requiring effortless attunement
- **Deep:** Leaderless emergence — both players must move in novel coordination without anyone initiating
- **Infinite:** Coordination complexity is unbounded; subtler grasping-in-relation always detectable
- **Checkpoint:** Each shared-movement round is complete; players can leave between rounds
- **Theta-decay:** 28-day half-life; relational somatic attunement degrades without shared practice
