# Somatic / White — Embodied-Somatic Game

## 1. Core Mechanic

The luminous body itself — this is the PRIMARY somatic modality for White. Non-grasping movement and transparent embodiment measured through the body's own activity. The game uses device sensors (accelerometer, gyroscope, touch pressure) to detect whether the player's physical engagement is effortless or strained.

Core tasks: hold the device with minimal grip while performing gestures; move through space without excess muscular engagement; breathe without controlling breath; be still without rigidity. The body IS the instrument and the measurement simultaneously.

## 2. Shadow Detection

- **2.1 Grasping-at-Body:** Excess grip pressure, muscular over-engagement, inability to hold the device lightly while performing complex gestures — the body that cannot release itself
- **2.2 Aversion-to-Body:** Minimal sensor engagement, holding the device at distance, avoiding full-body involvement — the body that refuses to be fully present
- **2.3 Somatic Bypassing:** Performing relaxation while accelerometer shows micro-tremors of effort — the body that pretends effortlessness while working beneath awareness
- **2.4 Identity-with-Body:** Over-attention to body-metrics, checking posture, adjusting grip — the body that monitors itself rather than being transparent

## 3. Drive-Residual Detection

- **Eros-residual:** Accelerating engagement — grip tightens over time, body leans forward, increasing investment in the somatic task
- **Agape-residual:** Decelerating engagement — body settles into minimal movement, avoids challenge, retreats to comfort
- **Agency-residual:** Forceful engagement — more pressure than needed, body asserts itself against the device
- **Communion-residual:** Dissolved engagement — body goes limp, loses tone, merges with gravity rather than maintaining transparent presence

## 4. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

**Catalyst:** Physical tasks that require presence without grasping. Hold the device at a specific angle — but lightly. Trace a pattern — but without grip-force spikes. Walk with the device — but without the body bracing against movement. The task demands embodiment while punishing grasping.

**Experience:** Real-time biofeedback — the game responds to grip pressure, movement smoothness, breath rhythm. When grasping appears, the game-world dims or slows. When transparency appears, the world brightens. The body FEELS the difference between grasping and release.

**Integration:** The nervous system learns through direct somatic feedback. No cognitive mediation — the body discovers effortless action through repeated encounter with its own grasping patterns. Integration is pre-verbal, pre-conceptual.

## 5. Progression & Infinite Checkpoint

- **Entry:** Simple hold-lightly tasks — baseline grip and tension measurement
- **Mid:** Complex movement-while-light — multi-axis gestures requiring coordination without control
- **Deep:** Stillness-in-motion — be physically active while maintaining the sensor signature of rest
- **Infinite:** Sensitivity threshold deepens indefinitely; subtler grasping is always detectable
- **Checkpoint:** Every breath-cycle is a natural pause point; session length follows the body's rhythm
- **Theta-decay:** 28-day half-life; the body's effortless capacity regresses toward habitual tension patterns
