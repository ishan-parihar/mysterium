# Somatic / White — Strategic-Planning Game

## 1. Core Mechanic

Planning within effortless action — the paradox of strategy at White is that genuine planning dissolves into spontaneous arising. The game presents movement-sequences that COULD be planned but measures whether the player pre-plans (grasping) or allows action to arise moment-by-moment.

The game shows upcoming movement demands. The player can prepare — or not. Measurement: does preparation improve or degrade movement quality? At White, genuine effortless action means the body responds optimally WITHOUT deliberate sequencing. Planning itself becomes the shadow.

## 2. Shadow Detection

- **2.1 Grasping-at-Body:** Over-planning movement sequences — mentally rehearsing body-positions, pre-tensing for upcoming demands, the body that cannot trust its own spontaneity
- **2.2 Aversion-to-Body:** Refusing to engage with upcoming demands — not because of genuine non-planning but because planning would require feeling the body's readiness
- **2.3 Somatic Bypassing:** Claiming spontaneity while covertly planning — "I don't plan, I just flow" while showing preparation signatures in movement onset latency
- **2.4 Identity-with-Body:** Planning AS the body — "my body knows what to do" as identity-claim rather than transparent trust in organismic intelligence

## 3. Drive-Residual Detection

- **Eros-residual:** Planning ahead excessively — always preparing for the NEXT movement rather than being present to THIS one
- **Agape-residual:** Refusing to anticipate — staying in current movement past its natural completion, not allowing the next to arise
- **Agency-residual:** Deliberate sequencing — imposing order on movement rather than allowing order to emerge
- **Communion-residual:** Formless drift — no sequence at all, movements disconnected, mistaking chaos for spontaneity

## 4. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

**Catalyst:** Movement sequences with visible upcoming demands. The game shows what's coming — curves, direction changes, rhythm shifts. The question: does the player PLAN or ALLOW? Measurement compares planned-execution vs. spontaneous-execution quality.

**Experience:** The game reveals when planning HELPS (residual grasping hasn't dissolved — the body still needs preparation) and when planning HINDERS (the body responds better without deliberation). This edge is the diagnostic.

**Integration:** Repeated exposure to the planning-vs-spontaneity threshold trains the nervous system to release deliberate sequencing. The body discovers it can handle complexity without pre-planning. Strategy dissolves into flow — not as bypass but as genuine capacity.

## 5. Progression & Infinite Checkpoint

- **Entry:** Simple sequences with preview — measure baseline planning-dependence
- **Mid:** Complex sequences — compare planned vs. unplanned execution quality
- **Deep:** Novel sequences with no preview — pure spontaneous response to unknown demands
- **Infinite:** Complexity is unbounded; there is always a level where planning wants to reassert
- **Checkpoint:** Each sequence is self-contained; player exits between movement-sets
- **Theta-decay:** 28-day half-life; spontaneous-action capacity regresses toward deliberate planning
