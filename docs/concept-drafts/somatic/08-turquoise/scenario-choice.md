# Somatic / White — Scenario-Choice Game

## 1. Core Mechanic

Non-dual somatic dilemmas — situations requiring effortless response rather than technique. The game presents scenarios where the body must act but no technique is correct. The player chooses HOW to respond somatically, and the choice reveals whether action arises from transparency or from residual grasping.

Scenarios are LLM-generated: a sudden demand, an ambiguous body-signal, a situation where doing and not-doing are equally valid. The "right" answer is not a specific choice but the QUALITY of choosing — effortless vs. effortful.

## 2. Shadow Detection

- **2.1 Grasping-at-Body:** Choices that over-prepare the body — bracing, tensing, gathering resources before acting. The body that cannot simply respond.
- **2.2 Aversion-to-Body:** Choices that bypass the body — going to mental strategy, avoiding somatic engagement, choosing "not to move" as dissociation rather than stillness
- **2.3 Somatic Bypassing:** Choices that perform effortlessness — selecting the "non-dual sounding" option without genuine spontaneity, choosing what a luminous being WOULD choose
- **2.4 Identity-with-Body:** Choices that protect body-identity — avoiding scenarios that threaten somatic self-concept, choosing comfort over transparency

## 3. Drive-Residual Detection

- **Eros-residual:** Consistently choosing action over stillness — residual drive toward movement-as-growth
- **Agape-residual:** Consistently choosing stillness over action — residual drive toward rest-as-safety
- **Agency-residual:** Choosing responses that assert somatic autonomy — "my body, my choice" at a level where ownership is residual
- **Communion-residual:** Choosing responses that merge with environment — dissolving body-boundary before genuine transparency warrants it

## 4. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

**Catalyst:** Somatic scenarios with no correct technique. A child falls — do you catch them (effortful) or trust the moment (potentially negligent)? Your body signals pain — do you attend (grasping) or release (bypassing)? Dilemmas where every option reveals something.

**Experience:** The game shows the player WHAT their choice pattern reveals — not which choice was "right" but what residual grasping drove the selection. The pattern across many choices is the diagnostic.

**Integration:** Scenarios become increasingly subtle. Early dilemmas have obvious grasping-options; later ones require genuine non-dual discernment. The player learns to feel the difference between effortless response and performed effortlessness.

## 5. Progression & Infinite Checkpoint

- **Entry:** Clear somatic dilemmas — obvious technique-vs-spontaneity choices
- **Mid:** Ambiguous scenarios — both options could be grasping or transparent depending on quality
- **Deep:** Paradoxical situations — where effortless action and non-action are indistinguishable
- **Infinite:** Scenario subtlety is unbounded; the LLM always finds the next edge of discernment
- **Checkpoint:** Each scenario is self-contained; player can leave after any dilemma
- **Theta-decay:** 28-day half-life; non-dual somatic discernment degrades without practice
