# Somatic / White — Language-Reflective Game

## 1. Core Mechanic

Verbal articulation of luminous body experience — how the player talks about embodiment reveals residual grasping patterns. The LLM presents somatic prompts (body sensations, movement experiences, embodiment questions) and scores responses for transparency vs. grasping in language.

Key probes: "Describe what your body is doing right now." "What happens when movement arises?" "Where does effort live?" The language itself reveals whether the body is transparent or still grasped.

## 2. Shadow Detection

- **2.1 Grasping-at-Body:** Language that reifies the body — "MY body," possessive framing, descriptions that solidify somatic experience into owned objects
- **2.2 Aversion-to-Body:** Language that distances — "the body does X" as dissociation rather than transparency, avoidance of certain somatic vocabulary, gaps in body-description
- **2.3 Somatic Bypassing:** Spiritual-sounding language that masks residual technique — "movement just happens" said with performative certainty, borrowed non-dual vocabulary without lived referent
- **2.4 Identity-with-Body:** Language that fuses self with body — inability to describe embodiment without self-reference, "I AM this movement" as identity rather than transparency

## 3. Drive-Residual Detection

- **Eros-residual:** Language oriented toward future body-states — "becoming more fluid," "approaching effortlessness"
- **Agape-residual:** Language oriented toward preserving current states — "maintaining this ease," "holding this openness"
- **Agency-residual:** Language emphasizing personal capacity — "I can move without effort," self as agent of effortlessness
- **Communion-residual:** Language dissolving all distinction — premature "there is no body" claims, boundary-erasure before genuine transparency

## 4. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

**Catalyst:** Somatic prompts that invite description of embodiment at increasing subtlety. Early prompts ask about gross movement; later prompts ask about the felt-sense of body-as-energy, the space between movements, the arising of action.

**Experience:** The LLM reflects back the grasping patterns embedded in the player's language — not as correction but as mirror. "You described your body as something you HAVE. What if it's something awareness IS?"

**Integration:** Repeated articulation at the edge of expressibility trains the player to find language that neither grasps nor avoids. The vocabulary itself becomes transparent — pointing without reifying.

## 5. Progression & Infinite Checkpoint

- **Entry:** Describe gross body sensations — baseline vocabulary reveals default relationship to embodiment
- **Mid:** Describe subtle body-energy — language must navigate between reification and dissociation
- **Deep:** Describe the absence of body-boundary — articulate transparency without performing it
- **Infinite:** Subtlety of prompt is unbounded; language always reveals the next layer of residual grasping
- **Checkpoint:** Each prompt-response pair is complete; player can leave after any exchange
- **Theta-decay:** 28-day half-life; linguistic precision around embodiment degrades without practice
