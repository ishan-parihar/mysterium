# Somatic × Turquoise — Language-Reflective Game

## 1. Core Mechanic

Verbal articulation of integral body-knowledge. The player describes, names, and explains their embodied experience. The LLM evaluates whether language reveals genuine integral somatic understanding (body-mind-emotion as unified field) or performance (borrowed terminology without felt-sense grounding). Depth of articulation distinguishes authentic integration from conceptual bypass.

Tasks:
- **Somatic narration:** Player describes what is happening in their body during compound-stance execution — in real-time or retrospectively.
- **Integration mapping:** Player articulates how body-states inform cognitive decisions and vice versa, using their own language.
- **Stage-holding:** Player explains how earlier somatic capacities (survival, power, discipline, relational) live within their current embodiment.
- **Shadow naming:** Player identifies when they are over-practicing, refusing hierarchy, performing luminosity, or fortifying mastery.

## 2. Shadow Diagnostic

| Shadow | Linguistic signature |
|---|---|
| DA (Somatic Perfectionist) | Hyper-technical somatic vocabulary; describes body in optimization language; cannot articulate "enough" or "rest" without qualifying |
| DAll (Sensitive Pluralist) | Relational/emotional language dominates; refuses hierarchical framing; "my body doesn't have levels, it has connections" |
| GA (Premature Energy Body) | Spiritual/energetic vocabulary without somatic specificity; "I feel the energy flowing" but cannot describe which muscles, which breath pattern |
| GAll (Integral Body Fortress) | Precise integral language but deflects questions about surrender, luminosity, or what lies beyond mastery; "I'm still integrating" as permanent deflection |

## 3. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

**Catalyst:** Prompts escalate from simple body-description to articulating paradox (how effort IS rest, how hierarchy IS relationship). LLM probes for specificity, felt-sense grounding, and willingness to name shadow.

**Experience:** Player's language reveals their shadow. The Perfectionist's language is exhausting. The Pluralist's language avoids depth. The Performer's language floats. The Fortress's language walls off.

**Integration:** LLM reflects the shadow-pattern back through follow-up prompts. "You described seven adjustments but never said 'this is enough' — what would enough feel like?" The player must articulate their way through the shadow, not around it.

## 4. Progression Architecture

| Phase | Prompt complexity | LLM evaluation depth |
|---|---|---|
| Entry | "Describe what your body is doing right now" | Checks for specificity and present-tense grounding |
| Mid | "How does your body inform your thinking?" | Checks for bidirectional integration language |
| Advanced | "How do all your previous body-capacities live in this moment?" | Checks for holonic inclusion without hierarchy-collapse |
| Mastery | "What is beyond this integration?" | Checks for genuine openness to luminous/non-dual without bypass |

Rubric-based scoring via LLM. Fixed prompts + adaptive follow-ups based on detected shadow-pattern. Player never sees the rubric.

## 5. Scoring & Drives

| Metric | Drive mapped | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| Somatic specificity (felt-sense grounding) | Agency | 0.25 |
| Integration articulation (bidirectional body↔mind language) | Eros | 0.25 |
| Holonic inclusion (earlier stages honored in language) | Agape | 0.25 |
| Openness to beyond (willingness to articulate surrender/luminosity) | Communion | 0.25 |

Shadow-drag (−0.20): applied when linguistic signature of a shadow persists across 3+ prompts without self-correction. Cross-line bonus (+0.15): when Cognitive or Intrapersonal line articulation correlates with somatic language depth.
