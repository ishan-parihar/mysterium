# Somatic × Turquoise — Strategic-Planning Game

## 1. Core Mechanic

Planning integral embodiment practice across time and domains. The player structures multi-session, multi-channel somatic development — deciding what to practice, when, in what sequence, and how to balance effort with rest at the meta-level. This probes whether the player can think ABOUT integral embodiment strategically without collapsing into either over-planning (DA) or refusing structure (DAll).

Tasks:
- **Practice architecture:** Design a multi-day embodiment plan that develops compound stances, cycling, and cross-domain integration in balanced sequence.
- **Resource allocation:** Distribute limited practice-time across channels, stages, and recovery periods. Over-allocation to any single domain reveals shadow.
- **Contingency planning:** Plan for disruption — what happens when the body is injured, tired, or emotionally flooded? Does the plan adapt or rigidify?
- **Integration scheduling:** Plan how somatic practice feeds into cognitive, emotional, and relational domains — and how those feed back.

## 2. Shadow Diagnostic

| Shadow | Planning signature |
|---|---|
| DA (Somatic Perfectionist) | Over-detailed plans; no rest days; every minute optimized; plan revision is itself a practice — infinite meta-optimization |
| DAll (Sensitive Pluralist) | Refuses structured planning; "the body knows what it needs"; plans are vague relational intentions, not actionable sequences |
| GA (Premature Energy Body) | Plans skip foundational compound-stance work; jumps to "energy practices" and "luminous body work" without building base |
| GAll (Integral Body Fortress) | Plans are comprehensive but never include surrender/dissolution practices; all structure, no space for the unknown |

## 3. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

**Catalyst:** Planning challenges escalate from single-domain scheduling to multi-domain integration architecture. Eventually the player must plan for their own transcendence — structuring practice that leads beyond structure.

**Experience:** The player's planning style reveals their shadow. The Perfectionist's plan is beautiful but exhausting. The Pluralist's plan is warm but unactionable. The Performer's plan skips steps. The Fortress's plan has no exit.

**Integration:** Adaptive constraints force shadow-confrontation. The Perfectionist receives "design a plan with 40% unstructured time." The Pluralist receives "create a specific daily sequence with measurable milestones." The Performer receives "your plan must demonstrate mastery of compound stances before any energy work." The Fortress receives "include one practice whose outcome you cannot predict."

## 4. Progression Architecture

| Phase | Planning scope | Domains | Constraint |
|---|---|---|---|
| Entry | Single day, single domain | Somatic only | Time-limited (30 min/day) |
| Mid | Multi-day, multi-domain | Somatic + one other line | Must include rest periods |
| Advanced | Multi-week, full integration | Somatic + all feeding lines | Must include contingencies |
| Mastery | Open-ended, self-transcending | All domains as holonic field | Must include surrender/unknown |

Plans are evaluated by LLM against rubrics for balance, specificity, adaptability, and shadow-absence. Player executes plans in other modalities — strategic-planning feeds embodied-somatic.
