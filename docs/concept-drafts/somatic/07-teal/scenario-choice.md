# Somatic × Turquoise — Scenario-Choice Game

## 1. Core Mechanic

Somatic dilemmas that require holding multiple body-states simultaneously while committing to decisive action. The player faces situations where the body must integrate contradictory demands — power AND receptivity, effort AND rest, individual mastery AND relational openness — and choose how to act without collapsing the integration.

Scenario types:
- **Compound-hold dilemmas:** Maintain 3+ somatic channels while a crisis demands action through one channel. Does the player collapse to single-channel or act from integration?
- **Effort-rest paradoxes:** Situations where maximum effort requires maximum rest simultaneously. The player must choose an approach that honors both.
- **Cross-altitude encounters:** Meeting beings at different somatic stages who need different things. Does the player attune downward (Agape) or pull upward (Eros) — and can they do both?
- **Surrender-or-fortify moments:** The body is integrated and functional — now something asks it to dissolve. Choose.

| DAll (Sensitive Pluralist) | Always chooses relational/receptive option; refuses scenarios framed as hierarchical; collapses compound stances to single-channel empathy |

| GAll (Integral Body Fortress) | Chooses mastery-consolidation in every scenario; refuses surrender options; reframes dissolution-prompts as "not yet integrated enough" |

## 3. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

**Catalyst:** Scenarios escalate from simple either/or to genuine paradox. Early: "Do you act from power or receptivity?" Later: "How do you act from power-AS-receptivity?" The dilemma IS the developmental edge.

**Experience:** The player's consistent choice-pattern reveals their shadow. The system tracks which options are never chosen, which are always chosen, and which produce hesitation.

**Integration:** Scenarios adapt to present the avoided choice as the only viable option. The Perfectionist faces "act NOW from imperfect integration." The Pluralist faces "this situation HAS a hierarchy — honor it somatically." The Performer faces "demonstrate the compound stance before releasing it." The Fortress faces "your mastery is complete — now let it go."

## 4. Progression Architecture

| Phase | Dilemma type | Channels required |
|---|---|---|
| Entry | Binary somatic choices (effort vs. rest) | 2 body-states |
| Mid | Triadic dilemmas (power + receptivity + action) | 3–4 body-states |
| Advanced | Paradox scenarios (opposites held simultaneously) | 5+ body-states |
| Mastery | Surrender dilemmas (integrated body asked to dissolve) | All channels + release |

Each scenario has 4–6 response options mapped to drives and shadow-quadrants. No option is "wrong" — but patterns across scenarios reveal shadow-fixation.

## 5. Scoring & Drives

| Metric | Drive mapped | Weight |

| Willingness to act from current integration (vs. delay) | Agency | 0.25 |

| Openness to dissolution/surrender when appropriate | Communion | 0.25 |

Shadow-drag (−0.20): applied when choice-pattern shows >70% selection of same shadow-quadrant across scenarios. Cross-line bonus (+0.15): when Moral or Intrapersonal line choices correlate with somatic-choice integration.
