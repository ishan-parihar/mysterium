# Somatic × Turquoise — Social-Cooperative Game

## 1. Core Mechanic

Shared integral embodiment — group compound stances, cross-altitude somatic attunement, and collective holonic movement. The player must coordinate their integrated body with others who may be at different somatic altitudes, creating group coherence without collapsing their own integration or demanding others match their level.

Tasks:
- **Group compound stances:** Multiple players hold complementary body-states that form a collective compound stance. Each player contributes channels the group needs.
- **Cross-altitude attunement:** Player attunes somatically to partners at Green (relational body), Orange (achievement body), or Amber (disciplined body) without losing integral capacity.
- **Collective holonic movement:** Group executes movements where each individual's gesture is a sub-whole of the group's holonic movement.
- **Shared effort-rest cycling:** Group synchronizes state-transitions — collective effort→collective rest — while maintaining individual integration.

## 3. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

**Catalyst:** Group tasks escalate from simple synchronization to cross-altitude leadership. The player must eventually hold their own integral embodiment WHILE attuning to partners at every previous stage — simultaneously teaching, learning, and co-creating.

**Experience:** Social context amplifies shadow. The Perfectionist becomes the group's tyrant. The Pluralist becomes the group's enabler. The Performer becomes the group's false guru. The Fortress becomes the group's absent member.

**Integration:** Role-rotation forces shadow-confrontation. The Perfectionist must follow a "less integrated" partner's lead. The Pluralist must structure a group practice with clear hierarchy. The Performer must demonstrate individual mastery before group work. The Fortress must allow group rhythm to alter their personal state.

## 4. Progression Architecture

| Phase | Group size | Altitude range | Coordination depth |
|---|---|---|---|
| Entry | Dyad | Same altitude | Simple synchronization |
| Mid | Triad | ±1 stage | Complementary channel-sharing |
| Advanced | 4–6 players | ±2 stages | Holonic group movement |
| Mastery | Open group | Full spectrum | Cross-altitude leadership + followership |

NPC partners at various somatic altitudes provide consistent challenge. Real-player co-op available but not required. Group coherence measured by collective timing, channel-complementarity, and attunement accuracy.
