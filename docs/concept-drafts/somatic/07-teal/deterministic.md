# Somatic × Turquoise — Deterministic Game

## 1. Core Mechanic

Objective measurement of integral embodiment through timed compound-stance execution, voluntary state-cycling speed, and cross-domain integration accuracy. The player must hold multiple body-states simultaneously, shift between effort and rest on command, and demonstrate that body informs cognition informs body in measurable feedback loops.

Tasks:
- **Compound stance trials:** Player executes stances requiring simultaneous activation of 3+ body-state channels (e.g., power-stance + breath-regulation + micro-relaxation). Accuracy measured by channel coherence.
- **State-cycling speed:** Rapid voluntary shifts between effort→rest→effort. Measured by transition latency and completeness of state-change.
- **Cross-domain integration:** Body-signal triggers cognitive task; cognitive output modifies somatic state. Measured by loop-closure time and accuracy.

## 2. Shadow Diagnostic

| Shadow | Behavioral signature |
|---|---|
| DA (Somatic Perfectionist) | Excessive micro-corrections; never settles into stance; transition latency paradoxically increases with practice as player over-refines |
| DAll (Sensitive Pluralist) | Refuses compound stances; defaults to single-channel relational-body postures; state-cycling incomplete — always returns to receptive rest |
| GA (Premature Energy Body) | Claims completion before channels cohere; fast transitions but shallow state-change; cross-domain loop skips somatic grounding |
| GAll (Integral Body Fortress) | Perfect compound stances but freezes when task demands releasing into unknown states; cycling stops at effort-peak, avoids full rest |

## 3. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

**Catalyst:** Trials escalate from 2-channel to 5-channel compound stances. State-cycling windows narrow. Cross-domain loops add emotional channels.

**Experience:** Player encounters their shadow pattern as felt difficulty. The Perfectionist feels "never good enough." The Pluralist feels "this is too hierarchical." The Performer feels exposed when coherence is measured. The Fortress feels terror at full-rest demands.

**Integration:** Adaptive difficulty holds player at their shadow-edge. Success requires releasing the shadow pattern — the Perfectionist must accept imperfect coherence, the Pluralist must embrace vertical integration, the Performer must slow down, the Fortress must surrender into rest.

## 4. Progression Architecture

| Phase | Channels | Cycling window | Cross-domain loops |
|---|---|---|---|
| Entry | 2 simultaneous | 4s transitions | Body→cognition only |
| Mid | 3–4 simultaneous | 2s transitions | Bidirectional body↔cognition |
| Advanced | 5+ simultaneous | <1s transitions | Body↔cognition↔emotion triangulation |
| Mastery | All channels fluid | Instantaneous | Seamless holonic integration |

Theta-decay ensures regression if player avoids compound-stance practice. Shadow-mode encounters activate when specific shadow signatures persist across 3+ sessions.

## 5. Scoring & Drives

| Metric | Drive mapped | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| Channel coherence (compound stance accuracy) | Agency | 0.25 |
| Transition completeness (state-cycling depth) | Agape | 0.25 |
| Loop-closure time (cross-domain speed) | Eros | 0.25 |

Drive-health formula: each drive scored 0–1 based on metric performance. Shadow-drag applied when behavioral signature persists (−0.20 to affected drive). Cross-line bonus (+0.15) when Willpower or Cognitive line scores correlate with somatic integration metrics.
