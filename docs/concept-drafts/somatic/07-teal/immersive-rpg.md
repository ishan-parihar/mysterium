# Somatic × Turquoise — Immersive-RPG Game

## 1. Core Mechanic

Ecological integral body — whether integrated embodiment appears spontaneously in free-play without explicit prompting. The player navigates an open RPG environment where somatic challenges emerge organically from narrative context. The assessment measures whether compound stances, holonic movement, and effort-rest cycling arise as natural responses or only when explicitly demanded.

Environmental probes:
- **Ambient somatic demands:** The game world presents situations requiring integrated embodiment without labeling them as "somatic challenges." A collapsing bridge requires power + balance + breath-regulation simultaneously.
- **NPC body-reading:** Characters in the world embody different somatic altitudes. The player's spontaneous somatic response to each NPC reveals their integration depth.
- **Ecological cycling:** The world has rhythms (day/night, seasons, danger/safety) that demand effort-rest cycling. Does the player's body follow the ecology or impose its own pattern?
- **Holonic encounters:** Situations where the player must act as a whole-within-a-whole — their body as part of a larger body (group, ecosystem, world).

## 2. Shadow Diagnostic

| Shadow | Free-play signature |
|---|---|
| DA (Somatic Perfectionist) | Treats every moment as practice; cannot simply BE in the world without optimizing embodiment; turns exploration into training |
| DAll (Sensitive Pluralist) | Defaults to relational-body in all encounters; never spontaneously activates power or discipline channels; world-engagement is always receptive |
| GA (Premature Energy Body) | Performs "flow state" without situational appropriateness; claims effortless action in contexts that genuinely require effort; bypasses challenge |
| GAll (Integral Body Fortress) | Excellent somatic responses to explicit challenges but body closes when world asks for vulnerability, surrender, or dissolution |

## 3. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

**Catalyst:** The world presents increasingly complex somatic situations without framing them as tests. The player must recognize somatic demands implicitly and respond from integration rather than from habit or performance.

**Experience:** Free-play reveals the shadow that structured practice conceals. The Perfectionist who performs well in deterministic trials reveals compulsive practice in open-world. The Pluralist who articulates integration beautifully reveals single-channel defaults in spontaneous action.

**Integration:** The world adapts to present the player's shadow-edge as narrative necessity. The Perfectionist's world offers beauty that requires stillness, not optimization. The Pluralist's world presents clear hierarchies that demand somatic honoring. The Performer's world strips away audience. The Fortress's world offers dissolution as gift, not threat.

## 4. Progression Architecture

| Phase | World complexity | Somatic demand type | Measurement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Entry | Single-biome, clear rhythms | Explicit environmental demands | Response appropriateness |
| Mid | Multi-biome, shifting rhythms | Mixed explicit/implicit demands | Spontaneous integration frequency |
| Advanced | Full ecology, emergent situations | Primarily implicit demands | Ecological attunement depth |
| Mastery | Living world, unpredictable | No explicit demands — all emergent | Embodiment-as-being (no separation between player and world-body) |

Telemetry tracks somatic responses across all world-interactions. Pattern analysis reveals whether integration is genuine (consistent across contexts) or performed (present only when expected).
