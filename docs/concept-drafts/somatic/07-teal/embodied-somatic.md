# Somatic × Turquoise — Embodied-Somatic Game

## 1. Core Mechanic

The integral body itself as gameplay. The player's actual body executes compound stances, holonic movements, and effort-rest-effort cycles. This is the primary somatic modality — the body IS the controller, the instrument, and the assessment simultaneously. All previous somatic capacities (survival reflexes, magical agency, power, discipline, achievement, relational sensitivity) must be present as integrated sub-wholes within each movement.

Tasks:
- **Compound stances:** Simultaneous activation of multiple body-state channels — e.g., grounded power + open chest + relaxed jaw + alert eyes + rhythmic breath. Measured via accelerometer/gyroscope/touch-pressure.
- **Holonic movement:** Movements that contain sub-movements as complete wholes — a single gesture that IS a power-stance, IS a breath-cycle, IS a relational opening, all at once.
- **Effort-rest-effort cycling:** Voluntary rapid alternation between maximum exertion and complete release. Measured by transition speed and depth of each state.
- **Somatic vision-logic:** Body-based pattern-of-patterns detection — the body recognizes complex rhythmic structures before cognition names them.

## 2. Shadow Diagnostic

| Shadow | Somatic signature |
|---|---|
| DA (Somatic Perfectionist) | Micro-adjustments never cease; stance never "arrives"; effort-rest cycling biased toward effort; body never fully releases |
| DAll (Sensitive Pluralist) | Single-channel dominance (receptivity); refuses power-channel activation; compound stances collapse to relational-body posture |
| GA (Premature Energy Body) | Movements appear fluid but lack channel-coherence on measurement; claims holonic movement but sub-movements are absent; fast but shallow |
| GAll (Integral Body Fortress) | Excellent compound stances but freezes at effort-rest boundary; cycling stops at peak-effort; body refuses full surrender into rest |

## 3. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

**Catalyst:** Movement sequences escalate in channel-count and cycling-speed. The body is asked to hold more, shift faster, integrate deeper. Eventually the body is asked to do something it cannot do through effort alone — requiring surrender.

**Experience:** The player's body reveals its shadow directly. No interpretation needed — the Perfectionist's body literally never stops adjusting. The Pluralist's body literally won't activate power. The Performer's body literally lacks depth. The Fortress's body literally won't release.

**Integration:** Adaptive sequences target the shadow-edge. The Perfectionist receives "hold this imperfect stance for 30 seconds without adjusting." The Pluralist receives "activate power-channel while maintaining relational openness." The Performer receives "slow this movement until every sub-movement is felt." The Fortress receives "from peak effort, release everything in one breath."

## 4. Progression Architecture

| Phase | Channels | Cycling speed | Holonic depth |
|---|---|---|---|
| Entry | 2–3 simultaneous | 4s transitions | Movement contains 2 sub-movements |
| Mid | 4 simultaneous | 2s transitions | Movement contains 3–4 sub-movements |
| Advanced | 5+ simultaneous | <1s transitions | Movement contains full stage-spectrum |
| Mastery | All channels as one field | Instantaneous | Every gesture is holonic |

Sensor fusion (accelerometer + gyroscope + touch-pressure + timing) provides objective measurement. Theta-decay ensures regression without practice.
