# Interpersonal × Turquoise — Deterministic

## 1. Core Mechanic

**Altitude-Detection and Cross-Stage Attunement Tasks.** The player encounters NPCs expressing needs, conflicts, or requests at specific developmental altitudes. The task: correctly identify the altitude, select the relationally appropriate response FOR THAT ALTITUDE, and demonstrate mentorship effectiveness by shifting the NPC toward growth without imposing one's own stage.

Three task families:
- **Altitude-Detection:** Given relational signals (language, values, needs), identify the NPC's centre of gravity. Scored on accuracy and speed.
- **Relational Flexibility:** Respond to the same relational situation from multiple altitude-appropriate frames in sequence. Scored on range and authenticity of each frame.
- **Mentorship Effectiveness:** Guide an NPC one half-stage upward through relational interaction. Scored on whether the NPC's responses shift without resistance or collapse.

## 2. Shadow Surfacing

- **DA (Relational Architect):** Over-analyses altitude signals, high detection accuracy but low connection warmth — the system detects when responses are technically correct but relationally cold.
- **DAll (Authentic Pluralist):** Refuses to differentiate altitudes, responds to all NPCs with Green-level vulnerability regardless of fit — scored as low flexibility.
- **GA (Premature Merger):** Claims oneness with NPCs at all altitudes but cannot accurately detect their actual stage — high warmth, low accuracy.
- **GAll (Integral Relational Fortress):** Perfect detection and flexibility scores but systematically avoids responses that require boundary-softening — the system tracks avoidance of communion-heavy options.

## 3. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

- **Catalyst:** NPCs present relational situations that demand altitude-specific responses. Difficulty scales by introducing NPCs at greater altitude distance from the player's centre, or presenting mixed-altitude groups.
- **Experience:** The player feels the tension between mapping (Agency) and meeting (Communion), between holding centre and dissolving into the other's frame.
- **Integration:** Successful trials require BOTH accurate detection AND warm connection. Neither alone scores full marks. The system rewards the integral synthesis.

## 4. Progression Architecture

- **Entry:** Single NPC, altitude close to player's own. Binary detection (above/below).
- **Mid:** Multiple NPCs at varied altitudes simultaneously. Must attune to each differently within the same interaction window.
- **Advanced:** NPCs whose altitude is shifting mid-interaction. The player must track movement and adjust in real-time.
- **Mastery:** Holonic team scenarios — 4-6 NPCs at different altitudes, player must hold the relational field for the entire group while attuning to each individually.

## 5. Drive Probes

| Drive | Probe |
|-------|-------|
| Eros | Does the player reach toward higher-altitude NPCs with genuine aspiration rather than performance? |
| Agape | Does the player descend to meet lower-altitude NPCs without condescension or collapse? |
| Agency | Does the player maintain their own centre while flexing across altitudes? |
| Communion | Does the player create genuine relational resonance, not just technically correct responses? |

Dual-domain scoring: each response is scored on both the vertical axis (Eros/Agape — did the player navigate altitude correctly?) and the horizontal axis (Agency/Communion — did the player maintain centre while genuinely connecting?).
