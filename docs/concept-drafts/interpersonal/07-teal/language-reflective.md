# Interpersonal × Turquoise — Language-Reflective

## 1. Core Mechanic

**Verbal Articulation of Integral Relating.** The player is prompted to describe, narrate, or reflect on relational situations using free-text or constrained-choice language. The LLM evaluates whether the player's language reveals genuine integral relational depth or performative use of integral vocabulary.

Three task families:
- **Relational Description:** Describe a relationship with someone at a different developmental altitude. Scored on whether the description holds both perspectives without collapsing into either.
- **Mentorship Narration:** Articulate how you would guide someone at a lower altitude. Scored on whether language reveals genuine attunement vs. condescension or over-identification.
- **Boundary Articulation:** Describe the felt-sense of maintaining centre while deeply connecting. Scored on whether language reveals lived experience vs. conceptual understanding only.

## 2. Shadow Surfacing

- **DA (Relational Architect):** Language is structurally sophisticated but emotionally thin — uses frameworks, maps, and models to describe relationships rather than felt connection. High complexity, low warmth.
- **DAll (Authentic Pluralist):** Language refuses altitude distinctions — 'we're all just humans,' 'hierarchy in relationship is violence,' 'I just meet people where they are' without acknowledging that 'where they are' differs.
- **GA (Premature Merger):** Language leaps to unity — 'we are one,' 'boundaries are illusion,' 'I feel what they feel' — without demonstrating the intermediate step of accurate altitude-detection.
- **GAll (Integral Relational Fortress):** Language is precise about attunement but avoids vulnerability — describes relating TO others but never WITH them at the boundary-dissolving edge.

## 3. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

- **Catalyst:** Prompts that require the player to articulate relational experiences that cannot be faked through vocabulary alone. The LLM detects structural markers of genuine integral relating vs. parroted frameworks.
- **Experience:** The player confronts the gap between what they can SAY about integral relating and what they have actually LIVED. The prompts are designed to reveal this gap gently.
- **Integration:** Over time, the player's language shifts from conceptual to embodied, from performative to authentic. The LLM tracks this trajectory across sessions.

## 4. Progression Architecture

- **Entry:** Simple relational descriptions with clear altitude differences. Binary scoring: does the player acknowledge the altitude gap or flatten it?
- **Mid:** Complex multi-party relational narratives. The LLM evaluates whether the player can hold multiple perspectives simultaneously in language.
- **Advanced:** Paradox articulation — describing how one can be fully present with someone at a different altitude without either collapsing the difference or creating distance.
- **Mastery:** The player's language reveals spontaneous integral relating — no longer effortful articulation but natural expression of cross-altitude attunement.

## 5. Drive Probes

| Drive | Probe |
|-------|-------|
| Eros | Does the player's language reach toward describing relational experiences beyond their current capacity with genuine aspiration? |
| Agape | Does the player's language demonstrate care for those at earlier stages without patronising or romanticising? |
| Agency | Does the player's language maintain a clear 'I' even when describing deep communion? |
| Communion | Does the player's language convey genuine relational warmth, not just structural accuracy? |

Dual-domain scoring: vertical axis evaluates altitude-awareness in language (Eros/Agape); horizontal axis evaluates relational presence in language (Agency/Communion). The LLM rubric weights both equally.
