# Interpersonal × Turquoise — Embodied-Somatic

## 1. Core Mechanic

**Body as Site of Integral Relating.** The player uses rhythm, timing, breath, and physical responsiveness to demonstrate embodied cross-altitude attunement. Relating is not just cognitive — the body must resonate at the frequency of the other while maintaining its own somatic centre.

Three task families:
- **Rhythmic Attunement:** NPCs at different altitudes move/pulse at different rhythms. The player must physically match each NPC's rhythm in sequence without losing their own baseline rhythm. Scored on accuracy of matching AND stability of return to centre.
- **Somatic Mirroring:** The player mirrors NPC body-language patterns (represented as gesture sequences) that encode altitude-specific relational styles. Scored on fidelity of mirroring AND whether the player's own somatic signature remains coherent.
- **Breath-Gated Presence:** The player must hold a specific breath pattern (representing centred presence) while simultaneously responding to relational demands. Scored on breath stability under relational load.

## 2. Shadow Surfacing

- **DA (Relational Architect):** The body becomes rigid and analytical — precise rhythm-matching but no felt resonance. High accuracy, low flow. The system detects mechanical precision without somatic warmth.
- **DAll (Authentic Pluralist):** The body collapses into a single relational rhythm — matches Green-level NPCs perfectly but cannot shift to other altitudes. Somatic range is restricted to one mode.
- **GA (Premature Merger):** The body loses its own rhythm entirely — merges with each NPC's pattern so completely that the player's baseline disappears. High mirroring, zero centre-maintenance.
- **GAll (Integral Relational Fortress):** The body maintains perfect centre but never fully resonates — the player's own rhythm dominates, with only surface-level matching of others. High stability, low attunement depth.

## 3. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

- **Catalyst:** Physical tasks that demand simultaneous centre-holding and other-attunement. The body cannot fake this — either the player can somatically hold both or they cannot.
- **Experience:** The felt-sense of integral relating AS A BODILY STATE — not a concept but a physical capacity. The player discovers where their body tightens (fortress), collapses (merger), rigidifies (architect), or restricts (pluralist).
- **Integration:** Progressive somatic training builds the physical capacity for integral relating. The body learns to resonate across altitudes while maintaining coherence.

## 4. Progression Architecture

- **Entry:** Single NPC, simple rhythm-matching with generous timing windows. The player learns to match AND return to centre.
- **Mid:** Two NPCs at different rhythms simultaneously. The player must alternate attunement while maintaining baseline.
- **Advanced:** Rapid altitude-switching — NPCs change their rhythm mid-interaction, requiring real-time somatic adaptation.
- **Mastery:** Full holonic field — 4+ NPCs at different rhythms, the player's body holds the entire relational field somatically, attuning to each without losing coherence.

## 5. Drive Probes

| Drive | Probe |
|-------|-------|
| Eros | Does the player's body reach toward higher-frequency rhythms with genuine aspiration rather than strain? |
| Agape | Does the player's body soften to meet slower/simpler rhythms without condescension or impatience? |
| Agency | Does the player's somatic baseline remain stable under relational load? |
| Communion | Does the player's body genuinely resonate with others, not just mechanically match? |

Dual-domain scoring: vertical axis evaluates somatic altitude-navigation (Eros/Agape — can the body move up and down?); horizontal axis evaluates somatic relational quality (Agency/Communion — does the body hold AND join?).
