# Interpersonal × Turquoise — Immersive-RPG

## 1. Core Mechanic

**Ecological Integral Relating in Free-Play.** The player navigates an open-world environment populated with NPCs at various developmental altitudes. No explicit tasks are given — the game observes whether cross-altitude attunement appears SPONTANEOUSLY in the player's relational behaviour. The world is designed to reward integral relating through emergent narrative consequences.

Three observation axes:
- **Spontaneous Altitude-Matching:** Does the player naturally adjust their relational approach when encountering NPCs at different altitudes, without being prompted?
- **Holonic Field Emergence:** When multiple NPCs are present, does the player spontaneously hold the relational field — facilitating, translating, attuning — or do they default to a single relational mode?
- **Boundary Fluidity:** Does the player's relational boundary flex naturally — opening for communion, consolidating for agency — or is it fixed regardless of context?

## 2. Shadow Surfacing

- **DA (Relational Architect):** In free-play, the player gravitates toward observation and mapping — watching NPC interactions, analysing group dynamics, but rarely entering the relational field directly. The world becomes a system to understand rather than a community to join.
- **DAll (Authentic Pluralist):** The player relates to all NPCs identically — deep, warm, Green-level connection regardless of altitude. Works beautifully with Green NPCs but creates friction with Red, Amber, or Turquoise NPCs who need different relational modes.
- **GA (Premature Merger):** The player attempts deep communion with every NPC immediately — no relational pacing, no altitude-detection, just instant intimacy. Some NPCs respond well; others withdraw or become confused.
- **GAll (Integral Relational Fortress):** The player navigates the world with perfect relational competence — attuning to each NPC appropriately — but never allows any relationship to deepen beyond attunement into genuine vulnerability or boundary-dissolution.

## 3. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

- **Catalyst:** The open world is seeded with relational opportunities that only yield their deepest rewards through integral relating. NPCs have hidden narrative arcs that unlock only when the player relates to them at their altitude AND maintains genuine connection.
- **Experience:** The player discovers their default relational mode through its consequences — the Architect misses connection, the Pluralist creates friction, the Merger overwhelms, the Fortress isolates.
- **Integration:** As the player's integral relating capacity grows, the world opens — new narrative paths, deeper NPC relationships, emergent group dynamics that only become visible to someone holding the holonic field.

## 4. Progression Architecture

- **Entry:** Small village with 4-5 NPCs at clearly different altitudes. The player's natural relational style is observed without pressure.
- **Mid:** Larger community with relational networks. The player's behaviour within the network reveals their relational centre of gravity.
- **Advanced:** Multi-community world where the player must navigate between groups at different collective altitudes. Integral relating becomes necessary for world-navigation.
- **Mastery:** The player becomes a relational nexus — NPCs seek them out for mentorship, groups cohere around their presence, and the world's developmental ecology responds to their integral relational field.

## 5. Drive Probes

| Drive | Probe |
|-------|-------|
| Eros | Does the player spontaneously seek out higher-altitude NPCs and relational challenges? |
| Agape | Does the player spontaneously serve lower-altitude NPCs without being asked or rewarded? |
| Agency | Does the player maintain their own narrative arc and centre while engaging relationally? |
| Communion | Does the player create genuine community — not just individual relationships but a relational field? |

Dual-domain scoring: vertical axis evaluates spontaneous altitude-navigation in free-play (Eros/Agape); horizontal axis evaluates spontaneous balance of self and other in unstructured relational space (Agency/Communion). All scoring is implicit — the player never knows they are being assessed.
