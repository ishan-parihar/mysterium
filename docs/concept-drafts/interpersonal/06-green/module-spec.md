# Interpersonal / Green — Module Specification

> **Line:** Interpersonal  **Stage:** Green / Pluralistic  **Vibration:** The first genuinely mutual meeting — where relating becomes dialogue, vulnerability becomes practice, and the other is encountered as irreducibly different yet deeply connected.

---

## 1. Capacity Definition

The capacity to engage in authentically mutual relationships characterised by deep listening, genuine vulnerability, pluralistic respect for different relational styles, and conflict-resolution through dialogue rather than contract or dominance. Green interpersonal transcends Orange's transactional coordination — relationships are no longer means to ends but practices of mutual becoming. The player can hold multiple relational perspectives simultaneously, offer and receive vulnerability without collapse, and navigate conflict through care-ethics rather than rule-application.

## 2. Shadow Archetypes

### 2.1 Dark-Addiction: The Relational Enmeshment

Cannot maintain self in relationship. Boundary dissolution masquerading as intimacy. "We are one" as premature fusion rather than earned unity. Loses identity in relating, weaponises vulnerability to prevent separation. The self disappears into the relational field — communion without agency.

### 2.2 Dark-Allergy: The Contractual Individualist

Refuses authentic relating entirely. Remains in Orange contractual coordination — clear expectations, professional boundaries, strategic networking. Dismisses vulnerability as "manipulation" and authenticity as "unprofessional." Agency without communion — the self preserved by refusing genuine meeting.

### 2.3 Golden-Addiction: The Premature Integral Relater

Performs Teal transpersonal connection without genuine Green mutual vulnerability. Claims "I relate to the divine in you" while unable to be genuinely vulnerable with one specific person. Eros without grounding — reaches for integral relating without having mastered mutual vulnerability.

### 2.4 Golden-Allergy: The Authentic Fortress

Authentic relating is present — genuine vulnerability, deep listening, mutual care. But refuses integral deepening. Terrified of transpersonal connection, of dissolving the hard-won authentic self into something larger. "I can be vulnerable but I won't dissolve." Green capacity intact but ceiling enforced.

## 3. Drive-Health Landscape

| Drive | Healthy expression | Pathological expression |
|---|---|---|
| Eros | Reaching toward deeper/wider relational capacity | Bypassing mutual vulnerability for transpersonal performance |
| Agape | Returning to embody authentic presence in each relationship | Collapsing into enmeshment, losing self in service of other |
| Agency | Maintaining sovereign self within mutual vulnerability | Refusing relating (DAll) or refusing growth (GAll) |
| Communion | Genuine joining without self-loss | Premature fusion (DA) or performed unity (GA) |

## 4. Healing Vectors

### 4.1 Heal/Evolve (Agape + Agency)

Agape returns the player to embodied relational presence; Agency maintains the self within that presence. Together they integrate dark shadows: the enmeshed one recovers boundaries (Agency) while remaining relationally present (Agape); the contractual individualist allows authentic feeling (Agape) while maintaining self-coherence (Agency).

### 4.2 Evolve/Heal (Eros + Communion)

Eros reaches toward integral relational capacity; Communion joins with the larger relational field. Together they dissolve golden shadows: the premature integral relater is drawn into genuine communion (not performed); the authentic fortress allows Eros to pull toward transpersonal depth without losing what Green has built.

### 4.3 Integration Criteria

Integration demonstrated when the player can: (a) maintain self-coherence while genuinely vulnerable, (b) offer authentic presence without enmeshment, (c) hold multiple relational perspectives without collapsing into one, (d) allow relational depth to deepen toward integral without resistance or bypass.

## 6. Compound Shadows & Cross-Module Relationships

### 6.1 The Enmeshed Empath (Interpersonal DA × Emotional DA)

Relational enmeshment combined with empathic overwhelm. Lost in others — cannot distinguish own feelings from relational field. Intervention: Agency restoration across both lines simultaneously.

### 6.2 The Professional Loner (Interpersonal DAll × Willpower DAll)

Refuses authentic relating combined with refusal of collaborative will. Effective but unreachable. Intervention: Communion invitation through low-stakes collaborative tasks.

### 6.3 The Spiritual Merger (Interpersonal GA × Spiritual GA)

Premature integral relating combined with premature interfaith transcendence. Performs transpersonal connection without person-to-person vulnerability. Intervention: Grounding in specific, embodied, one-to-one mutual vulnerability.

### 6.4 The Vulnerable Fortress (Interpersonal GAll × Intrapersonal GAll)

Authentic but refuses integral depth, combined with process-self that refuses integration. Stuck at Green ceiling. Intervention: Gentle Eros invitations that honour what Green has built while opening the door.

## 7. Shadow Surfacing Sequence

### 7.1 Progressive Revelation

Shadows surface in order of accessibility: DAll first (refusal is observable), DA second (enmeshment reveals under relational pressure), GAll third (visible only when growth-edge approached), GA last (requires sustained observation of performance vs. depth).

### 7.2 Per-Modality Surfacing Table

| Modality | Primary shadow | Secondary | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|---|
| Deterministic | DAll | DA | Objective measurement reveals avoidance or fusion |
| Language-Reflective | GA | DAll | Relational discourse reveals performance vs. refusal |
| Scenario-Choice | DA | GAll | Dilemmas force choice between enmeshment and boundary |
| Embodied-Somatic | DA | DAll | Body reveals relational collapse or rigidity |
| Strategic-Planning | DAll | GAll | Planning reveals contractual thinking or ceiling |
| Social-Cooperative | GA | DA | Live relating reveals performed depth or boundary loss |
| Immersive-RPG | GAll | GA | Free-play reveals ceiling enforcement or bypass |

### 7.3 Cross-Validation

No shadow diagnosed from a single modality. DA requires convergence from Scenario-Choice + Embodied-Somatic + Social-Cooperative. Golden shadows require convergence from Language-Reflective + Social-Cooperative + Immersive-RPG. Compound shadows require cross-line convergence with Emotional, Willpower, Spiritual, or Intrapersonal modules.
