## 1. Modality Identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Module | Interpersonal / White |
| Modality | Embodied-Somatic |
| Unique Lateral | Body as site of non-dual relating — physical communion, embodied boundary-transparency |
| Core Mechanic | Rhythm-synchronisation and movement-coordination tasks requiring somatic field-awareness |

## 2. Task Architecture

2.1 **Somatic Synchronisation:** Player and NPC partner(s) engage in rhythm-based coordination where success requires the body to move AS the relational field rather than as a separate agent synchronising with another.

2.2 **Boundary-Dissolution Movement:** Movement tasks where the player's avatar and partner avatars share a kinetic field. Optimal performance emerges when the player stops tracking 'my movement' vs. 'their movement'.

2.3 **Embodied Communion Probes:** Touch-based or proximity-based interactions where the body must be open to relational contact without grasping or withdrawing.

## 3. Shadow Detection Mechanics

| Shadow | Somatic Signal |
|---|---|
| 2.1 Grasping-at-Other | Tracking partner's body as separate object; micro-adjustments toward specific partner |
| 2.2 Aversion-to-Other | Somatic withdrawal; maintaining physical buffer; tension patterns in proximity tasks |
| 2.3 Relational Bypassing | Mechanical synchronisation without felt-sense; correct timing but absent embodied presence |
| 2.4 Identity-with-Relater | Leading-body pattern; always initiating contact; somatic self-positioning as connector |

## 4. Drive Residual Detection

| Drive | Somatic Marker |
|---|---|
| Eros | Reaching-forward posture; body anticipates relational novelty |
| Agape | Yielding-backward posture; body accommodates at expense of its own movement |
| Agency | Initiating-impulse; body moves first, then adjusts to field |
| Communion | Waiting-impulse; body freezes until field moves, then follows |

## 5. Difficulty Staircase

5.1 **Entry:** Two-body synchronisation, clear rhythmic structure, slow tempo.

5.2 **Mid:** Multi-body field, complex polyrhythms, tempo shifts requiring field-level tracking.

5.3 **Ceiling:** Arrhythmic field — no external structure. Communion must arise from the bodies themselves without imposed pattern.

## 6. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

6.1 **Catalyst:** Somatic configurations that activate residual body-boundary — e.g., sudden proximity shift, partner rhythm-break.

6.2 **Experience:** The body's grasping or aversion pattern becomes visible through performance disruption.

6.3 **Integration:** Body returns to field-transparency without effortful re-synchronisation. Spontaneous somatic communion.

## 7. Scoring

- Primary: synchronisation-fidelity (field-level, not partner-level)
- Secondary: somatic-transparency index (absence of self-referential movement correction)
- Effort-detection: distinguishes effortless communion from effortful matching
- Input: touch-timing, rhythm-accuracy, proximity-comfort, movement-initiation patterns

## 8. Implementation Notes

- Requires precise input-timing measurement (touch or motion)
- Visual representation must support field-perception over entity-perception
- Haptic feedback (mobile) can enhance embodied presence
- NPC movement must be organic enough to create genuine somatic field
- Accessibility: alternative input modes for players with motor limitations
