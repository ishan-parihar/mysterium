## 1. Modality Identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Module | Interpersonal / White |
| Modality | Strategic-Planning |
| Unique Lateral | Planning within non-dual relating — the paradox of structuring relationships without grasping |
| Core Mechanic | Relational-field coordination tasks requiring foresight without fixation |

## 2. Task Architecture

2.1 **Non-Grasping Coordination:** Player must arrange relational configurations for future events without fixing any entity into a predetermined role. Plans must be held lightly — responsive to field-emergence.

2.2 **Transparent Orchestration:** Multi-entity scenarios where the player coordinates group action. Success requires planning that serves the field rather than imposing structure upon it.

2.3 **Paradox-of-Structure Tasks:** Situations where planning is necessary but any fixed plan reifies boundaries. The player must hold intention without attachment to form.

## 3. Shadow Detection Mechanics

| Shadow | Planning Signal |
|---|---|
| 2.1 Grasping-at-Other | Plans that assign fixed roles to specific entities; over-specifying who does what |
| 2.2 Aversion-to-Other | Plans that minimise relational contact; structuring to avoid certain configurations |
| 2.3 Relational Bypassing | Plans that assume unity without accounting for actual difference; 'we'll all just flow' |
| 2.4 Identity-with-Relater | Plans that centre the planner as coordinator; cannot create self-organising structures |

## 4. Drive Residual Detection

| Drive | Planning Pattern |
|---|---|
| Eros | Over-planning for growth; structuring toward relational novelty at expense of stability |
| Agape | Over-planning for harmony; structuring to prevent any relational friction |
| Agency | Over-structuring; plans too detailed, leaving no room for field-emergence |
| Communion | Under-structuring; plans too vague, abdicating coordination responsibility |

## 5. Task Categories

5.1 **Field-Responsive Planning:** Create a coordination plan, then field-conditions shift. Measured: how quickly and gracefully the plan dissolves and reforms.

5.2 **Invisible Coordination:** Plan group action where no single entity is 'the coordinator'. Measured: whether the plan requires a central node.

5.3 **Temporal Communion:** Plan across time — future relational configurations that honour present field-state without fixing it. Measured: plan-flexibility index.

## 6. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

6.1 **Catalyst:** Planning tasks where the field resists structure — entities don't conform to assigned roles, conditions shift mid-plan.

6.2 **Experience:** The felt-sense of grasping at structure or avoiding coordination responsibility. The discomfort of holding intention without attachment.

6.3 **Integration:** Plans that arise from field-awareness rather than imposed structure. Coordination that serves emergence rather than controlling it.

## 7. Scoring

- Plan-flexibility: how well plans adapt to field-shifts without collapse
- Decentralisation index: degree to which plans avoid single-coordinator dependency
- Role-fluidity: absence of fixed entity-role assignment
- Emergence-allowance: space left in plans for unplanned relational configurations

## 8. Implementation Notes

- Plans must be testable against simulated field-evolution
- NPC entities must sometimes resist plans organically (not arbitrarily)
- Scoring must distinguish genuine field-responsive planning from lack of planning
- Time-pressure varies: some tasks allow contemplation, others require real-time coordination
- Visual planning interface must support fluid, non-hierarchical arrangement
