## 1. Modality Identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Module | Interpersonal / White |
| Modality | Language-Reflective |
| Unique Lateral | Verbal articulation of non-dual relating — how player talks about others reveals residual grasping |
| Core Mechanic | Open-ended relational narration scored by LLM rubric for residual self/other reification |

## 2. Task Architecture

2.1 **Relational Narration Prompts:** After shared experiences with NPC partners, player is invited to describe what happened. The prompt never mentions 'self' or 'other' — it asks about the event-as-such.

2.2 **Pronoun-Transparency Analysis:** LLM scores the degree to which player language reifies fixed self/other boundaries. Markers: frequency of 'I did / they did' vs. field-language; possessive constructions; attribution of agency to discrete entities.

2.3 **Communion Articulation:** Player describes moments of deep connection. LLM detects whether description maintains subtle separation ('I felt connected TO them') vs. transparent field-language ('connection arose').

## 3. Shadow Detection Mechanics

| Shadow | Linguistic Marker |
|---|---|
| 2.1 Grasping-at-Other | Persistent other-reification: 'they were…', 'she always…', fixed characterisation |
| 2.2 Aversion-to-Other | Absence language: avoids describing certain relational moments; deflects to solo experience |
| 2.3 Relational Bypassing | Premature unity-language without experiential grounding: 'we are all one' as formula |
| 2.4 Identity-with-Relater | Self-as-connector language: 'I brought us together', 'I held space', relater-role reification |

## 4. Drive Residual Detection

| Drive | Linguistic Signal |
|---|---|
| Eros | Language of reaching: 'I wanted to go deeper', 'there's more to discover in them' |
| Agape | Language of giving: 'I offered', 'I held', subtle service-identity |
| Agency | Language of initiation: 'I started', 'I led us to', causal self-attribution |
| Communion | Language of merging: 'we became one' without acknowledging the field's own movement |

## 5. Rubric Architecture

5.1 **Transparency Score (0–1):** Degree to which language is free of fixed self/other reification.

5.2 **Authenticity Score (0–1):** Degree to which transparent language reflects genuine experience vs. performed non-duality (detects shadow 2.3).

5.3 **Specificity Score (0–1):** Degree to which descriptions are experientially grounded rather than abstract spiritual formula.

## 6. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

6.1 **Catalyst:** Prompts that invite reification — 'tell me about your partner' (possessive framing as bait).

6.2 **Experience:** Player's language reveals where residual grasping lives — the words that arise unbidden.

6.3 **Integration:** Over sessions, language naturally shifts toward field-transparency without instruction. The system tracks trajectory, not single-point scores.

## 7. Scoring

- Composite of transparency × authenticity × specificity
- Trajectory-weighted: improvement rate matters more than absolute score
- Shadow-specific markers feed compound-shadow detection
- Cross-session linguistic evolution tracked with 28-day half-life

## 8. Implementation Notes

- LLM rubric must distinguish genuine non-dual language from spiritual bypassing (critical differentiation)
- Prompts must be varied enough to prevent formulaic responses
- Player never receives feedback about 'non-dual language' — only narrative acknowledgment
- Minimum 3 relational events before narration prompt to ensure experiential grounding
