## 1. Modality Identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Module | Interpersonal / White |
| Modality | Social-Cooperative |
| Unique Lateral | Live non-dual relating — communion with NPC partners, transparent relational field in real-time |
| Core Mechanic | Cooperative tasks requiring genuine field-communion rather than role-based collaboration |

## 2. Task Architecture

2.1 **Transparent Cooperation:** Joint tasks where success requires acting as a unified field rather than as cooperating individuals. No role-assignment, no turn-taking — simultaneous field-action.

2.2 **Communion Challenges:** Cooperative scenarios where the task itself is relational — the 'goal' is the quality of communion, not an external objective. The field-state IS the success metric.

2.3 **Relational-Field Maintenance:** Extended cooperative sequences where the relational field must remain transparent through changing conditions, challenges, and disruptions.

## 3. Shadow Detection Mechanics

| Shadow | Cooperative Signal |
|---|---|
| 2.1 Grasping-at-Other | Fixating on partner's actions; tracking 'them' rather than the field; over-responding to partner specifically |
| 2.2 Aversion-to-Other | Parallel action disguised as cooperation; maintaining functional distance within shared task |
| 2.3 Relational Bypassing | Performing cooperation without genuine communion; correct actions without felt-connection |
| 2.4 Identity-with-Relater | Positioning as the cooperative one; initiating all joint actions; cannot receive cooperation |

## 4. Drive Residual Detection

| Drive | Cooperative Signal |
|---|---|
| Eros | Pushing cooperation toward deeper intimacy; dissatisfied with current field-depth |
| Agape | Over-giving in cooperation; sacrificing own contribution to support partner |
| Agency | Micro-leading; subtly directing cooperative flow |
| Communion | Micro-deferring; waiting for partner to establish cooperative direction |

## 5. Cooperative Configurations

5.1 **Dyadic Field:** Player + one NPC partner. Simplest relational field. Detects basic boundary-transparency.

5.2 **Triadic Field:** Player + two NPC partners. Introduces field-complexity — can the player be in communion with a field rather than with individuals?

5.3 **Collective Field:** Player + multiple NPCs. The relational field becomes genuinely transpersonal. Individual tracking becomes impossible — only field-awareness works.

## 6. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

6.1 **Catalyst:** Cooperative disruptions — partner behaves unexpectedly, field-conditions shift, familiar cooperation patterns break.

6.2 **Experience:** The arising of grasping, aversion, bypassing, or self-identification within the cooperative field.

6.3 **Integration:** Spontaneous return to field-communion without effortful repair. The cooperation continues through the disruption without the player 'fixing' it.

## 7. Scoring

- Field-communion index: quality of relational field measured by synchronisation, responsiveness, mutual emergence
- Disruption-recovery: speed and effortlessness of return to communion after catalyst
- Role-absence: degree to which player avoids fixed cooperative role
- Compound-shadow detection via cross-modality pattern matching

## 8. Implementation Notes

- NPC partners must exhibit genuine relational intelligence — not scripted cooperation
- Multiplayer mode preferred; NPC simulation must be indistinguishable from human at this stage
- Field-quality metrics require sophisticated real-time analysis of interaction patterns
- Sessions are open-ended — the cooperative field determines natural completion
- No explicit 'win condition' — the field-state itself is the measure
