## 6. Progression Architecture

6.1 **Diagnosis phase:** Detect which residual grasping patterns remain active via all 7 modalities simultaneously.

6.2 **Catalyst phase:** Present relational configurations that activate the specific residual pattern without naming it.

6.3 **Integration phase:** Player's spontaneous dissolution of the pattern IS the integration — no explicit instruction.

## 7. Design Constraints

- No explicit feedback about 'non-duality' — the game never names what it measures at this stage.
- All encounters must be genuinely relational — no solo proxies.
- The system must distinguish authentic transparency from performed transparency (shadow 2.3).
- Infinite checkpoint model: sessions end when the relational field naturally completes.
- Lower-stage interpersonal health must remain active; theta-decay applies to all prior stages.

## 8. Relationship to Adjacent Modules

- **Below (Turquoise):** Integral others — cross-altitude attunement, holonic teams. White transcends and includes this by dissolving the attunement-structure itself.
- **Above:** None. White is the final stage. Mastery here = harvest-readiness on the interpersonal line.
