# Cognitive / White — Immersive-RPG Game

> **Modality:** Immersive-RPG (ecological free-play)  
> **Unique lateral:** Whether direct knowing appears spontaneously in free-play — trans-rational cognition as the player's default mode rather than a special state.

---

## 1. Core Mechanic

**Transparent Cognition in the Wild.** The player navigates a rich, open game-world where cognitive challenges arise organically — not as announced tasks but as features of the environment. The system observes whether the player's cognitive engagement with the world is effortful or transparent. At White, the entire game-world becomes a field of direct knowing.

Key environmental features:
- **Ambient complexity:** The world contains patterns, puzzles, and strategic situations woven into the environment. No UI markers distinguish "cognitive challenges" from scenery. The player either sees or doesn't.
- **Spontaneous knowing moments:** Narrative situations where the correct response requires direct knowing — no time for deliberation, no information for analysis. The player acts from transparency or from habit.
- **Still-mind zones:** Areas where the environment rewards cognitive stillness — rushing or analyzing produces worse outcomes than simply being present.
- **Knower-dissolution encounters:** NPCs and situations that respond differently depending on whether the player approaches as "a knower" or as transparent awareness. The game-world mirrors the player's cognitive stance.

## 2. Shadow Surfacing

### 2.1 Grasping-as-Knowing

Detected when: the player systematically analyzes environmental patterns rather than perceiving them directly. Manifests as exploration behavior — checking, rechecking, mapping — rather than flowing through the world with direct knowing.

### 2.2 Aversion-to-Knowing

Detected when: the player avoids certain world-regions or scenario-types without conscious awareness of avoidance. The open world reveals blind spots through what the player never visits, never engages, never notices.

### 2.3 Spiritual Bypassing

Detected when: the player performs "non-dual" engagement — moving through the world without genuine contact. Detected via missed opportunities: a player in genuine direct knowing notices everything; a bypassing player misses what their framework doesn't include.

### 2.4 Identity-with-Knowing

Detected when: the player's avatar-relationship reveals identification. Choices that benefit "the character" over choices that serve the moment. The player is still playing AS someone rather than being transparent to play itself.

## 3. Drive Detection

- **Eros residue:** Compulsive exploration of "deeper" world-layers — always seeking the hidden, the esoteric, the more-profound. Cannot rest in the surface.
- **Agape residue:** Stays in familiar, simple world-regions. Avoids complexity-rich environments even when narrative invites exploration.
- **Agency residue:** Solo exploration dominates. Avoids NPC collaboration. Approaches the world as territory to be mastered.
- **Communion residue:** Over-reliance on NPC guidance. Cannot navigate the world sovereignly. Seeks collective validation for direct knowing.

Healthy White: moves through the world as the world moves through awareness. No systematic preference for any engagement style.

## 4. Progression Arc

**Phase A — Open observation (sessions 1–4):** The world is presented without guidance. The player's natural engagement style is observed across all environmental features. Baseline cognitive stance mapped.

**Phase B — Subtle invitations (sessions 5–8):** The world begins to respond to the player's cognitive stance — rewarding transparency, gently frustrating grasping. Still-mind zones become more prominent. Knower-dissolution encounters increase. No explicit instruction — the world teaches through consequence.

**Phase C — Full transparency (sessions 9+):** The world and the player's cognition become indistinguishable. Every moment is both challenge and resolution. The game-world IS the player's mind made visible. Integration confirmed when no difference exists between "playing" and "being."

## 5. Infinite Checkpoint & Theta

- **Checkpoint:** The world persists. Player can exit at any moment and return to exactly where they were. No session boundaries within the world.
- **Theta-decay:** Half-life 28 days. Decay manifests as the world becoming "harder" — not objectively but because the player's cognitive transparency has regressed. Re-engagement naturally targets the regressed domains.
- **Session length:** Entirely player-determined. Minimum meaningful session: 5 minutes of world-engagement.
- **Felt-sense reward:** The world becomes luminous. What was once a game-environment becomes a living field of knowing. The player doesn't play the game — the game plays itself through the player's transparent awareness.
