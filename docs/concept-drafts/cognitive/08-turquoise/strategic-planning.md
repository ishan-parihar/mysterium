# Cognitive / White — Strategic-Planning Game

> **Modality:** Strategic-Planning (sequencing and foresight)  
> **Unique lateral:** Planning within non-dual cognition — action arising spontaneously, effortless sequencing. Strategy without a strategist.

---

## 1. Core Mechanic

**Spontaneous Sequencing.** The player faces complex multi-step challenges (resource allocation, path optimization, temporal coordination) where the system measures whether plans emerge spontaneously or through deliberate construction. At White, correct sequences arise whole — not assembled piece by piece.

Key task types:
- **Immediate-whole planning:** Complex scenarios presented briefly. The player must respond with a complete plan before deliberation is possible. Direct knowing produces coherent wholes; grasping produces fragments.
- **Adaptive flow:** Multi-step sequences where conditions change unpredictably. Success requires continuous spontaneous re-planning rather than commitment to a constructed plan.
- **Effortless optimization:** Resource-allocation problems with many variables. The system measures whether the player converges on optimal solutions through iterative analysis or immediate apprehension.
- **Wu-wei strategy:** Competitive scenarios where the winning strategy is non-action — waiting for the situation to resolve itself. Tests whether the player can plan to not-plan.

## 2. Shadow Surfacing

### 2.1 Grasping-as-Knowing

Detected when: the player constructs plans step-by-step rather than apprehending them whole. Manifests as sequential response patterns rather than simultaneous specification. High accuracy but effortful process.

### 2.2 Aversion-to-Knowing

Detected when: specific planning domains produce avoidance — the player defers, delegates, or gives incomplete plans in circumscribed areas. Strategic blind spots parallel cognitive blind spots.

### 2.3 Spiritual Bypassing

Detected when: the player refuses to plan ("planning is grasping") but this refusal produces worse outcomes than spontaneous planning would. Non-action used as avoidance rather than genuine wu-wei.

### 2.4 Identity-with-Knowing

Detected when: plans consistently center a protagonist/agent. The player cannot generate strategies where no central actor exists — distributed, emergent solutions are inaccessible because they require dissolving the planner.

## 3. Drive Detection

- **Eros residue:** Plans consistently reach toward maximal outcomes — optimization addiction. Cannot leave value on the table even when "good enough" is appropriate.
- **Agape residue:** Plans consistently under-reach — satisficing when the situation calls for full engagement.
- **Agency residue:** Plans center individual action. Strategies requiring receptivity, patience, or yielding are systematically underused.
- **Communion residue:** Plans dissolve into collective process. Strategies requiring decisive individual action are systematically avoided.

Healthy White: planning style matches situation. No systematic bias toward any strategy type.

## 4. Progression Arc

**Phase A — Strategic baseline (sessions 1–3):** Complex planning tasks across multiple domains. Establish whether planning is spontaneous or constructed, and map domain-specific patterns.

**Phase B — Spontaneity training (sessions 4–7):** Time-compressed planning tasks that make deliberation impossible. Wu-wei scenarios that reward non-action. Adaptive-flow challenges that punish plan-commitment. The player learns to let strategy arise.

**Phase C — Ecological strategy (sessions 8+):** Planning challenges embedded in ongoing narrative without announcement. The player must respond strategically to emerging situations without shifting into "planning mode." Strategy as continuous spontaneous response.

## 5. Infinite Checkpoint & Theta

- **Checkpoint:** Each planning scenario is self-contained. Player can exit between scenarios.
- **Theta-decay:** Half-life 28 days. Decay manifests as return of deliberate construction in previously spontaneous planning domains.
- **Session length:** 5–15 minutes typical. Minimum meaningful session: one planning scenario.
- **Felt-sense reward:** The ease of knowing what to do without figuring it out. Complex situations that once required analysis now resolve themselves through transparent presence.
