# Cognitive / White — Language-Reflective Game

> **Modality:** Language-Reflective (LLM-powered verbal assessment)  
> **Unique lateral:** How the player talks about knowing reveals residual grasping. Language is the fingerprint of the mind's relationship to its own cognition.

---

## 1. Core Mechanic

**Transparent Speech.** The player is presented with cognitive experiences (puzzles solved, patterns recognized, insights arrived at) and asked to describe what happened. The LLM analyzes not the content but the structural relationship to knowing: Does the language grasp? Does it claim? Does it avoid? Does it identify?

Key interaction types:
- **Post-task reflection:** After deterministic tasks, the player describes their experience. "How did you know that?" — the answer reveals everything.
- **Knowing-koans:** Paradoxical prompts about cognition itself. "Describe what happens before you know something." Responses reveal direct experience vs. conceptual narrative.
- **Silence-and-speech:** Periods of silence followed by spontaneous articulation. The LLM assesses whether speech arises from stillness or from discomfort with not-speaking.
- **Meta-cognitive dialogue:** The LLM engages the player in conversation about knowing, tracking when language becomes self-referential vs. transparent.

## 2. Shadow Surfacing

### 2.1 Grasping-as-Knowing

Linguistic markers: excessive analytical frameworks, need to explain rather than describe, language that maps rather than points. "I figured it out by..." vs. "It was just there."

### 2.2 Aversion-to-Knowing

Linguistic markers: deflection when certain topics arise, vagueness in specific domains, "I don't know" used as avoidance rather than honest report. The LLM tracks which knowing-domains produce linguistic contraction.

### 2.3 Spiritual Bypassing

Linguistic markers: non-dual vocabulary used as shield ("there is no knower"), premature transcendence language, spiritual jargon that distances rather than reveals. The LLM distinguishes lived report from borrowed framework.

### 2.4 Identity-with-Knowing

Linguistic markers: persistent first-person ownership ("I saw," "my insight"), inability to describe knowing without a subject, discomfort with impersonal constructions about cognition.

## 3. Drive Detection

- **Eros residue:** Language reaches toward higher formulations — always trying to say something MORE profound. Compulsive depth-seeking in speech.
- **Agape residue:** Language returns to simplicity prematurely — "it's just..." used to avoid full articulation of complex knowing.
- **Agency residue:** Language centers the self as agent — "I grasped it," "I penetrated the pattern."
- **Communion residue:** Language dissolves self into collective — "we all know this," "it's obvious" — avoiding sovereignty of individual knowing.

Healthy White: language is precise, impersonal, and alive. Neither claiming nor disclaiming.

## 4. Progression Arc

**Phase A — Listening (sessions 1–3):** The LLM primarily listens. Minimal prompting. The player's natural language about cognition is baselined. Shadow patterns mapped across multiple reflection contexts.

**Phase B — Mirroring (sessions 4–7):** The LLM reflects the player's language back, highlighting grasping structures without judgment. "You said 'I figured it out' — what happens if you describe it without an 'I'?" Gentle linguistic experiments.

**Phase C — Transparent dialogue (sessions 8+):** Full conversational engagement where the LLM and player explore the edge of language and knowing together. Integration confirmed when language becomes consistently non-grasping without becoming artificially impersonal.

## 5. Infinite Checkpoint & Theta

- **Checkpoint:** Each reflection prompt is a complete unit. Player can exit after any response.
- **Theta-decay:** Half-life 28 days. Decay manifests as return of grasping-language in previously transparent domains.
- **Session length:** 5–20 minutes typical. Minimum meaningful session: one reflection prompt + response.
- **Felt-sense reward:** The experience of speaking from stillness rather than from the need to speak. Language becomes lighter, more precise, more alive.
