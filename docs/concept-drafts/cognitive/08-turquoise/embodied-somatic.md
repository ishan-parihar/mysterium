# Cognitive / White — Embodied-Somatic Game

> **Modality:** Embodied-Somatic (body-based cognition)  
> **Unique lateral:** The body as site of trans-rational cognition — still-mind embodiment, effortless physical complexity. Knowing expressed through movement rather than thought.

---

## 1. Core Mechanic

**Effortless Complexity in Motion.** The player performs physically complex cognitive-motor tasks (rhythm sequences, spatial navigation, multi-limb coordination) while the system measures whether the body operates from direct knowing or deliberate control. At White, the body-mind is unified — complex action arises without a controller.

Key task types:
- **Still-mind rhythm:** Complex polyrhythmic patterns that degrade under conscious control. Success requires releasing the body to its own intelligence.
- **Spatial direct-knowing:** Navigation challenges solvable only by felt-sense — no visual map, no deliberation time. The body knows where to go.
- **Effortless escalation:** Physical complexity increases continuously. The score is the threshold where effort-signatures appear in motor output (timing jitter, force inconsistency).
- **Breath-cognition unity:** Cognitive tasks synchronized with breath. Performance reveals whether breath and thought are unified or competing for attention.

## 2. Shadow Surfacing

### 2.1 Grasping-as-Knowing

Detected when: the player consciously controls complex motor sequences — performance degrades as complexity increases because the deliberate mind cannot track all variables. Manifests as increasing timing jitter under load.

### 2.2 Aversion-to-Knowing

Detected when: specific movement domains show avoidance or contraction. The body refuses certain patterns — not from incapacity but from the same blind-spot structure that appears cognitively. Somatic flinch in specific domains.

### 2.3 Spiritual Bypassing

Detected when: the player performs "effortlessly" but with subtle dissociation — the body moves but awareness is not fully present. Detected via response to unexpected perturbations: genuine embodied knowing adapts instantly; dissociated performance breaks.

### 2.4 Identity-with-Knowing

Detected when: performance improves with self-referential framing ("feel YOUR body") vs. impersonal framing ("let movement happen"). The body still needs a "mover" to move well.

## 3. Drive Detection

- **Eros residue:** Compulsive escalation-seeking — pushing toward greater complexity rather than resting at the effortless edge.
- **Agape residue:** Premature simplification — retreating to simpler patterns before complexity demands it.
- **Agency residue:** Performance improves in solo-body tasks vs. tasks requiring receptivity to external rhythm.
- **Communion residue:** Performance improves in externally-paced tasks vs. self-generated rhythm.

Healthy White: no systematic difference across framing conditions. The body responds to what is.

## 4. Progression Arc

**Phase A — Embodied baseline (sessions 1–2):** Full motor-cognitive battery. Establish effortless-complexity ceiling and map domains where effort-signatures appear earliest.

**Phase B — Paradoxical embodiment (sessions 3–6):** Tasks where trying harder produces worse motor output. The system rewards release of control. Still-mind rhythm sequences lengthen. Breath-cognition unity tasks introduced.

**Phase C — Unified field (sessions 7+):** All modalities interleaved. Cognitive and motor demands simultaneous and unpredictable. The player's body-mind must operate as a single transparent system. No separation between "thinking" and "moving."

## 5. Infinite Checkpoint & Theta

- **Checkpoint:** Every 60-second movement block is a complete unit. Player can exit at any block boundary.
- **Theta-decay:** Half-life 28 days. Decay manifests as return of effort-signatures in previously effortless motor-cognitive domains.
- **Session length:** 3–12 minutes typical. Minimum meaningful session: 2 movement blocks.
- **Felt-sense reward:** The pleasure of the body moving itself — complex action without a doer. Physical grace as direct feedback of cognitive transparency.
