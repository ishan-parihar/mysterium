# Cognitive / White — Social-Cooperative Game

> **Modality:** Social-Cooperative (shared cognition)  
> **Unique lateral:** Shared trans-rational knowing — communion in direct knowing, non-verbal cognitive attunement. Thinking together without thinking.

---

## 1. Core Mechanic

**Collective Direct Knowing.** Two or more players face cognitive challenges that require shared knowing without explicit communication. At White, individual minds become transparent to each other — not through empathy or theory-of-mind (prior stages) but through shared participation in knowing itself.

Key task types:
- **Non-verbal coordination:** Complex patterns requiring synchronized response from multiple players without communication channels. Success requires attunement beyond inference.
- **Shared-field problems:** Puzzles where each player holds partial information but the solution requires a unified knowing that transcends information-sharing. No time for deliberate communication.
- **Emergent strategy:** Competitive scenarios against AI opponents where the player-group must act as a unified cognitive field — no leader, no plan, just coherent collective action.
- **Transparent disagreement:** Scenarios where players' direct knowings initially conflict. Resolution requires neither compromise nor dominance but a deeper knowing that includes both.

## 2. Shadow Surfacing

### 2.1 Grasping-as-Knowing

Detected when: the player attempts to explicitly coordinate — sending signals, establishing protocols, constructing shared frameworks. The mind grasps at communication rather than resting in shared knowing.

### 2.2 Aversion-to-Knowing

Detected when: the player withdraws from shared cognitive fields in specific domains — the same blind spots that appear individually now manifest as inability to join collective knowing in those areas.

### 2.3 Spiritual Bypassing

Detected when: the player claims attunement but acts incoherently with the group — performing communion while actually operating solo. Detected via correlation between player's actions and group-optimal actions.

### 2.4 Identity-with-Knowing

Detected when: the player cannot dissolve into collective knowing without losing coherence — either dominates the group-field (maintaining individual identity) or dissolves entirely (losing sovereign contribution). Cannot be both sovereign and joined.

## 3. Drive Detection

- **Eros residue:** Compulsive reaching toward "deeper" group connection — trying to make the shared field more profound rather than letting it be what it is.
- **Agape residue:** Premature settling into simple coordination — refusing the full complexity of genuine shared knowing.
- **Agency residue:** Consistently leads or initiates in group-field tasks. Cannot receive direction from the collective without asserting individual knowing.
- **Communion residue:** Consistently follows or merges. Cannot contribute sovereign knowing to the collective without dissolving into it.

Healthy White: sovereign and joined simultaneously. Contributes unique knowing while participating in shared field.

## 4. Progression Arc

**Phase A — Attunement baseline (sessions 1–3):** Paired and group tasks with varying communication constraints. Establish the player's natural mode of cognitive sharing and map where explicit coordination replaces direct attunement.

**Phase B — Communication stripping (sessions 4–7):** Progressive removal of communication channels. Tasks that punish explicit coordination and reward non-verbal attunement. The player learns to trust shared knowing.

**Phase C — Ecological communion (sessions 8+):** Group challenges embedded in ongoing narrative. No announcement of "cooperative mode." The player must seamlessly shift between sovereign and shared cognition as context requires. Integration confirmed when no mode-switching effort is detectable.

## 5. Infinite Checkpoint & Theta

- **Checkpoint:** Each group scenario is a complete unit. Player can exit between scenarios. Async pairing ensures partners are always available.
- **Theta-decay:** Half-life 28 days. Decay manifests as return of explicit coordination in previously attuned domains.
- **Session length:** 5–20 minutes typical. Minimum meaningful session: one paired scenario.
- **Felt-sense reward:** The uncanny pleasure of thinking together without trying — knowing what the other knows without being told. Cognitive intimacy beyond communication.
