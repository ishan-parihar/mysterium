# Cognitive / White — Module Specification

> **Line:** Cognitive  **Stage:** White / Super-Integral  **Vibration:** Trans-rational direct knowing — the mind transparent to itself, cognition serving being without grasping.

---

## 1. Capacity Definition

Trans-rational cognition: direct knowing that does not require conceptual mediation. The cognitive apparatus operates at full complexity (n=5 occasional working-memory, effortless task-switching, spontaneous pattern recognition) while remaining transparent — thought arises and dissolves without the knower grasping at it. Cognitive load coexists with spaciousness. The mind is no longer an instrument wielded by a self; it is a clear window through which knowing flows. Below this (Turquoise) the mind integrates all perspectives via vision-logic; here the mind releases the need to integrate and simply knows.

## 2. Shadow Archetypes

White shadows are not emergent/submergent in the standard sense — there is no stage above. Instead they are **residual grasping patterns** carried forward from all prior stages.

### 2.1 Residual Grasping-as-Knowing

The mind still reaches for concepts, still needs to "understand" rather than directly know. Residue of Orange analytical addiction and Turquoise meta-systematic compulsion. Manifests as subtle preference for complexity over simplicity, for mapping over being.

### 2.2 Residual Aversion-to-Knowing

Certain knowings remain avoided — blind spots the awareness refuses to illuminate. Residue of all prior dark-allergy patterns accumulated across the developmental arc. Manifests as inexplicable gaps in otherwise panoramic awareness.

### 2.3 Residual Spiritual Bypassing

Claims trans-rational knowing while still operating from conceptual frameworks. "I am beyond thought" as itself a thought. The subtlest golden-addiction — using non-dual language to avoid the rawness of direct contact. Residue of Green/Turquoise spiritual inflation.

### 2.4 Residual Identity-with-Knowing

Still identifies AS the knower rather than being transparent to knowing. The final self-grasping: awareness aware of itself as "mine." Residue of the fundamental subject-object structure that persists even after all content-level shadows are resolved.

## 3. Drive-Health Landscape

At White, all four drives express in **dynamic equanimity**. The game does not measure drive strength but detects residual imbalance:

- **Eros / Agape (vertical):** Neither compulsive reaching toward transcendence nor compulsive returning to ground. Both arise spontaneously as context requires.
- **Agency / Communion (horizontal):** Sovereign and joined simultaneously. No oscillation — both present in each moment.
- **Health indicator:** The ratio of spontaneous-to-effortful drive expression. Healthy White shows >90% spontaneous. Residual grasping manifests as micro-efforts detectable in response latency and pattern.

## 4. Healing Vectors

### 4.1 Release of Grasping

Catalyst presents cognitive complexity that invites grasping. Healing occurs when the player meets complexity without reaching — direct knowing replaces deliberation. Each successful release weakens the grasping-as-knowing residue.

### 4.2 Illumination of Aversion

Catalyst presents the specific knowings the player's awareness avoids. Healing occurs when the blind spot is seen without flinching — not understood, but simply illuminated. The aversion dissolves in the light of non-judgmental awareness.

### 4.3 Integration Criteria

White integration is confirmed when: (a) cognitive performance remains high without detectable effort signatures, (b) no systematic blind spots persist across sessions, (c) language about knowing is transparent rather than self-referential, (d) drive expression shows no residual oscillation pattern.

## 6. Compound Shadows & Cross-Module Relationships

### 6.1 The Transparent Grasper (Cognitive × Intrapersonal)

Residual knowing-grasping fuses with residual self-grasping: the player subtly identifies with awareness itself. "I am the one who sees" — transparent to content but opaque to the seeing.

### 6.2 The Effortless Striver (Cognitive × Willpower)

Residual knowing-grasping fuses with residual will-grasping: the player "effortlessly" tries. Performance is high and appears spontaneous, but micro-latency signatures reveal subtle volitional effort beneath the surface.

### 6.3 The Bypassing Knower (Cognitive × Spiritual)

Residual spiritual bypassing fuses with knowing-grasping: claims direct knowing while still operating through conceptual mediation. Language is non-dual but the underlying process is still representational.

### 6.4 The Luminous Avoider (Cognitive × Emotional)

Residual aversion-to-knowing fuses with residual aversion-to-feeling: panoramic awareness with specific affective blind spots. The mind is clear everywhere except where feeling-knowing intersects.

## 7. Shadow Surfacing Sequence

### 7.1 Invitation Phase (Sessions 1–3)

Present high-complexity cognitive tasks in spacious contexts. Observe whether the player grasps or rests. No confrontation — pure observation. Baseline effort-signatures and spontaneity indices established.

### 7.2 Illumination Phase (Sessions 4–8)

Gradually introduce stimuli targeting detected residual patterns. Grasping-as-knowing: tasks that reward non-deliberation. Aversion: stimuli in the player's blind-spot domains. Bypassing: tasks that distinguish genuine direct knowing from conceptual mimicry. Identity: tasks where "the knower" must dissolve for success.

### 7.3 Integration Phase (Sessions 9+)

All four residual patterns presented simultaneously in ecological contexts. Integration confirmed when compound shadows no longer produce detectable signatures. The player's cognitive system operates as a transparent whole — knowing without a knower, complexity without strain, presence without gaps.
