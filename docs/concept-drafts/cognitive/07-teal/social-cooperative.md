# Cognitive / Turquoise — Social-Cooperative

## 1. Game Identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Modality | Social-Cooperative (collaborative cognition, group dynamics) |
| Unique probe | Collaborative integral reasoning — cross-altitude attunement, mentorship across developmental stages, collective vision-logic |
| Core loop | Player engages with AI agents operating at different cognitive altitudes; must attune to each agent's stage, translate integral insights across levels, and co-construct solutions no single altitude could produce alone |
| Session length | 5–12 minutes (infinite checkpoint) |

## 2. Mechanics

### 2.1 Cross-Altitude Attunement

AI agents represent Red, Amber, Orange, Green, and Turquoise cognitive styles. Player must identify each agent's altitude from their reasoning patterns, then communicate integral insights in language each agent can receive. Scored on: altitude-identification accuracy, translation fidelity, agent-comprehension (measured by agent's subsequent reasoning quality).

### 2.2 Collective Holonic Problem-Solving

A problem is distributed across agents at different altitudes. Each agent holds a piece valid AT THEIR LEVEL. Player must integrate contributions without dismissing any — Red's power-insight, Amber's structural insight, Orange's strategic insight, Green's relational insight — into a Turquoise whole. Scored on: integration completeness, no-stage-dismissed metric, solution quality.

### 2.3 Developmental Mentorship

Player guides a lower-altitude agent toward the next stage WITHOUT imposing Turquoise prematurely. Must meet the agent where they are and offer the minimal catalyst for their specific growth-edge. Scored on: developmental appropriateness, agent-growth (post-interaction altitude shift), absence of stage-skipping.

## 3. Shadow Surfacing

| Shadow | Social signature | Diagnostic signal |
|---|---|---|
| DA (Meta-Mapper) | Maps every agent's altitude but never genuinely connects; relates through frameworks, not presence | High altitude-identification; low agent-rapport; mechanical translation |
| DAll (Green Pluralist) | Treats all agents as equals; refuses to acknowledge altitude differences | No altitude-differentiation in communication; uniform approach regardless of agent-stage |
| GA (Premature Non-Dualist) | Bypasses collaborative process; offers 'transcendent' solutions agents cannot receive | Solutions disconnected from agent-altitudes; low agent-comprehension scores |
| GAll (Integral Fortress) | Excellent cross-altitude attunement but refuses vulnerability; mentors from above, never alongside | High translation quality; absence of mutual-learning indicators; one-directional flow |

## 4. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

**Catalyst:** Problems require genuine cross-altitude collaboration. No single altitude (including Turquoise) can solve them alone. The player NEEDS the lower-altitude agents' contributions — integral cognition here means orchestrating a developmental ecology, not imposing a framework.

**Experience:** Player feels the difference between mapping others' altitudes and genuinely attuning to them. The Meta-Mapper feels the loneliness of framework-mediated relating. The Green Pluralist feels the cost of refusing to see altitude. The Premature Non-Dualist feels the disconnect of bypassing. The Integral Fortress feels the invitation to be changed by the encounter.

**Integration:** Adaptive scenarios target the player's specific relational shadow. Success requires BOTH altitude-awareness AND genuine presence. The game rewards integral relating — seeing the whole developmental field while being authentically IN relationship with each part.

## 5. Progression & Infinite Checkpoint

| Phase | Focus | Unlock condition |
|---|---|---|
| Diagnosis | Baseline cross-altitude attunement, translation, mentorship | Entry |
| Healing | Shadow-specific social scenarios targeting detected relational patterns | Shadow pattern above threshold |
| Evolution | Scenarios requiring simultaneous integral clarity AND mutual vulnerability | Drive-health > 0.6 |
| Maintenance | Novel agent-configurations preventing strategy-memorisation; theta-decay | Post-evolution |

Checkpoints between collaborative cycles. AI agents provide naturalistic interaction — player experiences collaboration, not assessment. Difficulty adapts via agent-altitude spread, problem complexity, and shadow-targeting intensity.
