# Cognitive / Turquoise — Language-Reflective

## 1. Game Identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Modality | Language-Reflective (LLM-scored verbal/written expression) |
| Unique probe | How the player TALKS about wholes — verbal articulation reveals genuine integral depth vs. performative use of integral vocabulary |
| Core loop | Player encounters complex phenomena and articulates understanding; LLM rubric scores for structural complexity, integration, and authenticity |
| Session length | 5–15 minutes (infinite checkpoint) |

## 2. Mechanics

### 2.1 Integral Articulation Prompts

Player is presented with multi-domain phenomena (e.g., a social crisis with biological, psychological, cultural, and systemic dimensions). Player articulates understanding in free-text. LLM scores on: number of perspectives integrated, hierarchical depth (flat listing vs. nested holarchy), dialectical structure (thesis-antithesis-synthesis-meta-synthesis), and self-referential awareness.

### 2.2 Framework Translation

Player receives a phenomenon described in ONE integral framework and must re-articulate it in a DIFFERENT framework without losing structural fidelity. Tests genuine understanding vs. vocabulary parroting. Scored on: structural isomorphism, information preservation, creative bridging.

### 2.3 Meta-Commentary

Player is shown their OWN previous responses and asked to comment on what level of cognition produced them. Tests recursive self-awareness of one's own cognitive operations. Scored on: accuracy of self-assessment, identification of blind spots, willingness to acknowledge limitations.

## 3. Shadow Surfacing

| Shadow | Linguistic signature | LLM detection rubric |
|---|---|---|
| DA (Meta-Mapper) | Excessive framework-stacking; every sentence references a model; no direct phenomenological language | Framework-density > threshold; experiential-language < threshold |
| DAll (Green Pluralist) | Refuses hierarchical language; 'all perspectives are valid'; no ranking or nesting | Absence of vertical differentiation; flat conjunction structures |
| GA (Premature Non-Dualist) | Uses trans-rational vocabulary ('beyond concepts', 'just is') without demonstrating integral reasoning first | Trans-rational claims without preceding integral demonstration |
| GAll (Integral Fortress) | Brilliant integral articulation that systematically avoids vulnerability, uncertainty, or dissolution language | High structural complexity; absence of surrender/mystery/not-knowing language |

## 4. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

**Catalyst:** Prompts are un-answerable from any single stage. Green multi-perspectivalism produces flat lists. Only genuine vision-logic produces nested holarchies. The LLM rubric makes the difference visible through scoring.

**Experience:** Player encounters the gap between vocabulary and genuine cognitive structure. The Meta-Mapper sees framework-addiction reflected. The Green Pluralist feels the cost of refusing depth. The Premature Non-Dualist is asked to SHOW the work. The Integral Fortress is invited toward mystery.

**Integration:** Adaptive prompts target the player's specific shadow. Healing prompts invite the Meta-Mapper into direct language. Evolution prompts invite the Integral Fortress toward not-knowing. Each response is a practice — not just a test.

## 5. Progression & Infinite Checkpoint

| Phase | Focus | Unlock condition |
|---|---|---|
| Diagnosis | Baseline articulation across 3 prompt types | Entry |
| Healing | Shadow-specific prompts targeting detected linguistic patterns | Shadow signature above threshold |
| Evolution | Prompts requiring simultaneous integral precision AND trans-rational openness | Drive-health > 0.6 |
| Maintenance | Periodic re-articulation; theta-decay prevention | Post-evolution |

Checkpoints after each prompt-response cycle. LLM scoring is invisible to player — they experience a conversation, not a test. Rubrics are fixed; LLM applies them consistently. Player never sees scores directly.
