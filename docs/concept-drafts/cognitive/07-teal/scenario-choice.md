# Cognitive / Turquoise — Scenario-Choice

## 1. Game Identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Modality | Scenario-Choice (branching dilemmas, choice-as-assessment) |
| Unique probe | Integral dilemmas requiring the player to hold ALL previous stages as valid partial truths while still committing to decisive action |
| Core loop | Player faces multi-stage dilemmas where every prior cognitive stage offers a legitimate partial answer; the integral response honours all while transcending |
| Session length | 6–14 minutes (infinite checkpoint) |

## 2. Mechanics

### 2.1 Holarchic Dilemmas

Each scenario presents a problem with solutions valid at Red (power), Amber (rule), Orange (strategy), Green (inclusion), AND Turquoise (integration). Player must demonstrate they SEE each stage's validity before choosing. Choices that dismiss lower stages score as shadow. Choices that refuse to choose score as shadow. Only choices that include-and-transcend score as integral.

### 2.2 Commitment Under Irreducible Complexity

After demonstrating multi-stage awareness, player must ACT. The dilemma is designed so no action satisfies all stages simultaneously. Integral cognition means choosing WITH FULL AWARENESS of what is sacrificed. Scored on: breadth of acknowledged trade-offs, depth of reasoning, decisiveness despite complexity.

### 2.3 Recursive Consequence Chains

Choices cascade. Second-order consequences reveal whether the player's initial reasoning was genuinely integral or merely performed. A choice that looked integral but was actually Green-disguised-as-Turquoise produces Green-level consequences. The game makes cognitive altitude visible through narrative outcomes.

## 3. Shadow Surfacing

| Shadow | Choice pattern | Diagnostic signal |
|---|---|---|
| DA (Meta-Mapper) | Endlessly analyses options; requests more information; delays commitment | High time-on-choice; frequent 'explore more' selections; low action-rate |
| DAll (Green Pluralist) | Chooses the most inclusive option without ranking; refuses to sacrifice any perspective | Consistent selection of 'honour all' options even when structurally incoherent |
| GA (Premature Non-Dualist) | Chooses 'transcend the dilemma' options without demonstrating integral reasoning | Selects meta-options without engaging stage-specific analysis first |
| GAll (Integral Fortress) | Brilliant analysis, decisive action, but systematically avoids choices requiring ego-surrender | Avoids all options coded with dissolution/vulnerability/not-knowing |

## 4. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

**Catalyst:** Dilemmas are constructed so that ONLY genuine vision-logic can navigate them. Green produces paralysis or false-inclusion. Orange produces reductive solutions. The scenario structure itself is the catalyst.

**Experience:** Player feels the weight of integral commitment — the loneliness of seeing all stages and still having to choose. The Meta-Mapper feels analysis-paralysis made visible. The Green Pluralist feels the cost of refusing hierarchy. The Premature Non-Dualist is forced to show their work. The Integral Fortress encounters dilemmas where the only integral choice requires surrender.

**Integration:** Consequence chains reward genuine integration and expose performance. Over time, the player develops the capacity to hold-and-act simultaneously — the defining Turquoise cognitive skill.

## 5. Progression & Infinite Checkpoint

| Phase | Focus | Unlock condition |
|---|---|---|
| Diagnosis | Baseline dilemma responses across 4 scenario types | Entry |
| Healing | Shadow-specific scenarios targeting detected choice patterns | Shadow pattern above threshold |
| Evolution | Dilemmas requiring simultaneous integral precision AND trans-rational surrender | Drive-health > 0.6 |
| Maintenance | Novel dilemmas preventing pattern-memorisation; theta-decay prevention | Post-evolution |

Checkpoints between scenarios. Each scenario is self-contained. Player never sees scoring — they experience a narrative with consequences. Adaptive difficulty adjusts scenario complexity and shadow-targeting based on cumulative profile.
