# Cognitive / Turquoise — Strategic-Planning

## 1. Game Identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Modality | Strategic-Planning (goal architecture, resource allocation, temporal reasoning) |
| Unique probe | Planning within integral complexity — holonic goal architecture, meta-planning (plans about how to plan), recursive sub-goal management |
| Core loop | Player constructs and executes multi-layered plans where goals are holons — simultaneously wholes (with sub-plans) and parts (of larger strategic arcs) |
| Session length | 5–14 minutes (infinite checkpoint) |

## 2. Mechanics

### 2.1 Holonic Goal Architecture

Player manages a goal-tree where each node is both a goal (with sub-goals) and a sub-goal (of a parent). Resources flow up and down the holarchy. Optimising at one level may sub-optimise another. Player must find the integral balance — the plan that honours all levels simultaneously. Scored on: cross-level coherence, resource efficiency, holarchic integrity.

### 2.2 Meta-Planning (Plans About Plans)

The planning STRATEGY itself must be chosen. Player selects not just what to do but HOW to plan — top-down decomposition, bottom-up emergence, middle-out integration, or adaptive switching. Different problem structures reward different meta-strategies. Scored on: meta-strategy appropriateness, flexibility of switching, awareness of planning-level.

### 2.3 Recursive Contingency

Plans must account for plans failing. Contingency plans have their own contingencies. Player builds recursive safety nets while maintaining forward momentum. The game introduces disruptions at multiple levels simultaneously. Scored on: recovery speed, structural preservation under disruption, depth of contingency preparation.

## 3. Shadow Surfacing

| Shadow | Planning signature | Diagnostic signal |
|---|---|---|
| DA (Meta-Mapper) | Over-plans; infinite contingency depth; never executes; the plan becomes the product | Plan complexity >> execution; high contingency depth; low action-initiation |
| DAll (Green Pluralist) | Flat plans; refuses hierarchical goal-structure; all goals treated as equal priority | No nesting; equal resource distribution; absence of prioritisation |
| GA (Premature Non-Dualist) | Skips planning; 'trusts emergence'; acts without structure; occasional brilliance, frequent chaos | Low plan-depth; high improvisation; inconsistent outcomes |
| GAll (Integral Fortress) | Brilliant holonic plans but refuses to include 'surrender to unknown' as valid strategic element | Complete plans with no uncertainty-tolerance; rigid execution; breaks under novel disruption |

## 4. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

**Catalyst:** Problems are unsolvable by any single planning paradigm. Top-down alone fails (too rigid). Bottom-up alone fails (too chaotic). Only meta-planning — choosing HOW to plan based on the problem's structure — succeeds.

**Experience:** Player feels the difference between planning-addiction and genuine strategic vision-logic. The Meta-Mapper feels the cost of infinite preparation. The Green Pluralist feels the cost of refusing hierarchy. The Premature Non-Dualist feels the cost of structurelessness. The Integral Fortress encounters problems where the plan must include its own dissolution.

**Integration:** Adaptive difficulty holds player at their genuine planning edge. Success requires BOTH structural precision AND adaptive flexibility. The game rewards plans that are holonic — complete at every level while remaining open to revision.

## 5. Progression & Infinite Checkpoint

| Phase | Focus | Unlock condition |
|---|---|---|
| Diagnosis | Baseline holonic planning, meta-strategy selection, contingency depth | Entry |
| Healing | Shadow-specific planning challenges targeting detected patterns | Shadow pattern above threshold |
| Evolution | Problems requiring simultaneous structural mastery AND surrender to emergence | Drive-health > 0.6 |
| Maintenance | Novel planning domains preventing strategy-memorisation; theta-decay | Post-evolution |

Checkpoints between planning cycles. Each cycle is self-contained with clear outcome feedback. Difficulty adapts via holarchy depth, disruption frequency, and meta-strategy demands.
