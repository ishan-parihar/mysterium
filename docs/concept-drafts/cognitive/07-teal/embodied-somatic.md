# Cognitive / Turquoise — Embodied-Somatic

## 1. Game Identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Modality | Embodied-Somatic (body-as-instrument, physical cognition) |
| Unique probe | The body as site of integral cognition — embodied vision-logic, physical holding of complexity without collapse |
| Core loop | Player uses rhythm, gesture, breath, and spatial movement to solve problems requiring simultaneous multi-level coordination — the body DOING what vision-logic THINKS |
| Session length | 4–10 minutes (infinite checkpoint) |

## 2. Mechanics

### 2.1 Holonic Rhythm Coordination

Four simultaneous rhythm streams (mapped to touch/tap zones). Each stream is independently meaningful AND part of a larger polyrhythmic whole. Player must maintain all four while detecting emergent cross-stream patterns. Physical analogue of n=4 holonic working memory. Scored on: per-stream accuracy, cross-stream coherence, emergent-pattern detection.

### 2.2 Gestural Meta-Shifting

Player performs gesture sequences where the RULE governing which gesture to perform shifts mid-sequence. Physical analogue of meta-WCST. The body must shift not just the action but the principle-of-action. Scored on: shift-latency, post-shift accuracy, recursive depth (gesture-rule vs. meta-rule shifts).

### 2.3 Breath-Gated Complexity Holding

Increasingly complex spatial patterns are presented. Player must hold the pattern in body-awareness (tracked via input timing synchronised to breath cycle). Complexity increases until the body-mind system reaches its genuine edge. Rushed breath collapses complexity. Scored on: maximum complexity held, breath-coherence under load, recovery after failure.

## 3. Shadow Surfacing

| Shadow | Somatic signature | Diagnostic signal |
|---|---|---|
| DA (Meta-Mapper) | Body becomes rigid; over-controls rhythm; cannot let the body lead | High precision, low flow; elevated tension indicators; mechanical timing |
| DAll (Green Pluralist) | Body stays in Green-level flow; refuses precision required for holonic coordination | Smooth but undifferentiated; low cross-stream independence; merged streams |
| GA (Premature Non-Dualist) | Abandons structure prematurely; 'flows' without maintaining holonic integrity | Early structure-abandonment; high flow-feel, low accuracy; inconsistent |
| GAll (Integral Fortress) | Excellent holonic coordination but body freezes at dissolution-edge; cannot release into trans-somatic | High accuracy until breath-gated surrender tasks; sharp performance cliff |

## 4. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

**Catalyst:** Tasks require the body to DO integral cognition — not think about it, but physically enact simultaneous multi-level coordination. The body cannot fake vision-logic. Either it holds four streams or it doesn't.

**Experience:** Player feels integral cognition as a SOMATIC state — the felt-sense of holding complexity without collapse. The Meta-Mapper feels their body's rigidity. The Green Pluralist feels the cost of refusing differentiation. The Premature Non-Dualist feels inconsistency in their body. The Integral Fortress feels the edge where control must yield to flow.

**Integration:** The body becomes a training ground for vision-logic. Physical mastery of holonic coordination transfers to cognitive flexibility. Each session builds embodied capacity that supports all other modalities.

## 5. Progression & Infinite Checkpoint

| Phase | Focus | Unlock condition |
|---|---|---|
| Diagnosis | Baseline rhythm coordination, gestural shifting, breath-complexity | Entry |
| Healing | Shadow-specific somatic sequences targeting detected body-patterns | Shadow signature above threshold |
| Evolution | Tasks requiring simultaneous precision AND surrender; holonic flow | Drive-health > 0.6 |
| Maintenance | Somatic maintenance preventing embodied regression; theta-decay | Post-evolution |

Checkpoints every 60–90 seconds. Difficulty adapts via stream-count, shift-frequency, and complexity-ceiling. Player experiences a rhythm game, not a cognitive test.
