# Cognitive / Turquoise — Immersive-RPG

## 1. Game Identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Modality | Immersive-RPG (ecological free-play, emergent assessment) |
| Unique probe | Ecological vision-logic — whether integral reasoning appears SPONTANEOUSLY in free-play without explicit prompting; the gold standard of genuine capacity |
| Core loop | Player navigates an open narrative environment where integral cognition is never demanded but always rewarded; the world is a holarchy that responds to the player's cognitive altitude |
| Session length | 8–20 minutes (infinite checkpoint) |

## 2. Mechanics

### 2.1 The Holarchic World

The game world is structured as nested holons — each zone is simultaneously a self-contained ecology AND a component of a larger system. NPCs operate at different developmental altitudes. Quests have solutions at every stage but produce different world-consequences depending on the cognitive altitude of the solution. The world REFLECTS the player's integral capacity back to them through systemic consequences.

### 2.2 Emergent Pattern Recognition

Hidden cross-zone patterns exist that are only visible to vision-logic. A player operating at Green sees local systems. A player operating at Turquoise sees patterns-of-patterns spanning the entire world. These meta-patterns unlock narrative threads, abilities, and world-states unavailable to lower-altitude play. Detection is never prompted — it must emerge from the player's own cognitive operations.

### 2.3 Spontaneous Integration Moments

The world periodically presents situations where multiple previously-separate narrative threads converge. The player's response reveals whether they can hold all threads simultaneously (Turquoise), must choose between them (Orange/Green), or collapse them into a single frame (Amber/Red). Scored implicitly by world-response quality and narrative-branch unlocked.

## 3. Shadow Surfacing

| Shadow | In-world behaviour | Diagnostic signal |
|---|---|---|
| DA (Meta-Mapper) | Player maps the world obsessively; collects all information; never commits to a quest-path; treats the world as a system to be decoded rather than lived in | High exploration, low quest-completion; information-hoarding; analysis-paralysis at choice-points |
| DAll (Green Pluralist) | Player refuses to rank quest-paths; helps everyone equally; cannot prioritise; world stagnates from lack of decisive action | Equal time across all NPCs; no hierarchical quest-selection; flat engagement pattern |
| GA (Premature Non-Dualist) | Player attempts to 'transcend' the game; ignores systemic details; makes grand gestures without grounding | Skips intermediate steps; low detail-engagement; high-level actions without foundation |
| GAll (Integral Fortress) | Player masters the world-system brilliantly but avoids all narrative threads involving surrender, loss, or ego-dissolution | High system-mastery; systematic avoidance of vulnerability-coded quests; incomplete narrative |

## 4. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

**Catalyst:** The world IS the catalyst. Its holarchic structure naturally rewards integral cognition and naturally exposes shadows. No explicit testing occurs — the player simply lives in a world that responds to their cognitive altitude.

**Experience:** Player discovers their own cognitive altitude through world-feedback. The Meta-Mapper discovers their world is mapped but unlived. The Green Pluralist discovers their world is stagnant. The Premature Non-Dualist discovers their grand gestures have no foundation. The Integral Fortress discovers their mastered world is emotionally hollow.

**Integration:** The world adapts to surface the player's specific shadow through narrative consequences. Healing happens through lived experience — not instruction. The player grows by BEING in a world that requires integral cognition for full flourishing.

## 5. Progression & Infinite Checkpoint

| Phase | Focus | Unlock condition |
|---|---|---|
| Diagnosis | Baseline free-play behaviour across world-zones | Entry |
| Healing | World-events targeting detected shadow patterns through narrative | Shadow pattern above threshold |
| Evolution | Narrative convergences requiring simultaneous integral mastery AND trans-rational surrender | Drive-health > 0.6 |
| Maintenance | World-evolution preventing stagnation; new holarchic layers; theta-decay | Post-evolution |

Checkpoints at any safe-zone. World-state persists continuously. This modality is the ecological validator — if integral cognition appears here spontaneously, it is genuine. All other modalities feed capacity that is tested ecologically here.
