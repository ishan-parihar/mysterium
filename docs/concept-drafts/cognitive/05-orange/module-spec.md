# Cognitive / Orange — Module Specification

> **Line:** Cognitive  **Stage:** Orange / Rational  **Vibration:** The mind that can think about thinking — formal operations, hypothetical-deductive reasoning, and the birth of metacognition.

---

## 1. Capacity Definition

Formal-operational cognition: the capacity to reason about possibilities rather than only actualities. Working memory at n=3, Tower of London 4-disk planning, first genuine dual-task capacity, WCST flexibility with moderate shift cadence, Stroop inhibition at SOA=800ms, complex span (simultaneous processing + storage), and hypothetical-deductive reasoning chains. Combo length 4–5. The defining leap from Amber: the player can now hold a hypothesis, test it against evidence, and revise — thinking about thinking itself.

## 2. Shadow Archetypes

### 2.1 Dark-Addiction (DA): The Hyper-Rationalist

Cannot stop analysing. Thinking becomes a defence against feeling and being. Workaholism of the mind, scientism, reductionism. Every experience is immediately abstracted into a model. Presents as brilliance but functions as avoidance — the mind running to escape the body and heart.

### 2.2 Dark-Allergy (DAll): The Anti-Intellectual

Refuses formal reasoning entirely. Remains in Amber concrete operations while rationalising the refusal: "overthinking is the problem," "just trust your gut," "intellectuals are disconnected." Dismisses abstraction as pretension. The capacity is available but actively rejected.

### 2.3 Golden-Addiction (GA): The Premature Systems-Thinker

Performs Green/Teal multi-perspectival reasoning without genuine formal-operational mastery. Talks fluently about "complexity," "emergence," and "integral" while unable to execute rigorous deduction. Skips the discipline of Orange to wear the costume of Teal.

### 2.4 Golden-Allergy (GAll): The Rationalist Fortress

Formal operations fully present but refuses the pluralistic opening that Green requires. Terrified of relativism. "There IS one right answer and I will find it." The capacity to think about thinking is used to fortify a single perspective rather than open to many.

## 4. Healing Vectors

### 4.1 Heal/Evolve (Agape + Agency → Dark Shadows)

DA: Agency owns the pattern ("I use thinking to avoid feeling") + Agape brings the insight back into the body. DAll: Agency claims the refused capacity ("I can think formally") + Agape grounds it in lived experience rather than pure abstraction.

### 4.2 Evolve/Heal (Eros + Communion → Golden Shadows)

GA: Eros reaches toward genuine formal mastery (not bypassing it) + Communion receives feedback that reveals the gap. GAll: Eros reaches toward pluralistic opening + Communion dissolves the fortress through genuine encounter with other perspectives.

### 4.3 Integration Criteria

Integrated Orange cognition: can deploy formal operations fluently, can also set them aside; can hold a hypothesis without clinging to it; can reason rigorously AND acknowledge the limits of reason; metacognition serves life rather than defending against it.

## 6. Compound Shadows & Cross-Module Relationships

### 6.1 The Cold Architect (Cognitive DA × Emotional DA)

Hyper-rationalism + emotional suppression = brilliant but disconnected. The mind operates at high capacity while the heart is frozen. Intervention: Emotional line games that require formal reasoning about feelings.

### 6.2 The Burnout (Cognitive DA × Willpower DA)

Compulsive analysis + compulsive achievement = exhaustion without rest. The mind never stops and the will never relents. Intervention: Strategic-planning games that reward deliberate non-action and resource conservation.

### 6.3 The Clever Conformist (Cognitive DAll × Moral DAll)

Refuses formal reasoning + refuses principled ethics = sophisticated rule-following without examination. Follows complex social rules without ever questioning them. Intervention: Scenario-choice games that make the cost of unexamined conformity visible.

### 6.4 The Armchair Philosopher (Cognitive GA × Somatic DAll)

Premature systems-thinking + refuses embodiment = all theory, no practice. Talks about holism while dissociated from the body. Intervention: Embodied-somatic games that require physical enactment of abstract reasoning.

## 7. Shadow Surfacing Sequence

### 7.1 Progressive Revelation

Shadows surface in order of accessibility: DAll first (most visible — avoidance of the task), DA second (emerges under load — over-engagement), GAll third (appears when pluralistic prompts are introduced), GA last (requires cross-stage comparison to detect).

### 7.2 Per-Modality Surfacing Table

| Modality | Primary Shadow Surfaced | Secondary | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|---|
| Deterministic | DAll (refusal visible in accuracy) | DA (perseveration under load) | Pure performance metrics |
| Language-Reflective | GA (verbal sophistication without depth) | DA (over-analysis of own process) | LLM rubric on metacognitive quality |
| Scenario-Choice | GAll (single-answer rigidity) | DAll (refusal to engage hypotheticals) | Branching dilemma responses |

| Social-Cooperative | GAll (refuses others' perspectives) | GA (performs collaboration without substance) | Distributed reasoning quality |
| Immersive-RPG | All four (ecological observation) | — | Spontaneous reasoning in free-play |

### 7.3 Cross-Validation

No shadow diagnosis is confirmed from a single modality. DA requires convergence across deterministic (high perseveration) + strategic-planning (over-planning) + embodied-somatic (body disconnection). Minimum 3 modalities must agree before shadow-drag is applied.
