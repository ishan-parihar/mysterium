# Cognitive / Green — Module Specification

> **Line:** Cognitive  **Stage:** Green / Pluralistic  **Vibration:** The mind that holds contradictions without collapsing — multi-perspectival reasoning as lived capacity rather than intellectual performance.

---

## 1. Capacity Definition

Late formal / early post-formal cognition. The defining leap: simultaneous perspective-holding across 4 valid viewpoints without premature closure. Complex span n=3-4 with processing load. Rapid task-switching (WCST high-shift cadence). Dialectical reasoning (thesis-antithesis-synthesis without forced resolution). Context-dependent rule application where the "correct" answer depends on which framework you inhabit. Contradiction tolerance — holding two valid but mutually exclusive conclusions as simultaneously true-in-context.

Below (Orange): formal operational, hypothetico-deductive, single-perspective rigour, n=3.
Above (Teal): vision-logic, all perspectives held as unified field, integral reasoning.

## 2. Shadow Archetypes

### 2.1 Dark-Addiction (DA): The Paralysed Pluralist

Cannot commit to any conclusion because every perspective is valid. Infinite regress of contextualisation. "But from another perspective..." as avoidance of agency. Analysis paralysis dressed as sophistication. Mean-green-meme intellectualism — the appearance of depth masking inability to act.

### 2.2 Dark-Allergy (DAll): The Rational Individualist

Refuses multi-perspectival reasoning. Clings to Orange single-perspective rigour. "There IS one right answer." Dismisses contextual thinking as relativism. Experiences pluralism as threat to cognitive coherence. Mistakes rigidity for clarity.

### 2.3 Golden-Addiction (GA): The Premature Integralist

Performs Teal vision-logic without genuine Green mastery. Claims to "see the whole" while unable to genuinely hold 4 perspectives simultaneously. Leaps to meta-systematic integration before inhabiting each perspective fully. Spiritual bypassing of cognitive complexity.

### 2.4 Golden-Allergy (GAll): The Pluralist Fortress

Multi-perspectival reasoning present but refuses integral commitment. Terrified of hierarchy. "All perspectives are equal" as dogma. Refuses to rank, prioritise, or synthesise. Mistakes the refusal to integrate for the achievement of pluralism.

## 4. Healing Vectors

### 4.1 Heal/Evolve (Agape + Agency)

Integrate dark shadows by grounding multi-perspectival awareness in embodied commitment. The Paralysed Pluralist heals by choosing — not collapsing perspectives, but acting FROM pluralistic awareness. The Rational Individualist heals by returning to the body's felt-sense of multiple truths.

### 4.2 Evolve/Heal (Eros + Communion)

Dissolve golden shadows by reaching upward through genuine relational joining. The Premature Integralist heals by slowing down into authentic communion with each perspective. The Pluralist Fortress heals by allowing Eros to reveal natural hierarchy within communion.

### 4.3 Integration Criteria

Player demonstrates: (a) holding 4 perspectives simultaneously for ≥8s without collapse, (b) committing to contextual action without dismissing unjudged perspectives, (c) tolerating contradiction without premature synthesis OR permanent paralysis, (d) switching between frameworks fluidly (shift cost <200ms at n=4).

## 6. Compound Shadows & Cross-Module Relationships

### 6.1 The Paralysed Empath (Cognitive DA × Emotional DA)

Paralysis-by-pluralism fused with empathic overwhelm. Cannot act because feeling too much from too many perspectives. Intervention: Agency-grounding tasks that honour feeling without drowning.

### 6.2 The Sophisticated Conformist (Cognitive DAll × Moral DAll)

Refuses pluralism AND refuses contextual ethics. Orange rationalism masquerading as depth. Intervention: Scenarios where single-perspective logic demonstrably fails.

### 6.3 The Systems Performer (Cognitive GA × Spiritual GA)

Premature integralism fused with premature interfaith synthesis. Talks about wholeness without genuine perspectival mastery. Intervention: Tasks requiring sustained inhabitation of single perspectives.

### 6.4 The Relativist Fortress (Cognitive GAll × Moral GAll)

Refuses hierarchy AND refuses moral commitment. Sophisticated paralysis. Intervention: Time-pressured commitment tasks where non-action has consequences.

## 7. Shadow Surfacing Sequence

### 7.1 Progressive Revelation

Sessions 1-3: Baseline multi-perspectival capacity (no shadow provocation). Sessions 4-8: Time pressure and commitment demands (surfaces DA/DAll). Sessions 9-14: Integral-level prompts (surfaces GA/GAll). Session 15+: Full shadow landscape active, adaptive targeting.

Shadow identification requires convergence across ≥3 modalities. Deterministic provides quantitative anchors; Language-Reflective provides qualitative depth; Immersive-RPG provides ecological validity. Discrepancies between modalities are themselves diagnostic (e.g., high deterministic + low ecological = GA pattern).
