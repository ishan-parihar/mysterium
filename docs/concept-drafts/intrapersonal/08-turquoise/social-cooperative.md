## 1. Modality Identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Module | Intrapersonal / White |
| Modality | Social-Cooperative |
| Unique lateral | Non-dual self in relation — relating without a fixed relater, transparent self-presentation |

## 2. Core Mechanic

Cooperative and relational tasks where the player interacts with NPCs or other players. The game detects whether the player maintains a fixed self-presentation or relates transparently — adapting fluidly to relational context without either performing a role or defending an identity.

## 3. Shadow Detection

| Shadow | Relational signature |
|---|---|
| 2.1 Grasping-at-Self | Consistent self-presentation across all relational contexts; impression-management; needs to be seen as 'someone' |
| 2.2 Aversion-to-Self | Withdrawal from relational exposure; avoids being known; deflects attention |
| 2.3 Self-Bypassing | Performs 'no self' in relationship — claims to have no preferences, no needs — while subtly controlling relational dynamics |
| 2.4 Identity-with-Witness | Relates from observer-position; present but not participatory; maintains subtle distance |

## 4. Task Architecture

### 4.1 Transparent Self-Presentation
- Relational scenarios requiring different self-presentations (authority, vulnerability, playfulness)
- Transparent self: shifts fluidly without identity-friction
- Grasping self: resists presentations that contradict self-image

### 4.2 Relational Dissolution Tasks
- Cooperative tasks where the player's established relational role becomes irrelevant
- How quickly does the player release relational identity when context shifts?
- Genuine transparency: relates freshly in each moment without relational-self baggage

### 4.3 Being-Known Probes
- Scenarios where the player is accurately perceived by NPCs — including shadow aspects
- Transparent self: no defensive reaction to being seen fully
- Grasping self: discomfort, correction, or withdrawal when perceived beyond self-image

## 5. Drive Probing

| Drive | Probe |
|---|---|
| Eros | Does the player reach toward deeper relational intimacy or maintain safe distance? |
| Agape | Can the player release relational self-image without collapsing into formlessness? |
| Agency | Can the player assert clearly in relationship without needing assertion to define them? |
| Communion | Can the player merge relationally without losing transparent functionality? |

## 6. Scoring

- Primary: Self-Presentation Rigidity — consistency of self-image across relational contexts (lower = more transparent)
- Secondary: Relational-Role Release — adaptation speed when relational context shifts
- Tertiary: Being-Known Tolerance — equanimity when perceived accurately
- Cross-session patterns reveal stable relational transparency vs. context-dependent performance

## 7. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

- **Catalyst:** Relational situations that make self-presentation visible as a choice rather than necessity
- **Experience:** The felt-sense of relating without a relater — presence without persona
- **Integration:** Residual relational self-grasping becomes visible in its relational effects; visibility dissolves it

## 8. Progression

- Entry: Baseline relational self-presentation patterns mapped
- Middle: Targeted relational scenarios surface specific grasping patterns
- Advanced: Relating becomes transparent — fluid, appropriate, undefended
- Infinite: Novel relational contexts prevent habituation; theta-decay on regression
