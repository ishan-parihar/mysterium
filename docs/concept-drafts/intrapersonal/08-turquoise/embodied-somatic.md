## 1. Modality Identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Module | Intrapersonal / White |
| Modality | Embodied-Somatic |
| Unique lateral | Body as site of non-dual self — embodied self-transparency, physical non-grasping at identity |

## 2. Core Mechanic

Movement and body-awareness tasks where the player's physical responses reveal whether identity is held in the body or whether the body functions transparently. Rhythm, posture, breath, and gesture tasks are designed so that somatic self-grasping produces measurable tension, hesitation, or rigidity patterns.

## 3. Shadow Detection

| Shadow | Somatic signature |
|---|---|
| 2.1 Grasping-at-Self | Muscular bracing, postural rigidity, breath-holding when identity is challenged |
| 2.2 Aversion-to-Self | Somatic avoidance — dissociation from body regions, numbness patterns, withdrawal from embodiment |
| 2.3 Self-Bypassing | Performed relaxation contradicted by micro-tension; 'spiritual body' posturing |
| 2.4 Identity-with-Witness | Body held in observation-mode; reluctance to fully inhabit movement; spectator-posture |

## 4. Task Architecture

### 4.1 Identity-Dissolution Movement
- Guided movement sequences that progressively dissolve body-identity anchors
- Player moves as 'themselves', then as 'no one', then as 'the movement itself'
- Somatic grasping: detectable shift in movement quality between frames

### 4.2 Transparent Action Tasks
- Physical tasks (rhythm, gesture, balance) performed without self-monitoring
- Genuine transparency: movement quality improves when self-reference drops
- Grasping: performance degrades or rigidifies when 'no one is doing it'

### 4.3 Breath-Identity Probe
- Breath patterns tracked during identity-relevant vs. neutral game events
- Transparent self: breath remains unperturbed by identity-challenges
- Grasping: breath disruption correlates with identity-threat moments

## 5. Drive Probing

| Drive | Probe |
|---|---|
| Eros | Does the body reach toward dissolution or brace against it? |
| Agape | Does the body release identity-tension gracefully or collapse? |
| Agency | Can the body act powerfully without a 'doer' claiming the movement? |
| Communion | Can the body merge with rhythm/environment without losing functional coherence? |

## 6. Scoring

- Primary: Somatic Self-Reference Index — tension differential between self-framed and transparent-framed tasks
- Secondary: Movement Fluidity — quality difference when identity-frame shifts
- Tertiary: Breath Coherence — stability across identity-relevant events
- All metrics derived from input timing, rhythm accuracy, and response patterns

## 7. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

- **Catalyst:** Physical tasks that make embodied self-grasping tangible through its somatic effects
- **Experience:** The felt-sense of the body moving without an owner — effortless action
- **Integration:** Somatic grasping patterns become felt directly; feeling them fully releases them

## 8. Progression

- Entry: Baseline somatic self-reference profile established
- Middle: Tasks target specific body-held grasping patterns
- Advanced: Movement becomes transparent — no detectable self-reference in physical action
- Infinite: Novel movement contexts prevent habituation; theta-decay ensures ongoing embodied transparency
