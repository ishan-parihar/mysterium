## 1. Modality Identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Module | Intrapersonal / White |
| Modality | Immersive-RPG |
| Unique lateral | Ecological non-dual self — whether self-transparency appears spontaneously in free-play |

## 2. Core Mechanic

Open-world free-play environment where the player navigates, explores, and responds to emergent situations without structured prompts. The game observes whether self-transparency manifests spontaneously — not as a response to specific probes but as the player's default mode of engagement with an unstructured world.

## 3. Shadow Detection

| Shadow | Ecological signature |
|---|---|
| 2.1 Grasping-at-Self | Character-building behaviour; accumulation of identity-markers; territory-claiming; reputation-seeking |
| 2.2 Aversion-to-Self | Avoidance of self-defining situations; wandering without engagement; refusal to leave traces |
| 2.3 Self-Bypassing | Performed detachment — moves through world without genuine contact; 'above it all' posturing |
| 2.4 Identity-with-Witness | Spectator-mode engagement; observes world rather than participating; maintains meta-distance |

## 4. Task Architecture

### 4.1 Unstructured World Navigation
- Open environment with no explicit objectives
- What does the player do when nothing is asked of them?
- Transparent self: engages fluidly with whatever arises; no identity-project driving behaviour

### 4.2 Emergent Identity-Challenges
- World events that spontaneously challenge identity (NPC misidentifies player, role dissolves, reputation contradicted)
- Transparent self: responds without friction
- Grasping self: corrects, defends, or withdraws

### 4.3 Trace Analysis
- Long-term behavioural patterns across sessions reveal identity-structure
- Does the player build a consistent character or flow as transparent presence?
- Accumulation patterns, territory patterns, relationship patterns all scored

## 5. Drive Probing

| Drive | Probe |
|---|---|
| Eros | Does free-play naturally move toward growth/dissolution or toward identity-consolidation? |
| Agape | Does the player release world-attachments gracefully when circumstances shift? |
| Agency | Does spontaneous action arise without a self claiming it? |
| Communion | Does the player merge with world-flow without losing functional responsiveness? |

## 6. Scoring

- Primary: Identity-Project Index — degree to which free-play serves self-construction vs. transparent engagement
- Secondary: Spontaneous Transparency — frequency of self-transparent moments without prompting
- Tertiary: Ecological Consistency — whether transparency in structured modalities transfers to free-play
- Longitudinal patterns weighted heavily; single-session data insufficient

## 7. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

- **Catalyst:** An open world that neither demands nor prevents self-construction — the player's default reveals itself
- **Experience:** The felt-sense of moving through a world without being someone moving through a world
- **Integration:** Residual identity-projects become visible against the background of transparent engagement; visibility allows release

## 8. Progression

- Entry: Baseline ecological identity-patterns mapped across multiple free-play sessions
- Middle: World events adaptively target detected grasping patterns
- Advanced: Free-play becomes indistinguishable from transparent living — no identity-project detectable
- Infinite: World complexity increases indefinitely; theta-decay ensures ongoing ecological transparency
