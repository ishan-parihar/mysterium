## 6. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

- **Catalyst:** Situations that invite the player to act, choose, or relate without a fixed self-reference
- **Experience:** The felt-sense of identity dissolving into transparent functioning
- **Integration:** Residual grasping patterns surface, are noticed, and release without effort

## 7. Infinite Checkpoint Contract

- Sessions end at any natural pause; state persists
- No 'completion' — self-transparency deepens indefinitely
- Theta-decay ensures neglected shadow quadrants resurface
- Lower-stage intrapersonal health (Turquoise witness, Green sensitivity-self, etc.) required for White unlock

## 8. Design Constraints

- All probes implicit — player never told they are being assessed
- Residual grasping detected through micro-behavioural signatures, not self-report
- LLM operates within each modality using rubrics; fixed mechanics + adaptive content
- Every modality probes all 4 shadow quadrants and all 4 drives in both domains
