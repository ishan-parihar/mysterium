## 1. Modality Identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Module | Intrapersonal / White |
| Modality | Scenario-Choice |
| Unique lateral | Non-dual identity dilemmas — situations requiring action without a fixed self |

## 2. Core Mechanic

Branching narrative scenarios where every choice-point is designed to reveal whether the player acts from a fixed identity or from transparent responsiveness. Dilemmas are constructed so that identity-attachment produces one pattern of choices while genuine self-transparency produces another.

## 3. Shadow Detection

| Shadow | Choice signature |
|---|---|
| 2.1 Grasping-at-Self | Choices that protect, maintain, or assert a consistent self-image across scenarios |
| 2.2 Aversion-to-Self | Choices that systematically avoid self-implicating options; deflection patterns |
| 2.3 Self-Bypassing | Choices that perform selflessness while subtly serving self-preservation |
| 2.4 Identity-with-Witness | Choices that maintain observer-distance; avoidance of full participatory engagement |

## 4. Task Architecture

### 4.1 Identity-Stake Dilemmas
- Scenarios where the 'correct' action contradicts the player's established character identity
- Transparent self: acts appropriately regardless of identity-consistency
- Grasping self: maintains character-consistency at the cost of situational wisdom

### 4.2 Role-Dissolution Scenarios
- Player is given a role, then the role becomes irrelevant or contradictory
- How quickly and completely does the player release the role?
- Residual grasping: continues role-behaviour after role is dissolved

### 4.3 Selfless-Action Paradoxes
- Scenarios where helping requires becoming invisible — no credit, no recognition
- Scenarios where the wisest action looks foolish from any fixed identity-position
- Genuine transparency: acts without concern for how action reflects on self

## 5. Drive Probing

| Drive | Probe |
|---|---|
| Eros | Does the player choose growth-options even when they dissolve current self-structure? |
| Agape | Does the player release self-interest without performing sacrifice? |
| Agency | Can the player act decisively without needing the action to define them? |
| Communion | Can the player merge with a situation's needs without losing functional clarity? |

## 6. Scoring

- Primary: Identity-Consistency Rigidity — how much choices are constrained by self-image
- Secondary: Role-Release Latency — speed of adaptation when identity-anchors dissolve
- Tertiary: Selfless-Action Authenticity — gap between performed and genuine self-transparency
- Cross-scenario consistency reveals stable patterns vs. situational responses

## 7. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

- **Catalyst:** Dilemmas where identity-attachment creates suffering or suboptimal outcomes
- **Experience:** The felt-sense of choosing without a chooser — action arising from situation rather than self
- **Integration:** Residual identity-grasping becomes visible through its constraint on choice; visibility releases constraint

## 8. Progression

- Entry: Baseline identity-rigidity mapped across scenario types
- Middle: Scenarios target specific grasping patterns with increasing subtlety
- Advanced: Choices arise transparently — no detectable identity-constraint on action
- Infinite: Novel scenario-types prevent habituation; theta-decay on regression
