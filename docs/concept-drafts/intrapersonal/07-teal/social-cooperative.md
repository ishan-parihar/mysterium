# Intrapersonal × Turquoise — Social-Cooperative

## 1. Game Identity

- **Modality:** Social-Cooperative (group dynamics, relational tasks)
- **Unique axis:** Integral self in relation — how the vision-logic self functions in group contexts, cross-altitude self-presentation without condescension or hiding
- **Core mechanic:** Player navigates group situations where their integral self-concept must interface with others at different developmental altitudes; reveals whether integral identity is genuinely relational or secretly isolated

## 2. Catalyst Mechanics

### 2.1 Cross-Altitude Self-Presentation
Player must present themselves authentically to groups operating at different stages. The integral self neither hides its complexity nor performs superiority. Tasks measure: can the player be genuinely themselves across developmental contexts without code-switching into inauthenticity or retreating into isolation?

### 2.2 Group Witnessing Tasks
Cooperative tasks where multiple players must witness each other's developmental process. The integral self must hold others' development with the same clarity it holds its own — without projecting, fixing, or hierarchically ranking. Scored on relational witnessing quality.

### 2.3 Integral Self in Conflict
Group scenarios where the player's integral self-concept is challenged, misunderstood, or rejected by others. The task measures: does the integral self collapse, rigidify, perform, or genuinely hold its ground while remaining open? Relational resilience of identity.

## 3. Shadow Surfacing

- **DA (Self-Witness Addict):** In group, becomes the one who observes group dynamics rather than participating — 'I see what's happening here' as substitute for being-in-relation, witnesses others' process to avoid their own relational vulnerability
- **DAll (Pluralistic Self):** In group, matches each person's altitude rather than holding integral ground — chameleon self that is 'many selves' becomes relational shapeshifting, no stable self to offer the group
- **GA (Premature No-Self):** In group, performs spiritual teacher role — 'there is no self here relating to you' — uses non-dual language to avoid genuine interpersonal contact, relates from performed emptiness
- **GAll (Integral Self Fortress):** In group, maintains perfect integral composure but never truly meets others — the fortress is relational armor, witnesses group dynamics flawlessly but remains untouched, unreachable

## 4. Progression Arc

### 4.1 Diagnosis Phase
Initial group tasks establish: how does the player's integral self-concept function relationally? Do they withdraw into observation (DA), shapeshift (DAll), perform transcendence (GA), or armor up (GAll)?

### 4.2 Healing Phase
Tasks calibrate toward shadow-specific relational challenges: DA players must participate without meta-commentary; DAll players must hold consistent self across altitude-shifts; GA players must show up as a developmental self rather than no-self; GAll players must allow others to affect them.

### 4.3 Evolution Phase
Tasks require genuine relational integral self: present, stable, open, vulnerable, witnessing without withdrawing, structured without armored, meeting others across altitude without condescension or collapse.

## 5. Scoring Integration

- **Primary metric:** Relational authenticity × altitude-stability × openness-to-impact
- **Drive scoring:** Eros via relational growth-invitation; Agape via meeting others where they are; Agency via holding integral ground in group; Communion via genuine permeability to others' experience
- **Shadow detection:** Observational withdrawal frequency (DA), self-inconsistency across contexts (DAll), spiritual-teacher performance (GA), relational impermeability (GAll)
- **Theta-decay:** Half-life 17 days; relational integral self degrades toward isolation or shapeshifting without practice
- **Cross-line bonus:** +0.15 when relational integral self transfers to Interpersonal line tasks
