# Intrapersonal × Turquoise — Strategic-Planning

## 1. Game Identity

- **Modality:** Strategic-Planning (resource allocation, long-horizon optimization)
- **Unique axis:** Planning integral self-development — structuring growth across all lines and levels consciously
- **Core mechanic:** Player designs and executes developmental plans for a self that spans multiple lines, stages, and domains; the planning itself reveals whether the player can hold the whole developmental landscape strategically without collapsing into shadow

## 2. Catalyst Mechanics

### 2.1 Multi-Line Development Planning
Player allocates limited resources (time, energy, attention) across developmental lines and stages. Must balance: which lines need healing (bottom-up), which need evolution (top-down), which need maintenance. The plan must be coherent across the whole AQAL matrix, not just optimizing one dimension.

### 2.2 Shadow-Aware Scheduling
Player must plan around their own shadow tendencies. Tasks present developmental schedules where certain allocations would feed shadows (e.g., over-investing in witnessing feeds DA; avoiding integration tasks feeds DAll). The integral planner sees and accounts for their own distortions.

### 2.3 Holonic Maintenance Architecture
Player designs systems for maintaining lower-stage health while pursuing upper-stage growth. Must structure theta-decay prevention, shadow-mode encounter scheduling, and cross-line synergy exploitation. The plan must demonstrate genuine understanding of holonic integrity.

## 3. Shadow Surfacing

- **DA (Self-Witness Addict):** Plans become infinitely recursive — planning to plan to develop, meta-strategies about meta-strategies, the plan itself becomes another witnessing layer rather than a commitment to action
- **DAll (Pluralistic Self):** Plans refuse prioritization — all lines equally weighted, no developmental vector, 'everything matters equally' as avoidance of integral hierarchy
- **GA (Premature No-Self):** Plans bypass developmental work — 'if there's no self, why plan development?' — or plans that jump directly to non-dual without intermediate steps
- **GAll (Integral Self Fortress):** Plans are architecturally perfect but exclude dissolution — every contingency mapped except the one where the planner surrenders the plan, no space for emergence or grace

## 4. Progression Arc

### 4.1 Diagnosis Phase
Initial planning tasks reveal: can the player hold the whole developmental landscape? Do they over-plan (DA), refuse to prioritize (DAll), bypass planning (GA), or over-structure (GAll)? Planning style maps shadow profile.

### 4.2 Healing Phase
Tasks calibrate toward shadow-specific planning challenges: DA players must create plans with hard action-deadlines; DAll players must create hierarchical priorities; GA players must honor sequential development; GAll players must include planned spaces of not-knowing.

### 4.3 Evolution Phase
Tasks require genuine integral planning: developmental architecture that is simultaneously structured and open, hierarchical and inclusive, committed and surrendered. Plans that demonstrate the planner has integrated the planning process itself.

## 5. Scoring Integration

- **Primary metric:** Plan coherence × action-commitment × shadow-awareness × emergence-space
- **Drive scoring:** Eros via upward-growth allocation; Agape via maintenance-and-healing allocation; Agency via decisive prioritization; Communion via cross-line synergy design
- **Shadow detection:** Recursive meta-planning (DA), flat non-hierarchical allocation (DAll), developmental bypass in plans (GA), over-determined no-emergence plans (GAll)
- **Theta-decay:** Half-life 17 days; planning capacity degrades toward habitual allocation patterns without practice
- **Cross-line bonus:** +0.15 when integral self-planning transfers to Cognitive line strategic tasks
