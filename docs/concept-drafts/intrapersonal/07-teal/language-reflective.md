# Intrapersonal × Turquoise — Language-Reflective

## 1. Game Identity

- **Modality:** Language-Reflective (LLM-scored verbal expression)
- **Unique axis:** Verbal articulation of integral self-concept — how the player talks about their own development reveals genuine depth vs. performed understanding
- **Core mechanic:** Player writes/speaks about their developmental process; LLM rubrics distinguish authentic vision-logic self-articulation from intellectual mimicry, spiritual bypassing, or Green-level complexity

## 2. Catalyst Mechanics

### 2.1 Developmental Autobiography Prompts
Player is asked to describe their own growth across specific domains. Prompts target: "How have you changed in how you relate to yourself?" "Describe a self you used to be that you now understand differently." The LLM scores for stage-appropriate complexity, genuine integration markers, and absence of shadow signatures.

### 2.2 Self-Concept Articulation
Player must articulate their current self-concept in response to increasingly challenging frames: "Who are you when all your roles are removed?" "How do you hold contradictory parts of yourself?" Responses reveal whether integral self-concept is lived or merely theorized.

### 2.3 Meta-Narrative Coherence
Player constructs a narrative of their own development that must hold multiple stages as valid without collapsing into relativism or premature transcendence. The LLM evaluates structural coherence, developmental accuracy, and emotional authenticity.

## 3. Shadow Surfacing

- **DA (Self-Witness Addict):** Language becomes infinitely recursive — 'I notice that I'm noticing that I'm aware of my awareness of...' — meta-layers without ground, sophisticated but disembodied
- **DAll (Pluralistic Self):** Language celebrates multiplicity but refuses synthesis — 'I contain multitudes and that's the whole story' — rich description without integral architecture
- **GA (Premature No-Self):** Language performs transcendence — 'there is no one here writing this' — spiritual vocabulary without developmental substance, bypasses personal history
- **GAll (Integral Self Fortress):** Language is architecturally perfect but emotionally sealed — flawless developmental maps with no vulnerability, no trembling at the edge of dissolution

## 4. Progression Arc

### 4.1 Diagnosis Phase
Initial prompts establish baseline self-articulation. LLM maps: vocabulary complexity, recursive depth, integration markers, shadow signatures in language patterns.

### 4.2 Healing Phase
Prompts shift toward shadow-specific catalysts: DA players are prompted to describe experience WITHOUT meta-commentary; DAll players are invited to find the thread connecting their many selves; GA players are asked to honor their developmental history; GAll players are invited to articulate what frightens them about dissolution.

### 4.3 Evolution Phase
Prompts require genuine vision-logic expression: articulating the self that INCLUDES the developmental process without being trapped in witnessing it. Language that is simultaneously personal and transpersonal, specific and universal.

## 5. Scoring Integration

- **Primary metric:** Articulation depth (LLM rubric) × authenticity markers − performance indicators
- **Drive scoring:** Eros via transcendent vocabulary grounded in experience; Agape via compassionate self-historical language; Agency via decisive self-definition; Communion via permeable self-description
- **Shadow detection:** Recursive meta-language density (DA), synthesis-refusal markers (DAll), bypass vocabulary without substance (GA), architectural perfection without vulnerability (GAll)
- **Theta-decay:** Half-life 17 days; articulation capacity degrades toward habitual patterns without practice
- **Cross-line bonus:** +0.15 when self-articulation quality transfers to Emotional line expression
