# Intrapersonal × Turquoise — Immersive-RPG

## 1. Game Identity

- **Modality:** Immersive-RPG (open-world, ecological, emergent gameplay)
- **Unique axis:** Ecological integral self — whether developmental self-awareness appears spontaneously in free-play without explicit prompting
- **Core mechanic:** Player navigates an open world where integral self-concept is never directly tested but emerges (or fails to emerge) through how the player relates to their own character's development, choices, and history across extended play

## 2. Catalyst Mechanics

### 2.1 Character-as-Developmental-Mirror
The player's character accumulates a visible developmental history — past choices, former abilities, abandoned paths. The world reflects this history back. How the player relates to their character's past reveals their own integral self-concept: do they integrate, deny, bypass, or fortress?

### 2.2 Spontaneous Self-Witnessing Opportunities
The world presents moments where the player can pause and witness their own gameplay patterns — not as explicit tasks but as environmental affordances. A reflective pool that shows past-self actions. A library that records the player's developmental arc. Engagement is voluntary and reveals capacity.

### 2.3 Ecological Identity Challenges
NPCs and world-events challenge the player's self-concept indirectly. A character who mirrors the player's shadow. A quest that requires acting from a stage the player has 'outgrown.' Environmental pressures that test whether integral identity holds under immersive stress without explicit framing.

## 3. Shadow Surfacing

- **DA (Self-Witness Addict):** Player spends excessive time at reflective affordances — the mirror pool, the developmental library, the self-tracking systems — using the game's witnessing tools compulsively rather than playing
- **DAll (Pluralistic Self):** Player maintains multiple contradictory playstyles without integration — different behavior in different zones with no coherent through-line, the character is many characters
- **GA (Premature No-Self):** Player ignores character history entirely — treats each moment as fresh with no continuity, refuses to engage with developmental-mirror mechanics, performs 'no attachment to past self'
- **GAll (Integral Self Fortress):** Player engages perfectly with all developmental systems but never takes risks that could disrupt their integral self-image — avoids quests that might reveal weakness, maintains flawless developmental presentation

## 4. Progression Arc

### 4.1 Diagnosis Phase
Extended free-play establishes behavioral baseline: how does the player spontaneously relate to their own developmental history in-game? Patterns emerge over sessions, not minutes. The system tracks engagement with reflective affordances, consistency across contexts, and risk-taking patterns.

### 4.2 Healing Phase
World subtly shifts to present shadow-specific ecological challenges: DA players find reflective affordances temporarily unavailable (must act without witnessing); DAll players encounter situations requiring consistent identity; GA players' past choices have unavoidable present consequences; GAll players face situations where their integral image must crack.

### 4.3 Evolution Phase
World presents opportunities for genuine ecological integral self: moments where developmental awareness arises naturally in action, where the player's relationship to their own character demonstrates lived integration rather than performed or defended identity.

## 5. Scoring Integration

- **Primary metric:** Ecological integration score (spontaneous self-awareness × behavioral consistency × developmental risk-taking)
- **Drive scoring:** Eros via voluntary engagement with growth-edges; Agape via honoring character history; Agency via consistent self-expression across contexts; Communion via openness to world-as-mirror
- **Shadow detection:** Reflective-affordance overuse (DA), behavioral inconsistency across zones (DAll), history-avoidance patterns (GA), risk-avoidance to protect self-image (GAll)
- **Theta-decay:** Half-life 17 days; ecological integral self degrades toward habitual play-patterns without novel challenge
- **Cross-line bonus:** +0.15 when ecological self-awareness transfers to Spiritual line immersive tasks
