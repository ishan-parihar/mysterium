# Intrapersonal × Turquoise — Embodied-Somatic

## 1. Game Identity

- **Modality:** Embodied-Somatic (body-based, rhythm, breath, movement)
- **Unique axis:** Body as site of integral self — embodied witness, physical holding of developmental history in the soma
- **Core mechanic:** Player engages body-awareness tasks that reveal whether integral self-concept is genuinely embodied or merely cognitive; the body holds developmental history and integration must include somatic memory

## 2. Catalyst Mechanics

### 2.1 Embodied Witness Tasks
Player maintains body-awareness while performing complex actions. The task measures whether witnessing is a disembodied cognitive operation (shadow) or a whole-body presence. Breath-gated awareness tasks where the witness must be FELT not just thought.

### 2.2 Somatic Stage-Memory Activation
Rhythm and movement patterns associated with different developmental stages are presented. Player must physically hold multiple stage-rhythms simultaneously — the body integrating what the mind claims to integrate. Measured via timing coherence across layered patterns.

### 2.3 Developmental Body-Scan
Progressive body-awareness sequences where player identifies which developmental self lives in which body region. Not intellectual mapping but felt-sense recognition. The integral body is one that can feel ALL its developmental layers without dissociating from any.

## 3. Shadow Surfacing

- **DA (Self-Witness Addict):** Body becomes object of observation rather than subject of experience — player watches body from outside, high accuracy on body-mapping but low embodiment scores, the witness floats above the soma
- **DAll (Pluralistic Self):** Body holds multiple states but refuses integration — player can access different somatic selves but won't allow them to interpenetrate, body remains compartmentalized
- **GA (Premature No-Self):** Body is bypassed entirely — player claims 'the body is not self' and disengages from somatic tasks, or performs body-transcendence without genuine body-inclusion
- **GAll (Integral Self Fortress):** Body is controlled rather than inhabited — perfect somatic regulation but no surrender, the body is managed by the witness rather than being the witness

## 4. Progression Arc

### 4.1 Diagnosis Phase
Baseline embodiment assessment: can the player maintain body-awareness during cognitive tasks? Is their witness embodied or disembodied? Do they dissociate from specific body regions (developmental wounds stored somatically)?

### 4.2 Healing Phase
Tasks calibrate toward shadow-specific somatic interventions: DA players practice being-in-body without observing-body; DAll players practice allowing somatic states to blend; GA players practice honoring body-as-self; GAll players practice releasing somatic control into somatic trust.

### 4.3 Evolution Phase
Tasks require genuine embodied integral self: the witness that IS the body, developmental history held in living tissue rather than cognitive maps, action emerging from somatic wholeness rather than mental command.

## 5. Scoring Integration

- **Primary metric:** Embodiment depth × witness-integration (not witness-over-body but witness-as-body)
- **Drive scoring:** Eros via upward somatic reaching (breath, posture); Agape via downward somatic grounding (weight, gravity); Agency via decisive somatic action; Communion via permeable somatic boundaries
- **Shadow detection:** Disembodied observation patterns (DA), somatic compartmentalization (DAll), body-bypass indicators (GA), somatic over-control (GAll)
- **Theta-decay:** Half-life 17 days; embodied witness degrades toward habitual dissociation without practice
- **Cross-line bonus:** +0.15 when embodied integral self transfers to Somatic line tasks
