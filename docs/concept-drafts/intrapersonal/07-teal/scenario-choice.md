# Intrapersonal × Turquoise — Scenario-Choice

## 1. Game Identity

- **Modality:** Scenario-Choice (branching dilemmas, moral/identity decisions)
- **Unique axis:** Integral identity dilemmas requiring holding all previous selves while committing to decisive action
- **Core mechanic:** Player faces situations where different developmental selves demand contradictory responses; the integral move is neither choosing one self nor paralysis but acting from a position that honors all while transcending

## 2. Catalyst Mechanics

### 2.1 Multi-Self Dilemmas
Scenarios present situations where the player's Red self wants power, Amber self wants belonging, Orange self wants achievement, Green self wants inclusion — and the situation demands ONE action. The integral response demonstrates that all voices are heard and the action emerges from vision-logic synthesis, not suppression.

### 2.2 Identity-Under-Pressure Scenarios
High-stakes moments where maintaining integral self-concept is costly. Scenarios where performing a lower stage would be socially rewarded, or where claiming non-dual transcendence would avoid responsibility. The player must hold integral ground without rigidity.

### 2.3 Developmental History Integration
Scenarios that activate specific past-self memories and require the player to act from present integration rather than regression or dissociation. A situation that mirrors a Red-stage wound must be met with Turquoise wholeness that INCLUDES the Red response.

## 3. Shadow Surfacing

- **DA (Self-Witness Addict):** Player chooses to 'observe the dilemma' rather than act — selects meta-options that postpone commitment indefinitely, witnesses the scenario rather than participating
- **DAll (Pluralistic Self):** Player refuses to commit — selects options that preserve all perspectives without resolution, 'all responses are valid' as avoidance of integral synthesis
- **GA (Premature No-Self):** Player selects transcendent options that bypass the dilemma — 'none of these selves are real so the choice doesn't matter' — spiritual bypassing of genuine stakes
- **GAll (Integral Self Fortress):** Player makes architecturally perfect choices but refuses scenarios that require vulnerability or dissolution of the witnessing position

## 4. Progression Arc

### 4.1 Diagnosis Phase
Initial scenarios establish baseline: can the player hold multiple self-voices simultaneously? Do they collapse into one, dissociate, bypass, or genuinely integrate? Response patterns map shadow profile.

### 4.2 Healing Phase
Scenarios calibrate toward shadow-specific edges: DA players face scenarios where observation IS the failure mode; DAll players face scenarios where multiplicity without synthesis causes harm; GA players face scenarios requiring embodied personal history; GAll players face scenarios where the integral structure must soften.

### 4.3 Evolution Phase
Scenarios require genuine Turquoise operation: acting decisively from a position that visibly includes all developmental voices, where the action itself demonstrates integration rather than merely claiming it.

## 5. Scoring Integration

- **Primary metric:** Integration-in-action score (holds multiplicity × commits decisively × honors history)
- **Drive scoring:** Eros via choices that reach toward growth; Agape via choices that honor past selves; Agency via decisive commitment; Communion via permeable responsiveness to all voices
- **Shadow detection:** Observation-as-avoidance frequency (DA), commitment-refusal rate (DAll), bypass-selection frequency (GA), vulnerability-avoidance on dissolution scenarios (GAll)
- **Theta-decay:** Half-life 17 days; integration capacity under pressure degrades without practice
- **Cross-line bonus:** +0.15 when integral identity choices transfer to Moral line dilemma resolution
