# Intrapersonal / Red — Module Specification Skeleton

---

## 1. Capacity Definition

**Egocentric self-identification** — the first stable "I" that can be grasped, named, and (primitively) reported on. At Red, the self is KNOWN but not yet EXAMINED. The player can say "I am angry" but cannot yet ask "why am I angry?" or "is this anger serving me?"

### Core capacities (Red ceiling)

| Capacity | Description | Red ceiling |
|---|---|---|
| Self-identification | "I am X" — stable self-label under pressure | Concrete, egocentric ("I am the strong one") |
| State-labelling | Naming current internal state | Basic affect + motive labels (angry, wanting, afraid, proud) |
| Self-prediction | "I will do X next" — predicting own behaviour | 1-step, immediate ("I will fight") |
| Self-pattern recognition | "I always do X when Y" — noticing own patterns | Single, obvious patterns only |
| Witness-pause capacity | Momentary stepping-back from identification | Brief, unstable, requires prompting |

### What is NOT yet available (above Red)

- Self-examination ("Why do I do this?") — requires Orange reflective capacity
- Multi-self awareness ("Part of me wants X") — requires Green
- Identity flexibility ("I could be different") — requires Orange+
- Developmental self-awareness ("I'm growing") — requires Teal
- Honest self-report on uncomfortable truths — requires Amber+ (conformist honesty) or Orange (reflective honesty)

### The Intrapersonal/Red paradox

The self is GRASPED but not EXAMINED. The player identifies AS the self ("I AM the warrior") rather than having a self they can look at. This is the developmental edge: can they begin to HAVE a self rather than BE a self? The first witness-pause is the crack in identification.

## 2. Shadow Archetypes

### 2.1 Dark-Addiction: "The Narcissist"

**Core pattern:** Over-identified with a GRANDIOSE self-image that doesn't match reality. "I am the greatest warrior" when evidence shows otherwise. The self-knowledge is not absent — it's INFLATED. They have a self-image, but it's a fantasy-self, not an accurate self.

**Drive pathology:**
- Agency: Sovereign self-image that cannot be challenged; rejects all feedback
- Communion: Cannot receive others' perception of them; "You don't know me"
- Eros: Demands recognition of a self that hasn't been earned; "I deserve more"
- Agape: Cannot return to simple, honest self-labelling; "I'm beyond that"

**Behavioural signatures:**
- Self-labels are consistently inflated (rates self higher than performance shows)
- Self-predictions are grandiose (predicts success beyond demonstrated capacity)
- Rejects feedback that contradicts self-image
- Cannot name weaknesses or limitations
- Self-report vocabulary is narrow: only positive/powerful states acknowledged
- Witness-pause is refused because it might reveal the gap between image and reality

**Surfacing conditions:**
- Present a self-assessment task where honest answers would reveal limitation
- Present feedback that contradicts self-image
- Present a situation where self-prediction fails (predicted success, got failure)
- The Narcissist responds with: denial, blame-shifting, or doubling down on inflated self-image

**What "healed" looks like:**
- Self-labels match behavioural evidence (accurate, not inflated)
- Can name limitations without collapse
- Accepts feedback as data (not as attack)
- Self-predictions are calibrated to actual capacity

---

### 2.2 Dark-Allergy: "The Unexamined"

**Core pattern:** Refuses all self-knowledge. "I don't need to know myself — I just ACT." The self exists (they have Red ego) but they will not LOOK at it. Self-examination is experienced as weakness, as slowing down, as unnecessary.

**Drive pathology:**
- Agency: Acts without self-knowledge; "Thinking about myself is a waste of time"
- Communion: Cannot share self-knowledge because they have none to share
- Eros: Cannot grow intrapersonally because they refuse the first step (looking)
- Agape: Cannot maintain self-knowledge because they never acquire it

**Behavioural signatures:**
- Skips all self-labelling opportunities
- Self-predictions are absent or random (doesn't bother predicting)
- Cannot name current state when asked ("I don't know, I'm just... doing")
- Witness-pause is never attempted
- Self-report is minimal, generic, or dismissive
- High action-rate with zero reflection between actions
- Other lines' self-report data is unreliable (no intrapersonal foundation)

**Surfacing conditions:**
- Present a self-labelling task (name your current state)
- Present a self-prediction task (what will you do next?)
- Present a witness-pause opportunity (stop and notice)
- The Unexamined responds with: dismissal, impatience, or generic non-answers

**What "healed" looks like:**
- Can name current state (even if simple: "angry," "wanting," "afraid")
- Attempts self-prediction (even if inaccurate)
- Engages witness-pause briefly (even if uncomfortable)
- Recognises that self-knowledge has practical value (better predictions = better outcomes)

---

### 2.3 Golden-Addiction: "The Premature Witness"

**Core pattern:** Performs self-examination WITHOUT genuine Red self-identification. Claims "I observe my anger" but has never fully BEEN angry. The witness is premature — it's a spiritual bypass of the Red ego, not a genuine transcendence of it. They perform Orange/Green intrapersonal capacity without the Red foundation.

**Drive pathology:**
- Agency: Cannot assert from genuine self because they've "transcended" assertion
- Communion: Joins others in "self-awareness" language without genuine content
- Eros: Reaches for higher intrapersonal capacity without grounding in Red self-identification
- Agape: Cannot return to simple "I am angry" because they've "moved beyond" identification

**Behavioural signatures:**
- Uses sophisticated self-awareness language ("I notice my pattern of...")
- But self-predictions are INACCURATE (the "witness" doesn't actually know the self)
- Self-labels are abstract rather than concrete ("I'm processing" rather than "I'm angry")
- Cannot name simple, concrete states (too "evolved" for basic labelling)
- Witness-pause is PERFORMED (looks like stepping back) but doesn't produce accurate self-knowledge
- Cross-validation: language-reflective scores HIGH but deterministic scores LOW
- The gap between articulation and accuracy is the diagnostic signature

**Surfacing conditions:**
- Present a task requiring CONCRETE self-labelling (not abstract "processing")
- Present a self-prediction task (predictions will be inaccurate despite sophisticated language)
- Present a situation requiring genuine Red assertion (they'll "witness" instead of acting)
- The Premature Witness responds with: sophisticated language that doesn't match behaviour

**What "healed" looks like:**
- Can say "I am angry" and MEAN it (genuine identification, not performed observation)
- Self-predictions are accurate (the witness actually KNOWS the self)
- Concrete state-labels match observable indicators
- Witness-pause produces ACCURATE self-knowledge, not just the appearance of self-awareness

---

### 2.4 Golden-Allergy: "The Identity-Clinger"

**Core pattern:** Refuses the witness-pause. Terrified of seeing the self AS OBJECT. "I AM the warrior" and any suggestion that they could OBSERVE themselves being the warrior is experienced as annihilation-threat. The first subject→object shift is refused because it feels like death of the self.

**Drive pathology:**
- Agency: Hyper-identified with self-as-subject; "I AM this, and nothing else"
- Communion: Cannot share self-knowledge because sharing requires objectifying the self
- Eros: Refuses the developmental edge (witness-pause) because it threatens identity
- Agape: Clings to current self-identification; cannot let it become object

**Behavioural signatures:**
- Strong, clear self-identification ("I am the warrior") — Red ego is HEALTHY
- But REFUSES any stepping-back from that identification
- Witness-pause produces anxiety, anger, or shutdown
- Self-labelling is possible but ONLY in identity-confirming directions
- Cannot name states that contradict self-image (the warrior cannot be afraid)
- Reacts to witness-pause prompts with hostility or avoidance
- Self-prediction is accurate WITHIN the identity but blind to anything outside it

**Surfacing conditions:**
- Present a witness-pause opportunity (step back and observe yourself)
- Present a self-label that contradicts identity ("Are you afraid?")
- Present a situation where the self-image doesn't fit (warrior in a situation requiring vulnerability)
- The Identity-Clinger responds with: hostility, denial, or identity-reassertion

**What "healed" looks like:**
- Can briefly witness the self without identity-collapse
- Can name states that contradict self-image (the warrior CAN be afraid)
- Witness-pause is tolerable (not comfortable, but not annihilating)
- Self-identification remains strong AND can be briefly objectified

## 3. Drive-Health Landscape

| Drive | Weight | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Agency | 0.35 | Self-knowledge at Red is primarily SOVEREIGN — "I know what I am" |
| Communion | 0.15 | Minimal relational self-knowledge at Red (self-in-relation comes later) |
| Eros | 0.30 | The developmental edge — can they reach toward self-examination? |
| Agape | 0.20 | Can they return to simple self-labelling without dismissing it? |

**Total drive-health weight for consciousness index:** 0.35

### Rationale for 0.35
Intrapersonal is the META-LINE — it enables honest self-knowledge across all other lines. But at Red, its contribution is modest because the capacity is primitive (labelling, not examining). Its weight increases dramatically at Orange+ where reflective self-knowledge becomes the engine of all development.

## 4. Healing Vectors

### 4.1 For Dark-Addiction (The Narcissist)

**Vector:** Agape + Agency (return to honest self-examination with sovereign courage)

**Mechanism:**
1. Present UNDENIABLE behavioural evidence (you predicted X, you did Y)
2. Frame accuracy as POWER: "The warrior who knows his true strength fights better than the one who imagines strength he doesn't have"
3. Reward ACCURATE self-assessment over inflated self-assessment
4. Gradually: accurate self-knowledge → better predictions → better outcomes → the narcissist sees that truth is more powerful than fantasy

### 4.2 For Dark-Allergy (The Unexamined)

**Vector:** Agape + Agency (return to self-examination with sovereign will)

**Mechanism:**
1. Make self-knowledge USEFUL (predict your next action → if correct, gain advantage)
2. Frame self-examination as STRATEGIC: "The warrior who knows himself cannot be surprised"
3. Start with TINY self-knowledge demands (name ONE state; predict ONE action)
4. Gradually: small self-knowledge → practical advantage → more self-knowledge → the unexamined sees that looking inward is a weapon

### 4.3 For Golden-Addiction (The Premature Witness)

**Vector:** Eros + Communion (reach for genuine capacity through honest joining)

**Mechanism:**
1. Require CONCRETE demonstrations (not just language)
2. Cross-validate: does the "witness" actually KNOW the self? (prediction accuracy)
3. Frame: "The witness who doesn't know what it's witnessing is blind. First KNOW yourself. Then witness."
4. Gradually: concrete self-knowledge → accurate witness → genuine intrapersonal capacity (not performed)

### 4.4 For Golden-Allergy (The Identity-Clinger)

**Vector:** Eros + Communion (reach for the witness-pause through safe joining)

**Mechanism:**
1. Make the witness-pause SAFE (identity doesn't collapse; you're still the warrior AFTER you observe)
2. Frame: "The warrior who can see himself fight is STRONGER, not weaker. Observation is power."
3. Start with MICRO witness-pauses (0.5 seconds; barely stepping back)
4. Gradually: micro-pauses → brief pauses → stable witness → the identity-clinger discovers that observation doesn't destroy identity

## 5. Scoring Parameters

| Parameter | Value | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Half-life | 19 days | Moderate-slow. Self-knowledge is relatively stable once established but degrades without practice. Faster than Moral (21d) because Red intrapersonal is simpler (labelling vs. consequence-chains). |
| Max decay | 20% | Cannot lose more than 20% of demonstrated capacity |
| Decay curve | Exponential | Standard |
| Recovery rate | 1.3× | Faster recovery than initial acquisition (self-knowledge re-emerges quickly) |
| Practice sensitivity | HIGH | Even brief self-labelling practice arrests decay |

### Decay-arrest conditions
- Any self-labelling activity (even in other modules) arrests intrapersonal decay
- Witness-pause use in combat arrests decay
- Self-prediction attempts (even inaccurate) arrest decay

## 6. Compound Shadows & Cross-Module Relationships

### 6.1 The Blind Warrior (Intrapersonal + Willpower)

**Loop:** Intrapersonal dark-allergy (won't look) + Willpower dark-addiction (compulsive action) = massive force with zero self-knowledge. They ACT without knowing WHY they act. The will drives them but they cannot name what drives the will.

**Detection:** High willpower scores + zero intrapersonal engagement. Action without reflection. Cannot answer "why did you do that?"

**Breaking the loop:** Willpower tasks that REQUIRE self-knowledge to succeed (predict your fatigue point; name what drives this goal). Make self-knowledge a POWER tool.

### 6.2 The Self-Absorbed (Intrapersonal + Interpersonal)

**Loop:** Intrapersonal dark-addiction (narcissistic self-focus) + Interpersonal dark-allergy (cannot see others) = obsessed with self-image, blind to others. All attention is inward (but inaccurately) and none is outward.

**Detection:** High intrapersonal engagement (but inflated) + zero interpersonal engagement. Talks about self constantly but cannot describe others.

**Breaking the loop:** Self-knowledge tasks that REQUIRE others' input (how do others see you? Does their perception match yours?). Make accurate self-knowledge dependent on interpersonal data.

### 6.3 The Spiritual Bypasser (Intrapersonal + Spiritual)

**Loop:** Intrapersonal golden-addiction (premature witness) + Spiritual golden-addiction (premature transcendence) = performs "enlightened self-awareness" without genuine Red grounding. Claims to have transcended ego without ever having a healthy ego.

**Detection:** Sophisticated intrapersonal + spiritual language + LOW accuracy on concrete self-knowledge tasks + LOW Red-stage capacity scores.

**Breaking the loop:** Require CONCRETE Red demonstrations before accepting "transcendent" claims. "Before you witness your anger, BE angry. Before you transcend ego, HAVE an ego."

### 6.4 The Emotional Stranger (Intrapersonal + Emotional)

**Loop:** Intrapersonal dark-allergy (won't look inward) + Emotional dark-allergy (won't feel) = disconnected from entire interior. Cannot name states because they refuse to look AND refuse to feel.

**Detection:** Zero intrapersonal engagement + zero emotional engagement. Flat affect + no self-report. "I'm fine" to everything.

**Breaking the loop:** Somatic entry point — the body feels even when the mind refuses. Use embodied-somatic modality to surface states that bypass the refusal to look/feel.

## 7. Shadow Surfacing Sequence

The game surfaces shadows in a specific developmental order:

```
1. Self-identification → Does a stable "I am X" exist?
   ├─ If inflated → Narcissist pathway
   ├─ If absent/refused → Unexamined pathway
   └─ If present and accurate → proceed

2. State-labelling → Can they name current states?
   ├─ If abstract/performed → Premature Witness pathway
   ├─ If refused → Unexamined pathway
   └─ If concrete and accurate → proceed

3. Self-prediction → Can they predict own behaviour?
   ├─ If grandiose → Narcissist pathway
   ├─ If absent → Unexamined pathway
   ├─ If sophisticated but inaccurate → Premature Witness pathway
   └─ If calibrated → proceed

4. Witness-pause → Can they step back briefly?
   ├─ If refused with hostility → Identity-Clinger pathway
   ├─ If performed without accuracy → Premature Witness pathway
   ├─ If refused with dismissal → Unexamined pathway
   └─ If brief and genuine → healthy

5. Integration → Can they hold self-as-object AND self-as-subject?
   └─ This is the Red ceiling / Amber threshold
```
