# Intrapersonal / Green — Module Specification

> **Line:** Intrapersonal  **Stage:** Green / Pluralistic  **Vibration:** The self discovers it contains multitudes and learns to hold them without collapsing into one or dissolving into none.

---

## 1. Capacity Definition

The pluralistic self — the capacity to hold multiple valid self-concepts simultaneously without needing to resolve them into a single identity. Identity-as-process rather than identity-as-achievement. Paradox tolerance applied to the self. "Part of me wants X, part wants Y" held as genuine multiplicity rather than confusion to be resolved. Self-in-relation: the self as constituted partly by its contexts and relationships. The first developmental moment where the self can genuinely contain contradiction without anxiety.

## 2. Shadow Archetypes

### 2.1 Dark-Addiction: The Fragmented Self

Cannot maintain coherent identity because all sub-selves are treated as equally valid with no centre. Identity dissolution masquerading as openness. "I am everything and nothing." Performative multiplicity — claims many selves but cannot act from any of them. Paralysis through infinite self-possibility.

### 2.2 Dark-Allergy: The Fixed Achiever

Refuses multiplicity entirely. Remains in Orange self-authorship: "I know exactly who I am." Dismisses paradox as confusion, contextual identity as weakness. Rigid self-concept maintained through denial of genuine inner plurality. Treats self-contradiction as failure rather than depth.

### 2.3 Golden-Addiction: The Premature Witness

Performs Teal witness-awareness without having genuinely inhabited Green multiplicity. Claims "I am the awareness behind all selves" while unable to actually hold paradox in lived experience. Spiritual bypass of the messy work of plural selfhood. Transcendence without inclusion.

### 2.4 Golden-Allergy: The Multiplicity Fortress

Genuine pluralistic self is present but refuses the integral move toward commitment and prioritisation. Terrified that choosing one self-aspect would betray the others. "All my parts are equally valid" becomes a fortress against growth. Sophisticated paralysis dressed as radical acceptance.

## 4. Healing Vectors

### 4.1 Heal/Evolve (Agape + Agency)

Agape returns to each sub-self with full presence; Agency provides a centre that holds them. Integrates dark shadows by grounding multiplicity in embodied coherence. The Fragmented Self finds a centre; the Fixed Achiever discovers genuine parts.

### 4.2 Evolve/Heal (Eros + Communion)

Eros reaches toward integral commitment; Communion dissolves the boundary between self-parts. Dissolves golden shadows by allowing multiplicity to self-organise. The Premature Witness must return to genuine paradox; the Multiplicity Fortress allows hierarchy to emerge relationally.

### 4.3 Integration Criteria

Player demonstrates: (a) naming 3+ genuine self-aspects without anxiety, (b) acting from different aspects contextually without fragmentation, (c) tolerating self-contradiction without resolving or dissolving, (d) beginning to sense which aspects serve which contexts (proto-hierarchy).

## 6. Compound Shadows & Cross-Module Relationships

### 6.1 The Fragmented Empath (Intrapersonal DA × Emotional DA)

Identity dissolution combined with empathic overwhelm. Lost in self AND others simultaneously. Cannot distinguish own multiplicity from absorbed emotional states of others.

### 6.2 The Fixed Loner (Intrapersonal DAll × Interpersonal DAll)

Rigid self-concept combined with refusal of authentic relating. Unchanging and unreachable. The fixed identity is maintained precisely by avoiding relationships that would reveal its multiplicity.

### 6.3 The Witness Performer (Intrapersonal GA × Spiritual GA)

Premature witness combined with premature interfaith synthesis. Performed transcendence — looks integral but has bypassed the genuine pluralistic work in both lines.

### 6.4 The Multiplicity Fortress (Intrapersonal GAll × Moral GAll)

Refuses self-hierarchy combined with refusal of moral hierarchy. Sophisticated paralysis — cannot prioritise selves or values, dressed as radical egalitarianism.

## 7. Shadow Surfacing Sequence

### 7.1 Progressive Revelation

Sessions 1–3: baseline multiplicity capacity (how many selves can be held). Sessions 4–8: stress-test coherence (do selves fragment or rigidify under pressure). Sessions 9–14: golden shadow probes (does player bypass into witness or refuse commitment). Sessions 15+: compound shadow detection via cross-line correlation.
