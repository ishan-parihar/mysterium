# Intrapersonal / Green — Immersive-RPG Game Concept

> **Axis:** Ecological plural self — whether identity-as-process appears spontaneously in free-play.  **Why this axis for this module:** Free-form RPG play reveals whether multiplicity is a lived capacity or only a task-performance; spontaneous identity-flexibility in narrative immersion is the ecological validity test.

---

## 1. Game Identity

**Title:** The Shifting Protagonist  
**Core loop:** Player inhabits a character whose identity is genuinely plural — different NPCs know different versions of the protagonist, and the world responds to which self-aspect the player expresses. Free-play choices reveal whether the player naturally inhabits multiplicity or defaults to shadow patterns when not explicitly prompted.  
**Session length:** 8–15 minutes per narrative checkpoint.  
**Infinite checkpoint:** Yes — narrative saves at each decision node.

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Catalyst type:** An open narrative world where identity-flexibility is rewarded but never demanded — the catalyst is ecological, arising from the world's responsiveness to the player's natural self-expression patterns.  
**Shadow surfacing:** DA surfaces as incoherent narrative choices (protagonist becomes unrecognisable across scenes). DAll surfaces as rigid role-lock (player never varies the protagonist's expression regardless of context). GA surfaces as narrator-mode (player observes the story rather than inhabiting it, meta-commentary without engagement). GAll surfaces as exhaustive role-exploration without narrative commitment (tries everything, commits to nothing, story never advances).  
**Drive probes:** Agency = protagonist maintains recognisable centre across contexts. Communion = protagonist adapts genuinely to relational demands. Eros = protagonist risks new self-expression in high-stakes moments. Agape = protagonist returns to core identity after expansion.

## 3. Game Design

**Mechanic 1 — Multi-Faceted Protagonist:** The player character has 4–6 established identity facets (warrior/scholar/lover/trickster/healer/leader). Different quests and NPCs respond to different facets. Player chooses which to express in each encounter.  
**Mechanic 2 — Reputation Ecology:** Each NPC community knows the protagonist through a particular facet. Showing a new facet to a community creates narrative tension (surprise, distrust, deepened respect — depending on authenticity). The world tracks coherence across facets.  
**Mechanic 3 — Identity Crucible:** Narrative climaxes force the protagonist to act from multiple facets simultaneously or choose between them under pressure. How the player navigates these moments reveals ecological multiplicity capacity.  
**Progression:** Early game establishes facets individually. Mid-game creates cross-facet encounters. Late game demands integrated multiplicity. Endgame probes the Green→Teal edge (can the protagonist witness their own multiplicity?).

## 4. Item Pool

- 6 protagonist facets with distinct dialogue styles, abilities, and relational signatures
- 4 NPC communities (each initially knows 1–2 facets)
- 16 narrative encounters (4 per facet-pair, designed to surface specific shadow types)
- 4 identity crucible scenarios (one per shadow quadrant as primary attractor)
- Branching narrative: 3 major story arcs that reward different expressions of healthy multiplicity

## 5. Technical Requirements

**Input:** Dialogue selection, facet-foregrounding choice per encounter, free exploration between narrative nodes.  
**Metrics captured:** Facet-usage distribution, context-appropriateness of facet choices, narrative coherence score, crucible navigation patterns, spontaneous multiplicity index (facet-switching without prompt).  
**Adaptive engine:** Narrative encounters selected based on shadow hypothesis. Crucible difficulty scales with demonstrated capacity. NPC communities adjust perceptiveness to player level.  
**Narrative engine:** Branching dialogue with facet-tagged options. World-state tracks reputation per facet per community. Coherence computed as recognisability-across-difference (not sameness).  
**Accessibility:** All interaction via selection. No time pressure on narrative choices. Audio narration available. Visual facet indicators for clarity.
