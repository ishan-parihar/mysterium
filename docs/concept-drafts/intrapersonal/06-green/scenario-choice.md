# Intrapersonal / Green — Scenario-Choice Game Concept

> **Axis:** Identity dilemmas where sub-selves conflict, or multiplicity conflicts with commitment.  **Why this axis for this module:** Choice under identity-conflict is the crucible where pluralistic selfhood is tested — can the player choose without collapsing or refusing?

---

## 1. Game Identity

**Title:** The Crossroads Council  
**Core loop:** Player faces life-dilemmas where different self-aspects advocate for incompatible choices. Player must navigate the inner council — listening to each voice, holding the tension, and eventually choosing (or not choosing) — revealing shadow patterns through their decision process.  
**Session length:** 6–10 minutes per dilemma (1–2 dilemmas per session).  
**Infinite checkpoint:** Yes — each dilemma is self-contained with its own resolution arc.

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Catalyst type:** Forced-choice scenarios where no option satisfies all self-aspects, creating genuine identity tension that demands pluralistic navigation.  
**Shadow surfacing:** DA surfaces as inability to choose (all voices equally loud, no centre). DAll surfaces as immediate choice without consulting other voices (one self dominates). GA surfaces as meta-choice ("I'll just witness the choosing") that avoids genuine engagement. GAll surfaces as explicit refusal to choose ("all options are valid, choosing would betray a part of me").  
**Drive probes:** Agency = capacity to choose from centre. Communion = honouring relational voices in the council. Eros = choosing toward growth. Agape = choosing with compassion for the unchosen parts.

## 3. Game Design

**Mechanic 1 — Council Assembly:** 3–5 inner voices present their case for different choices. Player can "amplify" or "acknowledge" each voice. Pattern of engagement reveals shadow type.  
**Mechanic 2 — Tension Holding:** After hearing all voices, a tension meter rises. Player must hold the tension (not collapse into premature choice or avoidance) until genuine clarity emerges. Optimal hold-time is adaptive per player.  
**Mechanic 3 — Committed Choice:** Player chooses. Post-choice, unchosen voices respond. Player must integrate their responses (acknowledge grief, honour the part, commit anyway). Quality of integration scored.  
**Progression:** Early dilemmas are low-stakes (weekend plans). Mid dilemmas are relational (career vs. relationship). Late dilemmas are existential (identity commitment vs. openness). Final dilemmas probe the Green→Teal edge.

## 4. Item Pool

- 24 dilemma scenarios (8 low-stakes, 8 relational, 8 existential)
- 5 archetypal inner voices per dilemma (the achiever, the nurturer, the adventurer, the protector, the seeker — contextually adapted)
- 4 post-choice integration challenges per dilemma (one per shadow type as potential attractor)
- Branching outcomes: each choice leads to a brief consequence vignette that reveals whether the choice was integrated or shadow-driven

## 5. Technical Requirements

**Input:** Tap/click to amplify voices, hold to sustain tension, confirm to commit choice, text or selection for integration response.  
**Metrics captured:** Voice-engagement pattern (which voices heard/ignored), tension hold-duration, choice latency, integration quality score, shadow-type classification per dilemma.  
**Adaptive engine:** Dilemma selection based on shadow-type hypothesis — presents scenarios that probe the suspected shadow. Difficulty = stakes level + number of competing voices.  
**Narrative coherence:** Dilemmas drawn from a life-narrative pool that maintains continuity across sessions (same fictional life, escalating complexity).  
**Accessibility:** All voices have distinct visual and audio signatures. No time pressure on choice (only on tension-hold). Simplified voice-count for younger players.
