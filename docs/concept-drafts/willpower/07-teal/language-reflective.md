# Willpower × Turquoise — Language-Reflective

## 1. Core Mechanic

The player engages in **Integral Commitment Articulation** — prompted verbal/written responses about purpose, goals, and will. An LLM-driven rubric evaluates depth of integral understanding, distinguishing genuine holonic will from performative language, Green-level flatness, or premature transcendence claims.

### 1.1 Prompt Architecture

- **Purpose Articulation:** "Describe what you are committed to and why it matters across all scales"
- **Conflict Resolution:** "Two goals at different levels conflict — how do you hold both?"
- **Surrender vs. Structure:** "When do you let go of a goal? How do you know?"
- **Evolutionary Framing:** "How does your immediate action serve something beyond yourself?"

### 1.2 Rubric Dimensions

| Dimension | Low (Green) | Mid (Turquoise) | High (approaching White) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Holonic depth | Single-level or flat | Multi-level nested | Nested + fluid |
| Agency-communion balance | One-sided | Both named | Both embodied in language |
| Temporal span | Present/near-future | Multi-generational | Evolutionary timescale |
| Surrender capacity | Absent or premature | Contextual | Integrated with structure |

## 2. Shadow Diagnostics

| Shadow | Linguistic Signal |
|---|---|
| DA (Will-Architect) | Elaborate holonic language but no concrete action-commitments; abstract architecture without grounding |
| DAll (Collaborative Pluralist) | Refuses hierarchy in language; "all goals matter equally"; Green-coded collaboration language only |
| GA (Premature Surrenderer) | Spiritual bypass language — "I surrender to the flow" without demonstrating structural capacity |
| GAll (Integral Will Fortress) | Rigid commitment language; inability to articulate conditions for release; "I will never let go" |

### 2.1 LLM Scoring Protocol

The rubric scores each response on 4 axes (holonic depth, balance, temporal span, surrender capacity) from 1–7. Shadow flags trigger when specific axis patterns emerge:
- DA: holonic depth ≥ 6 but action-concreteness ≤ 2
- DAll: holonic depth ≤ 3 consistently
- GA: surrender capacity ≥ 6 but holonic depth ≤ 3
- GAll: holonic depth ≥ 5 but surrender capacity ≤ 2

## 3. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

**Catalyst:** Prompts that create genuine tension between structure and surrender — situations where the "right" answer requires holding both without collapsing to either.

**Experience:** The felt-sense of articulating something true about one's will — the moment language catches up to lived commitment and the player recognises their own depth (or performance).

**Integration:** Longitudinal tracking of language complexity and authenticity. The system surfaces growth by showing earlier responses alongside current ones (implicit comparison, never judgmental).

## 4. Progression Arc

1. **Diagnosis (sessions 1–3):** Baseline articulation capacity; shadow-language identification
2. **Healing (sessions 4–10):** Prompts specifically designed to challenge the detected shadow-language pattern
3. **Evolution (sessions 11+):** Increasingly subtle prompts requiring genuine integral will articulation

### 4.1 Mastery Indicators

- Consistent rubric scores ≥ 5 across all 4 dimensions
- Shadow-flag absence for 5+ consecutive sessions
- Increasing action-concreteness alongside holonic depth
- Language that integrates structure AND surrender without contradiction

## 5. Integration with Module

Language-reflective scores cross-validate deterministic (does articulation match execution?) and scenario-choice (does stated commitment match actual choices?). Discrepancies between language and behaviour are the primary shadow-detection signal. Drive-health weight 0.30 applies; shadow-drag 0.22 when language-behaviour gap exceeds threshold.
