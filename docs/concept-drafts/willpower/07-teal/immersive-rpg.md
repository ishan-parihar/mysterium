# Willpower × Turquoise — Immersive-RPG

## 1. Core Mechanic

The player enters an **Ecological Integral Will Environment** — an open-world RPG zone where holonic commitment must emerge spontaneously from free-play. No explicit goal-hierarchy is presented. The world contains nested challenges at multiple scales, and the system observes whether the player naturally organises their engagement holonically or falls into shadow patterns.

### 1.1 World Design

- **Multi-Scale Ecology:** The zone contains personal quests, relational obligations, community needs, and an evolutionary crisis — all active simultaneously, none explicitly prioritised by the game
- **Emergent Architecture:** The player must self-organise their engagement across scales. The world rewards holonic will (actions at one level that serve multiple levels) and reveals shadow (single-level fixation, avoidance, bypass)
- **NPC Ecosystem:** Characters at different developmental altitudes with their own will-expressions. The player's interactions reveal their natural collaborative style.
- **Temporal Pressure:** Real-time progression means neglected levels degrade — the player cannot infinitely defer

### 1.2 Observation Metrics

- Spontaneous goal-hierarchy formation (does the player naturally nest their engagement?)
- Scale-switching fluidity (how smoothly does the player move between personal and collective action?)
- Will-expression under ambiguity (what does the player do when no clear directive exists?)
- Shadow emergence under stress (which pattern appears when temporal pressure increases?)

## 2. Shadow Diagnostics

| Shadow | Free-Play Pattern |
|---|---|
| DA (Will-Architect) | Spends session planning routes and optimising engagement order; never begins the actual quests |
| DAll (Collaborative Pluralist) | Engages only with NPC collaboration; avoids solo evolutionary quests; stays in relational mode |
| GA (Premature Surrenderer) | Wanders without direction claiming "I'm following the flow"; no discernible architecture emerges |
| GAll (Integral Will Fortress) | Follows a rigid self-imposed plan; refuses to respond to emergent world-events; "I have my architecture" |

### 2.1 Ecological Validity

This modality has highest ecological validity — it measures what the player actually does when unconstrained. Shadow patterns here are the most reliable because they emerge without prompting. Compound shadows are most visible:
- "Architect of Everything": plans the entire zone but completes nothing
- "Cosmic Bypasser": wanders beautifully but accomplishes nothing developmental
- "Holonic Fortress": executes perfectly but cannot adapt to the living world

## 3. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

**Catalyst:** The world itself is the catalyst — its multi-scale complexity demands integral will. The absence of explicit structure forces the player to generate their own holonic architecture or reveal their shadow.

**Experience:** The felt-sense of integral will as ecological engagement — the moment when personal action, relational care, collective service, and evolutionary purpose align spontaneously. Or the recognition of one's shadow pattern in the mirror of free-play.

**Integration:** Session-over-session tracking of ecological will patterns. The system identifies growth trajectories and shadow-persistence without ever making them explicit to the player. The world subtly adapts to challenge the player's edge.

## 4. Progression Arc

1. **Diagnosis (sessions 1–5):** Extended free-play observation; shadow-pattern identification through ecological behaviour
2. **Healing (sessions 6–12):** World subtly shifts to challenge the detected shadow — creates conditions where the shadow-pattern fails and the healthy alternative succeeds
3. **Evolution (sessions 13+):** Increasingly complex ecological demands until the player demonstrates spontaneous holonic will across all scales

### 4.1 Mastery Indicators

- Spontaneous multi-scale engagement without prompting
- Scale-switching fluidity ≥ 0.7 (smooth transitions between levels)
- No single shadow pattern dominating for 3+ consecutive sessions
- Ecological will-expression matches or exceeds structured modality scores

## 5. Integration with Module

Immersive-RPG is the ultimate validation — if holonic will appears spontaneously in free-play, it is genuinely integrated. Discrepancies between structured modalities (deterministic, strategic-planning) and free-play reveal performance vs. genuine capacity. Drive-health weight 0.30; shadow-drag 0.22. This modality has veto power: if free-play shows persistent shadow, structured modality scores are discounted.
