# Willpower × Turquoise — Embodied-Somatic

## 1. Core Mechanic

The player engages in **Integral Will Embodiment** — physical tasks requiring the body to express holonic commitment. Multi-level rhythm patterns, sustained physical engagement across varying intensities, and somatic alignment tasks measure whether integral will lives in the body or only in cognition.

### 1.1 Task Structure

- **Multi-Level Rhythm:** Simultaneous maintenance of 3+ rhythmic patterns at different tempos — body as holonic architecture (hands at one tempo, feet at another, breath at a third)
- **Sustained Commitment Under Perturbation:** Physical task requiring steady output while environmental rhythm shifts; measures embodied will-stability
- **Tension-Release Cycles:** Deliberate engagement followed by deliberate release — the somatic signature of structure-and-surrender
- **Alignment Postures:** Physical positions requiring simultaneous engagement of multiple muscle groups in coordinated hierarchy (core supports limbs supports extremities)

### 1.2 Measurement

- Rhythm coherence across levels (accelerometer/touch patterns)
- Commitment duration before collapse
- Release quality (clean vs. gradual vs. sudden)
- Recovery speed after perturbation

## 2. Shadow Diagnostics

| Shadow | Somatic Signal |
|---|---|
| DA (Will-Architect) | Excessive preparation movements; adjusts posture endlessly before committing; body mirrors cognitive over-planning |
| DAll (Collaborative Pluralist) | Flat rhythm — all levels at same intensity; refuses hierarchical engagement; body stays at Green-level uniformity |
| GA (Premature Surrenderer) | Collapses to release too quickly; cannot sustain multi-level engagement; body "surrenders" before architecture is established |
| GAll (Integral Will Fortress) | Cannot release; sustained tension without recovery cycles; body holds rigidly even when release is cued |

### 2.1 Detection Metrics

- DA: preparation-to-execution time ratio > 2.5
- DAll: inter-level intensity variance < 0.1
- GA: mean commitment duration < 40% of capacity before release
- GAll: release-quality score < 0.2 (inability to let go cleanly)

## 3. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

**Catalyst:** Physical tasks demanding both sustained hierarchical engagement AND clean release — the body cannot fake integral will. Cognitive bypass is impossible when the soma is the instrument.

**Experience:** The felt-sense of integral will as embodied reality — the moment when multi-level commitment becomes effortless because the body has learned the architecture. Or the moment of genuine release after sustained engagement.

**Integration:** Body-memory consolidation — the somatic pattern of holonic will becomes available as a resource in non-game contexts. The player's body knows what integral commitment feels like.

## 4. Progression Arc

1. **Diagnosis (sessions 1–3):** Baseline somatic will capacity; shadow-pattern identification through body-response
2. **Healing (sessions 4–10):** Targeted somatic challenges — forced sustained engagement (GA), forced release (GAll), forced hierarchy (DAll), forced execution (DA)
3. **Evolution (sessions 11+):** Increasingly complex multi-level embodiment until fluid somatic will — engaged, hierarchical, and releasable

### 4.1 Mastery Indicators

- 3+ level rhythm coherence ≥ 0.8 sustained for 60+ seconds
- Clean tension-release cycles with recovery < 3 seconds
- Perturbation recovery without level-collapse
- No shadow signal exceeding threshold for 5 consecutive sessions

Cross-validates deterministic (does physical execution match cognitive planning?) and language-reflective (does articulated commitment match somatic capacity?).
