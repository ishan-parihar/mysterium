# Willpower × Turquoise — Deterministic

## 1. Core Mechanic

The player faces a **Holonic Goal Alignment Engine** — a multi-level goal hierarchy presented visually as nested holons. Tasks require correctly ordering, nesting, and prioritising goals across evolutionary timescales. The system measures accuracy of holonic structuring, speed of alignment recognition, and commitment stability under perturbation.

### 1.1 Task Structure

- **Goal-Hierarchy Sorting:** Given 8–12 goals spanning personal, relational, collective, and evolutionary scales, player arranges them into correct holonic nesting
- **Alignment Detection:** Rapid identification of which goals serve which super-goals; timed matching under increasing complexity
- **Commitment Perturbation:** Mid-task disruptions test whether player maintains holonic coherence or collapses to single-level optimisation
- **Multi-Level Execution:** Simultaneous tracking of goals at 3+ levels while executing the immediate sub-goal accurately

### 1.2 Adaptive Difficulty

Staircase adjusts: number of holonic levels (3→7), perturbation frequency, time pressure, and ambiguity of goal-relationships.

## 2. Shadow Diagnostics

| Shadow | Behavioural Signal |
|---|---|
| DA (Will-Architect) | Perfect hierarchy construction but failure to execute immediate sub-goals; endless re-arrangement |
| DAll (Collaborative Pluralist) | Flat arrangement — refuses nesting, treats all goals as equal priority |
| GA (Premature Surrenderer) | Skips hierarchy entirely; selects "evolutionary purpose" without constructing supporting architecture |
| GAll (Integral Will Fortress) | Rigid hierarchy; refuses to release structure when perturbation reveals a better arrangement |

### 2.1 Detection Metrics

- DA: hierarchy-edit-count / execution-count ratio > 3.0
- DAll: nesting-depth consistently ≤ 2 despite available levels
- GA: time-to-completion < 20% of expected (bypassing construction)
- GAll: perturbation-adaptation-rate < 0.15 (refuses restructuring)

## 3. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

**Catalyst:** The goal-hierarchy becomes genuinely complex — no single arrangement is obviously correct. Player must hold vision-logic tension.

**Experience:** Felt-sense of holonic will emerging — the moment when the architecture "clicks" and all levels serve one evolutionary impulse without any level being sacrificed.

**Integration:** Post-task reflection prompt (implicit): the system presents the player's hierarchy alongside an "ideal" and measures convergence over sessions.

## 4. Progression Arc

1. **Diagnosis (sessions 1–3):** Baseline holonic structuring capacity; shadow-pattern identification
2. **Healing (sessions 4–10):** Targeted perturbations that specifically challenge the detected shadow
3. **Evolution (sessions 11+):** Increasing complexity until player demonstrates fluid holonic will — accurate, adaptive, and executable

### 4.1 Mastery Indicators

- Holonic accuracy ≥ 85% at 5+ levels
- Perturbation recovery < 2 seconds
- Execution-to-planning ratio ≥ 0.7
- No single shadow signal exceeding threshold for 5 consecutive sessions

## 5. Integration with Module

Deterministic scores feed the drive-health composite with weight 0.30. Shadow signals cross-reference with language-reflective (do they talk about holonic will but fail to execute it?) and strategic-planning (can they plan holonically over time?). Cross-line bonus +0.15 activates when Cognitive line shows parallel holonic capacity.
