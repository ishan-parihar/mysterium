# Willpower × Turquoise — Scenario-Choice

## 1. Core Mechanic

The player faces **Holonic Will Dilemmas** — branching scenarios where goals at different holonic levels genuinely conflict. No option satisfies all levels simultaneously. The player must demonstrate integral prioritisation: honouring all levels while making a decisive commitment that serves evolutionary purpose.

### 1.1 Dilemma Architecture

- **Level Conflicts:** Personal goal vs. relational goal vs. collective goal vs. evolutionary goal — all legitimate, none dismissible
- **Temporal Conflicts:** Immediate execution vs. long-term architecture vs. evolutionary patience
- **Agency-Communion Tensions:** Sovereign will vs. will-in-service; when does holding your ground serve the whole?
- **Structure-Surrender Edges:** When does maintaining the architecture become rigidity? When does releasing become avoidance?

### 1.2 Choice Evaluation

Choices scored not on "correctness" but on:
- Holonic awareness: does the choice acknowledge all levels?
- Decisiveness: does the player commit or defer indefinitely?
- Integration: does the choice serve multiple levels simultaneously?
- Shadow-absence: does the choice avoid the player's identified shadow pattern?

## 2. Shadow Diagnostics

| Shadow | Choice Pattern |
|---|---|
| DA (Will-Architect) | Chooses "restructure the goal hierarchy" options; defers action to plan more |
| DAll (Collaborative Pluralist) | Chooses "honour all equally" options; refuses to prioritise; consensus-seeking |
| GA (Premature Surrenderer) | Chooses "release attachment to outcome" without having engaged the dilemma |
| GAll (Integral Will Fortress) | Chooses "maintain commitment regardless"; refuses contextual adaptation |

### 2.1 Scenario Categories

1. **The Mentor's Dilemma:** Your evolutionary project requires solitude, but a student at Green needs guidance now
2. **The Architecture Collapse:** Your holonic plan is working, but new information suggests a fundamentally different structure
3. **The Surrender Invitation:** Everything is aligned — do you maintain active will or release into effortless flow?
4. **The Green Pull:** Your community wants collaborative decision-making on something requiring integral leadership

## 3. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

**Catalyst:** Dilemmas with no clean resolution — every choice sacrifices something real. The player cannot optimise their way out.

**Experience:** The felt-sense of integral will as lived commitment — choosing decisively while holding grief for what is not chosen.

**Integration:** Post-choice reflection shows consequences across all holonic levels. The player sees how their choice rippled through the system — deepening holonic awareness for next time.

## 4. Progression Arc

1. **Diagnosis (sessions 1–3):** Baseline choice patterns; shadow identification through repeated preference
2. **Healing (sessions 4–10):** Scenarios specifically targeting the detected shadow — forcing the player into avoided territory
3. **Evolution (sessions 11+):** Increasingly subtle dilemmas where all options look integral — only genuine vision-logic distinguishes depth

### 4.1 Mastery Indicators

- Choice patterns show no consistent shadow-avoidance
- Holonic awareness scores ≥ 4/5 across all dilemma types
- Decisiveness maintained even in maximum-ambiguity scenarios
- Post-choice reflection demonstrates genuine multi-level holding
