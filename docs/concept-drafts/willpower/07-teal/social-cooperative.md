# Willpower × Turquoise — Social-Cooperative

## 1. Core Mechanic

The player engages in **Shared Integral Will** — cooperative tasks requiring cross-altitude collaboration where participants at different developmental levels must align their wills without flattening the hierarchy. The player must mentor, coordinate, and serve the collective evolutionary purpose while maintaining sovereign agency.

### 1.1 Task Structure

- **Cross-Altitude Coordination:** Player (at Turquoise) coordinates with AI agents operating at Green, Orange, and Amber will-levels. Each agent has legitimate goals; player must create holonic architecture that includes all without reducing to lowest common denominator.
- **Mentoring Will-Development:** Guide a Green-level agent toward integral will without imposing — the agent must discover holonic structuring through facilitation, not instruction.
- **Collective Purpose Emergence:** Group task where evolutionary purpose must emerge from collaboration rather than being imposed by the player.
- **Shared Sacrifice:** Scenarios where the player's personal holonic architecture must be partially released to serve the collective — tests communion-capacity of integral will.

### 1.2 Evaluation Criteria

- Inclusion: are all altitude-levels genuinely served by the collective architecture?
- Sovereignty: does the player maintain integral will without collapsing to Green consensus?
- Mentoring quality: does the Green-level agent show will-development over the interaction?
- Emergence: does collective purpose arise organically or through imposition?

## 2. Shadow Diagnostics

| Shadow | Social Pattern |
|---|---|
| DA (Will-Architect) | Designs elaborate collective architecture but doesn't engage relationally; treats others as nodes in a system |
| DAll (Collaborative Pluralist) | Collapses to Green consensus; refuses to lead or prioritise; "everyone's will matters equally" |

| GAll (Integral Will Fortress) | Imposes holonic architecture without collaboration; refuses to adapt plan based on others' input |

### 2.1 Detection Metrics

- DA: relational-engagement score < 0.3 despite high architecture-quality
- DAll: hierarchy-assertion = 0; all decisions deferred to consensus
- GA: leadership-contribution < 20% of group interactions
- GAll: adaptation-to-input ratio < 0.2 (ignores others' contributions)

## 3. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

**Catalyst:** The collective task genuinely requires integral will — Green consensus cannot solve it, but neither can unilateral imposition. The player must find the integral-cooperative mode.

**Experience:** The felt-sense of shared integral will — when sovereign agency and collective service become one movement. When leading and following are simultaneous. When the architecture serves everyone because everyone's will is genuinely included.

**Integration:** Relationship patterns persist across sessions. AI agents "remember" the player's collaborative style — growth in shared will is reflected in increasingly sophisticated collective challenges.

## 4. Progression Arc

1. **Diagnosis (sessions 1–3):** Baseline cooperative will capacity; shadow identification through social patterns
2. **Healing (sessions 4–10):** Targeted social challenges — forced relational engagement (DA), forced leadership (DAll), forced structure-provision (GA), forced adaptation (GAll)
3. **Evolution (sessions 11+):** Increasingly complex cross-altitude scenarios until fluid shared integral will — sovereign, inclusive, and emergent

### 4.1 Mastery Indicators

- Cross-altitude inclusion score ≥ 0.8
- Sovereignty maintained (no Green-collapse) across 5+ cooperative sessions
- Mentored agent shows measurable will-development
- Collective purpose emergence without imposition for 3+ consecutive scenarios
