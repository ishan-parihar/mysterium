# Willpower / Green — Module Specification

> **Line:** Willpower  **Stage:** Green / Pluralistic  **Vibration:** The will that holds multiple valid commitments in creative tension without collapsing into paralysis or premature resolution.

---

## 1. Capacity Definition

Pluralistic will is the capacity to sustain genuine commitment across multiple legitimate goals simultaneously, revising priorities through reflective dialogue rather than hierarchical override. It is the discipline of NOT over-committing — holding agency within communion, serving community without dissolving sovereign will. The player demonstrates this capacity by maintaining goal-fidelity under competing demands, collaborating on shared commitments, and revising goals without abandoning them.

## 2. Shadow Archetypes

### 2.1 Dark-Addiction: The Commitment Paralysis

Cannot commit because every goal appears equally valid. Over-deliberates, scatters will across too many causes, burns out through over-inclusion. "But what about this other worthy cause?" The will fragments into impotence through excessive sensitivity to competing claims.

### 2.2 Dark-Allergy: The Lone Achiever

Refuses collaborative will entirely. Remains in Orange individual achievement mode — "I don't need consensus to act." Dismisses shared commitment as groupthink. Maintains effective agency but at the cost of relational depth and communal purpose.

### 2.3 Golden-Addiction: The Premature Surrenderer

Performs Teal "surrender to the whole" without having genuinely developed Green collaborative will. Claims "I serve the universe" while unable to commit authentically to one community. Bypasses the hard work of pluralistic negotiation by leaping to transpersonal abstraction.

### 2.4 Golden-Allergy: The Pluralist Fortress

Collaborative will is present but refuses integral commitment. Terrified of hierarchy in service. "No one goal is more important than another" becomes a fortress against prioritisation. The will that can collaborate but cannot transcend its own stage.

## 3. Drive-Health Landscape

| Drive | Healthy Expression | Pathological Expression |
|---|---|---|
| Eros | Reaches toward integral prioritisation while honouring plurality | Bypasses collaborative process; premature hierarchy |
| Agape | Returns to embody commitments already made; grounds scattered will | Clings to lower-stage individual achievement |
| Agency | Maintains sovereign voice within collaborative commitment | Refuses communion; lone-wolf achievement |
| Communion | Joins shared purpose; co-creates goals with others | Dissolves into group; loses individual will |

## 4. Healing Vectors

### 4.1 Heal/Evolve (Agape + Agency)

Integrates dark shadows by grounding scattered will (Agape) through sovereign choice (Agency). The player learns to commit to ONE thing fully while honouring the validity of others. Embodies the discipline of "yes to this, not-yet to that."

### 4.2 Evolve/Heal (Eros + Communion)

Dissolves golden shadows by reaching toward integral vision (Eros) through relational joining (Communion). The player learns that prioritisation is not betrayal — that hierarchy-in-service emerges naturally from deep collaborative engagement.

### 4.3 Integration Criteria

Integration is demonstrated when the player can: (a) hold 3+ valid goals without paralysis, (b) commit fully to one without dismissing others, (c) revise commitments through dialogue rather than collapse, (d) maintain individual will within group commitment.

## 6. Compound Shadows & Cross-Module Relationships

### 6.1 The Scattered Empath (Willpower DA × Emotional DA)

Commitment paralysis amplified by empathic overwhelm. Cannot act because feeling too much from too many directions. Every cause triggers genuine empathy, making triage feel like betrayal.

### 6.2 The Lone Achiever (Willpower DAll × Interpersonal DAll)

Refuses collaborative will AND refuses authentic relating. Effective but isolated. The compound makes both shadows self-reinforcing — isolation justifies individual action, which deepens isolation.

### 6.3 The Surrendered Bypasser (Willpower GA × Spiritual GA)

Premature surrender to "the whole" combined with premature interfaith universalism. Avoidance of genuine effort disguised as transcendence. Neither commits to community nor to practice.

### 6.4 The Paralysed Moralist (Willpower DA × Moral DA)

Commitment paralysis meets contextual ethics paralysis. Cannot act on any principle because every principle has valid counter-claims. Total inaction dressed as moral sensitivity.

## 7. Shadow Surfacing Sequence

### 7.1 Progressive Revelation

Shadows surface in order: DAll first (most accessible — refusal of collaboration is behaviorally obvious), then DA (paralysis emerges under multi-goal pressure), then GAll (revealed when integral challenges are introduced), then GA (deepest — only visible when transpersonal framing is available).

### 7.3 Cross-Validation

A shadow is confirmed only when 2+ modalities independently surface it. Compound shadows require convergent evidence from their constituent lines' modules. The system never labels — it adjusts catalyst frequency.
