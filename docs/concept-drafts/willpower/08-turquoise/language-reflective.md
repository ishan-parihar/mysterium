| Modality | Language-Reflective |

| Core Mechanic | Open-ended prompts about action, will, and doing — LLM-scored for grasping markers |

## 2. Catalyst Design

The player responds to prompts about action, intention, and will. The LLM analyses language for residual grasping patterns — not what the player *claims* about effortless will, but the structural features of their language that reveal whether will has actually become transparent.

### 2.1 Linguistic Markers of Residual Grasping
- **Outcome-grasping:** future-tense dominance, conditional constructions ("if I succeed then..."), result-referencing
- **Action-aversion:** passive voice, hedging, absence of action verbs for specific domains
- **Will-bypassing:** spiritual jargon without embodied specificity, performed non-attachment language
- **Doer-identity:** first-person-as-agent constructions, ownership language around actions

### 2.2 Markers of Transparent Will
- Present-tense action language without outcome-reference
- Actions described without agent-emphasis
- Specificity without grasping — concrete but unattached
- Absence of spiritual performance — plain language about plain action

## 3. Shadow Probes

| Shadow | Linguistic Probe |
|---|---|
| 2.1 Grasping-at-Outcome | "Describe something you're working toward" — does language fixate on result or inhabit process? |
| 2.2 Aversion-to-Action | "What actions do you find yourself not taking?" — can player name avoidance without rationalising? |
| 2.3 Will-Bypassing | "How do you experience effortless action?" — does language perform or embody? |
| 2.4 Identity-with-Doer | "Who acts when you act?" — does language dissolve or reinforce the doer? |

## 4. Progression Architecture

### 4.1 Diagnosis Phase
Open prompts establish baseline linguistic patterns. LLM identifies dominant grasping markers and their frequency across response categories.

### 4.2 Healing Phase
Prompts are tailored to the player's specific residual patterns. Outcome-graspers are invited to describe action-in-itself. Bypassers are invited to be concrete. The prompts themselves are catalysts — the act of articulating transparent will cultivates it.

### 4.3 Evolution Phase
Prompts become increasingly subtle. The system tracks whether grasping markers decrease over sessions without the player being told what is measured. Language naturally shifts as will becomes transparent.

## 5. Drive Detection

| Drive | Linguistic Signal |
|---|---|
| Eros | Language of striving, ascending, becoming — residual vertical grasping |
| Agape | Language of releasing, descending, giving — residual vertical attachment to letting-go |
| Agency | Language of self-initiation, ownership — residual horizontal self-claiming |
| Communion | Language of merging, dissolving boundaries — residual horizontal other-merging |

## 6. Scoring

LLM rubric scores each response on 4 grasping dimensions (0–1 each). Session score = mean across responses. Drive-balance = variance across four drive-linguistic-signals. Rubric is fixed; content is adaptive. Half-life 28 days; max decay 10%.

## 7. Infinite Checkpoint

Each prompt-response pair is a checkpoint. Player can leave after any response. No prompt depends on prior prompts within a session. State saved per response.
