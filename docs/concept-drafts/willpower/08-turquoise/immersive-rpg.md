## 2. Catalyst Design

The player inhabits an open narrative environment with no explicit goals. The system observes whether action arises spontaneously and transparently — without external prompting, without internal grasping, without performed non-doing. This is the ecological validity test: does effortless will appear in the wild?

### 2.1 Ecological Will Metrics
- **Spontaneous action rate:** does player act without being prompted? (aversion = low rate)
- **Action-quality in absence of goals:** is unprompted action effortful or transparent?
- **Narrative engagement without attachment:** does player engage story without needing resolution?
- **Environmental responsiveness:** does player respond to world-changes fluidly or rigidly?

### 2.2 Free-Play Transparency Indicators
- Action arises without deliberation or avoidance
- Player engages world without seeking specific outcomes
- Narrative threads are followed and released without grasping
- Player's behaviour is indistinguishable from "play" — no strain, no performance

## 3. Shadow Probes

| Shadow | Ecological Probe |
|---|---|
| 2.1 Grasping-at-Outcome | Player creates self-imposed goals in goal-free environment; seeks "completion" |
| 2.2 Aversion-to-Action | Player becomes passive in absence of external prompts; waits for direction |
| 2.3 Will-Bypassing | Player performs "just being" while actually disengaged; spiritual laziness in free-play |
| 2.4 Identity-with-Doer | Player narrates own actions to self; maintains running self-commentary |

## 4. Progression Architecture

### 4.1 Diagnosis Phase
Open environments with no goals, varied affordances. System maps the player's ecological will profile — what happens when nothing is required? Where does will go when freed from structure?

### 4.2 Healing Phase
Environments subtly tuned to the player's residual patterns. Outcome-graspers inhabit worlds where nothing can be "completed." Avoiders inhabit worlds rich with gentle invitations. Bypassers inhabit worlds that reward genuine engagement. Doer-identified inhabit worlds where actions have no traceable agent.

### 4.3 Evolution Phase
Fully open environments with maximum complexity and zero structure. System measures whether transparent action has become the player's default mode — spontaneous, responsive, unattached, alive.

| Agape | Free-play gravitates toward rest/simplicity — residual downward seeking |
| Agency | Free-play centres on solo exploration — residual self-assertion |
| Communion | Free-play centres on NPC interaction — residual other-seeking |

## 6. Scoring

Ecological will index (0–1) per session. Spontaneous-action-rate, action-quality, narrative-engagement, and environmental-responsiveness scored independently. Drive-balance = variance in free-play orientation. Half-life 28 days; max decay 10%. All four compound shadows observable here as ecological manifestations.

## 7. Infinite Checkpoint

The open world auto-saves continuously. Player can leave at any moment. No narrative thread requires completion. The world persists and evolves between sessions. This modality is the final integration test — effortless will in its natural habitat.
