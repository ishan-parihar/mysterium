|---|---|
| Module | Willpower × White |
| Modality | Strategic-Planning |
| Unique Lateral | The paradox of planning without grasping at plans — holding intention lightly while acting precisely |
| Core Mechanic | Planning tasks where the system measures attachment to plans vs. transparent planning |

## 2. Catalyst Design

The player creates plans and executes them in environments that shift unpredictably. The diagnostic signal is not planning quality but the player's relationship to their plans — can they plan precisely AND release the plan instantly when reality shifts? Effortless will plans without grasping at the plan.

### 2.1 Plan-Attachment Metrics
- **Revision resistance:** how long player holds an invalidated plan before releasing
- **Plan-identity fusion:** does player defend plans or update them transparently?
- **Execution rigidity:** does player follow plan mechanically or respond to emerging reality?
- **Planning-as-control:** does planning reduce anxiety (grasping) or arise naturally (transparent)?

### 2.2 Transparent Planning Indicators
- Plans arise spontaneously from situation-reading, not from anxiety
- Plans are held lightly — revised without emotional cost
- Execution adapts moment-to-moment while maintaining coherent direction
- Planning and non-planning are equally available — no preference

## 3. Shadow Probes

| Shadow | Planning Probe |
|---|---|
| 2.1 Grasping-at-Outcome | Plans fixate on end-states; player resists plan-revision when outcomes shift |
| 2.2 Aversion-to-Action | Player over-plans to avoid acting; planning becomes procrastination |
| 2.3 Will-Bypassing | Player refuses to plan ("I just flow") while actually being reactive, not responsive |
| 2.4 Identity-with-Doer | Player's plans centre on "I will do X" — plans are self-narratives, not situation-responses |

## 4. Progression Architecture

### 4.1 Diagnosis Phase
Planning tasks with varying disruption levels. System maps the player's plan-attachment profile — which disruption types trigger grasping, which planning contexts reveal residual patterns.

### 4.2 Healing Phase
Tasks tuned to the player's specific plan-attachment patterns. Outcome-graspers face tasks where goals shift mid-execution. Avoiders face tasks requiring immediate action from minimal planning. Bypassers face tasks where genuine planning is necessary. Doer-identified face tasks where plans execute themselves.

### 4.3 Evolution Phase
Complex multi-phase tasks with continuous disruption. System measures whether transparent planning has generalised — plans arise, shift, dissolve, and re-form without strain across all contexts.

## 5. Drive Detection

| Drive | Planning Signal |
|---|---|
| Eros | Plans always escalate in ambition — residual growth-grasping |
| Agape | Plans always simplify toward less — residual release-grasping |
| Agency | Plans centre on individual action — residual self-assertion |
| Communion | Plans centre on coordination — residual other-dependence |

## 6. Scoring

Plan-transparency index (0–1) per task. Revision-resistance, execution-rigidity, and planning-motivation scored independently. Drive-balance = variance in plan-orientation across equivalent tasks. Half-life 28 days; max decay 10%. Compound shadow "The Effortless Striver" (Willpower × Cognitive) is primary detection target.

## 7. Infinite Checkpoint

Each planning task is a checkpoint. Player can leave between tasks. No task depends on prior tasks. State saved per completed task.
