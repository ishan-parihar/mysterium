## 1. Modality Identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Module | Willpower × White |
| Modality | Social-Cooperative |
| Unique Lateral | Shared effortless will — communion in action, non-grasping collaboration |
| Core Mechanic | Cooperative tasks where the system measures whether shared action arises without control or submission |

## 2. Catalyst Design

The player engages in cooperative action with AI agents or other players. The diagnostic signal is the quality of shared will — does collaboration arise spontaneously, or does the player control, submit, perform, or grasp? Effortless shared will means action coordinates without anyone directing or anyone yielding.

### 2.1 Shared-Will Metrics
- **Control signature:** does player impose direction on shared action?
- **Submission signature:** does player collapse into other's direction?
- **Coordination latency:** how quickly does shared action synchronise without explicit negotiation?
- **Role fluidity:** can player lead and follow without preference or identity-attachment?

### 2.2 Non-Grasping Collaboration Indicators
- Shared action emerges without negotiation or hierarchy
- Player neither controls nor submits — participates transparently
- Role shifts happen without friction or self-consciousness
- Shared outcomes are neither claimed nor disclaimed

## 3. Shadow Probes

| Shadow | Social Probe |
|---|---|
| 2.1 Grasping-at-Outcome | Player steers collaboration toward preferred outcomes |
| 2.2 Aversion-to-Action | Player withdraws from shared action, lets others carry |
| 2.3 Will-Bypassing | Player performs "going with the flow" while subtly directing |
| 2.4 Identity-with-Doer | Player claims credit or responsibility for shared outcomes |

## 4. Progression Architecture

### 4.1 Diagnosis Phase
Cooperative tasks with varied partner styles (assertive, passive, chaotic, harmonious). System maps the player's relational-will topology — which partner types trigger grasping, which collaborative contexts reveal residual patterns.

### 4.2 Healing Phase
Cooperative tasks tuned to the player's specific relational-will patterns. Controllers are paired with equally strong partners. Submitters are placed in leadership-required contexts. Bypassers face partners who call out performed surrender. Doer-identified face tasks where contribution is untraceable.

### 4.3 Evolution Phase
Complex multi-agent tasks with shifting roles and emergent goals. System measures whether non-grasping collaboration has generalised — shared will arises spontaneously regardless of partner type or task structure.

## 5. Drive Detection

| Drive | Social Signal |
|---|---|
| Eros | Pushes collaboration toward growth/complexity — residual upward pull on group |
| Agape | Pushes collaboration toward simplicity/release — residual downward pull on group |
| Agency | Takes individual action within group context — residual self-assertion in shared space |
| Communion | Dissolves into group action — residual self-loss in shared space |

## 6. Scoring

Shared-will transparency index (0–1) per cooperative task. Control, submission, coordination, and role-fluidity scored independently. Drive-balance = variance in relational-will orientation. Half-life 28 days; max decay 10%. Compound shadow "The Surrendered Controller" (Willpower × Interpersonal) is primary detection target.

## 7. Infinite Checkpoint

Each cooperative task is a checkpoint. Player can leave between tasks. No task depends on prior tasks. State saved per completed interaction.
