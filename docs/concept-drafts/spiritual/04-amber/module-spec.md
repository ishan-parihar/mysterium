# Spiritual / Amber — Module Specification

> **Line:** Spiritual  **Stage:** Amber / Mythic-Membership  **Vibration:** The sacred is accessed through communal tradition — our stories, our rituals, our code hold the truth.

---

## 1. Capacity Definition

Mythic-membership faith: the capacity to embed one's meaning-making within a communal tradition, to experience the sacred through shared narrative, ritual, and moral code. The player can hold a tradition as genuinely sacred (not merely transactional as in Red, nor animistically diffuse as in Magenta) while maintaining healthy relationship to that tradition — neither rigidly literalist nor prematurely transcendent. Healthy Amber spirituality provides belonging, continuity, moral grounding, and access to the transpersonal through collective symbol.

## 2. Shadow Archetypes

### 2.1 Dark-Addiction (DA): The True Believer

Rigid mythic literalism. The tradition is absolute, unquestionable, and exclusively correct. Other traditions are heresy. Doubt is sin. Sacred violence is justified in defence of the faith. The player cannot hold paradox, metaphor, or plurality — everything is concrete, literal, and final.

### 2.2 Dark-Allergy (DAll): The Faithless

Refuses all communal tradition. Remains in Red transactional spirituality ("what's in it for me?") or Magenta animism ("everything is equally sacred"). Dismisses organised tradition as manipulation, control, or delusion. Cannot receive the gifts of belonging, lineage, or collective sacred narrative.

### 2.3 Golden-Addiction (GA): The Premature Mystic

Performs post-conventional spirituality — rational theology, interfaith dialogue, integral frameworks — without genuine mythic-membership underneath. Skips the embodied belonging of tradition. Speaks the language of Teal while having no Amber root. Ungrounded transcendence.

### 2.4 Golden-Allergy (GAll): The Tradition-Clinger

Genuine mythic-membership faith is present and healthy, but the player refuses the developmental call to examine the tradition rationally (Orange). Terrified of doubt. Avoids questions that might destabilise faith. Clings not from rigidity (DA) but from fear of loss.

## 4. Healing Vectors

### 4.1 Heal/Evolve (Agape + Agency → Dark Shadows)

DA: Agape returns the player to the *felt experience* of tradition (not the rigid concept); Agency allows them to hold that experience as *their own* without needing it to be universal. DAll: Agape reconnects them to the gifts tradition actually offered; Agency lets them choose a tradition without feeling controlled.

### 4.2 Evolve/Heal (Eros + Communion → Golden Shadows)

GA: Eros draws them *into* tradition (not past it); Communion lets them belong genuinely rather than observe from above. GAll: Eros invites the first question, the first doubt; Communion holds them in community *through* the questioning so faith isn't lost but deepened.

### 4.3 Integration Criteria

The player can: (a) name their tradition and feel genuine belonging, (b) hold the tradition's stories as sacred without requiring literal-historical truth, (c) encounter other traditions without threat or premature synthesis, (d) tolerate doubt as faith-deepening rather than faith-destroying.

## 6. Compound Shadows & Cross-Module Relationships

### 6.1 The Holy Warrior (Spiritual DA × Willpower DA)

Rigid faith fuses with rigid duty-will. The player becomes a fanatical crusader — unshakeable conviction married to relentless execution. Faith justifies force; will provides the engine.

### 6.2 The Meaning-Void (Spiritual DAll × Intrapersonal DAll)

No communal faith + no role-identity = existential emptiness. The player belongs to nothing, identifies as nothing sacred. Nihilism without the intellectual framework to hold it.

### 6.3 The Spiritual Bypasser (Spiritual GA × Intrapersonal GA)

Premature mysticism + premature individualism = ungrounded transcendence. Claims non-dual awareness and unique selfhood without the communal/traditional substrate either requires.

### 6.4 The Dogmatic Moralist (Spiritual DA × Moral DA)

Rigid faith + rigid morality = theocratic fundamentalism. The tradition's moral code becomes absolute law; deviation is both sin and crime. No mercy, no context, no growth.

## 7. Shadow Surfacing Sequence

### 7.1 Progressive Revelation

Sessions 1–3: Establish baseline tradition-relationship (which tradition resonates, how deeply). Sessions 4–8: Introduce mild challenges (other traditions, internal contradictions). Sessions 9–15: Escalate to full shadow-probing (doubt, exclusion, bypassing, clinging). Session 16+: Maintenance and integration verification.

### 7.3 Cross-Validation

Shadow identification requires convergence across ≥3 modalities. A single modality showing DA does not confirm DA — it flags for deeper probing. The immersive-RPG modality serves as ecological validator for patterns detected in structured modalities.
