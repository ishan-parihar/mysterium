# Spiritual / Green — Module Specification

> **Line:** Spiritual  **Stage:** Green / Pluralistic  **Vibration:** The sacred discovered in many traditions simultaneously — pluralistic faith as genuine interfaith appreciation rather than spiritual tourism.

---

## 1. Capacity Definition

The capacity to hold multiple spiritual traditions as genuinely valid paths to the sacred without collapsing them into sameness or ranking them hierarchically. This is NOT relativism — it is the developmental achievement of appreciating the PARTICULAR beauty of each tradition while recognizing that no single tradition holds exclusive truth. The player demonstrates: cross-tradition literacy, sustained multi-path practice, interfaith empathy, and sacred-in-diversity perception. Distinguished from Orange (which examines traditions rationally) by felt reverence for plurality itself, and from Teal (which integrates traditions into one meta-framework) by honoring irreducible difference.

## 2. Shadow Archetypes

### 2.1 Dark-Addiction: The Spiritual Dilettante

Samples every tradition without depth in any. "All paths are valid" becomes an excuse for no commitment. Spiritual tourism — attends a sweat lodge Monday, Zen sesshin Tuesday, Sufi dhikr Wednesday, commits to none. Cannot sustain practice beyond novelty phase. Mean-green-meme spirituality: uses pluralism to avoid the discipline any single path demands.

### 2.2 Dark-Allergy: The Rational Atheist

Refuses pluralistic faith entirely, remaining in Orange rational examination. "Spirituality is just cultural conditioning." Dismisses interfaith appreciation as "relativism" or "wishful thinking." The sacred remains an object of analysis rather than a lived multiplicity. Intellectually sophisticated but spiritually arid.

### 2.3 Golden-Addiction: The Premature Non-Dualist

Performs Teal integral spirituality without genuine Green interfaith appreciation. Claims "all is one" while unable to genuinely appreciate the PARTICULAR beauty of any single tradition. Leaps to meta-framework without having dwelt in the diversity. Uses non-dual language to bypass the hard work of cross-tradition immersion.

### 2.4 Golden-Allergy: The Interfaith Fortress

Pluralistic faith is present but refuses integral deepening. Terrified that "one truth" would invalidate diversity. "No tradition is more true than another" becomes a rigid dogma that refuses developmental ranking of spiritual capacity. Cannot acknowledge that traditions themselves have depth-structures. Sophisticated relativism masquerading as tolerance.

## 4. Healing Vectors

### 4.1 Heal/Evolve (Agape + Agency → Dark Shadows)

DA healing: Agape returns the dilettante to depth — "choose ONE tradition and practice it for real." Agency provides the sovereign commitment to sustain. DAll healing: Agape softens the rational fortress — "let yourself be moved." Agency provides the courage to be vulnerable to the sacred.

### 4.2 Evolve/Heal (Eros + Communion → Golden Shadows)

GA healing: Communion grounds the non-dualist in actual interfaith relationship — "sit WITH a Buddhist, don't just claim Buddhism is part of your meta-theory." Eros provides the genuine upward reach that isn't performative. GAll healing: Eros draws the fortress toward integral depth — "what if one truth doesn't destroy many paths?" Communion provides the relational safety to risk hierarchy.

### 4.3 Integration Criteria

Integration demonstrated when the player can: (a) sustain committed practice in at least one tradition while genuinely appreciating others, (b) articulate what is unique and irreplaceable about multiple traditions without collapsing them, (c) tolerate the tension between "many valid paths" and "some paths go deeper," (d) move fluidly between interfaith appreciation and integral synthesis without fixating on either.

## 6. Compound Shadows & Cross-Module Relationships

### 6.1 The Spiritual Tourist (Spiritual DA × Willpower DA)

Spiritual dilettantism combined with commitment paralysis. Samples every tradition, commits to nothing — not because all paths seem valid, but because the will to sustain ANY practice is absent. The compound makes both shadows invisible: "I'm exploring" masks both spiritual shallowness and volitional weakness.

### 6.2 The Rational Void (Spiritual DAll × Intrapersonal DAll)

Refuses pluralistic faith combined with rigid self-concept. Results in a meaning-less and unchanging inner life. Cannot find the sacred in diversity AND cannot find the sacred in self-reflection. Double-locked against transformation.

### 6.3 The Non-Dual Performer (Spiritual GA × Intrapersonal GA)

Premature non-dualism combined with premature witness-consciousness. Performed transcendence without ground — claims to witness all arising while simultaneously claiming all traditions are one, but has genuinely inhabited neither the witness nor the traditions.

### 6.4 The Interfaith Fortress (Spiritual GAll × Moral GAll)

Refuses spiritual hierarchy combined with refuses moral hierarchy. Sophisticated relativism — "no tradition is deeper than another" parallels "no moral framework is better than another." Both refuse the developmental insight that would liberate them.
