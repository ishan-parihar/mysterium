# Spiritual / Turquoise — Deterministic

## 1. Core Mechanic

Objective measurement of integral spiritual capacity through cross-tradition synthesis tasks. The player encounters spiritual concepts, practices, and symbols from multiple traditions and must identify structural correspondences, rank developmental depth, and distinguish direct-experience markers from conceptual knowledge.

**Task types:**
- **Correspondence matching:** Given a concept from tradition A, identify the structural equivalent in traditions B, C, D (not surface similarity — deep structural isomorphism)
- **Developmental ranking:** Order spiritual capacities/practices by developmental altitude (not tradition-specific hierarchy — cross-tradition developmental logic)
- **Direct-experience detection:** Distinguish descriptions rooted in direct experience from those rooted in conceptual mapping alone

## 2. Drive Probes

| Drive | Probe mechanism |
|---|---|
| **Eros** | Accuracy of upward developmental ranking — can the player see what is genuinely deeper vs. merely different? |
| **Agape** | Accuracy of honouring partial truths — does the player correctly identify what each tradition contributes uniquely? |
| **Agency** | Speed and confidence of synthesis — does the player author novel correspondences or only recall known ones? |
| **Communion** | Willingness to mark "I don't know / this exceeds my map" — does the player surrender certainty when appropriate? |

## 3. Shadow Surfacing

| Shadow | Behavioural signature |
|---|---|
| **DA (Spiritual Architect)** | Perfect correspondence-matching but zero direct-experience detection; maps everything, recognises no lived markers |
| **DAll (Interfaith Pluralist)** | Refuses developmental ranking; marks all traditions as "equal depth"; cannot identify structural hierarchy |
| **GA (Premature Non-Dualist)** | Claims all items are "ultimately one" without demonstrating actual synthesis; collapses distinctions prematurely |
| **GAll (Integral Fortress)** | Excellent synthesis and ranking but never selects "exceeds my map"; refuses communion-surrender items |

## 4. Progression Arc

1. **Diagnosis (trials 1–8):** Baseline across all four task types; identify dominant shadow
2. **Catalyst (trials 9–20):** Adaptive difficulty targets weakest drive; shadow-specific provocations increase
3. **Integration (trials 21–30):** All four drives must score within healthy range simultaneously; direct-experience items weighted heavily

**Difficulty staircase:** Traditions become more obscure; correspondences become more structural (less surface); developmental rankings require finer discrimination; direct-experience markers become subtler.

## 5. Scoring

- **Primary metric:** Synthesis accuracy (correct correspondences × developmental ranking precision)
- **Secondary metric:** Direct-experience sensitivity (true-positive rate on lived-vs-conceptual items)
- **Drive balance:** Variance across four drive scores penalised (healthy = balanced)
- **Shadow-drag:** Active shadow reduces module score by 0.20 × shadow intensity
- **Cross-line bonus:** +0.15 when Cognitive line (structural reasoning) and Intrapersonal line (self-awareness) are both healthy
