# Spiritual / Turquoise — Scenario-Choice

## 1. Core Mechanic

The player faces integral spiritual dilemmas — situations where multiple traditions offer conflicting guidance, where synthesis is required but no single tradition holds the complete answer, and where honouring all paths must coexist with recognising developmental depth. Choices reveal whether the player can hold the tension of integral spirituality without collapsing into any shadow quadrant.

**Scenario types:**
- **Tradition-conflict:** Two traditions offer contradictory guidance for the same situation; player must synthesise without dismissing either
- **Depth-vs-breadth:** A situation where going deeper in one tradition conflicts with maintaining integral breadth; player must navigate the tension
- **Teacher-student:** A spiritual teacher from a lower altitude offers genuine wisdom; player must honour without condescension or false equality
- **Mystery-encounter:** A situation that genuinely exceeds all known frameworks; player must respond without forcing it into a map

## 2. Drive Probes

| Drive | Choice signature |
|---|---|
| **Eros** | Choices that seek the deeper pattern behind the conflict rather than siding with one tradition |
| **Agape** | Choices that honour what each tradition uniquely contributes to the situation |
| **Agency** | Choices that author a novel synthesis rather than defaulting to a known framework's answer |
| **Communion** | Choices that surrender to not-knowing when the situation genuinely exceeds all maps |

## 3. Shadow Surfacing

| Shadow | Choice pattern |
|---|---|
| **DA (Spiritual Architect)** | Always chooses "map the conflict" over "act within it"; delays action for more framework-building |
| **DAll (Interfaith Pluralist)** | Chooses "both are equally valid" in every conflict; refuses to identify deeper structural truth |
| **GA (Premature Non-Dualist)** | Chooses "transcend the dilemma" prematurely; claims the conflict is illusory without demonstrating synthesis |
| **GAll (Integral Fortress)** | Makes excellent synthesis choices but refuses mystery-encounter surrender; always has an answer |

## 4. Progression Arc

1. **Diagnosis (scenarios 1–4):** One scenario per type; choices reveal dominant shadow orientation
2. **Catalyst (scenarios 5–12):** Scenarios adaptively target the active shadow — DA faces "act now without mapping"; DAll faces "one tradition IS deeper here"; GA faces "show the synthesis work"; GAll faces "you cannot synthesise this, only surrender"
3. **Integration (scenarios 13–16):** Complex scenarios requiring all four drives simultaneously — synthesise, honour, author, AND surrender within a single dilemma

**Escalation:** Early scenarios have clear "best" choices; later scenarios have genuinely ambiguous tensions where all four drives must be balanced; final scenarios have no correct answer — only the quality of holding the tension is measured.

## 5. Scoring

- **Primary metric:** Drive-balance across choices (healthy = no single drive dominates)
- **Secondary metric:** Synthesis quality (novel integration vs. known-framework application)
- **Tension-holding:** Bonus for choices that maintain ambiguity rather than premature resolution
- **Shadow-drag:** 0.20 × shadow intensity when choice patterns match active shadow
- **Cross-line bonus:** +0.15 when Moral line (ethical reasoning) and Intrapersonal line (self-awareness of bias) are both healthy
