# Spiritual / Turquoise — Immersive-RPG

## 1. Core Mechanic

Ecological integral spirituality in free-play. The player inhabits a world where multiple spiritual traditions coexist as living cultures — temples, monasteries, sacred groves, mystery schools — and must navigate this landscape without explicit instruction. The game measures whether cross-tradition synthesis appears spontaneously in the player's behaviour, or whether shadow patterns emerge when no structure is imposed.

**World elements:**
- **Multi-tradition landscape:** Sacred sites from diverse traditions exist as explorable locations; each offers genuine practices, teachings, and community
- **Living practitioners:** NPCs embody different traditions at different altitudes; some are Green pluralists, some are integral, some are non-dual; the player must discern
- **Sacred events:** Spontaneous spiritual phenomena occur in the world — synchronicities, visions, encounters with mystery — that no single tradition fully explains
- **The Uncharted:** Regions of the world that no tradition has mapped; genuine mystery zones where all frameworks fail

## 2. Drive Probes

| Drive | Ecological marker |
|---|---|
| **Eros** | Player spontaneously seeks deeper pattern across traditions encountered; visits multiple sites and finds connections |
| **Agape** | Player spends genuine time in each tradition's space; doesn't rush through or dismiss any; honours the ecology |
| **Agency** | Player creates novel spiritual practice from encountered elements; builds personal altar/ritual from multiple sources |
| **Communion** | Player enters the Uncharted; sits with mystery; doesn't force encountered phenomena into known frameworks |

## 3. Shadow Surfacing

| Shadow | Ecological signature |
|---|---|
| **DA (Spiritual Architect)** | Compulsively catalogues all traditions; builds a "spiritual map" in-game journal; never sits in any single practice |
| **DAll (Interfaith Pluralist)** | Visits all traditions equally but never synthesises; treats the world as a spiritual buffet; no integration visible |
| **GA (Premature Non-Dualist)** | Rushes to the Uncharted without visiting traditions; claims to "transcend" the landscape; skips the ecology |
| **GAll (Integral Fortress)** | Masterful synthesis visible but avoids the Uncharted; stays where frameworks work; never enters genuine mystery |

## 4. Progression Arc

1. **Diagnosis (free-play hours 1–3):** Observe spontaneous behaviour; which traditions does the player visit? How long do they stay? Do they synthesise? Do they enter the Uncharted?
2. **Catalyst (hours 4–10):** World adapts — DA's maps become unreliable; DAll encounters traditions in genuine conflict; GA's "transcendence" is challenged by embodied practitioners; GAll's mapped regions develop mystery-zones
3. **Integration (hours 11–14):** The world presents situations requiring all four ecological markers — the player must synthesise, honour, author, and surrender spontaneously without prompting

**Ecological escalation:** Early world is welcoming; later world presents tradition-conflicts, altitude-challenges, and genuine mystery that resists all mapping; final phase: the world itself becomes the teacher.

## 5. Scoring

- **Primary metric:** Spontaneous synthesis (cross-tradition connections made without prompting, measured by behaviour patterns)
- **Secondary metric:** Ecological balance (time distribution across traditions × depth of engagement × Uncharted exploration)
- **Emergence index:** Novel spiritual behaviours that arise from the player's own integration, not from any tradition's script
- **Shadow-drag:** 0.20 × shadow intensity when ecological signature matches active shadow
- **Cross-line bonus:** +0.15 when Intrapersonal line (self-directed exploration) and Cognitive line (pattern recognition) are both healthy
