# Spiritual / Turquoise — Embodied-Somatic

## 1. Core Mechanic

The body as site of integral spirituality. The player engages in embodied cross-tradition practices — rhythm, breath, posture, and movement patterns drawn from multiple traditions — and must physically hold the sacred through the body rather than through conceptual understanding alone. The game measures whether integral spiritual capacity lives in the body or only in the mind.

**Practice types:**
- **Cross-tradition breath:** Breath patterns that synthesise elements from pranayama, hesychasm, Sufi zikr, and Zen susokukan — the player must find the structural unity in the body, not the concept
- **Sacred posture integration:** Holding postures that combine elements from multiple traditions; the body must find the common ground
- **Rhythm synthesis:** Drumming/tapping patterns that weave sacred rhythms from different traditions into one coherent embodied flow
- **Stillness-in-motion:** Moving meditation that integrates walking meditation, tai chi, and contemplative prayer into one embodied practice

## 2. Drive Probes

| Drive | Somatic marker |
|---|---|
| **Eros** | Body reaches toward deeper integration — increasing coherence across tradition-elements over time |
| **Agape** | Body honours each tradition's contribution — no element is rushed through or dismissed in the physical practice |
| **Agency** | Body authors novel synthesis — movement/breath patterns emerge that are not from any single tradition |
| **Communion** | Body surrenders to the practice — releases muscular control, allows the sacred to move through rather than being directed |

## 3. Shadow Surfacing

| Shadow | Somatic signature |
|---|---|
| **DA (Spiritual Architect)** | Body is mechanical — executes patterns precisely but without felt-sense; treats embodiment as another map to master |
| **DAll (Interfaith Pluralist)** | Body switches between traditions without integrating; juxtaposes rather than synthesises; no coherent embodied whole |
| **GA (Premature Non-Dualist)** | Body goes limp/formless prematurely; skips the discipline of cross-tradition integration; "the body is already one" |
| **GAll (Integral Fortress)** | Body executes perfect synthesis but remains tense/controlled; never releases into surrender; muscular rigidity persists |

## 4. Progression Arc

1. **Diagnosis (sessions 1–3):** Baseline embodied capacity across all four practice types; identify somatic shadow signature
2. **Catalyst (sessions 4–10):** Adaptive practices target somatic shadow — DA gets felt-sense emphasis; DAll gets integration-demanding sequences; GA gets discipline-requiring practices; GAll gets surrender-inviting practices
3. **Integration (sessions 11–14):** Practices require all four somatic markers simultaneously — coherent, honouring, novel, and surrendered in one embodied flow

**Difficulty staircase:** Traditions become more physically divergent; synthesis requires greater somatic creativity; surrender demands increase; the gap between "knowing the pattern" and "being the pattern" widens.

## 5. Scoring

- **Primary metric:** Embodied coherence (physiological markers of integrated practice — breath regularity, movement fluidity, tension release)
- **Secondary metric:** Novelty of synthesis (movement patterns that emerge beyond any single tradition's vocabulary)
- **Felt-sense ratio:** Proportion of practice time in felt-sense vs. mechanical execution
- **Shadow-drag:** 0.20 × shadow intensity when somatic signature matches active shadow
- **Cross-line bonus:** +0.15 when Somatic line (body intelligence) and Intrapersonal line (internal awareness) are both healthy
