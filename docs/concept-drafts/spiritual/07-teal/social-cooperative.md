# Spiritual / Turquoise — Social-Cooperative

## 1. Core Mechanic

Shared integral spirituality — the player engages in group cross-tradition practice, spiritual mentorship across developmental altitudes, and collective synthesis. The game measures whether integral spiritual capacity can be held in relationship, not just in solitary practice. NPCs and co-op partners represent different traditions and altitudes; the player must facilitate genuine group synthesis without domination or false equality.

**Cooperative tasks:**
- **Cross-tradition circle:** Facilitate a group practice drawing from multiple traditions; each participant holds their tradition's gift; the group must find coherence
- **Altitude mentorship:** Mentor a lower-altitude practitioner without condescension; receive mentorship from a higher-altitude practitioner without submission
- **Collective synthesis:** The group faces a spiritual question no single tradition can answer; collaborative integral reasoning required
- **Shared surrender:** The group encounters mystery together; collective not-knowing must be held without anyone forcing a premature answer

## 2. Drive Probes

| Drive | Social marker |
|---|---|
| **Eros** | Guides the group toward deeper pattern; facilitates upward movement without forcing |
| **Agape** | Ensures every tradition/participant is genuinely heard; protects minority voices in the circle |
| **Agency** | Offers novel synthesis that moves the group beyond any individual's framework |
| **Communion** | Models surrender; admits not-knowing in front of others; allows the group to exceed the self |

## 3. Shadow Surfacing

| Shadow | Social signature |
|---|---|
| **DA (Spiritual Architect)** | Dominates the circle with framework-talk; maps the group's experience rather than participating in it |
| **DAll (Interfaith Pluralist)** | Refuses to facilitate depth; keeps the group at "sharing" level; blocks developmental movement as "hierarchical" |
| **GA (Premature Non-Dualist)** | Declares group unity prematurely; "we're all already one" before the group has done the synthesis work |
| **GAll (Integral Fortress)** | Excellent facilitation but cannot model surrender; always the teacher, never the student; controls the group's process |

## 4. Progression Arc

1. **Diagnosis (encounters 1–3):** Baseline social-spiritual capacity; identify relational shadow pattern
2. **Catalyst (encounters 4–9):** Adaptive group dynamics target shadow — DA gets "listen without mapping"; DAll gets "the group needs you to name what's deeper"; GA gets "show the group your synthesis process"; GAll gets "let someone else lead into mystery"
3. **Integration (encounters 10–12):** Complex group situations requiring all four social-spiritual capacities simultaneously — facilitate, honour, synthesise, and surrender in relationship

**Escalation:** Early groups are cooperative; later groups include resistant members, tradition-conflicts, and altitude-gaps; final encounters require holding genuine group mystery without resolution.

## 5. Scoring

- **Primary metric:** Group coherence achieved (did the circle find genuine synthesis vs. polite agreement?)
- **Secondary metric:** Relational balance (did the player facilitate without dominating? honour without flattening? synthesise without imposing?)
- **Mentorship quality:** Appropriate altitude-sensitivity in both giving and receiving guidance
- **Shadow-drag:** 0.20 × shadow intensity when social signature matches active shadow
- **Cross-line bonus:** +0.15 when Interpersonal line (relational skill) and Emotional line (empathic attunement) are both healthy
