# Spiritual / Turquoise — Language-Reflective

## 1. Core Mechanic

The player articulates integral spiritual understanding through open-ended verbal responses. LLM-scored rubrics detect whether language reveals genuine cross-tradition synthesis, direct experience of the sacred, or performative spiritual vocabulary masking conceptual-only understanding.

**Prompt types:**
- **Synthesis prompts:** "Describe how [concept from tradition A] and [concept from tradition B] express the same deeper pattern"
- **Experience prompts:** "Describe a moment when the sacred was present — in your own words, not any tradition's language"
- **Hierarchy prompts:** "Why might [practice X] develop capacities that [practice Y] does not, without diminishing Y's value?"
- **Surrender prompts:** "What do you not yet understand about the sacred? What remains beyond your current map?"

## 2. Drive Probes

| Drive | Linguistic marker |
|---|---|
| **Eros** | Language reaches toward deeper pattern; uses structural vocabulary; shows yearning for the whole |
| **Agape** | Language honours each tradition's unique gift; avoids dismissal; shows genuine appreciation of partial truths |
| **Agency** | Language is original — player's own synthesis, not parroted frameworks; novel metaphors and connections |
| **Communion** | Language includes genuine not-knowing; admits limits; shows willingness to be exceeded by the mystery |

## 3. Shadow Surfacing

| Shadow | Linguistic signature |
|---|---|
| **DA (Spiritual Architect)** | Dense meta-language, framework jargon, maps-of-maps; no first-person experiential language; "the architecture of the sacred is..." |
| **DAll (Interfaith Pluralist)** | Diplomatic equality language; refuses comparative depth; "all paths are equally valid" without structural reasoning |
| **GA (Premature Non-Dualist)** | Premature unity language; "it's all one" without demonstrated synthesis; borrowed non-dual vocabulary; skips the work |
| **GAll (Integral Fortress)** | Sophisticated synthesis language but deflects surrender prompts; "I understand the mystery" rather than "the mystery exceeds me" |

## 4. Progression Arc

1. **Diagnosis (prompts 1–4):** One prompt per type; baseline linguistic profile; identify dominant shadow from language patterns
2. **Catalyst (prompts 5–12):** Adaptive prompts target shadow — DA gets experience-demanding prompts; DAll gets hierarchy prompts; GA gets "show your work" prompts; GAll gets surrender prompts
3. **Integration (prompts 13–16):** Prompts require all four linguistic markers simultaneously; player must synthesise, honour, author, and surrender in a single response

**Rubric escalation:** Early prompts accept partial markers; later prompts require all four drives expressed in language; final prompts demand first-person experiential language that is simultaneously structurally precise.

## 5. Scoring

- **Primary metric:** Rubric score across four drive-markers (0–1 per drive per response, averaged)
- **Secondary metric:** Experiential-to-conceptual ratio (proportion of first-person lived language vs. framework language)
- **Authenticity penalty:** Detected parroting (known framework phrases without novel integration) reduces score
- **Shadow-drag:** 0.20 × shadow intensity when linguistic signature matches active shadow
- **Cross-line bonus:** +0.15 when Cognitive line (articulation precision) and Emotional line (felt-sense language) are both healthy
