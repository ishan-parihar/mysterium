# Spiritual / Turquoise — Module Specification

## 1. Module Identity

- **Line:** Spiritual
- **Stage:** Turquoise (7 — Integral / Vision-Logic)
- **Ray:** Indigo
- **Core capacity:** Integral spirituality — all paths as facets of one architecture; cross-tradition synthesis; the sacred IS the whole pattern
- **Below (Green):** Pluralistic faith — multiple paths held as equally valid, interfaith appreciation without hierarchy
- **Above (White):** Non-dual — surrender of grasping, pathless path, full mana, no doctrine remains

## 2. Drive Model at This Module

| Drive | Healthy expression | Pathological expression |
|---|---|---|
| **Eros** | Yearning toward the one pattern behind all traditions | Compulsive mapping without arrival (DA fuel) |
| **Agape** | Honouring each tradition's partial truth with compassion | Refusing to rank or synthesise (DAll fuel) |
| **Agency** | Authoring one's own integral spiritual practice | Premature declaration of non-dual attainment (GA fuel) |
| **Communion** | Surrendering personal framework to the mystery | Refusing surrender, clinging to the integral map (GAll fuel) |

## 3. Shadow Quadrants

| Quadrant | Name | Pattern |
|---|---|---|
| **Dark-Addiction (DA)** | The Spiritual Architect | Addicted to integral frameworks; maps traditions endlessly; uses meta-awareness to avoid direct sacred experience |
| **Dark-Allergy (DAll)** | The Interfaith Pluralist | Refuses integral hierarchy; stays in Green appreciation; "no tradition is more true than another" |
| **Golden-Addiction (GA)** | The Premature Non-Dualist | Performs White non-dual spirituality without genuine integral mastery; claims "all is one" without synthesis capacity |
| **Golden-Allergy (GAll)** | The Integral Spiritual Fortress | Integral spirituality present but refuses non-dual opening; terrified of losing the map |

## 4. Compound Shadows

1. **The Sacred Mapper** (Spiritual DA × Cognitive DA): maps all traditions, experiences none
2. **The Interfaith Holdout** (Spiritual DAll × Moral DAll): Green-level appreciation only, refuses integral ethics
3. **The Non-Dual Performer** (Spiritual GA × Intrapersonal GA): performed enlightenment without genuine no-self
4. **The Synthesis Fortress** (Spiritual GAll × Willpower GAll): synthesises everything, surrenders nothing

## 5. Developmental Vectors

| Vector | Direction | Shadow dissolved | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Heal/Evolve** | Bottom-up | Dark shadows (DA, DAll) | Agape + Agency ground the architect in direct experience; integrate Green's gifts without regression |
| **Evolve/Heal** | Top-down | Golden shadows (GA, GAll) | Eros + Communion invite surrender; dissolve premature non-dual claims and fortress rigidity |
