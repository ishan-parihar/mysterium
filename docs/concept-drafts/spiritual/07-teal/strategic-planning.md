# Spiritual / Turquoise — Strategic-Planning

## 1. Core Mechanic

The player plans an integral spiritual life — structuring cross-tradition practice across time, balancing depth and breadth, sequencing developmental work, and allocating finite attention across multiple traditions without collapsing into any shadow quadrant. The game presents resource-allocation and scheduling challenges that reveal how the player organises their relationship to the sacred.

**Planning tasks:**
- **Practice portfolio:** Design a weekly practice schedule drawing from multiple traditions; balance depth in each with integral breadth
- **Developmental sequencing:** Given a set of spiritual capacities at different altitudes, plan the order of development that honours both hierarchy and inclusion
- **Teacher selection:** Allocate limited mentorship time across teachers from different traditions; balance learning with integration
- **Retreat architecture:** Plan a multi-day retreat that weaves practices from multiple traditions into a coherent developmental arc

## 2. Drive Probes

| Drive | Planning signature |
|---|---|
| **Eros** | Plans that build toward deeper integration over time; long-term developmental arcs visible in the schedule |
| **Agape** | Plans that give genuine time to each tradition; no tradition is tokenised or rushed |
| **Agency** | Plans that include novel synthesis periods — time for the player's own integral practice to emerge |
| **Communion** | Plans that include unstructured time — space for the mystery to arrive; not every moment is mapped |

## 3. Shadow Surfacing

| Shadow | Planning pattern |
|---|---|
| **DA (Spiritual Architect)** | Over-scheduled; every moment mapped; no unstructured time; the plan IS the practice; planning replaces doing |
| **DAll (Interfaith Pluralist)** | Equal time to all traditions regardless of developmental relevance; refuses to prioritise; flat allocation |
| **GA (Premature Non-Dualist)** | Plans skip foundational work; jumps to "advanced" non-dual practices; no developmental sequencing |
| **GAll (Integral Fortress)** | Excellent developmental sequencing but no unstructured/surrender time; every moment has a purpose; no space for mystery |

## 4. Progression Arc

1. **Diagnosis (tasks 1–3):** Baseline planning across portfolio, sequencing, and retreat design; identify planning shadow
2. **Catalyst (tasks 4–9):** Adaptive constraints target shadow — DA gets "plan with 40% unstructured time"; DAll gets "you can only choose 3 traditions — which and why?"; GA gets "build the foundation first"; GAll gets "include a day with no plan"
3. **Integration (tasks 10–12):** Complex planning requiring all four drives — structured yet spacious, hierarchical yet honouring, authored yet surrendered

**Escalation:** Early tasks have generous resources; later tasks impose scarcity (limited time, limited access, competing demands); final tasks require planning under genuine uncertainty where the outcome cannot be predicted.

## 5. Scoring

- **Primary metric:** Plan coherence (developmental logic × tradition-honouring × novelty × spaciousness)
- **Secondary metric:** Adaptability (how well the player revises plans when constraints change mid-task)
- **Balance index:** Variance across four drive-signatures in planning penalised
- **Shadow-drag:** 0.20 × shadow intensity when planning pattern matches active shadow
- **Cross-line bonus:** +0.15 when Cognitive line (strategic reasoning) and Willpower line (commitment to plan) are both healthy
