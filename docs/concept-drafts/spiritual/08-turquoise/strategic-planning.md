## 1. Modality Identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Module | Spiritual / White |
| Modality | Strategic-Planning |
| Probe axis | The paradox of structuring spiritual life when being IS practice |
| Core mechanic | Planning tasks that reveal whether player can hold structure without grasping or anti-structure without aversion |

## 2. Diagnostic Target

The non-dual paradox of planning: life requires structure, but non-dual presence means no moment is more "practice" than another. This modality measures whether the player can plan without creating sacred/ordinary hierarchies within their plans.

## 3. Task Architecture

### 3.1 Life-Structure Design

Player designs a daily/weekly schedule for a character. System analyzes whether "spiritual" activities are given special status (dedicated time, protection from interruption) versus integrated transparently.

- Metric: sacred-ordinary segregation index (target: zero)
- Metric: flexibility score (willingness to restructure without distress)

### 3.2 Priority Paradox Tasks

Player must prioritize competing demands. Some are conventionally "spiritual" (meditation, retreat, contemplation) and some "mundane" (work, errands, socializing). System measures systematic bias.

- Healthy: context-responsive prioritization without categorical preference
- Shadow: spiritual activities always protected (2.1) or always deprioritized to prove non-attachment (2.2)

### 3.3 Interruption Planning

Player creates plans, then faces interruptions. System measures: does the player build "sacred buffers" around spiritual activities? Does disruption of spiritual plans cause more distress than disruption of mundane plans?

### 3.4 Goal-Setting Without Seeking

Player sets intentions for a character's development. System analyzes whether spiritual goals are framed as attainment (seeker-identity) or as natural unfolding.

- Metric: attainment-language in spiritual goals vs. mundane goals
- Metric: timeline-urgency differential (spiritual goals given special urgency = grasping)

## 4. Shadow Mapping

| Shadow | Planning signature |
|---|---|
| 2.1 Grasping-at-Sacred | Over-protects spiritual time, creates sacred-mundane hierarchy in schedule |
| 2.2 Aversion-to-Sacred | Refuses to plan spiritual engagement, avoids structure around depth |
| 2.3 Spiritual Bypassing | Plans claim "all is practice" but structure reveals hidden preferences |
| 2.4 Seeker-Identity | Plans organized around spiritual attainment narrative |

## 5. Drive Probes

| Drive | Planning probe | Healthy signal |
|---|---|---|
| Eros | How growth is structured | No special urgency for spiritual growth |
| Agape | How maintenance is structured | No condescension toward "basic" life-tasks |
| Agency | How action is initiated | Plans without doer-identity |
| Communion | How receptivity is structured | Doesn't over-schedule openness |

## 6. The Effortless-Action Metric

Across planning tasks, system builds a profile of how much EFFORT the player allocates to spiritual versus mundane domains. Non-dual presence = equal effort distribution. Any asymmetry reveals residual grasping.

## 7. Progression Logic

- **Diagnosis:** Baseline planning-preference profile across sacred/mundane domains
- **Catalyst:** Planning scenarios that specifically challenge the player's asymmetry
- **Integration:** Plans become genuinely flat — structure serves life without sacred-ordinary hierarchy

## 8. Design Constraint

Tasks are framed as life-management puzzles, never as "spiritual planning exercises." The player organizes a character's life; the system reads the implicit hierarchy. No feedback reveals what is being measured.
