## 1. Modality Identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Module | Spiritual / White |
| Modality | Immersive-RPG |
| Probe axis | Whether transparent presence appears spontaneously in free-play |
| Core mechanic | Open-world ecological observation — does non-dual sacred arise without prompting? |

## 2. Diagnostic Target

The ultimate test of non-dual integration: does transparent presence appear spontaneously when the player is simply playing, without any spiritual framing, prompting, or context? The immersive-RPG modality creates an open ecology and watches.

## 3. Ecological Design

### 3.1 The Transparent World

The game world at White contains no explicitly sacred locations, no temples, no meditation spots. Every location is equally ordinary. The system measures whether the player's engagement quality varies by location or remains constant.

### 3.2 Ambient Presence Markers

The world contains subtle environmental cues that could be read as sacred or ordinary depending on the player's state. A sunset, a quiet room, a crowded market — all neutral. The system reads differential engagement.

### 3.3 NPC Ecology

NPCs at various developmental altitudes populate the world. Some are "spiritual," some are not. System measures whether the player's presence-quality shifts based on who they're interacting with.

### 3.4 Temporal Ecology

The game world has rhythms — busy periods, quiet periods, transitions. System measures whether the player's equanimity holds across temporal contexts or clusters around "quiet = sacred."

## 4. Shadow Detection in Free-Play

| Shadow | Free-play signature |
|---|---|
| 2.1 Grasping-at-Sacred | Gravitates toward quiet/beautiful locations, lingers in "sacred" spaces |
| 2.2 Aversion-to-Sacred | Avoids stillness, fills silence with activity, stays in busy areas |
| 2.3 Spiritual Bypassing | Claims equal engagement but behavioral data shows preference clustering |
| 2.4 Seeker-Identity | Seeks out "spiritual" NPCs, positions self in teacher/guide roles |

## 5. Drive Probes via Ecology

| Drive | Ecological probe | Healthy signal |
|---|---|---|
| Eros | Does player seek "higher" experiences in the world? | Explores without hierarchy |
| Agape | Does player engage with "mundane" world-tasks? | Equal care for all activities |
| Agency | Does player act with spiritual self-consciousness? | Transparent action |
| Communion | Does player seek sacred-communion with world? | Present without grasping |

## 6. The Spontaneity Metric

The core measurement: does presence arise WITHOUT the player trying? The system tracks:
- Engagement consistency across all world-contexts (target: flat)
- Absence of seeking-behavior toward sacred stimuli
- Absence of avoidance-behavior toward mundane stimuli
- Quality of attention that doesn't vary with content

## 7. Progression Logic

- **Diagnosis:** Extended free-play session builds ecological-presence profile
- **Catalyst:** World subtly introduces the player's specific grasping-trigger into ordinary contexts
- **Integration:** Player's presence becomes genuinely ecological — the same quality everywhere, always

## 8. Design Constraint

This modality must feel like pure open-world gameplay. No spiritual framing whatsoever. The player is simply living in a world. The system's entire diagnostic power comes from the fact that the player doesn't know presence is being measured. The moment it feels like a spiritual game, the measurement is contaminated.

## 9. Relationship to Harvest

When the ecological-presence profile is genuinely flat — when transparent presence arises spontaneously in all contexts without seeking or avoiding — the player has demonstrated the harvest-readiness that the Law-of-One describes. This is not an endpoint but a deepening spiral.
