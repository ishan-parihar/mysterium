## 1. Modality Identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Module | Spiritual / White |
| Modality | Social-Cooperative |
| Probe axis | Communion in presence — non-grasping spiritual attunement with others |
| Core mechanic | Cooperative tasks measuring whether shared sacred space is grasped at or transparently inhabited |

## 2. Diagnostic Target

Non-dual presence in relationship means neither seeking spiritual communion as special nor avoiding depth with others. This modality measures whether the player can be present WITH others without making that presence into a spiritual event.

## 3. Task Architecture

### 3.1 Shared Silence Tasks

Players (or player + NPCs) engage in cooperative tasks that include silence. System measures whether the player treats shared silence as "sacred" (grasping) or avoids it (aversion).

- Metric: behavioral differential in silent vs. verbal cooperative phases
- Metric: effort to extend or escape silence

### 3.2 Spiritual Attunement Without Performance

Cooperative tasks where one partner is in distress. System measures whether the player's supportive presence carries spiritual self-consciousness ("I am holding space") or is simply present.

- Metric: self-referencing during support (identity-grasping)
- Metric: quality differential when supporting "spiritual" vs. "mundane" distress

### 3.3 Group Presence Tasks

Multi-agent scenarios where the group enters flow/presence together. System measures whether the player grasps at group-sacred-states or can let them arise and dissolve without attachment.

- Metric: effort to maintain group-flow when it naturally fades
- Metric: disappointment signal when group-presence dissolves

### 3.4 Teaching Without Teacher-Identity

Cooperative scenarios where the player has knowledge another needs. System measures whether sharing carries seeker/teacher identity or is transparent transmission.

- Metric: hierarchical positioning in spiritual sharing
- Metric: attachment to being received/understood

## 4. Shadow Mapping

| Shadow | Social signature |
|---|---|
| 2.1 Grasping-at-Sacred | Seeks spiritual communion, makes shared presence "special" |
| 2.2 Aversion-to-Sacred | Avoids depth in relationship, keeps interactions surface-level |
| 2.3 Spiritual Bypassing | Performs deep presence while subtly managing the interaction |
| 2.4 Seeker-Identity | Positions self as the spiritually attuned one in the dyad |

## 5. Drive Probes

| Drive | Social probe | Healthy signal |
|---|---|---|
| Eros | Deepening connection | Neither chasing depth nor avoiding it |
| Agape | Allowing connection to lighten | No disappointment when depth fades |
| Agency | Initiating in relationship | Acts without spiritual self-consciousness |
| Communion | Receiving from others | Receives without making it sacred |

## 6. Compound Shadow Detection

| Compound | Social manifestation |
|---|---|
| Sacred Grasper (×Emotional) | Seeks spiritual-emotional fusion with others |
| Mystery Avoider (×Cognitive) | Intellectualizes shared presence to avoid its depth |
| Enlightenment Performer (×Intrapersonal) | Performs "holding space" as identity |
| Seeker-Identified (×Willpower) | Effortfully maintains spiritual presence in relationship |

## 7. Progression Logic

- **Diagnosis:** Baseline relational-presence profile across cooperative scenarios
- **Catalyst:** Cooperative tasks that specifically invite the player's relational-grasping pattern
- **Integration:** Shared presence becomes ordinary — neither sought nor avoided, simply what happens when beings meet

## 8. Design Constraint

Cooperative tasks are framed as ordinary teamwork. The "spiritual" dimension is never named. Two players solving a puzzle in silence is just a puzzle — the system reads whether either player treats the silence as sacred. No feedback reveals the spiritual measurement axis.
