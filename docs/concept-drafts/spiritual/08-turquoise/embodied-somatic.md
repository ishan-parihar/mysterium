## 1. Modality Identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Module | Spiritual / White |
| Modality | Embodied-Somatic |
| Probe axis | Body as site of non-dual sacred — embodied presence, physical non-grasping |
| Core mechanic | Somatic tasks measuring whether body-state is treated as sacred/ordinary without distinction |

## 2. Diagnostic Target

Non-dual presence is not disembodied. The body IS the sacred — not a vehicle for it, not an obstacle to it. This modality measures whether the player's somatic engagement reveals residual grasping at "spiritual" body-states or aversion to "ordinary" body-states.

## 3. Task Architecture

### 3.1 Breath Equanimity Tasks

Player engages with breath-based mechanics. System measures whether "meditative" breathing patterns are grasped at versus "ordinary" breathing. Target: identical engagement quality regardless of breath-state.

- Metric: effort differential between "sacred breath" and "functional breath"
- Metric: return-seeking after meditative breath is interrupted

### 3.2 Posture-State Decoupling

Player performs actions in various postures (seated meditation posture vs. slouched vs. active). System measures whether performance or engagement quality varies with posture-state.

- Healthy signal: equal presence regardless of physical configuration
- Shadow signal: better performance in "spiritual" postures (grasping) or deliberate avoidance of them (aversion)

### 3.3 Sensation Equanimity

Player encounters pleasant, neutral, and unpleasant somatic stimuli (vibration patterns, rhythm challenges, tension/release). System measures approach/avoid bias.

- Target: flat response profile across sensation valence
- Shadow: preferential engagement with "blissful" sensations (sacred grasping)

### 3.4 Movement Without Seeking

Rhythm and movement tasks where some patterns feel "flow-like" and others feel "effortful." System measures whether player chases flow-states or rests equally in effort.

## 4. Shadow Mapping

| Shadow | Somatic signature |
|---|---|
| 2.1 Grasping-at-Sacred | Chases bliss-states, prefers meditative body-configurations |
| 2.2 Aversion-to-Sacred | Avoids stillness, discomfort with deep somatic presence |
| 2.3 Spiritual Bypassing | Claims body-equanimity but micro-tensions reveal preference |
| 2.4 Seeker-Identity | Adopts "spiritual" postures/breathing as identity-markers |

## 5. Drive Probes

| Drive | Somatic probe | Healthy signal |
|---|---|---|
| Eros | Response to intensifying sensation | Neither chasing nor bracing |
| Agape | Response to sensation diminishing | Neither relief nor disappointment |
| Agency | Initiating movement | Action without spiritual self-consciousness |
| Communion | Receiving sensation | Open without merger into bliss |

## 6. Biometric Integration (Optional)

If device supports: heart-rate variability consistency across sacred/ordinary somatic contexts. HRV coherence that doesn't spike specifically during "spiritual" activities indicates genuine equanimity.

## 7. Progression Logic

- **Diagnosis:** Baseline somatic preference-map across breath, posture, sensation, movement
- **Catalyst:** Tasks that present the player's preferred somatic-spiritual state, then remove it
- **Integration:** Body becomes transparent — neither sacred nor ordinary, just present

## 8. Design Constraint

The game never labels any somatic state as "spiritual." All tasks are presented as ordinary body-engagement. The diagnostic power comes from the player's own implicit categorization revealed through differential engagement patterns.
