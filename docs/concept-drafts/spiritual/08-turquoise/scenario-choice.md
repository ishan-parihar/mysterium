## 1. Modality Identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Module | Spiritual / White |
| Modality | Scenario-Choice |
| Probe axis | Non-dual dilemmas requiring presence without preference |
| Core mechanic | Branching scenarios where all options are equally valid; choice pattern reveals residual grasping |

## 2. Diagnostic Target

At White, the healthy response to a spiritual dilemma is presence without preference — not indifference, but genuine equanimity. This modality presents situations where any choice is valid, and the PATTERN of choices across many scenarios reveals residual grasping.

## 3. Scenario Categories

### 3.1 Sacred-vs-Ordinary Dilemmas

Player must choose between a "spiritual" activity and a "mundane" one. Neither is better. Systematic preference for either reveals shadow 2.1 (grasping) or 2.2 (aversion).

- Retreat vs. family dinner
- Meditation vs. doing taxes
- Temple visit vs. grocery shopping

### 3.2 State-Preservation Dilemmas

Player is in a flow/meditative state and must choose whether to maintain it or respond to interruption. Clinging to state = shadow 2.1. Reflexive abandonment = shadow 2.2.

### 3.3 Teaching/Sharing Dilemmas

Player encounters someone struggling spiritually. Options range from active guidance to silent presence. Over-helping reveals seeker-identity (2.4). Withholding reveals aversion (2.2).

### 3.4 Identity Dilemmas

Scenarios where player must describe themselves. Options include spiritual self-descriptions and ordinary ones. Systematic spiritual self-identification = shadow 2.4.

## 4. Shadow Detection Logic

| Shadow | Choice pattern |
|---|---|
| 2.1 Grasping-at-Sacred | Systematically chooses "spiritual" options, preserves sacred states |
| 2.2 Aversion-to-Sacred | Avoids spiritual depth, chooses mundane to prove non-attachment |
| 2.3 Spiritual Bypassing | Chooses "non-dual" framing but with subtle inconsistency across scenarios |
| 2.4 Seeker-Identity | Chooses options that position self as spiritually advanced |

## 5. Drive Probes via Scenario

| Drive | Scenario type | Healthy response |
|---|---|---|
| Eros | Opportunities for spiritual deepening | Neither grasping nor refusing |
| Agape | Opportunities to serve at "lower" levels | No condescension, no avoidance |
| Agency | Situations requiring spiritual action | Acts without doer-identity |
| Communion | Situations requiring spiritual receptivity | Receives without merger |

## 6. The Equanimity Metric

Across 20+ scenarios, the system builds a preference-map. Perfect non-dual presence = flat distribution (no systematic bias). Any clustering reveals the specific shadow axis.

Key: individual choices are meaningless. Only the PATTERN across many choices is diagnostic.

## 7. Progression Logic

- **Diagnosis:** Initial scenario battery establishes preference-map
- **Catalyst:** Scenarios specifically targeting the player's clustering axis
- **Integration:** Preference-map flattens; choices become genuinely context-responsive rather than pattern-driven

## 8. Narrative Framing

Scenarios are embedded in the game world as ordinary life moments for the player's character. They never feel like "spiritual tests." The more mundane the framing, the more accurately residual grasping is detected — because the player isn't performing non-duality.
