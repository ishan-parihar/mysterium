## 2. Shadow Model — Residual Grasping Patterns

At White, shadows are not gross distortions but residual micro-grasping patterns that prevent full transparency.

| # | Shadow | Pattern | Signature |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2.1 | Residual Grasping-at-Sacred | Still attached to spiritual experience as special | Preference for numinous over ordinary |
| 2.2 | Residual Aversion-to-Sacred | Avoids certain spiritual depths, blind spots remain | Subtle flinch from full mystery |
| 2.3 | Residual Spiritual Bypassing | Claims non-dual while subtly preferring states | Performed awakening, dualistic underneath |
| 2.4 | Residual Identity-with-Seeker | Identifies AS the spiritual one | Subtlest self-grasping — "I am awake" |

## 3. Drive Expression

At White, all four drives manifest in equanimous expression. The game detects residual imbalance rather than gross pathology.

- **Eros:** Transparent upward movement without grasping at higher states
- **Agape:** Effortless descent without aversion to lower expression
- **Agency:** Action without doer-identity
- **Communion:** Presence-with without merger or separation

## 7. Integration with Harvest Mechanic

White/Spiritual is the capstone module. Full integration here signals readiness for the 4th-density harvest narrative arc. The module does not "complete" — it deepens indefinitely. Theta-decay applies: neglect of transparent presence causes subtle re-grasping patterns to re-emerge.

## 8. Design Principle

The game at this level must be nearly invisible. The less it feels like a spiritual game, the more accurately it measures non-dual presence. Any mechanic that makes the player feel spiritual is itself a diagnostic signal of residual grasping.
