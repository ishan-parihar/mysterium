# Spiritual / Orange — Module Specification

> **Line:** Spiritual  **Stage:** Orange / Rational  **Vibration:** The sacred examined — meaning survives the crucible of reason or is discarded honestly.

---

## 1. Capacity Definition

The ability to subject one's ultimate concerns to rational scrutiny — to demythologize inherited belief, construct personal meaning-frameworks from examined principles, and tolerate the uncertainty that genuine inquiry produces. Orange spiritual capacity is self-authored meaning: neither borrowed from tradition nor dissolved into relativism, but forged through honest examination. The player demonstrates this through logical consistency of value-hierarchies, willingness to revise beliefs under evidence, and capacity to hold purpose without requiring external authority.

## 2. Shadow Archetypes

### 2.1 Dark-Addiction: The Militant Rationalist

Reduces all meaning to the measurable. Aggressive atheism becomes identity. "Spirituality is delusion." Cannot tolerate mystery or ambiguity. Scientism functions as unexamined religion. The rational tool becomes the only tool — anything beyond its reach is declared nonexistent.

### 2.2 Dark-Allergy: The Unquestioning Believer

Refuses rational examination of faith. Remains in Amber mythic-membership: "Don't question God." Terrified of doubt. Treats inquiry as sin. The rational capacity exists but is walled off from the spiritual domain specifically.

### 2.3 Golden-Addiction: The Premature Pluralist

Performs Green interfaith appreciation without genuine Orange rational examination. Claims "all paths are valid" before ever genuinely questioning their own tradition. Skips the hard work of demythologization. Spiritual bypassing dressed as sophistication.

### 2.4 Golden-Allergy: The Rational Fortress

Rational examination is present and genuine but refuses pluralistic opening. Terrified of "anything goes." Insists there is ONE rational answer to the meaning question. Has done the Orange work but turquoise-knuckles the result, refusing the next developmental invitation.

## 3. Drive-Health Landscape

| Drive | Healthy Expression | Pathological Expression |
|---|---|---|
| Eros | Reaches toward universal principles beyond tradition | Grasps at premature synthesis (GA) or refuses mystery (DA) |
| Agape | Returns examined meaning to embodied daily life | Clings to pre-rational comfort (DAll) or reduces meaning to formula (GAll) |
| Agency | Authors personal meaning-framework sovereignly | Militant rejection of all external meaning-sources (DA) |
| Communion | Shares examined meaning in philosophical dialogue | Collapses into group-think pluralism (GA) or refuses dialogue (GAll) |

## 4. Healing Vectors

### 4.1 Heal/Evolve (Agape + Agency)

Integrates dark shadows. The Militant Rationalist learns to embody (Agape) their own sovereign meaning (Agency) without needing to destroy others' meaning. The Unquestioning Believer reclaims their own rational authority (Agency) and grounds it in lived experience (Agape) rather than abstract rebellion.

### 4.2 Evolve/Heal (Eros + Communion)

Dissolves golden shadows. The Premature Pluralist is drawn upward (Eros) into genuine shared inquiry (Communion) that demands they first do their own rational work. The Rational Fortress is invited toward larger meaning (Eros) through authentic philosophical community (Communion).

### 4.3 Integration Criteria

Player demonstrates: (a) logical consistency in stated value-hierarchy, (b) willingness to revise beliefs when evidence warrants, (c) tolerance of irreducible mystery without collapsing into either scientism or mysticism, (d) capacity to articulate personal meaning without requiring others' agreement or tradition's authority.

## 6. Compound Shadows & Cross-Module Relationships

### 6.1 The Meaning Void (Spiritual DA × Emotional DAll)

Militant rationalism + emotional suppression = existential emptiness. Player has eliminated all meaning-sources rationally AND cannot feel the absence. Intervention: somatic-emotional games surface the felt-sense of emptiness before cognitive games re-examine what was discarded.

### 6.2 The Spiritual Bypasser (Spiritual GA × Intrapersonal GA)

Premature pluralism + premature process-self = ungrounded transcendence. Claims universal perspective without having done the personal examination. Intervention: deterministic games expose logical gaps in the claimed synthesis.

### 6.3 The Dogmatic Achiever (Spiritual DAll × Willpower DA)

Unquestioning belief + compulsive achievement = works for God without examining why. Achievement drive serves unexamined faith. Intervention: scenario-choice games create conflicts between achievement-meaning and examined purpose.

### 6.4 The Rational Nihilist (Spiritual DA × Moral DA)

Militant rationalism + moral calculation = meaning reduced to utility. Everything is optimizable, nothing is sacred. Intervention: social-cooperative games expose the poverty of purely instrumental meaning through philosophical community.

## 7. Shadow Surfacing Sequence

### 7.1 Progressive Revelation

Sessions 1–3: Baseline meaning-reasoning without shadow provocation. Sessions 4–8: Gentle catalyst — items that invite but don't force examination. Sessions 9–15: Direct shadow probes — items designed to activate specific quadrants. Sessions 16+: Integration challenges requiring all four drives in healthy expression.

### 7.2 Per-Modality Surfacing Table

| Modality | Primary Shadow Surfaced | Secondary |
|---|---|---|
| Deterministic | DA (logical rigidity) | GAll (single-answer insistence) |
| Language-Reflective | GA (performed pluralism) | DAll (borrowed language) |
| Scenario-Choice | DAll (comfort over truth) | DA (truth over meaning) |
| Embodied-Somatic | DA (disembodied rationalism) | DAll (body-as-sin) |

### 7.3 Cross-Validation

No shadow is diagnosed from a single modality. DA requires convergence across deterministic + embodied + scenario. DAll requires convergence across language + scenario + social. GA requires convergence across language + social + immersive. GAll requires convergence across deterministic + strategic + social.
