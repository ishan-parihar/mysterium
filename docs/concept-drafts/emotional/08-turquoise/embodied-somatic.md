## 1. Modality Identity

- **Module:** Emotional / White
- **Modality:** Embodied-Somatic
- **Unique Lateral:** Body as site of luminous affect — embodied equanimity, physical non-grasping at feeling
- **What this measures that no other modality measures:** Whether equanimity is genuinely embodied or only cognitive — does the body grasp, brace, or contract around emotional stimuli, or does it remain open and transparent?

## 2. Core Mechanic — Embodied Affect-Transparency

### 2.1 Somatic Response Tasks
Physical interactions that reveal whether the body participates in equanimity or secretly holds residual grasping:
- **Rhythm-affect coupling:** Emotional stimuli paired with rhythmic tasks. Transparent affect = rhythm unperturbed. Residual grasping = rhythm disruption at specific valences.
- **Tension-release sequences:** Player performs tension-release patterns while emotional content varies. Grasping shows as incomplete release or premature tension.
- **Breath-affect coherence:** Breathing patterns monitored during emotional exposure. Transparent affect = breath remains natural. Grasping = subtle breath-holding or acceleration.

### 2.2 Body-as-Witness Tasks
The player locates feeling in the body and then releases it:
- **Somatic scanning under affect:** Where does the body contract? True equanimity has no preferential contraction sites.
- **Movement-through-emotion:** Physical movement sequences that require passing through emotional territories. Grasping shows as movement hesitation or acceleration.
- **Stillness-under-perturbation:** Can the body remain still and open while emotional stimuli intensify? Not rigid stillness (suppression) but alive stillness (transparency).

### 2.3 Distinguishing Transparency from Suppression
Critical measurement: genuine embodied equanimity has micro-variability, aliveness, subtle responsiveness. Somatic suppression is rigid, flat, over-controlled. The system distinguishes these through variability metrics.

## 3. Shadow Detection via Body

### 3.1 Residual Grasping-at-Feeling
Somatic markers: body leans toward preferred emotions, subtle approach postures, lingering in pleasant somatic states.

### 3.2 Residual Aversion-to-Feeling
Somatic markers: micro-bracing before difficult emotions, breath-holding, subtle withdrawal postures, specific body regions that "go offline" during certain affects.

### 3.3 Residual Emotional Bypassing
Somatic markers: overall flatness of somatic response (too still, too even), dissociation signatures, body "checked out" while mind claims equanimity.

### 3.4 Residual Identity-with-Feeler
Somatic markers: body organizes around a felt-center ("I" located in chest/head), somatic self-referencing, inability to let sensation be impersonal.

## 4. Drive Probes via Body

| Drive | Somatic Probe | Residual Signal |
|---|---|---|
| Eros | Intensity-escalation sequences | Body reaches toward intensity rather than resting |
| Agape | Compassion-posture tasks | Body compulsively orients toward others' pain |
| Agency | Regulation vs. release tasks | Body defaults to control rather than openness |
| Communion | Attunement vs. solitude tasks | Body seeks resonance rather than resting in its own field |

## 5. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

- **Catalyst:** Somatic task reveals where the body still grasps — specific emotions, specific body regions, specific patterns
- **Experience:** Subsequent tasks bring attention to exactly those somatic grasping-sites through movement, breath, and rhythm
- **Integration:** Body naturally releases residual holding as awareness meets contraction without force

## 6. Infinite Checkpoint Structure

Each session produces a somatic-transparency map. Progress = decreasing contraction-sites, increasing micro-variability (aliveness without grasping). Sessions can be as brief as a single breath-cycle task. The body's residual patterns shift slowly — the 28-day half-life reflects somatic integration timescales.

## 7. Compound Shadow Surfacing

- **Luminous Suppressor:** Body flat while cognitive performance maintained — somatic dissociation masked as equanimity
- **Compassion Performer:** Body performs care-postures without genuine somatic resonance — the gesture without the feeling
- **Feeling-Identified:** Body cannot release the "feeler" posture — always organized around a somatic self-center
