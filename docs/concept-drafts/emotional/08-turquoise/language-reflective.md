## 1. Modality Identity

- **Module:** Emotional / White
- **Modality:** Language-Reflective
- **Unique Lateral:** Verbal articulation of luminous affect — how the player talks about feeling reveals residual grasping
- **What this measures that no other modality measures:** The linguistic structure of emotional self-report — whether language about feeling is transparent, grasping, avoidant, performative, or self-referential

## 2. Core Mechanic — Luminous Affect Articulation

### 2.1 Open-Ended Affect Reports
After encountering emotional stimuli or situations, the player articulates their experience in free text. The LLM analyzes:
- **Ownership markers:** "I feel" vs. "there is feeling" vs. "feeling arises" — each reveals a different relationship to affect
- **Temporal grasping:** Does the player narrate emotion as ongoing, or as already-dissolved? Residual grasping extends emotional narratives.
- **Preference language:** Subtle evaluative terms ("beautiful equanimity," "unwanted anger") reveal residual valence-assignment.
- **Absence language:** What the player does NOT mention — affective blind spots appear as linguistic gaps.

### 2.2 Affect-Description Prompts
Specific prompts designed to surface residual grasping:
- "Describe what is happening in your emotional field right now"
- "What was that experience like?" (after a perturbation)
- "How do you relate to difficult feelings when they arise?"
- "What does compassion feel like from the inside?"

### 2.3 Comparative Articulation
Player describes the same emotional territory at different times. Linguistic drift reveals whether equanimity is stable or performed.

## 3. Shadow Detection via Language

### 3.1 Residual Grasping-at-Feeling
Linguistic markers: extended positive-affect descriptions, savoring language, reluctance to move past pleasant states, "I love this feeling" patterns.

### 3.2 Residual Aversion-to-Feeling
Linguistic markers: minimization of difficult affect, rapid topic-shifting away from certain emotions, "I don't really feel that anymore" dismissals, conspicuous absence of certain affect-words.

### 3.3 Residual Emotional Bypassing
Linguistic markers: spiritual jargon replacing felt-sense language ("it's all just energy"), premature transcendence claims, inability to describe raw affect without framing it as "already integrated."

### 3.4 Residual Identity-with-Feeler
Linguistic markers: persistent first-person ownership ("MY equanimity"), narrative self as emotional protagonist, inability to describe feeling without a feeler at center.

## 4. Drive Probes via Language

| Drive | Prompt Domain | Residual Signal |
|---|---|---|
| Eros | Describe longing, depth, intensity | Language that reaches toward rather than rests in |
| Agape | Describe compassion, care, giving | Language of compulsion rather than effortless flow |
| Agency | Describe regulation, mastery, control | Language of management rather than transparency |
| Communion | Describe connection, resonance, merger | Language of need rather than natural attunement |

## 5. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

- **Catalyst:** LLM identifies residual grasping patterns in player's language and generates follow-up prompts that gently surface the pattern
- **Experience:** Player encounters their own linguistic habits reflected back — not as correction but as invitation to articulate more transparently
- **Integration:** Over sessions, language naturally shifts from grasping/avoidant/performative/self-referential toward transparent, present-tense, non-owned affect-description

## 6. Infinite Checkpoint Structure

Each session produces a linguistic-transparency score across four dimensions. The LLM tracks longitudinal shifts in affect-language. Sessions can be as brief as a single prompt-response pair. Progress = language becoming increasingly transparent without becoming artificially detached.

## 7. Compound Shadow Surfacing

- **Luminous Suppressor:** Player uses cognitive/analytical language about emotions — "I understand that sadness is arising" without any felt-sense vocabulary
- **Compassion Performer:** Player produces beautiful compassion-language that lacks embodied specificity — generic spiritual affect-talk
- **Feeling-Identified:** Player cannot describe emotion without centering self — every affect-report is autobiography
