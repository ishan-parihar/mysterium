## 1. Modality Identity

- **Module:** Emotional / White
- **Modality:** Deterministic
- **Unique Lateral:** Objective equanimity measurement — affect-transparency metrics, non-grasping response to emotional stimuli
- **What this measures that no other modality measures:** Whether the player's affective system responds to stimuli without contraction, preference, or avoidance — measured through objective behavioral and timing metrics

## 2. Core Mechanic — Luminous Affect Transparency Tasks

### 2.1 Stimulus Presentation
Emotionally charged stimuli (images, sounds, micro-narratives) presented in rapid sequence. The system measures:
- **Response latency asymmetry:** Does the player respond faster/slower to certain affect-valences? Residual grasping creates latency signatures.
- **Interference patterns:** Does a preceding emotional stimulus alter performance on a subsequent neutral task? Transparent affect produces zero interference.
- **Approach/avoidance micro-signatures:** Subtle directional biases in response patterns reveal residual preference or aversion.

### 2.2 Equanimity Under Perturbation
Baseline task performance established, then emotional perturbations introduced. Transparent affect = no performance deviation. Residual grasping = measurable deviation proportional to grasping intensity.

### 2.3 Affect-Valence Blindness Test
Player categorizes stimuli along non-emotional dimensions while emotional content varies. True equanimity produces flat response curves across all valences. Residual grasping produces characteristic bumps.

## 3. Shadow Detection

### 3.1 Residual Grasping-at-Feeling
Detected via: elevated engagement metrics for preferred emotional textures, longer dwell-times on "pleasant" stimuli, subtle approach bias.

### 3.2 Residual Aversion-to-Feeling
Detected via: faster dismissal of avoided emotions, performance dips when unavoidable, micro-avoidance patterns in response trajectories.

### 3.3 Residual Emotional Bypassing
Detected via: flat response curves that are TOO flat — suppression produces characteristic over-regulation signatures distinct from genuine transparency. Genuine equanimity has micro-variability; suppression is rigid.

### 3.4 Residual Identity-with-Feeler
Detected via: self-referential processing signatures — stimuli that implicate "the one who feels" produce different latency profiles than impersonal stimuli.

## 4. Drive Probes

| Drive | Probe | Residual Signal |
|---|---|---|
| Eros | Stimuli with increasing emotional depth/intensity | Latency acceleration toward intense stimuli |
| Agape | Stimuli depicting others' suffering | Compulsive engagement beyond transparent witnessing |
| Agency | Tasks requiring affect-regulation vs. affect-release | Preference for regulation over release |
| Communion | Stimuli depicting emotional connection vs. solitude | Asymmetric response to relational vs. solitary affect |

## 5. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

- **Catalyst:** Task reveals specific residual grasping pattern through objective metrics
- **Experience:** Player encounters the pattern as gameplay feedback — not labeled as "your shadow" but felt as the task becoming subtly harder in that dimension
- **Integration:** Repeated exposure with adaptive difficulty dissolves the grasping — performance curves flatten naturally as transparency deepens

## 6. Infinite Checkpoint Structure

Each session produces a transparency profile across all four shadow dimensions. Progress = increasing flatness of response curves. The game never "ends" — it continues to detect ever-subtler residual patterns. Sessions as short as 90 seconds produce valid signal at this stage.

## 7. Compound Shadow Surfacing

- **Luminous Suppressor:** Cognitive load tasks paired with emotional stimuli — suppression shows as cognitive performance maintained at cost of affective flatness
- **Equanimity Grasper:** Perturbation tasks that threaten equanimity itself — grasping shows as defensive response to equanimity-disrupting stimuli
