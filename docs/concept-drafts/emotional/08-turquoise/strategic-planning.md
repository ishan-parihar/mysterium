## 1. Modality Identity

- **Module:** Emotional / White
- **Modality:** Strategic-Planning
- **Unique Lateral:** Planning within equanimity — the paradox of structuring emotional life without grasping
- **What this measures that no other modality measures:** Whether the player can hold future-oriented emotional intentions without those intentions becoming attachments — can you plan compassion without grasping at outcomes?

## 2. Core Mechanic — Equanimous Emotional Planning

### 2.1 The Planning Paradox
At White, the player must navigate: How do you structure emotional engagement with life without that structure becoming a new form of grasping? The game presents planning tasks where:
- Plans must be held lightly — rigid plans reveal grasping
- Plans must be genuine — no-plan reveals bypassing
- Plans must serve others — self-serving plans reveal identity-with-feeler
- Plans must include dissolution — plans that cannot be released reveal aversion to loss

### 2.2 Emotional Stewardship Scenarios
Multi-step planning tasks involving emotional territories:
- **Relational planning:** How to be present for someone's grief over time without attachment to outcome
- **Creative planning:** How to structure creative emotional expression without grasping at the product
- **Service planning:** How to plan compassionate action without the plan becoming obligation
- **Transition planning:** How to prepare for emotional transitions (loss, change, ending) without pre-grieving or avoidance

### 2.3 Plan-and-Release Cycles
Player creates a plan, then conditions change. How does the player relate to the dissolved plan? Transparent equanimity releases without residue. Grasping shows as frustration, clinging, or premature re-planning.

## 3. Shadow Detection via Planning

### 3.1 Residual Grasping-at-Feeling
Planning markers: over-structures positive emotional experiences, creates elaborate plans to maintain pleasant states, plans that serve feeling-preservation.

### 3.2 Residual Aversion-to-Feeling
Planning markers: plans that avoid emotional exposure, contingency-heavy structures that prevent vulnerability, emotional risk-aversion in planning.

### 3.3 Residual Emotional Bypassing
Planning markers: refuses to plan ("I just flow"), claims planning is attachment, uses non-planning as spiritual performance while actually avoiding engagement.

### 3.4 Residual Identity-with-Feeler
Planning markers: plans centered on "my emotional journey," self-referential planning, inability to plan for others' emotional needs without centering own experience.

## 4. Drive Probes via Planning

| Drive | Planning Domain | Residual Signal |
|---|---|---|
| Eros | Plans involving depth and meaning | Plans that pursue intensity rather than serve what-is |
| Agape | Plans involving care and service | Plans driven by obligation rather than natural generosity |
| Agency | Plans involving structure and execution | Over-structured plans that control rather than guide |
| Communion | Plans involving togetherness | Plans that engineer connection rather than allow it |

## 5. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

- **Catalyst:** Planning task reveals where the player's relationship to future emotional life is still contracted
- **Experience:** Subsequent tasks place the player at the exact edge — planning scenarios calibrated to their specific residual pattern
- **Integration:** Player develops capacity to hold genuine intention without attachment — plans become transparent containers rather than rigid structures

## 6. Infinite Checkpoint Structure

Each session presents 2-4 planning scenarios with follow-up perturbations. Progress = plans becoming simultaneously more genuine AND more lightly held. The paradox resolves not through formula but through deepening transparency. Sessions produce a planning-equanimity profile across all four shadow dimensions.

## 7. Compound Shadow Surfacing

- **Equanimity Grasper:** Plans designed to protect equanimity — the plan itself is a defense against disturbance
- **Compassion Performer:** Elaborate compassion-plans that perform care without genuine availability
- **Luminous Suppressor:** Hyper-rational plans that manage emotional life without feeling it
