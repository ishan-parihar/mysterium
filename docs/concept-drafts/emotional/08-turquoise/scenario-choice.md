## 1. Modality Identity

- **Module:** Emotional / White
- **Modality:** Scenario-Choice
- **Unique Lateral:** Non-dual emotional dilemmas — situations requiring compassion without attachment
- **What this measures that no other modality measures:** Whether the player can act from genuine compassion in situations where attachment, aversion, bypassing, or self-reference would produce subtly different choices

## 2. Core Mechanic — Non-Dual Emotional Dilemmas

### 2.1 Dilemma Structure
Scenarios where all options are emotionally valid but each reveals a different relationship to affect:
- Option A: The grasping-at-feeling response (acts from attachment to emotional connection)
- Option B: The aversion-to-feeling response (acts from avoidance of emotional pain)
- Option C: The bypassing response (acts from "transcendent" detachment)
- Option D: The transparent response (acts from genuine compassion without contraction)

The transparent option is never obvious. It often looks like the simplest, least dramatic choice.

### 2.2 Scenario Domains
- **Witnessing suffering:** Someone is in pain. How do you respond when helping might be grasping and not-helping might be aversion?
- **Releasing attachment:** Something beautiful is ending. How do you relate to the dissolution?
- **Receiving difficult affect:** Someone directs anger/grief/need at you. What arises?
- **Paradox of care:** Situations where compassion and non-attachment appear to conflict.

### 2.3 Adaptive Complexity
The LLM generates scenarios calibrated to the player's specific residual patterns. If grasping-at-feeling is detected, scenarios emphasize letting-go. If aversion is detected, scenarios require staying-with.

## 3. Shadow Detection via Choice

### 3.1 Residual Grasping-at-Feeling
Choice pattern: consistently selects options that maintain emotional connection, even when release would serve.

### 3.2 Residual Aversion-to-Feeling
Choice pattern: consistently selects options that minimize emotional exposure. Frames withdrawal as wisdom.

### 3.3 Residual Emotional Bypassing
Choice pattern: selects "transcendent" options that sound equanimous but actually bypass the emotional reality.

### 3.4 Residual Identity-with-Feeler
Choice pattern: selects options that center the self's emotional experience rather than responding transparently to what-is.

## 4. Drive Probes via Scenario

| Drive | Scenario Type | Residual Signal |
|---|---|---|
| Eros | Scenarios involving depth, intensity, meaning | Choices that pursue emotional depth beyond transparency |
| Agape | Scenarios involving others' needs, suffering | Choices driven by compulsive care rather than effortless compassion |
| Agency | Scenarios requiring action vs. non-action | Choices that assert control over emotional situations |
| Communion | Scenarios involving togetherness vs. solitude | Choices that seek merger rather than natural attunement |

## 5. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

- **Catalyst:** Scenario reveals residual pattern through choice-tendency across multiple dilemmas
- **Experience:** Subsequent scenarios are calibrated to the edge — placing the player exactly where their residual grasping is most active
- **Integration:** Player's choices naturally shift toward transparency as the pattern is encountered repeatedly without judgment

## 6. Infinite Checkpoint Structure

Each session presents 3-7 dilemmas. Choice patterns accumulate into a residual-grasping profile. The scenario pool is effectively infinite via LLM generation. Progress = increasing proportion of transparent choices without the choices becoming formulaic.

## 7. Compound Shadow Surfacing

- **Compassion Performer:** Scenarios where genuine compassion requires doing nothing — the performer always acts
- **Equanimity Grasper:** Scenarios where equanimity must be released to meet the moment — the grasper refuses to be disturbed
- **Luminous Suppressor:** Scenarios requiring felt-sense response — the suppressor intellectualizes the dilemma
