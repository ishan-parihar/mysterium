## 1. Modality Identity

- **Module:** Emotional / White
- **Modality:** Immersive-RPG
- **Unique Lateral:** Ecological equanimity — whether luminous affect appears spontaneously in free-play
- **What this measures that no other modality measures:** Whether equanimity is the player's natural mode in unstructured emotional territory — not prompted, not tested, but spontaneously arising in open-world engagement

## 2. Core Mechanic — Ecological Emotional Transparency

### 2.1 Open-World Emotional Ecology
The RPG world presents a rich emotional environment without explicit prompts or tests:
- NPCs with complex emotional lives that the player encounters naturally
- Environmental affect — landscapes, weather, music that carry emotional charge
- Narrative events that arise organically — loss, beauty, conflict, tenderness
- No instructions to "be equanimous" — the game simply observes how the player IS

### 2.2 Implicit Measurement
The system tracks the player's spontaneous emotional behavior:
- **Approach/avoidance patterns:** Which emotional territories does the player naturally gravitate toward or avoid in free-play?
- **Engagement duration:** How long does the player linger in emotionally charged situations vs. neutral ones?
- **Response spontaneity:** When emotional events arise, does the player respond with transparent immediacy or with visible processing (grasping/avoiding/performing)?
- **Ecological consistency:** Is equanimity stable across all emotional territories, or does it break down in specific domains?

### 2.3 The World as Mirror
The game world is designed so that the player's residual grasping patterns shape their experience:
- Players with residual grasping-at-feeling will naturally seek emotional intensity in the world
- Players with residual aversion will naturally avoid certain emotional territories
- Players with residual bypassing will engage superficially with emotional content
- Players with residual identity-with-feeler will narrate their experience to NPCs

## 3. Shadow Detection via Ecology

### 3.1 Residual Grasping-at-Feeling
Ecological markers: seeks out emotionally intense NPCs/events, replays beautiful moments, hoards emotionally charged items, builds emotional "collections."

### 3.2 Residual Aversion-to-Feeling
Ecological markers: avoids grief-stricken NPCs, skips emotionally difficult narrative branches, prefers neutral/safe zones, rushes through uncomfortable emotional territory.

### 3.3 Residual Emotional Bypassing
Ecological markers: engages with emotional content at surface level only, treats NPCs' emotions as puzzles to solve rather than realities to witness, moves through the world without being touched.

### 3.4 Residual Identity-with-Feeler
Ecological markers: constantly checks own emotional state, journals/narrates experience, makes the world about "my journey," cannot witness without self-referencing.

## 4. Drive Probes via Ecology

| Drive | Ecological Domain | Residual Signal |
|---|---|---|
| Eros | Depth-content in the world (mysteries, sacred sites) | Compulsive depth-seeking rather than natural curiosity |
| Agape | NPCs in need throughout the world | Cannot pass suffering without intervening |
| Agency | World-shaping opportunities | Must control/fix/improve rather than witness |
| Communion | Social spaces, gatherings, relationships | Cannot be alone in the world without seeking others |

## 5. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

- **Catalyst:** The world reveals the player's residual patterns through their spontaneous behavior — no test, no prompt, just the mirror of free choice
- **Experience:** The world subtly adjusts to present more of what the player avoids and less of what they grasp — not punitively, but as natural ecological rebalancing
- **Integration:** Over time, the player's ecological behavior becomes genuinely equanimous — all territories equally available, no avoidance, no grasping, no performance

## 6. Infinite Checkpoint Structure

Every moment of free-play produces signal. There are no discrete "sessions" — the RPG world is always measuring. Progress = increasing ecological equanimity across all emotional territories. The world never runs out of emotional content to present.

## 7. Compound Shadow Surfacing

- **Luminous Suppressor:** Engages with the world intellectually — solves emotional puzzles without being moved
- **Compassion Performer:** Helps every NPC but without genuine presence — the helper identity rather than transparent compassion
- **Equanimity Grasper:** Creates a "peaceful" play-style that avoids all disturbance — equanimity as world-avoidance
- **Feeling-Identified:** The world becomes autobiography — every event is about "my growth," "my journey," "my feelings"
