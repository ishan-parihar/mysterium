## 1. Modality Identity

- **Module:** Emotional / White
- **Modality:** Social-Cooperative
- **Unique Lateral:** Shared luminous affect — communion in compassion, non-grasping emotional attunement
- **What this measures that no other modality measures:** Whether the player can be emotionally present with others without grasping at connection, performing compassion, or losing transparency in the relational field

## 2. Core Mechanic — Non-Grasping Emotional Communion

### 2.1 Cooperative Affect-Transparency Tasks
Multi-player or simulated-other tasks where emotional attunement is required but attachment is not:
- **Shared witnessing:** Both players encounter emotional stimuli together. Does the player's equanimity shift in the presence of another? Does it become performance?
- **Compassion without merger:** One player is in distress (simulated). Can the other be fully present without absorbing, fixing, or withdrawing?
- **Attunement without tracking:** Can the player sense another's emotional state without compulsively monitoring it?

### 2.2 Relational Equanimity Challenges
Scenarios where the relational field itself becomes the test:
- **Emotional asymmetry:** Player is equanimous, other is reactive. Does equanimity hold without becoming superiority or withdrawal?
- **Shared joy without grasping:** Can the player participate in collective positive affect without clinging to it?
- **Relational dissolution:** A cooperative bond ends. Does the player release without residue?
- **Conflict without contraction:** Disagreement arises. Does the player's emotional field contract or remain transparent?

### 2.3 Group Luminous Affect
Tasks involving group emotional fields:
- Can the player contribute to group equanimity without imposing it?
- Can the player remain transparent when the group is reactive?
- Can the player attune to group affect without losing individual transparency?

## 3. Shadow Detection via Social Field

### 3.1 Residual Grasping-at-Feeling
Social markers: seeks emotional intensity in relationship, prolongs relational warmth beyond its natural duration, uses others as sources of preferred affect.

### 3.2 Residual Aversion-to-Feeling
Social markers: withdraws from others' emotional intensity, maintains distance disguised as equanimity, avoids relational situations that might disturb peace.

### 3.3 Residual Emotional Bypassing
Social markers: performs compassion without genuine presence, offers spiritual platitudes instead of felt-attunement, "holds space" as technique rather than being.

### 3.4 Residual Identity-with-Feeler
Social markers: centers own emotional experience in relational encounters, cannot be present to another without self-referencing.

| Eros | Depth-seeking in relationship | Pursues emotional intimacy beyond what the moment offers |
| Agape | Care-giving encounters | Compulsive helping rather than effortless availability |

## 5. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

- **Catalyst:** Social task reveals where relational equanimity breaks down — where the player grasps, avoids, performs, or self-references in the field between self and other
- **Experience:** Subsequent cooperative tasks are calibrated to the specific relational edge
- **Integration:** Player develops genuine non-grasping emotional communion — fully present, fully transparent, fully available without contraction

## 6. Infinite Checkpoint Structure

Each session involves 2-5 cooperative encounters (simulated or real). Progress = increasing relational transparency without withdrawal. The social field is the most revealing mirror for residual grasping — what we hide from ourselves appears in relationship.

## 7. Compound Shadow Surfacing

- **Compassion Performer:** Most visible here — the gap between performed and genuine compassion is exposed in real relational encounter
- **Feeling-Identified:** Cannot be present to another without "I" at center — every relational moment becomes autobiography
- **Equanimity Grasper:** Withdraws from relational intensity to protect peace — equanimity as relational avoidance
