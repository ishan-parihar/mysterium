# Emotional / Turquoise — Social-Cooperative

## 1. Core Mechanic

Shared integral affect — the capacity to attune to group-level emotional fields with other players and to mentor across developmental altitudes without condescension or collapse. This modality probes whether vision-logic emotion can function interpersonally: can the player hold equanimity while co-regulating a group, read the collective emotional wave while remaining sovereign, and offer cross-altitude emotional mentorship that genuinely serves?

### 1.1 Task Structure

- **Phase A — Group Attunement:** Multiple players simultaneously engage with a shared emotional field (a collective narrative, a group challenge, a shared loss or celebration). The task: synchronise emotional reading of the field without losing individual sovereignty. Scored on convergence of pattern-detection AND maintenance of individual centre.
- **Phase B — Cross-Altitude Mentorship:** Players at different emotional-developmental altitudes are paired. The Turquoise-level player must attune to the other's emotional reality, offer support that meets them where they are, and gently catalyse growth — without imposing, rescuing, or withdrawing.
- **Phase C — Collective Equanimity:** The group faces an emotionally intense shared challenge. The task: maintain collective equanimity — not by suppressing individual affect but by each player holding their sovereign centre while remaining permeable to the group field. The group succeeds only if all members achieve this simultaneously.

### 1.2 Cooperative Scoring

- Individual sovereignty score (maintained personal centre)
- Attunement accuracy (convergent field-reading with group)
- Mentorship quality (appropriate altitude-meeting, non-imposition)
- Collective coherence (group-level equanimity emergence)
- No player can succeed by sacrificing another's sovereignty

## 2. Shadow Diagnostics

| Shadow | Social Signature |
|--------|-----------------|
| DA (Cartographer) | Excellent group-field reading but no genuine emotional contribution to the collective; observes without participating; mentorship is analytical not relational |
| DAll (Pluralist) | Over-merges with group affect; loses sovereignty in service of connection; mentorship collapses into co-dependent empathy; cannot hold altitude difference |
| GA (Premature Equanimist) | Offers "equanimity" to the group that is actually emotional withdrawal; mentorship is dismissive ("just be present"); group feels unsupported |
| GAll (Fortress) | Holds personal centre beautifully but cannot extend it to serve the group; mentorship is excellent one-on-one but refuses collective vulnerability |

## 3. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

- **Catalyst:** The social demand makes integral affect relational — it cannot remain a private achievement. Other players' genuine emotional states create unpredictable complexity that no amount of personal practice can fully prepare for.
- **Experience:** The felt-sense of shared integral emotion — sovereign individuals creating collective equanimity not through conformity but through mutual holding. Cross-altitude mentorship as love-in-action.
- **Integration:** The player develops the capacity to be an emotional anchor in group fields — not by controlling the field but by offering their equanimity as a resource others can attune to. Integral affect becomes a gift, not just a personal attainment.

## 4. Drive Probes

| Drive | Probe Mechanism |
|-------|----------------|
| Eros | Collective equanimity tasks where the group must reach beyond its current capacity together — does the player catalyse group growth or hold the group at current level? |
| Agape | Mentorship tasks with players at significantly lower altitudes — does the player descend with genuine compassion or maintain comfortable distance? |
| Agency | Group-pressure scenarios where the collective emotional wave pulls toward merger — does the player maintain sovereignty while remaining connected? |
| Communion | Tasks requiring genuine emotional vulnerability with the group — does the player offer their real affect or only their equanimity? |

## 5. Progression Arc

- **Entry:** Paired attunement with one other player; simple shared emotional scenarios; basic mentorship with clear altitude difference.
- **Midpoint:** Small-group attunement (3–5 players); complex shared emotional fields; mentorship across multiple altitudes simultaneously; collective equanimity under moderate intensity.
- **Mastery:** Large-group attunement; cross-altitude mentorship in real-time group dynamics; collective equanimity under high intensity; the player as emotional anchor for the field.
- **Shadow Mode:** Targeted social challenges — e.g., "contribute emotionally, not analytically" for DA, "hold your centre while others merge" for DAll, "show the group your genuine struggle" for GA, "let the group hold you" for GAll.
