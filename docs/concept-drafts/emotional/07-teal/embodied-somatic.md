# Emotional / Turquoise — Embodied-Somatic

## 1. Core Mechanic

The body as site of integral affect. This modality probes whether vision-logic emotion is embodied or merely cognitive — whether equanimity lives in the nervous system or only in the conceptual mind. The player engages somatic tasks that require holding group-level emotional intensity in the body without discharge, dissociation, or collapse. Integral affect that hasn't reached the body is not yet integral.

### 1.1 Task Structure

- **Phase A — Somatic Field Holding:** The player is exposed to emotionally intense group-level stimuli (audio landscapes of collective grief, joy, rage, fear layered simultaneously). The task: maintain a specific somatic rhythm (breath pattern, tap cadence, postural stability) while the emotional field intensifies. Deviation from rhythm = body losing equanimity.
- **Phase B — Embodied Altitude Discrimination:** Different somatic signatures correspond to different emotional altitudes. The player must identify which altitude is activating their body — distinguishing between Red-rage tension, Green-empathy heart-opening, and Turquoise-equanimity stillness as felt bodily states.
- **Phase C — Somatic Release:** After sustained holding, the player is guided through intentional somatic release — the body letting go of held affect. The probe: can the player release without collapsing? Can equanimity survive the transition from holding to flowing?

### 1.2 Input Modalities

- Breath rhythm (microphone or accelerometer)
- Tap cadence (touch timing patterns)
- Postural stability (gyroscope data on mobile)
- Response-time stability as proxy for autonomic regulation

## 2. Shadow Diagnostics

| Shadow | Somatic Signature |
|--------|-------------------|
| DA (Cartographer) | Body remains uninvolved — rhythm is mechanically perfect but shows no physiological response to emotional stimuli; dissociation from soma |
| DAll (Pluralist) | Body over-responds to all stimuli equally — cannot maintain rhythm because every emotional signal activates full somatic empathy; overwhelm |
| GA (Premature Equanimist) | Body is artificially still — suppressed rather than equanimous; rhythm is rigid rather than fluid; no genuine somatic response to intensity |
| GAll (Fortress) | Body holds beautifully during Phase A and B but cannot release in Phase C — somatic armouring; equanimity as tension rather than flow |

## 3. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

- **Catalyst:** The somatic demand bypasses cognitive performance. The body cannot fake integral affect — it either holds equanimity or it doesn't. Group-level emotional intensity forces the nervous system to develop genuine capacity.
- **Experience:** The felt-sense of embodied integral emotion — the body simultaneously open to the full emotional field AND stable in its own centre. Not numb, not overwhelmed, not rigid — alive and still.
- **Integration:** Repeated somatic practice builds vagal tone, interoceptive accuracy, and the nervous system's capacity to hold complexity without fight/flight/freeze. The body becomes the ground of integral affect.

## 4. Drive Probes

| Drive | Probe Mechanism |
|-------|----------------|
| Eros | Phase C release tasks — does the body open upward toward greater flow or contract back to holding? Willingness to let equanimity deepen |
| Agape | Somatic response to lower-altitude emotional stimuli — does the body hold compassionate space or tense against "primitive" affect? |
| Agency | Rhythm maintenance under peak intensity — does the body maintain sovereign cadence or entrain to the group-field's rhythm? |
| Communion | Multi-field somatic tasks — does the body extend its holding capacity to include unfamiliar emotional systems or contract to known territory? |

## 5. Progression Arc

- **Entry:** Single-emotion stimuli, simple breath rhythm, generous timing tolerance. Body learns basic affect-holding.
- **Midpoint:** Multi-altitude stimuli, complex rhythm patterns, altitude-discrimination tasks. Body develops integral holding capacity.
- **Mastery:** Full group-field intensity, sustained holding + intentional release cycles, cross-system somatic attunement. Body embodies vision-logic affect.
- **Shadow Mode:** Targeted somatic challenges — e.g., provocation-during-rhythm for DA (forces body to feel), single-altitude-isolation for DAll (forces hierarchy), release-demand for GAll (forces letting go), intensity-escalation for GA (exposes suppression).
