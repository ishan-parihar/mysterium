# Emotional / Turquoise — Language-Reflective

## 1. Core Mechanic

The player articulates their experience of group-level emotional phenomena through structured and free-form verbal expression. The LLM evaluates whether language reveals genuine integral affect (vision-logic emotion lived from within) or performative markers (borrowed vocabulary without embodied depth). The probe axis: how a person talks about affect-patterns reveals whether they are mapping from outside or inhabiting from within.

### 1.1 Task Structure

- **Phase A — Affect Narration:** After observing an emotional scenario (group conflict, collective grief, systemic joy), the player describes what they perceived. Scoring distinguishes system-level language from reductive single-perspective accounts, and embodied language from detached analytical framing.
- **Phase B — Equanimity Articulation:** The player is asked to describe their own emotional state while witnessing intense group affect. Genuine equanimity produces language that is both calm AND specific; performed equanimity produces vague spiritual bypassing or flat denial.
- **Phase C — Cross-Altitude Translation:** The player must describe the same emotional event from multiple developmental perspectives, then articulate the integral view that holds all perspectives without collapsing into any.

### 1.2 LLM Rubric Axes

- Specificity vs. abstraction (genuine integral affect is precise, not vague)
- Embodiment markers (somatic language, felt-sense references)
- Hierarchy acknowledgment (can name altitude differences without judgment)
- Sovereignty markers (first-person centre maintained amid system-description)
- Permeability markers (openness to being affected while remaining centred)

## 2. Shadow Diagnostics

| Shadow | Linguistic Signature |
|--------|---------------------|
| DA (Cartographer) | Highly analytical, system-level vocabulary but absent first-person affect language; describes emotions as objects never as lived experience |
| DAll (Pluralist) | Refuses altitude language; equalises all emotional expressions; uses Green-coded empathy vocabulary exclusively; "everyone's feelings are valid" without integration |
| GA (Premature Equanimist) | Spiritual bypassing vocabulary — "I am at peace," "emotions don't touch me" — combined with inability to describe specific group-level patterns |
| GAll (Fortress) | Rich integral vocabulary AND embodied language but systematically avoids language of release, surrender, or transcendence; describes holding but never letting go |

## 3. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

- **Catalyst:** The articulation demand forces the player to move beyond passive pattern-recognition into active meaning-making. Language externalises internal process, making shadow-patterns visible.
- **Experience:** The act of finding words for integral affect IS the developmental practice — language shapes perception, and precise integral-emotional language builds the capacity it describes.
- **Integration:** Over time, the player's vocabulary evolves from borrowed frameworks to authentic personal language for vision-logic emotion, tracked longitudinally by the LLM.

## 4. Drive Probes

| Drive | Probe Mechanism |
|-------|----------------|
| Eros | Prompts asking the player to describe what lies beyond their current emotional capacity — language of aspiration vs. language of arrival |
| Agape | Prompts asking the player to describe compassion for earlier emotional stages — integration language vs. condescension |
| Agency | Prompts during high-affect scenarios — does language maintain first-person sovereignty or dissolve into group-description? |
| Communion | Prompts asking the player to describe emotional resonance with systems unlike their own — extension language vs. contraction |

## 5. Progression Arc

- **Entry:** Simple affect narration of clear group-emotional scenarios; binary rubric (system-level vs. individual-level).
- **Midpoint:** Complex scenarios requiring altitude-differentiation; equanimity articulation under moderate provocation; multi-axis rubric scoring.
- **Mastery:** Cross-altitude translation tasks; longitudinal vocabulary evolution tracking; spontaneous integral-affect language in unprompted contexts.
- **Shadow Mode:** Targeted prompts designed to surface specific shadow-language — e.g., "describe your emotional state right now" for DA, "which emotional perspective here is most developed?" for DAll.
