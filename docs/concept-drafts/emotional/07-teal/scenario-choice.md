# Emotional / Turquoise — Scenario-Choice

## 1. Core Mechanic

The player faces integral emotional dilemmas — situations where multiple developmental altitudes of emotion are simultaneously valid, yet action is required. The dilemma structure: you must honour all emotional stages present in the system AND commit to a decisive action that inevitably privileges some emotional reality over others. No action is neutral. The probe: can the player hold integral affect while still acting?

### 1.1 Task Structure

- **Phase A — Field Presentation:** A complex emotional scenario unfolds involving multiple agents at different developmental altitudes (a grieving child, an angry adolescent, a rationalising adult, a spiritually bypassing elder — all in the same system). The player must read the full field before choosing.
- **Phase B — Action Under Complexity:** The player chooses an intervention. All options are emotionally costly — each honours some altitude while potentially invalidating another. The integral choice holds maximum perspectives while still committing.
- **Phase C — Consequence Witnessing:** The emotional aftermath of the choice unfolds. The player must witness the full consequence — including the pain their choice caused — without retreating into analysis, overwhelm, premature peace, or emotional shutdown.

### 1.2 Dilemma Design Principles

- No "correct" answer — only more or less integral responses
- Every option has genuine emotional cost
- Inaction is itself a choice with consequences
- The scenario cannot be solved by empathy alone (Green ceiling)
- The scenario cannot be solved by detachment alone (premature White)

## 2. Shadow Diagnostics

| Shadow | Choice Signature |
|--------|-----------------|
| DA (Cartographer) | Delays action indefinitely to "gather more data"; when forced, chooses the option that maintains analytical distance; avoids emotionally costly options |
| DAll (Pluralist) | Refuses to choose or chooses the option that avoids any hierarchy; paralysed by the impossibility of honouring all feelings equally |
| GA (Premature Equanimist) | Chooses rapidly with performed calm; selects the "transcendent" option that avoids engaging with lower-altitude pain; consequence-witnessing is flat |
| GAll (Fortress) | Makes excellent integral choices but refuses to witness consequences that require emotional release; shuts down during Phase C if the aftermath demands surrender |

## 3. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

- **Catalyst:** The dilemma structure makes it impossible to remain in comfortable integral observation. Action is demanded. Every action has emotional weight. The player cannot hide in any single altitude.
- **Experience:** The felt-sense of integral emotional commitment — holding the full field, choosing anyway, bearing the cost, remaining present to consequence. This IS Turquoise emotional maturity.
- **Integration:** Repeated exposure builds the capacity to act from integral affect rather than from any single emotional altitude. The player develops "committed equanimity" — peace that acts.

## 4. Drive Probes

| Drive | Probe Mechanism |
|-------|----------------|
| Eros | Scenarios where the integral choice requires reaching toward unfamiliar emotional territory — does the player stretch or retreat to known ground? |
| Agape | Scenarios where the integral choice requires returning to hold pain at a lower altitude — does the player descend with compassion or avoid? |
| Agency | Phase C witnessing — does the player maintain sovereign presence amid emotional aftermath or collapse into guilt/detachment? |
| Communion | Scenarios requiring the player to trust the group-field's capacity to hold consequence — does the player over-control or allow? |

## 5. Progression Arc

- **Entry:** Two-agent scenarios with clear altitude difference; binary choice; mild consequence.
- **Midpoint:** Multi-agent scenarios with 3–4 altitudes present; multiple choice options each with distinct cost; extended consequence witnessing.
- **Mastery:** System-level scenarios where the "agents" are entire communities; choices with irreversible consequences; consequence witnessing that demands genuine equanimity.
- **Shadow Mode:** Scenarios specifically designed to trigger the player's dominant shadow — e.g., time-pressure for DA, forced-hierarchy for DAll, pain-exposure for GA, release-demand for GAll.
