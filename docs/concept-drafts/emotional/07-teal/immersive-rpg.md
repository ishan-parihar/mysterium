# Emotional / Turquoise — Immersive-RPG

## 1. Core Mechanic

Ecological integral affect — whether vision-logic emotion appears spontaneously in free-play without structured prompting. The player navigates an open emotional ecology: a living world populated by NPCs at multiple developmental altitudes, group-level emotional weather systems, and emergent affective events. No explicit tasks are given. The probe: does the player naturally read group-level patterns, maintain equanimity, and act from integral affect when no one is asking them to?

### 1.1 World Design

- **Emotional Weather:** The game world has ambient group-level emotional states that shift dynamically — collective grief after a loss, rising tension before conflict, shared joy during celebration. These are not background flavour; they are the primary gameplay terrain.
- **Altitude-Diverse NPCs:** Characters operate at different emotional-developmental altitudes. Some are Red-reactive, some Green-empathic, some performing Turquoise. The player's interactions with each reveal their own altitude.
- **Emergent Crises:** Unscripted emotional events arise from NPC interactions — a conflict escalates, a community grieves, a leader bypasses. The player can intervene, observe, or participate. All responses are scored.
- **The Sovereign Centre Mechanic:** The player-character has an internal "centre" indicator that responds to the emotional ecology. Maintaining centre while remaining engaged is the core gameplay loop.

### 1.2 Implicit Scoring

All scoring is invisible to the player. The system tracks:
- Frequency and accuracy of group-level pattern recognition (inferred from player actions)
- Equanimity maintenance (centre-indicator stability during emotional intensity)
- Quality of interventions (integral vs. single-altitude responses)
- Spontaneous mentorship (helping lower-altitude NPCs without being asked)
- Ecological stewardship (actions that serve the whole emotional field, not just individuals)

## 2. Shadow Diagnostics

| Shadow | Ecological Signature |
|--------|---------------------|
| DA (Cartographer) | Spends all time observing emotional weather patterns; never intervenes; treats NPCs as data-points; centre is stable because disengaged |
| DAll (Pluralist) | Over-engages with every NPC equally; cannot prioritise; centre destabilises in complex multi-altitude scenarios; avoids any action that implies hierarchy |
| GA (Premature Equanimist) | Centre is artificially stable; ignores emotional weather; interacts with NPCs from performed detachment; avoids crises entirely |
| GAll (Fortress) | Engages deeply with individual NPCs but avoids group-level emotional events; centre is strong but contracted; refuses scenarios requiring surrender |

## 3. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

- **Catalyst:** The open ecology removes all scaffolding. There is no "correct" path, no explicit task, no feedback loop telling the player they're doing well. Integral affect must arise from genuine capacity, not compliance.
- **Experience:** The felt-sense of being an emotionally sovereign presence in a complex living world — reading the field, holding centre, acting when called, resting when not. This is what Turquoise emotional life actually feels like.
- **Integration:** Extended free-play builds the habit of integral affect — it becomes the player's default mode rather than a performance they activate when tested. The ecological context makes vision-logic emotion a way of being, not a skill to deploy.

## 4. Drive Probes

| Drive | Probe Mechanism |
|-------|----------------|
| Eros | Does the player seek out emotional edges in the ecology — unfamiliar territory, higher-altitude NPCs, novel group-dynamics? Or stay in comfortable zones? |
| Agape | Does the player spontaneously return to support lower-altitude NPCs and communities? Or only engage with peers and above? |
| Agency | During emotional storms (high-intensity group events), does the player maintain sovereign action or freeze/flee/merge? |
| Communion | Does the player extend their emotional presence to the whole ecology or only to their immediate relational circle? |

## 5. Progression Arc

- **Entry:** Small ecology (village-scale), 3–4 altitude levels, mild emotional weather, clear NPC needs. Player learns to read the field without explicit prompts.
- **Midpoint:** City-scale ecology, full altitude diversity, moderate emotional weather with emergent crises, ambiguous NPC needs. Player develops spontaneous integral response.
- **Mastery:** Civilisation-scale ecology, extreme emotional weather, cascading crises, NPCs who resist help or deceive. Player as ecological emotional anchor — their presence stabilises the field.
- **Shadow Mode:** The ecology is specifically designed to trigger the player's dominant shadow — e.g., a world that rewards observation over action for DA, a world where all NPCs demand equal attention for DAll, a world of performed peace for GA, a world that demands surrender for GAll.
