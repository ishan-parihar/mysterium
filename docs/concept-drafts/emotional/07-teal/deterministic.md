# Emotional / Turquoise — Deterministic

## 1. Core Mechanic

The player observes a multi-agent emotional field — a system of 5–12 entities each expressing affect at different developmental altitudes simultaneously. The task: detect the emergent group-level emotional pattern, identify which entities are driving vs. receiving the wave, and predict the system's next affective state. Stimuli are dynamic emotional ecosystems, not isolated faces.

### 1.1 Task Structure

- **Phase A — Pattern Detection:** A group emotional field is displayed (animated avatars, colour-coded affect streams, relational arrows). Player identifies the dominant group-level emotion, the counter-current, and the pivot-point entity.
- **Phase B — Equanimity Probe:** While tracking the field, personal emotional provocations are injected (stimuli calibrated to the player's known triggers). Equanimity is measured via response-time stability and accuracy maintenance.
- **Phase C — Cross-System Reading:** Two or more emotional fields are shown simultaneously. Player identifies resonance patterns between systems — where one group's affect is influencing another's.

### 1.2 Adaptive Difficulty

Complexity scales via: number of entities, number of simultaneous altitude-levels expressed, speed of affective shifts, intensity of personal provocations, number of cross-system fields.

## 2. Shadow Diagnostics

| Shadow | Behavioural Signature |
|--------|----------------------|
| DA (Cartographer) | Perfect pattern-detection scores but equanimity collapses under provocation — maps affect without inhabiting it |
| DAll (Pluralist) | Refuses to identify dominant patterns; treats all affective signals as equally weighted; accuracy drops on hierarchy tasks |
| GA (Premature Equanimist) | Equanimity scores artificially high (flat response) but pattern-detection accuracy is low — performs calm without reading the field |
| GAll (Fortress) | Excellent pattern-detection AND equanimity but systematically avoids cross-system tasks — won't extend beyond known emotional territory |

## 3. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

- **Catalyst:** The emotional field presents genuine complexity that cannot be reduced to single-perspective empathy. The provocation layer ensures the player cannot merely observe — they must hold equanimity while reading.
- **Experience:** The felt-sense of vision-logic affect — simultaneously tracking multiple emotional altitudes, feeling the group wave without being swept, maintaining sovereign centre while remaining permeable.
- **Integration:** Successful trials build the neural pathway of integral affect: pattern-recognition + equanimity + cross-system reading as a unified capacity, not three separate skills.

## 4. Drive Probes

| Drive | Probe Mechanism |
|-------|----------------|
| Eros | Trials where the group-field is evolving toward higher coherence — does the player track the emergent pattern or cling to current-state mapping? |
| Agape | Trials where a lower-altitude entity is struggling — does the player's attention compassionately include it without losing system-view? |
| Agency | Provocation intensity — does the player maintain sovereign centre or merge with the group field? |
| Communion | Cross-system trials — does the player extend attunement beyond their primary field or contract? |

## 5. Progression Arc

- **Entry:** 5-entity single-field, no provocations, binary pattern identification.
- **Midpoint:** 8-entity field with altitude diversity, mild provocations, ternary pattern identification (dominant + counter + pivot).
- **Mastery:** 12-entity multi-field with strong provocations, cross-system resonance detection, predictive accuracy on next-state.
- **Shadow Mode:** Targeted trials that specifically surface the player's dominant shadow — e.g., provocation-heavy for DA, hierarchy-forced for DAll.
