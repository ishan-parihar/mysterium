# Emotional / Green — Module Specification

> **Line:** Emotional  **Stage:** Green / Pluralistic  **Vibration:** The empathic field that holds many feelings at once without collapsing into any single one.

---

## 1. Capacity Definition

Pluralistic empathy — the ability to simultaneously hold multiple, often contradictory emotional perspectives without reducing them to a single narrative. At Green, the emotional line moves beyond Orange's reflective self-regulation ("I notice I am angry") into genuine multi-perspective affect: feeling WITH others across difference, co-regulating in real-time, extending empathic accuracy to out-groups, and using affect as a bridge between worldviews. The core capacity is contradiction tolerance — maintaining emotional coherence while holding 10+ distinct affective signatures simultaneously.

## 2. Shadow Archetypes

### 2.1 Dark-Addiction: The Empathic Overwhelm

Cannot distinguish own emotions from others'. Boundary dissolution masquerading as sensitivity. "I feel everything everyone feels" becomes identity. Compassion fatigue, emotional enmeshment, weaponised sensitivity that demands others manage their affect to protect the empath. Submergent fixation: clinging to undifferentiated emotional fusion as though it were empathy.

### 2.2 Dark-Allergy: The Regulated Individualist

Refuses empathic opening entirely. Remains in Orange self-regulation: "I manage MY feelings; don't bring yours." Dismisses multi-perspective affect as "drama" or "codependence." Submergent aversion: rejecting the lower capacity's relational dimension rather than transcending it.

### 2.3 Golden-Addiction: The Premature Transpersonal Feeler

Performs Teal integral affect — claims to "feel the collective," speaks of "the field" — without genuine Green empathic mastery. Cannot actually co-regulate with one person. Bypasses the messy, particular, contradictory work of pluralistic empathy for a clean transpersonal narrative.

### 2.4 Golden-Allergy: The Empathic Fortress

Pluralistic empathy genuinely present but refuses integral deepening. Terrified of transpersonal affect — "I can hold many feelings but I won't dissolve into them." Emergent aversion: Green capacity intact but the call toward Teal is actively resisted.

## 3. Drive-Health Landscape

| Drive | Healthy expression | Pathological expression |
|---|---|---|
| Eros | Reaching toward integral affect; yearning to feel MORE perspectives | Bypassing into transpersonal without grounding (GA) |
| Agape | Returning to embody empathy in concrete relationships | Drowning in others' feelings without boundary (DA) |
| Agency | Maintaining sovereign emotional centre while empathising | Refusing empathic opening to protect autonomy (DAll) |
| Communion | Joining emotional fields; co-regulation; affect-bridging | Enmeshment; losing self in relational feeling (DA) |

## 4. Healing Vectors

### 4.1 Heal/Evolve (Agape + Agency → Dark Shadows)

Agape returns the overwhelmed empath to their OWN body; Agency re-establishes the boundary that allows empathy without dissolution. For DAll, Agape softens the refusal while Agency ensures the opening is sovereign, not coerced.

### 4.2 Evolve/Heal (Eros + Communion → Golden Shadows)

Eros draws the Empathic Fortress toward transpersonal depth; Communion ensures the reaching is relational, not solitary. For GA, Communion grounds the transpersonal claim in actual co-regulation with real others.

### 4.3 Integration Criteria

Player demonstrates: (a) accurate multi-perspective affect recognition across 10+ signatures, (b) stable contradiction tolerance under load, (c) co-regulation without boundary loss, (d) empathic accuracy extending to out-groups, (e) affect-bridging between opposed perspectives.

## 6. Compound Shadows & Cross-Module Relationships

### 6.1 The Overwhelmed Empath (Emotional DA × Somatic DA)

Empathic overwhelm fuses with somatic enmeshment — others' feelings register as physical pain, making group settings intolerable.

### 6.2 The Regulated Isolate (Emotional DAll × Interpersonal DAll)

Refuses empathic opening AND refuses authentic relating. Competent, self-regulated, and utterly alone.

### 6.3 The Feeling Performer (Emotional GA × Intrapersonal GA)

Premature transpersonal affect meets premature process-self. Performs depth — narrates "feeling the collective" while the witnessing self isn't genuinely present.

### 6.4 The Paralysed Feeler (Emotional DA × Willpower DA)

Empathic overwhelm meets commitment paralysis. Feels everything, does nothing. Every action might hurt someone, so no action is taken.

## 7. Shadow Surfacing Sequence

### 7.1 Progressive Revelation

Sessions 1–3: Baseline empathic accuracy and contradiction tolerance. Sessions 4–8: Introduce empathic load to surface DA/DAll. Sessions 9–14: Introduce transpersonal prompts to surface GA/GAll. Sessions 15+: Cross-shadow interactions and compound patterns.
