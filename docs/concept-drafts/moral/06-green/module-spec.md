# Moral / Green — Module Specification

> **Line:** Moral  **Stage:** Green / Pluralistic  **Vibration:** The examined moral life that holds multiple valid ethical frameworks in genuine tension without collapsing into relativism or fleeing into premature integration.

---

## 1. Capacity Definition

Green moral capacity is contextual ethical reasoning — the ability to honour BOTH care (Gilligan) and justice (Kohlberg Stage 6) as valid moral orientations, apply moral judgement situationally across cultural contexts, and commit to ethical action despite acknowledging the partiality of any single framework. The player demonstrates genuine pluralistic moral intelligence: holding multiple moral truths simultaneously while still acting with conviction.

## 2. Shadow Archetypes

### 2.1 Dark-Addiction: The Moral Relativist

Cannot commit to any moral position because all positions are contextual. "Who am I to judge?" becomes paralysis-by-pluralism. Weaponised tolerance that refuses moral engagement. Mean-green-meme morality where contextual awareness dissolves all ethical ground.

### 2.2 Dark-Allergy: The Principled Individualist

Refuses contextual ethics entirely. Clings to Orange universal principles — "there ARE moral absolutes." Dismisses care-ethics as soft, relational ethics as unprincipled. Cannot tolerate moral ambiguity or situated judgement.

### 2.3 Golden-Addiction: The Premature Integral Ethicist

Performs Teal developmental ethics without genuine Green mastery. Claims to "see all moral stages" while unable to genuinely hold care AND justice simultaneously in lived practice. Bypasses the hard work of contextual sensitivity by leaping to meta-ethical frameworks.

### 2.4 Golden-Allergy: The Contextual Fortress

Contextual ethics genuinely present but refuses integral commitment. Terrified of moral hierarchy — "no moral framework is better than another." Refuses developmental ranking of ethical systems. Sophisticated pluralism that cannot transcend itself.

## 3. Drive-Health Landscape

| Drive | Healthy Expression | Pathological Expression |
|---|---|---|
| Eros | Reaches toward integral ethics through genuine curiosity | Bypasses contextual mastery; premature meta-ethics (GA) |
| Agape | Returns to embody care-ethics in concrete relationships | Dissolves into relativism; no ground to return to (DA) |
| Agency | Commits to ethical action despite contextual complexity | Rigidifies into universal principles; refuses context (DAll) |
| Communion | Joins multiple moral perspectives in genuine dialogue | Merges with all perspectives; loses ethical selfhood (DA) |

## 4. Healing Vectors

### 4.1 Heal/Evolve (Agape + Agency → Dark Shadows)

Agape grounds the relativist back into embodied care; Agency provides the commitment to act. The Moral Relativist heals by choosing to care about something specific. The Principled Individualist heals by returning to relational ground while maintaining sovereign ethical voice.

### 4.2 Evolve/Heal (Eros + Communion → Golden Shadows)

Eros draws the Contextual Fortress toward developmental perspective; Communion dissolves the isolation of premature integration. The Premature Integral Ethicist heals by genuinely joining others in contextual moral struggle. The Contextual Fortress evolves by allowing moral hierarchy to emerge from communion.

### 4.3 Integration Criteria

Player demonstrates: (a) simultaneous care-justice reasoning without privileging either, (b) contextual moral commitment without paralysis, (c) acknowledgement of moral development without weaponising hierarchy, (d) ethical action under genuine uncertainty.

## 6. Compound Shadows & Cross-Module Relationships

### 6.1 The Paralysed Saint (Moral DA × Willpower DA)

Moral relativism compounds with commitment paralysis — cannot act on any value because all values are contextual AND willpower to commit is absent. Intervention: micro-commitments to specific care-relationships.

### 6.2 The Righteous Loner (Moral DAll × Interpersonal DAll)

Refuses contextual ethics AND refuses authentic relating — principled but isolated. Universal morality without relational ground. Intervention: ethical reasoning within genuine relationship.

### 6.3 The Ethics Performer (Moral GA × Cognitive GA)

Premature integral ethics compounds with premature systems-thinking — performed moral sophistication without embodied understanding. Intervention: confrontation with genuine moral dilemmas that resist systematisation.

### 6.4 The Tolerant Fortress (Moral GAll × Cognitive GAll)

Refuses moral hierarchy AND refuses cognitive hierarchy — sophisticated paralysis where no framework can be ranked. Intervention: developmental evidence that emerges from the player's own moral growth data.

## 7. Shadow Surfacing Sequence

### 7.1 Progressive Revelation

Sessions 1–3: baseline contextual sensitivity (no shadow provocation). Sessions 4–7: introduce care-justice conflicts that surface DA/DAll. Sessions 8–12: introduce developmental-ranking prompts that surface GA/GAll. Session 13+: full shadow ecology active.
