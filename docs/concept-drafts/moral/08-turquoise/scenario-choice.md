## 1. Modality Identity

- **Module:** Moral / White
- **Modality:** Scenario-Choice
- **Unique Axis:** Non-dual moral dilemmas — situations requiring compassion without deliberation
- **Core Mechanic:** Branching scenarios where no option is 'right' and the quality of presence in choosing reveals residual grasping

## 2. What This Measures

At White, moral dilemmas are not solved — they are held. This modality presents scenarios where all options involve some form of suffering, and the assessment is not WHAT the player chooses but HOW they choose: with grasping or with transparency, with residual identity or with open presence.

## 3. Shadow Detection

- **2.1 Grasping-at-Rightness:** Player searches for the 'correct' answer. Hesitation patterns. Revisiting choices. Seeking confirmation.
- **2.2 Aversion-to-Harm:** Player avoids scenarios involving unavoidable suffering. Chooses options that minimize personal moral discomfort over genuine compassion.
- **2.3 Moral Bypassing:** Player dismisses the dilemma's weight. "It doesn't matter." Chooses quickly without genuine engagement.
- **2.4 Moralist-Identity:** Player chooses consistently with a moral self-image rather than responding to the unique situation.

## 4. Task Architecture

### 4.1 Kosmocentric Dilemmas

Scenarios involving all-sentient ethics where:
- No option avoids all harm
- Compassion must extend to all parties including the 'wrong' ones
- The situation cannot be resolved by principle — only by presence

### 4.2 Transparency Probes

After choice, player is offered the option to explain — or not. Choosing to explain when unnecessary reveals residual grasping-at-rightness. The non-explanation is itself data.

### 4.3 Recursive Dilemmas

Scenarios that fold back on themselves — where the 'compassionate' choice creates new suffering, requiring the player to hold paradox without collapsing.

## 5. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

- **Catalyst:** Dilemmas that cannot be solved 'correctly' — forcing the player beyond deliberation
- **Experience:** The felt-sense of choosing without ground, acting without certainty of rightness
- **Integration:** Choice becomes transparent — arising from presence rather than from moral calculation

## 6. Drive Probing

| Drive | Probe |
|---|---|
| Eros | Does the player reach toward the most inclusive compassion even when it costs? |
| Agape | Can the player release attachment to the outcome of their choice? |
| Agency | Does the player act decisively even without moral certainty? |
| Communion | Does the choice attune to the relational web without losing clarity? |

## 7. Scoring & Adaptation

- Choice content scored by LLM for kosmocentric alignment (not 'correctness')
- Choice process scored by behavioral markers: hesitation, revision, explanation-seeking
- Transparency index = f(decisiveness without rigidity, non-claiming, paradox tolerance)
- Difficulty scales by increasing moral ambiguity and removing scaffolding cues

## 8. Compound Shadow Surfacing

- **Righteous Transparent:** Chooses well but needs to know they chose well — seeks validation post-choice
- **Compassion Avoider:** Systematically avoids scenarios with unavoidable suffering — or chooses the least emotionally costly option
- **Ethics Performer:** Makes 'kosmocentric' choices but with performative quality — the choice serves self-image
- **Moralist-Identified:** Choice patterns reveal a fixed moral identity rather than situational responsiveness
