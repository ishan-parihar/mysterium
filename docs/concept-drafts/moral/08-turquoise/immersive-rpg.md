## 1. Modality Identity

- **Module:** Moral / White
- **Modality:** Immersive-RPG
- **Unique Axis:** Ecological kosmocentric ethics — whether spontaneous compassion appears in free-play without prompting
- **Core Mechanic:** Open-world narrative encounters where moral situations arise organically and the player's unprompted responses reveal transparency or residual grasping

## 2. What This Measures

The ultimate test of kosmocentric ethics: does compassion arise spontaneously when no one is watching, when no task frames the moment as 'moral'? This modality embeds ethical situations within immersive free-play and measures whether the player responds from presence — or whether moral action only appears when explicitly cued.

## 3. Shadow Detection

- **2.1 Grasping-at-Rightness:** Player only acts ethically when the situation is framed as moral. Unprompted compassion is absent or inconsistent.
- **2.2 Aversion-to-Harm:** Player avoids areas or NPCs associated with suffering. Navigates away from moral complexity in free-play.
- **2.3 Moral Bypassing:** Player engages with moral content superficially — acknowledges suffering without genuine response. Moves on quickly.
- **2.4 Moralist-Identity:** Player seeks out moral situations to perform goodness. Compassion appears performative — directed at an imagined audience.

## 4. Task Architecture

### 4.1 Ambient Moral Ecology

The game world contains ongoing ethical situations that are never flagged as tasks:
- NPCs in quiet distress (not quest-marked)
- Environmental harm unfolding in background
- Opportunities for compassion that offer no reward or recognition

### 4.2 Unprompted Response Windows

Moments where the player CAN act compassionately but is never asked to. The absence or presence of response — and its quality — is the data.

### 4.3 Ecological Consequence Threads

Player's unprompted moral actions (or inactions) ripple through the world over time. The game tracks whether the player notices these consequences — or remains oblivious.

## 5. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

- **Catalyst:** A world that contains suffering without pointing at it — requiring the player's own moral sensitivity
- **Experience:** The player discovers where their compassion is genuine vs. performative, spontaneous vs. cued
- **Integration:** Moral action becomes ecological — arising naturally within the flow of being, without separation between 'moral moments' and 'regular play'

## 6. Drive Probing

| Drive | Probe |
|---|---|
| Eros | Does the player naturally move toward depth — noticing subtle suffering, engaging with complexity? |
| Agape | Does compassion arise without expectation of recognition or reward? |
| Agency | Does the player act on moral perception without waiting for permission or prompting? |
| Communion | Does the player's moral action attune to the ecological web — sensing interconnection? |

## 7. Scoring & Adaptation

- Unprompted moral actions tracked invisibly — frequency, quality, consistency
- Response quality scored by LLM for kosmocentric alignment (genuine compassion vs. performance)
- Ecological sensitivity index = f(noticing rate, response quality, consequence-awareness)
- Difficulty scales by making moral situations more subtle, more ambient, less distinguishable from 'regular' world content

## 8. Compound Shadow Surfacing

- **Righteous Transparent:** Acts compassionately but only in ways that could be witnessed — moral action disappears in true privacy
- **Compassion Avoider:** Navigates the world avoiding zones of suffering — path-analysis reveals systematic moral avoidance
- **Ethics Performer:** Seeks out moral situations and responds elaborately — compassion as content rather than presence
- **Moralist-Identified:** Builds a moral reputation within the game world — the character becomes 'the good one' as identity project
