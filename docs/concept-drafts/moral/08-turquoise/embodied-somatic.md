## 1. Modality Identity

- **Module:** Moral / White
- **Modality:** Embodied-Somatic
- **Unique Axis:** Body as site of kosmocentric ethics — embodied compassion, physical non-grasping at rightness
- **Core Mechanic:** Rhythm and movement tasks where moral content is delivered through somatic channels, measuring bodily ease vs. tension in ethical engagement

## 2. What This Measures

Residual moral grasping lives in the body before it appears in thought. This modality measures whether kosmocentric ethics is embodied — whether compassion flows through physical action without somatic bracing, tension, or avoidance.

## 3. Shadow Detection

- **2.1 Grasping-at-Rightness:** Somatic bracing when moral content appears — body tightens around 'getting it right'. Elevated tension in morally ambiguous moments.
- **2.2 Aversion-to-Harm:** Physical withdrawal patterns when stimuli involve suffering. Breath-holding. Micro-flinch on harm-laden content.
- **2.3 Moral Bypassing:** Dissociation from somatic moral signals — body goes flat/numb during ethical content. Performative ease masking disconnection.
- **2.4 Moralist-Identity:** Postural rigidity — body holds a 'moral posture' rather than remaining fluid. Somatic signature of identity-maintenance.

## 4. Task Architecture

### 4.1 Compassion-Flow Rhythms

Movement sequences where moral micro-scenarios are embedded in rhythm tasks. Scoring measures whether moral content disrupts somatic flow or is held without tension.

### 4.2 Ethical Breath-Gating

Breath-paced tasks where moral stimuli appear at specific respiratory phases. Measures whether ethical engagement disrupts breath regulation — or flows with it.

### 4.3 Non-Grasping Posture Probes

Tasks requiring physical openness (relaxed hands, soft gaze, open posture) while engaging with morally challenging content. Measures whether body can remain non-grasping during ethical engagement.

## 5. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

- **Catalyst:** Moral content delivered through somatic channels — making bodily grasping visible
- **Experience:** Player notices where their body tightens, braces, or withdraws around ethics
- **Integration:** Compassion becomes embodied — ethical engagement without somatic residue

## 7. Scoring & Adaptation

- Somatic tension indices derived from input patterns (rhythm accuracy, breath consistency, response smoothness)
- Baseline established from non-moral somatic tasks (cross-referenced with Somatic line data)
- Moral disruption score = deviation from baseline when ethical content is introduced
- Difficulty scales by increasing moral weight of embedded content while maintaining somatic demand

## 8. Compound Shadow Surfacing

- **Righteous Transparent:** Body performs ease but micro-tensions reveal residual checking — somatic 'making sure'
- **Compassion Avoider:** Physical withdrawal or numbing when harm-content appears in somatic stream
- **Ethics Performer:** Exaggerated somatic openness — performing embodied compassion rather than being it
- **Moralist-Identified:** Fixed somatic signature during moral content — body holds 'the good person' posture
