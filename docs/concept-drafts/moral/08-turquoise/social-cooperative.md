## 1. Modality Identity

- **Module:** Moral / White
- **Modality:** Social-Cooperative
- **Unique Axis:** Shared kosmocentric ethics — communion in compassion, non-grasping moral attunement with others
- **Core Mechanic:** Cooperative moral tasks where players must attune to shared ethical reality without imposing, performing, or withdrawing

## 2. What This Measures

Kosmocentric ethics in isolation is incomplete. This modality measures whether compassion extends into shared moral space — whether the player can participate in collective ethical action without grasping at moral leadership, performing attunement, or withdrawing into private righteousness.

## 3. Shadow Detection

- **2.1 Grasping-at-Rightness:** Moral leadership-seeking in group contexts. Correcting others' ethics. Needing the group to be 'right'.
- **2.2 Aversion-to-Harm:** Withdrawal from collective moral complexity. Inability to hold space for others' moral struggles without fixing.
- **2.3 Moral Bypassing:** Performed unity — agreeing with everything, dissolving genuine moral tension into premature harmony.
- **2.4 Moralist-Identity:** Positioning as the group's moral compass. Subtle self-appointment as ethical guide.

## 4. Task Architecture

### 4.1 Collective Compassion Coordination

Group tasks requiring shared moral action where no single player holds the 'right' answer. Scoring measures:
- Attunement quality (genuine responsiveness to others' moral reality) — 30%
- Non-imposition (contributing without dominating) — 25%
- Tension tolerance (holding group moral disagreement without collapsing) — 25%
- Transparency (absence of moral self-positioning within the group) — 20%

### 4.2 Moral Attunement Without Agreement

Cooperative scenarios where players hold different ethical perspectives. Measures whether the player can attune to others' moral reality without needing agreement — communion without fusion.

### 4.3 Shared Silence Tasks

Cooperative tasks where the most ethical action is non-action — holding space rather than intervening. Measures whether the player can be morally present without doing.

## 5. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

- **Catalyst:** Group moral situations that reveal residual grasping at moral role or position
- **Experience:** Player notices where they grasp at being the ethical one in the group — or withdraw from shared moral reality
- **Integration:** Moral participation becomes transparent — contributing without claiming, attuning without performing

## 6. Drive Probing

| Drive | Probe |
|---|---|
| Eros | Does the player draw the group toward greater moral depth without forcing? |
| Agape | Can the player release moral leadership when others carry it? |
| Agency | Does the player contribute moral clarity without dominating? |
| Communion | Does the player attune to collective moral reality without losing individual discernment? |

## 7. Scoring & Adaptation

- Social moral behavior scored by LLM analysis of interaction patterns
- Attunement quality measured by responsiveness to others' moral contributions
- Self-positioning index = f(leadership-seeking, correction frequency, agreement patterns)
- Difficulty scales by increasing group moral complexity and reducing structural guidance

## 8. Compound Shadow Surfacing

- **Righteous Transparent:** Subtly guides the group toward 'correct' ethics while appearing non-directive
- **Compassion Avoider:** Withdraws from group moral struggle — present but not engaged with collective suffering
- **Ethics Performer:** Performs deep attunement and universal love — the group's spiritual presence without genuine vulnerability
- **Moralist-Identified:** Becomes the group's moral reference point — others defer, and the player subtly accepts this role
