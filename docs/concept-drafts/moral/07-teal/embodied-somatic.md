# Moral / Turquoise — Embodied-Somatic

## 1. Game Identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Modality | Embodied-Somatic |
| Unique axis | Body as site of integral ethics — embodied moral intuition at holonic level |
| Core mechanic | Rhythm, timing, and gesture-based moral responses that bypass cognitive mapping and reveal embodied holonic intuition |
| Session length | 4–7 minutes per checkpoint |

## 2. Mechanic Specification

**The Moral Body:** Ethical scenarios presented with time-pressure that forces somatic response before cognitive mapping can engage. The body reveals what the mind performs.

- **Holonic Reflex Trials:** Rapid-fire moral micro-scenarios requiring immediate gestural response (swipe/tap patterns). Speed prevents cognitive architecture from intervening — reveals whether holonic ethics is embodied or merely intellectual.
- **Depth-Span Rhythm:** Musical/rhythmic sequences where each beat represents a holonic level. Player must maintain rhythm across all levels simultaneously — embodied multi-level awareness. Breaking rhythm at specific levels reveals where holonic integration is incomplete.
- **Moral Breath-Gating:** Ethical decisions gated by breath-cycle. Inhale = receive the dilemma. Hold = feel the holonic weight. Exhale = act. The breath pattern reveals whether the player can hold moral complexity somatically before acting.
- **Kosmocentric Release Gesture:** Late-game trials where the correct response is to release all gestural structure — to stop sorting, stop ranking, and simply be present. Tests whether the body can release integral structure toward kosmocentric openness.

**Somatic markers:** Response latency, gesture fluidity, rhythm consistency, breath coherence, and release capacity all feed the scoring model.

## 3. Shadow Surfacing

| Shadow | How surfaced |
|---|---|
| DA — Ethical Architect | Body freezes during action trials; high latency, hesitant gestures; the soma wants to map before moving |
| DAll — Contextual Pluralist | Undifferentiated gestural responses; body treats all moral claims identically; no somatic depth-discrimination |
| GA — Premature Compassionist | Body leaps to release gestures prematurely; skips holonic rhythm trials; reaches for kosmocentric stillness without earning it |
| GAll — Integral Ethics Fortress | Body executes holonic rhythms perfectly but tenses/contracts during release trials; somatic rigidity at the kosmocentric edge |

## 4. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

**Catalyst:** The body cannot lie. When cognitive moral sophistication outpaces somatic integration, the gap becomes visible in gesture, rhythm, and breath. The game makes this gap felt.

**Experience:** Player discovers where their moral reasoning is genuinely embodied vs. merely intellectual. The soma reveals the actual developmental centre of gravity — which may differ from verbal self-report.

**Integration:** Progressive trials that require: immediate action after holonic perception (healing DA), differentiated somatic response to depth-levels (healing DAll), sustained holonic rhythm before release (healing GA), and genuine somatic softening at the kosmocentric edge (healing GAll).

## 5. Scoring and Drives

| Metric | Drive signal |
|---|---|
| Holonic rhythm consistency across levels | Agape health |
| Action-trial response speed and commitment | Agency health |
| Release gesture authenticity (not premature) | Eros health |
| Multi-level somatic coherence | Communion health |
| Freeze/hesitation in action trials | DA shadow-drag |
| Undifferentiated somatic response | DAll shadow-drag |
| Premature release gestures | GA shadow-drag |
| Somatic rigidity during release trials | GAll shadow-drag |
