# Moral / Turquoise — Deterministic

## 1. Game Identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Modality | Deterministic |
| Unique axis | Objective holonic ethics measurement — depth-span trade-off accuracy, developmental ranking, multi-level harm assessment |
| Core mechanic | Timed holonic sorting and harm-trade-off calculations with objectively scorable outcomes |
| Session length | 4–8 minutes per checkpoint |

## 2. Mechanic Specification

**The Holonic Tribunal:** Player receives multi-level ethical scenarios presented as nested holonic structures. Each scenario contains entities at different developmental altitudes with competing needs. Player must:

- **Depth-Span Sorting:** Rank moral claims by holonic depth (how many levels of complexity they integrate) and span (how many entities they affect). Scored against the objectively correct holonic ordering.
- **Harm-Trade-Off Matrices:** Given quantified harm-vectors across multiple levels, select the action that protects the most depth for the most span. Timed — faster correct responses indicate internalised holonic reasoning vs. laborious calculation.
- **Developmental Ranking:** Order ethical frameworks (egocentric → ethnocentric → worldcentric → kosmocentric) and identify which stage a given moral argument originates from. Accuracy reveals whether player genuinely perceives developmental hierarchy or merely memorises labels.
- **Multi-Level Cascade:** Scenarios where protecting one holon damages another at a different level. Player must identify the resolution that honours the most holonic depth without collapsing into flatland equality.

**Adaptive difficulty:** Early trials use clear depth-span differentials. Advanced trials introduce near-equivalent holonic claims requiring fine-grained integral discrimination.

## 3. Shadow Surfacing

| Shadow | How surfaced |
|---|---|
| DA — Ethical Architect | Player scores perfectly on ranking but freezes on action-selection trials; high accuracy, high latency on execution items |
| DAll — Contextual Pluralist | Player refuses to rank frameworks or consistently selects 'all equal' options; low accuracy on developmental ordering |
| GA — Premature Compassionist | Player selects 'universal compassion' answers that ignore holonic depth; high confidence, low accuracy on trade-off matrices |
| GAll — Integral Ethics Fortress | Player excels at holonic ranking but fails items requiring release of structure toward kosmocentric openness |

## 4. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

**Catalyst:** Scenarios where holonic ranking alone is insufficient — the correct answer requires both precise ranking AND willingness to act on it, or to release ranking toward compassion.

**Experience:** Player feels the tension between mapping and acting, between ranking and releasing. The game makes visible the gap between knowing the holonic order and embodying it.

**Integration:** Trials progressively require faster action-selection after ranking (healing DA), forced ranking after pluralist refusal (healing DAll), holonic precision after compassion claims (healing GA), and kosmocentric release after successful ranking (healing GAll).

## 5. Scoring and Drives

| Metric | Drive signal |
|---|---|
| Depth-span ranking accuracy | Agape health (integrates lower into higher) |
| Action-selection speed post-ranking | Agency health (executes moral decisions) |
| Kosmocentric release items accepted | Eros health (reaches toward transcendence) |
| Cross-altitude mentorship items | Communion health (shares moral insight) |
| Ranking without acting pattern | DA shadow-drag |
| Flat-equality selections | DAll shadow-drag |
| Compassion-bypass selections | GA shadow-drag |
| Refusal of kosmocentric items | GAll shadow-drag |
