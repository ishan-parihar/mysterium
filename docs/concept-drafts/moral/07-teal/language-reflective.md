# Moral / Turquoise — Language-Reflective

## 1. Game Identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Modality | Language-Reflective |
| Unique axis | Verbal articulation of integral moral reasoning — how player talks about ethics reveals depth vs. performance |
| Core mechanic | Open-ended moral reasoning prompts scored by LLM rubric for holonic depth, performative markers, and genuine integration |
| Session length | 5–10 minutes per checkpoint |

## 2. Mechanic Specification

**The Integral Ethics Dialogue:** Player engages in written moral reasoning about holonic dilemmas. The LLM evaluates not WHAT position is taken but HOW the reasoning is structured.

- **Holonic Articulation Prompts:** "Explain why you would prioritise X over Y in this scenario." Rubric scores for: genuine depth-span reasoning vs. memorised integral jargon, acknowledgement of partial truths in lower stages, willingness to name developmental hierarchy without condescension.
- **Framework Translation:** Player must articulate the same moral position from multiple developmental perspectives (Red, Amber, Orange, Green, Turquoise) — demonstrating genuine understanding of each stage's moral logic, not caricature.
- **Moral Autobiography:** Player narrates their own ethical development — where they've been, what they've integrated, what remains partial. Scored for honesty, developmental accuracy, and absence of spiritual bypassing.
- **Kosmocentric Edge Prompts:** "When would you release holonic ranking?" — responses reveal whether player can articulate the limit of integral ethics without collapsing into pre-rational relativism.

**LLM rubric markers:** Genuine holonic reasoning uses specific depth-span language naturally. Performative reasoning over-uses integral terminology, avoids concrete examples, and cannot translate across stages.

## 3. Shadow Surfacing

| Shadow | How surfaced |
|---|---|
| DA — Ethical Architect | Responses are elaborate ethical maps with no personal moral commitment; language is architectural, never embodied |
| DAll — Contextual Pluralist | Responses refuse developmental hierarchy; language defaults to 'it depends', 'all perspectives are valid', never ranks |
| GA — Premature Compassionist | Responses leap to kosmocentric language without demonstrating integral mastery; 'all is love' without holonic specificity |
| GAll — Integral Ethics Fortress | Responses demonstrate precise holonic reasoning but become rigid or anxious when prompted toward kosmocentric release |

## 4. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

**Catalyst:** Prompts that require the player to move between mapping and committing, between ranking and releasing. The language itself becomes the diagnostic — you cannot fake holonic depth in extended discourse.

**Experience:** Player encounters the gap between their verbal moral sophistication and their embodied moral reality. The LLM reflects back patterns the player cannot see in themselves.

**Integration:** Progressive prompts that require: concrete moral commitments after mapping (healing DA), explicit ranking after pluralist hedging (healing DAll), holonic specificity after compassion claims (healing GA), and articulated release after successful ranking (healing GAll).

## 5. Scoring and Drives

| Metric | Drive signal |
|---|---|
| Holonic depth in reasoning (genuine vs. jargon) | Agape health |
| Moral commitment language (action-oriented) | Agency health |
| Kosmocentric articulation (genuine release) | Eros health |
| Cross-stage translation accuracy | Communion health |
| Architectural language without commitment | DA shadow-drag |
| Pluralist hedging frequency | DAll shadow-drag |
| Premature kosmocentric language | GA shadow-drag |
| Rigidity when prompted toward release | GAll shadow-drag |
