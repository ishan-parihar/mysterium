# Moral / Turquoise — Strategic-Planning

## 1. Game Identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Modality | Strategic-Planning |
| Unique axis | Planning integral moral life — structuring holonic ethical commitments across time |
| Core mechanic | Multi-turn resource allocation across holonic ethical projects with temporal trade-offs and developmental cascades |
| Session length | 8–15 minutes per checkpoint |

## 2. Mechanic Specification

**The Holonic Stewardship Game:** Player manages a network of ethical commitments across multiple holonic levels over simulated time. Resources (attention, energy, influence) are finite. Allocation reveals moral priorities.

- **Multi-Level Project Planning:** Player allocates moral effort across projects at different holonic depths — personal integrity, family ethics, community justice, systemic reform, planetary stewardship. Each level has its own timeline and decay rate. Neglecting any level degrades the whole.
- **Developmental Cascade Mechanics:** Investing in higher-depth projects creates downstream benefits for lower levels (trickle-down holonic health). But neglecting foundations causes collapse upward. Player must find the allocation that sustains all levels.
- **Temporal Moral Trade-Offs:** Short-term ethical actions vs. long-term systemic change. Immediate harm-reduction vs. structural transformation. The integral planner holds both timescales simultaneously.
- **Kosmocentric Horizon Events:** Late-game events where the optimal strategy is to release planned structure and respond to emergent moral reality. Tests whether the player can abandon their ethical architecture when life demands presence over planning.

**Adaptive complexity:** Early rounds have clear optimal allocations. Advanced rounds introduce genuine uncertainty where no plan is provably optimal — requiring integral moral intuition beyond calculation.

## 3. Shadow Surfacing

| Shadow | How surfaced |
|---|---|
| DA — Ethical Architect | Player over-plans, creates elaborate allocation schemes, delays execution, requests additional planning rounds |
| DAll — Contextual Pluralist | Player allocates equally across all levels regardless of holonic depth; refuses to prioritise; flat distribution |
| GA — Premature Compassionist | Player allocates disproportionately to highest-level projects while foundations decay; 'planetary ethics' while personal integrity crumbles |
| GAll — Integral Ethics Fortress | Player creates optimal holonic allocation but cannot adapt when horizon events demand structural release; rigid adherence to plan |

## 4. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

**Catalyst:** The planning game makes visible the player's actual moral priorities — not what they claim to value but where they allocate finite resources. The gap between stated values and revealed preferences becomes undeniable.

**Experience:** Player feels the weight of holonic stewardship — the impossibility of perfecting all levels simultaneously, the courage required to prioritise depth, the wisdom required to release plans when reality shifts.

**Integration:** Progressive rounds that require: execution after planning (healing DA), explicit depth-prioritisation (healing DAll), foundation-maintenance before transcendence (healing GA), and adaptive release of optimal plans (healing GAll).
