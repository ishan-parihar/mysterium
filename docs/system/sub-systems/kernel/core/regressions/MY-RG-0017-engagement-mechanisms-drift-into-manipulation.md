## The regression

Retention mechanics accrete **one plausible step at a time**. A streak, a countdown, an NPC that misses
you, a "you're so close" nudge — each is defensible in isolation, each is added by someone with a
reasonable argument, and the collection is a dark pattern. Nothing in the process has to be malicious
for the result to be manipulation. This is why the guard cannot be a sentiment ("we will be ethical")
but must be a **test** and a **register**.

## The standard

`AGENTS.md` §5.7 and `09 §2.4`: the game is addictive through the **felt sense of growth**, not through
dopamine manipulation. 45 §7.1 lists the allowed mechanisms and binds each to the canon that already
owns it; 45 §7.2 lists the forbidden ones as executable checks:

- variable-ratio reward on anything developmental (cosmetic loot and scripted narrative beats only);
- loss aversion or streak punishment — and theta-decay (`25`) is a *world* property that must never be
  surfaced as a threat;
- artificial scarcity and FOMO — no time-limited encounters, no expiring offers;
- dark-pattern notifications — no guilt framing, no "your NPCs miss you", no countdown;
- session length as a target (the player sets the actual);
- personalization used to retain a player *away* from the development they asked for;
- manufactured parasocial obligation — an NPC may not be scripted to suffer from the player's absence;
- analogical falsification — bending a concept's structure to fit a preferred domain.

## Prevention — the two tests

Any mechanism that increases return must pass **both**, and the result is recorded per mechanism in the
implementation's engagement register:

1. **Endorsement test.** Shown a plain description of the mechanism and its effect, would the player
   *endorse* it? (Not "would they fail to object".)
2. **Reversal test.** *"This contemplative practice is designed to keep you here by …"* — if that
   sentence, read to the player's own clinician or child, is uncomfortable, the mechanism is out.

A mechanism that cannot be described plainly fails the first test by construction, which is the point:
opacity is where the drift lives.
