## The regression

A cohort needs to be seeded. A cold-start has no individual evidence, so population structure is the
only honest source. Both needs are real, and both are served by clustering players — and clustering is
the exact mechanism by which a developmental practice starts **telling people what they are**.

The path is short and each step is defensible. A prior is computed. It is cached. It is named, because
a name is easier to talk about in a meeting. It appears in the profile, because it is already there. It
is shown to the player, because transparency. And now the system has issued an identity, in a product
whose entire premise is that identity is what the player authors (`16 §4`). The label then
self-confirms: content is chosen to fit it, so the player's experience is consistent with it, so the
evidence that it is wrong never arrives.

`47 §5.3`'s load-bearing rule is the mirror image of the same hazard at the interest layer: the domain
a person *is* must not become the site where their weakness is trained, because the practice would be
degrading the one thing that works.

## Prevention

1. **An archetype is a prior with an expiry.** It is superseded the moment individual evidence
   accumulates. A prior that cannot expire has become a label.
2. **Never stored in an identity field.** `MY-AD-0012` forbids mixing competence and identity; a
   typology is identity by another route.
3. **Never surfaced to the player** — not as a suggestion, not as a "you might like…", not as an
   explainer for why content was chosen. The *scaffold* is surfaced and editable
   (`MY-AD-0023` — "this was shown to you as a worked example first", in plain language); the
   *cluster* is not.
4. **A T2 reading is never presented as a fact about the person.** Displaying one is this regression
   regardless of framing — "you seem to be…" is a label with a hedge.
5. **Permitted purposes are two, and only two:** cold-start scaffold selection, and cohort formation
   (`38`) matched on **mode and salience pattern rather than on domain** — two people who both love
   music may have nothing else in common, while a listener and an engineer who both `produce` have a
   great deal.
6. **Clustering axes must be the T1 set.** A clustering whose dimensions are unvalidated distinctions
   launders tier-3 material into a label, which is *worse* than a tier-3 reading: a reading is
   hedged and ephemeral, a label is authored by the system and looks authoritative.
7. **Load-bearing interests are excluded from the structural pole** (`47 §5.3`). Fluency may be the
   *surface* of a bridge into difficult material; it must not be the *site* where the player's weak
   dimension is trained.
