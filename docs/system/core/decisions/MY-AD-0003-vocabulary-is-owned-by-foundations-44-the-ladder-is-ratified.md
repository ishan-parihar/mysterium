## Context
The vocabulary was scattered across six documents, so each redefined it slightly — which is how
stage/density conflation entered the canon (`docs/audits/DOC-SET-AUDIT-2026-09-20` D1/D2).

## Decision
`foundations/44` owns the alphabet: the five axes and their firewalls, the ratified ladder, the
term→owner table and the blacklist. The eight stages are KosmOS **L1–L8**; `White` retires as a
stage (content splits into L8 Turquoise and the Violet closure event); L9 and D5+ are out of
scope as developmental states.

## Consequences
- Positive: one place to answer "what does this word mean" and "who owns it".
- Negative: term changes now require a sweep plus a blacklist entry (see MY-RG-0001).
- Enforcement: gates DG5 (blacklist) and DG7 (ownership) in `scripts/arch.py`.
