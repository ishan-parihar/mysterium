## Context
Earlier designs treated the harvest as a destination with an endgame. That made the system
optimise for arrival, which contradicts the holon-is-never-outgrown commitment.

## Decision
The objective is **continuing developmental progression across all dimensions — every line, every
stage below the centre of gravity — without residual entropy and without residual shadows.**
Selection (`foundations/24`), strategy (`foundations/27`) and the composite index
(`foundations/25`) may not use the closure as a target function.

## Consequences
- Positive: `Samsara` becomes the normal condition of a healthy practice, not a failure state.
- Positive: theta-decay and floor-health gating become first-class mechanics.
- Negative: no "win screen" as a retention hook; growth must be felt (see MY-AD-0006).
