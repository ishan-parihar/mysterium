## 1. The Choice as teleological singularity

Of the twenty-two archetypes in the Archetypal Mind, Ra states that only Archetype Twenty-Two — The Choice — is "relatively fixed and single." It is the absolute attractor toward which all third-density experience converges. But the Choice is not a binary switch flipped at a single moment. It is the **emergent convergence** of myriad smaller polarities — across lines, stages, drives, and encounters — into one coherent direction.

The lesser cycle (Matrix → Potentiator → Catalyst → Experience) processes individual moments of development. The greater cycle (Significator → Transformation → Great Way → Choice) processes the lifetime. The Choice is where the greater cycle terminates — where thousands of micro-polarities, each textured by the specific line×stage×drive context in which they arose, resolve into a single macro-orientation.

**The singular nature of The Choice means:**
- There is no "third path." The player polarises STO, polarises STS, or remains uncrystallised.
- The game does not judge which polarity is "correct." Both are valid evolutionary paths (foundations/06 §6).
- The game's job is to provide *authentic conditions for crystallisation* — not to steer toward either pole.
- The Choice emerges from below — it is not imposed from above.

## 2. Why polarity is multi-dimensional

### 2.1 The limits of binary STO/STS

A 1-D scalar (−1.0 to +1.0) fails to capture the reality of polarity for three reasons:

1. **Line-specificity.** A player can be radiative in their Moral line (compassionate ethics) while absorptive in their Cognitive line (intellectual dominion). These are not contradictions — they are the uncrystallised state of a developing entity. A scalar collapses this texture into noise.

2. **Drive-grounding.** Polarity is not abstract "good vs evil." It is grounded in the four drives (foundations/05). An STO expression at Cognitive/Red looks like *tactical service* — using strategic cognition to protect others. An STS expression looks like *cognitive dominion* — using strategic cognition to subjugate. The scalar cannot distinguish these from Moral/Green's *contextual compassion* (STO) vs *manipulative pluralism* (STS). Each cell has its own polarity *texture*.

3. **Shadow entanglement.** The 4-quadrant shadow model (foundations/10) means that pathological drive expressions — dark-addictions, dark-allergies, golden-addictions, golden-allergies — colour every polarity trace. A player whose Agency is dark-addicted (domination) produces absorptive traces not because they have *chosen* STS but because they are *wounded*. The polarity engine must distinguish chosen orientation from shadow-driven distortion.

### 2.2 The resolution: multi-scale structure

The model must serve two requirements simultaneously:
- **Infinite local texture** — each encounter, each cell, each line has its own polarity character
- **Eventual macro-convergence** — all texture must resolve toward the singular Choice

The solution is a four-level hierarchy where each level aggregates the one below it, progressively abstracting texture into direction until the macro-singularity emerges.

## 3. STO/STS reconciled: source and flow

### 3.1 Ra's framing: energetic direction

Ra describes polarity in terms of energetic flow:
- **STO** = radiation. Energy flows outward from the entity toward other-selves. The open heart.
- **STS** = absorption. Energy flows inward toward the entity from other-selves. The closed circuit.

### 3.2 The grounded framing: source of nourishment

A deeper question: *where does the entity draw its sustenance?*
- **STO** = draws nourishment from above (transcendent source). Connection to intelligent infinity through the upward channel. The entity receives from the higher and becomes *overflowing* — naturally radiating the surplus outward.
- **STS** = draws nourishment from below (material/sensory source). Connection to the manifest through the downward channel. The entity draws from the lower and becomes *concentrating* — naturally absorbing further to maintain the concentration.

### 3.3 The synthesis: source determines flow

These are not two separate axes. They are a **coupled phenomenon** viewed from different angles:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                                                                 │
│   SOURCE (where nourishment comes from)                         │
│       ↓ determines                                              │
│   INTERNAL STATE (overflow vs concentration)                    │
│       ↓ produces                                                │
│   ENERGETIC DIRECTION (radiation vs absorption)                 │
│                                                                 │
│   ┌───────────────────────┐    ┌───────────────────────────┐   │
│   │  STO PATH             │    │  STS PATH                 │   │
│   │                       │    │                           │   │
│   │  Source: above        │    │  Source: below            │   │
│   │  (transcendent,       │    │  (material, sensory,      │   │
│   │   intelligent ∞)      │    │   finite resources)       │   │
│   │         ↓             │    │         ↓                 │   │
│   │  State: OVERFLOW      │    │  State: CONCENTRATION     │   │
│   │  (cup runneth over)   │    │  (cup must be filled)     │   │
│   │         ↓             │    │         ↓                 │   │
│   │  Flow: RADIATIVE      │    │  Flow: ABSORPTIVE         │   │
│   │  (gives freely from   │    │  (takes further to        │   │
```

### 3.4 Implications for the polarity engine

The `source_of_nourishment` dimension in the per-encounter trace (§4.1) is the *causal* variable. The `energetic_direction` dimension is the *observable effect*. They are tracked separately because:
- Source is inferred from the player's relationship to transcendence vs materiality within the encounter
- Direction is observed from the player's behaviour toward other entities within the encounter
- When they are coupled (source=above → direction=radiative), the trace is *coherent*
- When they are decoupled (source=above but direction=absorptive), the trace signals *spiritual bypassing* or *shadow distortion* — valuable diagnostic data

### 3.5 Ra's 51%/95% asymmetry explained

The asymmetry in harvest thresholds (STO ≈ 51%, STS ≈ 95%) follows naturally from the source model:
- Drawing from above (infinite source) requires only a *slight* opening — even 51% orientation toward radiation is sufficient because the source is inexhaustible
- Drawing from below (finite source) requires *near-total* efficiency of absorption — 95% — because finite resources demand complete control to sustain the concentration

This asymmetry is preserved in the multi-scale model (§9).

## 4. The four-level polarity structure

### 4.1 Level 1 — Per-encounter polarity trace

A polarity trace is recorded at the completion of every encounter that contains choice-points. It captures the multi-dimensional polarity signal of that single moment.

```ts
type DriveExpression =
  | 'healthy-balanced'
  | 'dark-addicted'
  | 'dark-averted'
  | 'golden-addicted'
  | 'golden-averted';

type EnergeticDirection = 'radiative' | 'absorptive' | 'sovereign' | 'diffuse';
type StageOrientation = 'reaching-higher' | 'integrating-lower' | 'homeostatic' | 'regressive';
type SourceOfNourishment = 'higher-realm-aligned' | 'lower-realm-aligned' | 'ambivalent';

interface PolarityTrace {
  // Identity
  encounter_id: string;
  timestamp: number;
  line: Line;
  stage: Stage;

  // The four dimensions
  drive_directionality: Record<Drive, DriveExpression>;
  energetic_direction: EnergeticDirection;
  stage_orientation: StageOrientation;
  source_of_nourishment: SourceOfNourishment;

  // Metadata
  catalytic_intensity: number;    // 0.0–1.0; how much genuine catalyst was present
  choice_count: number;           // how many choice-points contributed
  coherence: number;              // 0.0–1.0; internal consistency of the trace dimensions
}
```

**Update rules:**
- One trace per encounter completion (not per choice-point — the trace is the *gestalt* of the encounter)
- `catalytic_intensity` is determined by: stakes present, ambiguity of choices, personal cost incurred, novelty of dilemma
- `coherence` measures whether the four dimensions align (e.g., radiative + higher-realm + reaching-higher + healthy drives = maximally coherent STO trace)
- Incoherent traces (e.g., radiative direction but lower-realm source) signal shadow distortion and are flagged for the shadow system (foundations/10)

**What affects it:**
- The player's in-encounter choices (primary)
- The encounter's designed polarity texture (which dimensions are probed)
- The player's current shadow state (distorts drive expressions)

### 4.2 Level 2 — Per-cell polarity vector (64 cells)

Each line×stage cell maintains an aggregated polarity vector derived from all traces recorded in that cell.

```ts
interface CellPolarityVector {
  // Identity
  line: Line;
  stage: Stage;

  // Aggregated state
  dominant_pattern: PolarityPattern;
  exploratory_breadth: number;        // 0–N; how many distinct patterns sampled
  coherence: number;                  // 0.0–1.0; consistency of dominant pattern across recent traces
  crystallization: number;            // 0.0–1.0; coherence × sample-size factor
  polarity_texture_id: string;       // reference to foundations/23 ontology entry

  // Trace history (sliding window)
  recent_traces: PolarityTrace[];     // last N traces (N ≈ 20)
  total_trace_count: number;
  weighted_trace_mass: number;        // sum of catalytic_intensity across all traces
}

interface PolarityPattern {
  dominant_direction: EnergeticDirection;
  dominant_source: SourceOfNourishment;
  dominant_stage_orientation: StageOrientation;
  drive_signature: Record<Drive, DriveExpression>;  // most frequent expression per drive
}
```

**Update rules:**
- After each new trace in this cell, recalculate `dominant_pattern` from the sliding window
- `exploratory_breadth` = count of distinct `PolarityPattern` configurations observed (quantized to a manageable set)
- `coherence` = proportion of recent traces whose pattern matches `dominant_pattern` (within tolerance)
- `crystallization` = `coherence × sigmoid(total_trace_count / threshold)` where threshold ≈ 12 traces
- `polarity_texture_id` is assigned when crystallization > 0.6 — it names the cell's characteristic polarity (see §5)

**What affects it:**
- New traces (primary)
- Shadow healing events (can shift drive expressions from pathological to healthy, altering the dominant pattern)
- Transformation events (foundations/17) can reset crystallization to 0, forcing re-exploration

### 4.3 Level 3 — Per-line polarity profile (8 profiles)

Each line maintains a cross-stage polarity profile that captures the line's overall orientation.

```ts
interface LinePolarityProfile {
  line: Line;

  // Aggregated direction
  direction: 'sto-leaning' | 'sts-leaning' | 'exploring' | 'null';
  direction_strength: number;         // 0.0–1.0; how strongly the line leans
  crystallized_cell_count: number;    // how many of this line's 8 cells have crystallization > 0.6
  coherent_cell_count: number;        // how many crystallized cells agree in direction

  // Line-specific polarization rate
  polarization_velocity: 'fast' | 'moderate' | 'slow';

  // Cross-stage texture
  stage_progression: Record<Stage, CellPolarityVector | null>;
}
```

**Polarization velocity by line:**

| Line | Velocity | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Moral | Fast | Moral choices carry inherent polarity signal; every dilemma is a polarity probe |
| Spiritual | Fast | Source-of-nourishment is directly probed; transcendent vs material orientation |
| Emotional | Moderate | Empathy/absorption distinction maps to STO/STS but requires pattern accumulation |
| Interpersonal | Moderate | Service/exploitation of others is legible but contextual |
| Intrapersonal | Moderate | Self-relationship reveals orientation but indirectly |
| Willpower | Slow | Discipline is polarity-neutral; direction emerges only from *what* is willed |
| Cognitive | Slow | Thinking is polarity-neutral; direction emerges from *application* of cognition |
| Somatic | Slow | Body-use is polarity-neutral; direction emerges from *purpose* of embodiment |

**Update rules:**
- Recalculated whenever any cell in the line updates
- `direction` = 'sto-leaning' if majority of crystallized cells show radiative + higher-realm patterns; 'sts-leaning' if absorptive + lower-realm; 'exploring' if mixed; 'null' if < 2 cells crystallized
- `direction_strength` = `coherent_cell_count / crystallized_cell_count` (undefined → 0 if no crystallized cells)

### 4.4 Level 4 — Master polarity (emergent)

The global polarity state — the macro-arc Choice itself.

```ts
interface MasterPolarity {
  // State
  mode: 'exploration' | 'crystallizing' | 'crystallized';
  direction: 'STO' | 'STS' | null;
  crystallization_index: number;      // 0.0–1.0

  // Emergence conditions
  coherent_lines: number;             // lines with direction_strength > 0.6 AND agreeing direction
  altitude_floor: Stage;              // lowest stage at which coherence is measured
  harvestable: boolean;               // meets threshold per §9

  // History
  mode_transitions: Array<{ from: string; to: string; timestamp: number }>;
}
```

**Update rules:**
- `mode = 'exploration'` when `coherent_lines < 3`
- `mode = 'crystallizing'` when `3 ≤ coherent_lines < 6` AND `altitude_floor ≥ Orange`
- `mode = 'crystallized'` when `coherent_lines ≥ 6` AND `altitude_floor ≥ Orange`
- `direction` = the agreeing direction of the coherent lines (null in exploration)
- `crystallization_index` = `coherent_lines / 8 × average(direction_strength across coherent lines)`
- `harvestable` = see §9 for threshold logic

**What affects it:**
- Per-line profile updates (primary)
- Transformation events (can drop mode back to 'crystallizing' or even 'exploration')
- The master polarity is NEVER directly written — it is always derived from below

## 5. The polarity texture catalogue (overview)

### 5.1 What a polarity texture is

Each line×stage cell has a characteristic *polarity texture* — a named pattern that describes what STO and STS *look like* at that specific developmental frequency. The texture grounds abstract polarity in the concrete shadows and drives of the concept-draft for that module.

The full 64-cell catalogue lives in **foundations/23** (the per-cell polarity ontology). This section provides the principle and worked examples only.

### 5.2 Texture derivation

A cell's polarity texture is derived from:
1. The module's **shadow archetypes** (foundations/10; concept-draft §2) — which drive pathologies characterize this cell
2. The module's **drive-health landscape** (concept-draft §3) — what healthy vs pathological expression looks like
3. The module's **healing vectors** (concept-draft §4) — how STO and STS each resolve shadows differently

The STO texture names the pattern where the player uses the cell's capacity in service of radiation/overflow. The STS texture names the pattern where the player uses the same capacity in service of absorption/concentration.

### 5.3 Worked examples

| Cell | STO Texture | STS Texture | Grounding |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cognitive / Red** | *Tactical Service* — strategic cognition deployed to protect, plan for others, outthink threats to the group | *Cognitive Dominion* — strategic cognition deployed to subjugate, outmanoeuvre rivals, hoard information advantage | From concept-draft: Agency healthy (sovereign thinking for others) vs Agency dark-addicted (intelligence as weapon) |
| **Moral / Green** | *Contextual Compassion* — holds multiple ethical frameworks in service of genuine care; commits to action despite uncertainty | *Manipulative Pluralism* — weaponises contextual awareness to avoid commitment; uses "all perspectives valid" to control narratives | From concept-draft: Communion healthy (genuine moral dialogue) vs Communion dark-addicted (dissolves into relativism for control) |
| **Spiritual / White** | *Transparent Vessel* — non-dual presence that radiates without agenda; sacred-as-ordinary overflows naturally | *Absolute Sovereign* — non-dual awareness concentrated into total self-sufficiency; absorbs all experience into the self without remainder | From concept-draft: all drives equanimous (STO) vs residual grasping-at-sacred concentrated into self-identity (STS) |
| **Emotional / Amber** | *Empathic Guardian* — emotional attunement to group norms used to nurture belonging and protect vulnerable members | *Emotional Enforcer* — emotional attunement weaponised to enforce conformity, shame deviants, control through guilt | From shadow model: Communion healthy (shares feeling) vs Communion dark-addicted (fusion used as control) |

### 5.4 The texture assignment mechanic

When a cell's `crystallization` exceeds 0.6, the system assigns a `polarity_texture_id` by matching the cell's `dominant_pattern` against the texture catalogue (foundations/23). This assignment:
- Names the player's characteristic orientation at that cell
- Informs the encounter scheduler (foundations/24) about what kind of catalyst to offer next
- Provides the consequence engine (§8) with semantic context for propagation

## 6. Aggregation, coherence, crystallization

### 6.1 Trace → Cell aggregation

Each new polarity trace contributes to its cell's vector with weight proportional to `catalytic_intensity`:

```
contribution_weight = trace.catalytic_intensity × novelty_factor × cost_factor

where:
  novelty_factor = 1.0 if this pattern is new to the cell; decays toward 0.3 for repeated identical patterns
  cost_factor = 1.0 + (personal_cost_incurred × 0.5)  // sacrifice amplifies signal
```

The sliding window (last ~20 traces) determines the `dominant_pattern`. Older traces beyond the window still contribute to `total_trace_count` and `weighted_trace_mass` but no longer influence the dominant pattern directly. This ensures the cell reflects *current* orientation while remembering *historical* depth.

### 6.2 Cell coherence calculation

```
cell.coherence = matching_traces / window_size

where:
  matching_traces = count of traces in window whose pattern matches dominant_pattern
  (match tolerance: ≥3 of 4 dimensions agree)
```

Coherence ranges from 0.0 (every trace is different — pure exploration) to 1.0 (every trace matches — pure consistency). A coherence of 0.5 is typical during active exploration; 0.8+ signals consolidation.

### 6.3 Cell crystallization

```
cell.crystallization = cell.coherence × sigmoid((total_trace_count - min_traces) / scale)

where:
  min_traces = 5   (below this, crystallization = 0 regardless of coherence)
  scale = 7        (controls how quickly sample-size contributes)
  sigmoid(x) = 1 / (1 + e^(-x))
```

This ensures that:
- A cell with high coherence but few traces does not crystallize prematurely
- A cell with many traces but low coherence does not crystallize (exploration state)
- Crystallization requires BOTH consistency AND sufficient sampling

### 6.4 Cell → Line aggregation

Per-line direction is determined by the crystallized cells within that line:

```
crystallized_cells = cells where crystallization > 0.6
sto_cells = crystallized_cells where dominant_direction == 'radiative' AND source == 'higher-realm-aligned'
sts_cells = crystallized_cells where dominant_direction == 'absorptive' AND source == 'lower-realm-aligned'

if crystallized_cells.length < 2:
  line.direction = 'null'
elif sto_cells.length > sts_cells.length:
  line.direction = 'sto-leaning'
  line.direction_strength = sto_cells.length / crystallized_cells.length
elif sts_cells.length > sto_cells.length:
  line.direction = 'sts-leaning'
  line.direction_strength = sts_cells.length / crystallized_cells.length
else:
  line.direction = 'exploring'
  line.direction_strength = 0.0
```

### 6.5 Line → Master aggregation

```
coherent_lines = lines where direction_strength > 0.6 AND direction matches majority
altitude_check = all coherent_lines have crystallized cells at altitude ≥ Orange

master.coherent_lines = coherent_lines.length
master.crystallization_index = coherent_lines.length / 8 × mean(direction_strength)

if coherent_lines.length < 3:
  master.mode = 'exploration'
elif coherent_lines.length < 6 OR !altitude_check:
  master.mode = 'crystallizing'
else:
  master.mode = 'crystallized'
```

### 6.6 Crystallization reversibility

Crystallization is reversible at every level — but at increasing cost:

| Level | Reversal mechanism | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Cell** | Sustained contrary traces (8+ high-intensity traces against dominant pattern) | Crystallization drops to 0; exploratory_breadth resets; ~20 encounters of re-exploration |
| **Line** | Multiple cells reverse or new cells crystallize in contrary direction | Requires cell-level reversals across 2+ stages; months of play |
| **Master** | Transformation event (foundations/17) — genuine frame-change | Drops mode to 'exploration'; all line direction_strengths halved; the player re-experiences the polarity question from scratch at their current altitude |

The cost mechanic ensures that crystallization has *weight* — it is not trivially undone — while preserving absolute free will. A player who genuinely reconsiders their orientation CAN reverse, but the game treats this as a Transformation (a death-and-rebirth of the Significator's polarity structure).

## 7. Polarity-conditioned encounter selection (overview)

The polarity state at all four levels informs the encounter scheduler (foundations/24). This section specifies the *principle*; scheduler internals are deferred to foundations/24.

### 7.1 Three scheduling modes

| Master mode | Scheduler behaviour | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **Exploration** | Offers maximum diversity of polarity textures. Both STO and STS catalysts presented equally. Ambiguous choices dominate. No pattern is reinforced preferentially. | The player needs to *sample* orientations authentically before committing. |
| **Crystallizing** | Offers catalysts that *test* the emerging polarity. Counter-polarity temptations increase. Ambiguity decreases — choices become starker. The emerging direction is challenged, not confirmed. | Crystallization must be *earned* through resistance to counter-pull. The refining fire. |
| **Crystallized** | Offers catalysts appropriate to the chosen polarity's *depth*. STO players receive deep-service catalysts (sacrifice, collective shadow integration). STS players receive deep-dominion catalysts (mastery, sovereignty challenges). Both receive their polarity's *specific shadow risks*. | A crystallized entity needs catalyst that deepens and tests the chosen path, not re-opens the question. |

### 7.2 Per-cell scheduling influence

Beyond the master mode, individual cell states influence local encounter selection:

- **Uncrystallized cells** receive diverse polarity probes regardless of master mode
- **Crystallized cells** receive depth-appropriate catalysts aligned with their texture
- **Cells with high exploratory_breadth but low coherence** receive *convergence catalysts* — encounters designed to force a choice between sampled patterns

### 7.3 The temptation mechanic

A crystallizing player receives counter-polarity catalysts scaled to their current direction_strength:

```
temptation_frequency = 0.15 + (0.35 × direction_strength)
// At direction_strength 0.6: ~36% of encounters contain counter-polarity temptation
// At direction_strength 0.9: ~47% of encounters contain counter-polarity temptation
```

If the player holds orientation against temptation: crystallization deepens.
If the player yields: crystallization drops, potentially triggering mode regression.

This is not punishment — it is the cosmological principle that polarity crystallizes through *tested commitment*, not through unchallenged repetition.

## 8. The consequence propagation engine

### 8.1 The consequence record

When a player makes a polarising choice, a consequence record is generated carrying the full polarity trace — not a scalar.

```ts
interface ConsequenceRecord {
  // Source
  source_encounter_id: string;
  source_trace: PolarityTrace;        // the full multi-dimensional trace, not a number
  choice_summary: string;             // human-readable for LLM context

  // Propagation targets
  local_outcomes: LocalOutcome[];
  holonic_updates: HolonicUpdate[];
  scheduler_signal: SchedulerSignal;
  world_state_delta: WorldStateDelta | null;

  // Ripple metadata
  ripple_distance: 'local' | 'cross-stage' | 'global';
  affected_lines: Line[];
  affected_stages: Stage[];
}
```

### 8.2 Propagation layers

| Layer | Scope | Persistence | Trigger |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Local** | Immediate encounter | Encounter-duration | Every choice-point |
| **Holonic** | Affected NPCs, factions, environments | Permanent state change | Deferred; processed between encounters |
| **Polarity** | Player's four-level polarity structure | Permanent (accumulated) | Immediate on encounter completion |
| **Scheduler** | Next encounter selection pool | Session-to-session | After polarity update |
| **World-state** | Great Way macro-environment | Cross-session, stage-bound | Aggregated across many choices |

### 8.3 Ripple distance rules

- **Local ripple** (default): Most choices. Affects only the immediate encounter and the player's polarity trace.
- **Cross-stage ripple** (catalytic_intensity > 0.7): High-weight choices ripple one stage up or down. A pivotal Red-stage choice can alter Amber-stage encounter conditions.
- **Global ripple** (master.mode == 'crystallized' AND catalytic_intensity > 0.9): Only locked-phase, high-intensity choices ripple globally. These are rare, late-game, narratively momentous events.

### 8.4 Line-boundedness and cross-line propagation

Choices made in one line's encounters primarily affect that line's polarity vector and future encounters. Cross-line propagation occurs when:
- The encounter involves multiple lines simultaneously (compound encounters)
- The choice's polarity trace shows drive expressions that affect a shared drive across lines
- The consequence explicitly targets another line's holonic state (e.g., a Moral choice that alters an Interpersonal NPC's disposition)

### 8.5 LLM consequence awareness

The Holon Context Engine (foundations/22) receives a consequence summary after each choice-point containing:
- The full `PolarityTrace` (not a scalar — the LLM sees the multi-dimensional signal)
- The affected holons and their updated states
- The ripple distance and affected systems
- The player's current master mode and direction (for narrative tone calibration)

The LLM does not *compute* consequences — the polarity engine does. The LLM *narrates* them with awareness of the player's polarity texture.

## 9. The crystallization thresholds and harvestability

### 9.1 Ra's thresholds in the multi-scale model

The Ra material specifies:
- **STO harvest:** ~51% service-to-others orientation
- **STS harvest:** ~95% service-to-self orientation

In the multi-scale model, these translate to *structural* requirements, not a single percentage:

### 9.2 STO harvestability

```
harvestable_sto =
  master.mode == 'crystallized'
  AND master.direction == 'STO'
  AND master.coherent_lines >= 6        // 6 of 8 lines coherent
  AND altitude_floor >= Orange           // coherence measured at Orange+
  AND mean(direction_strength) >= 0.51   // the 51% threshold applied to strength
  AND all_prior_stages_healthy           // holon never outgrown
  AND violet_ray_integration >= 0.8      // foundations/06 §7.5
```

The 51% threshold is *gentle* — it requires only that a slight majority of the player's crystallized orientation points toward radiation. This reflects Ra's teaching: even a slight opening of the heart is sufficient.

### 9.3 STS harvestability

```
harvestable_sts =
  master.mode == 'crystallized'
  AND master.direction == 'STS'
  AND master.coherent_lines >= 7        // 7 of 8 lines coherent (stricter)
  AND altitude_floor >= Orange
  AND mean(direction_strength) >= 0.95   // the 95% threshold — near-total
  AND all_prior_stages_healthy
  AND violet_ray_integration >= 0.8
```

The 95% threshold is *demanding* — near-total absorption across nearly all lines. This reflects the cosmological asymmetry: separation requires extraordinary sustained effort because unity is the default state.

### 9.4 The White-stage gate

Harvestability is checked only at White stage (the final stage). A player who meets the structural requirements at earlier stages is *on track* but not yet harvestable — the full octave must be traversed. The White-stage gate requires:
1. Arrival at White (all prior stages healthy — holonic integrity)
2. Structural harvestability per §9.2 or §9.3
3. Violet-ray integration quality (foundations/06 §7.5)

### 9.5 The Samsara loop

A player who reaches White but is uncrystallised (master.mode != 'crystallized' OR thresholds unmet) enters the **Samsara ending**: the game does not end. The player continues in a post-White loop receiving increasingly intense catalysts designed to force crystallization. There is no "game over" for the uncrystallised — only "not yet."

## 10. The exploratory state (legitimate, not failure)

### 10.1 The design principle

Being in `exploration` mode is the **default and legitimate** state through the first two acts of the game (Infrared through Amber, roughly). The game does NOT push the player toward early crystallization. This is the antidote to mechanical-feeling moral systems where the player feels judged from the first choice.

### 10.2 Why exploration is sacred

In Ra's cosmology, the Veil of Forgetting exists precisely to create conditions where the Choice can be *authentic*. Authentic choice requires:
- Genuine sampling of both orientations
- Lived experience of radiation AND absorption
- Time to discover one's *natural* orientation rather than performing one

A player who crystallizes at Red stage has not made an authentic Choice — they have made a *reactive* one, likely driven by shadow rather than genuine orientation. The game honours this by treating early crystallization as *suspicious* (low altitude_floor prevents master crystallization) rather than rewarding it.

### 10.3 Mechanical protections

| Protection | Implementation |
|---|---|
| No early master crystallization | `altitude_floor >= Orange` required for mode = 'crystallizing' |
| No polarity feedback | Veil-validity (§11) ensures the player never knows their polarity state |
| Diverse early catalysts | Exploration mode scheduler offers maximum diversity |
| No punishment for inconsistency | Low coherence at early stages is expected and does not trigger negative consequences |
| Shadow-driven traces flagged separately | Traces produced by shadow distortion (incoherent dimensions) are weighted lower in aggregation |

### 10.4 The transition to crystallizing

The transition from exploration to crystallizing is *organic* — it happens when the player's natural patterns begin to cohere across multiple lines at sufficient altitude. The game does not announce this transition. The player may notice (through narrative consequences) that the world is responding more consistently to their choices — but this is felt, not scored.

## 11. The Veil-validity requirement

### 11.1 The principle

Per the Veil of Forgetting (foundations/20): The Choice carries ontological weight ONLY if made in conditions of unknowing. If the player knows they are being measured on polarity, their choices become strategic calculations rather than authentic expressions of orientation.

### 11.2 Design commitments

1. **No polarity UI.** The four-level polarity structure is NEVER displayed. No meter, bar, percentage, alignment indicator, or texture name is surfaced.
2. **No STO/STS language.** These terms never appear in gameplay UI, quest descriptions, or choice prompts. They may appear in the deep codex (post-Turquoise) as cosmological theory — never as gameplay feedback.
3. **No moral labelling.** Choice prompts never say "good," "evil," "light," "dark," "selfless," "selfish." They present *actions* with *consequences*.
4. **Consequences, not judgements.** The game responds with narrative consequences (NPCs react, the world shifts) — never with moral evaluation.
5. **No post-hoc reveal.** The game never tells the player their polarity state. Even at harvest, the experience is *felt* (the world responds to who you have become) rather than *scored*.
6. **No texture names surfaced.** The polarity texture catalogue (foundations/23) is developer-facing only. Players never see "Tactical Service" or "Cognitive Dominion" as labels.

### 11.3 The paradox of documentation

This document specifies the polarity engine in full technical detail — for developers and designers. The player never sees this document's contents reflected in the game's surface. The engine operates entirely beneath the narrative layer.

## 12. The 9th-stage opening

### 12.1 Beyond White

After White, the harvest opens into **4th-density experience** — the density of love/understanding, where entities form social-memory complexes (foundations/06 §7.4).

### 12.2 STO harvest experience

The STO-harvestable player experiences:
- Dissolution of the individual boundary into the social-memory complex
- The player's character becomes a **mentor presence** in other players' worlds (multiplayer) or in the NPC ecosystem (single-player)
- A contemplative rite where the player authors their character's final wisdom
- Graduation into violet-ray radiance — the character is retired, their full polarity structure preserved as an immutable record

The multi-scale polarity data becomes the character's *legacy signature* — a complete map of how they polarized across all 64 cells, visible to developers/researchers but never to the player themselves.

### 12.3 STS harvest experience

The STS-harvestable player experiences:
- Crystallization of absolute sovereignty — the self as the only real entity
- The player's character becomes a **dark mentor** or **adversarial presence** in other players' worlds
- A contemplative rite where the player authors their character's final declaration of dominion
- Graduation into violet-ray absorption — the character is retired into the antagonist pantheon

### 12.4 The uncrystallised

The uncrystallised player does not harvest. They remain in the 3rd-density loop. The game is infinitely patient. The Samsara ending (§9.5) provides increasingly intense catalyst without ever forcing the Choice.

## Critical Design Commitment

> **The game does NOT pre-judge polarity direction.** STO and STS are both valid evolutionary paths in the Law of One. Both must be playable with mechanical fairness — equal content, equal depth, equal encounter availability. The system observes and aggregates; it does not steer. The asymmetry is in the *crystallization threshold* (§9), not in the *availability of catalyst*.
>
> **The game DOES observe** whether the player is in exploration vs crystallization mode and adjusts encounter selection accordingly (foundations/24). This observation serves the player's authentic development — it is not judgement.
