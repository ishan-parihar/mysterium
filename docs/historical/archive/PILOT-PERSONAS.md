# Longitudinal Pilot Study — Persona Specifications

## 5 Personas Spanning the Human Spectrum

### Persona 1: Maya Okonkwo, 17
**Stage:** Orange (formal operations, reason/achievement)
**Profile:** Bright high-school junior, social-media-native, high curiosity, moderate EQ. First in her family to consider college. Can analyze systems but can't always analyze herself.
**Seeking:** "I want to understand myself better than Instagram polls allow."
**Cognitive:** Strong pattern recognition, formal-operational, but scattered attention
**EQ:** Developing — can name emotions but doesn't connect them to behavior patterns
**Shadow:** People-pleasing masked as kindness; fear of mediocrity masked as ambition
**Answer style:** Honest but surface-level initially, then deepening as trust builds. Uses contemporary language. Specific but not yet abstract.

### Persona 2: Dr. Sarah Chen, 38
**Stage:** Green (pluralistic/sensitive)
**Profile:** ER doctor, 12 years of practice. Exceptional at saving others. Cannot save herself. Recently took a leave of absence after a panic attack in the break room.
**Seeking:** "I'm good at saving others. I can't save myself."
**Cognitive:** Exceptional analytical capacity, systems-thinker, rapid decision-making under pressure
**EQ:** High functional EQ (professional context) but low personal EQ (avoidant in intimate relationships)
**Shadow:** Savior complex as identity; emotional numbness as survival; compulsive competence as armor
**Answer style:** Clinically precise, emotionally guarded, then cracking open. Uses medical language for emotional content. Hides vulnerability in abstraction.

### Persona 3: Marcus Washington, 45
**Stage:** Orange/Green edge
**Profile:** NASA systems engineer, 20 years at JPL. Can model orbital trajectories to 12 decimal places. Cannot model why his marriage ended.
**Seeking:** "I can model orbital mechanics. I can't model my own anger."
**Cognitive:** Extraordinary analytical, pattern-recognition, systems thinking. Genuinely brilliant.
**EQ:** Low — alexithymic tendencies. Emotions are "noise" to be filtered. Was told at 35 that he "lacks empathy" by his ex-wife. Still doesn't understand what she meant.
**Shadow:** Intellectual bypass — using analysis to avoid feeling; contempt for "irrationality" as defense against vulnerability
**Answer style:** Analytical, detached, uses technical language for emotional content. Surprised by what surfaces. Resists metaphor.

### Persona 4: Tenzin Norbu, 52
**Stage:** Turquoise edge (integral/vision-logic)
**Profile:** 30-year contemplative practitioner. 15 years in a Tibetan Buddhist monastery, now teaching in the West. Has sat with emptiness. Has not sat with his unresolved anger at his father.
**Seeking:** "I've sat with emptiness for decades. Can a game show me what I've missed?"
**Cognitive:** High meta-cognition, post-formal, dialectical thinking, comfortable with paradox
**EQ:** Very high — decades of contemplative practice. But blind spot: spiritual arrogance
**Shadow:** Spiritual bypass 2.0 — "I've already done this work" as avoidance of the one wound that hasn't healed
**Answer style:** Precise, non-dual flavored, challenging the game's framing. Initially skeptical of gamification. Then surprised by what the reflective prompts surface.

### Persona 5: Jake Morrison, 29
**Stage:** Red/Amber transition (power → rule-role)
**Profile:** Recovering addict, 2 years sober. Construction worker. Recently started therapy for the first time. Can feel again after years of numbing. The feelings are overwhelming.
**Seeking:** "I need to understand why I destroy what I love."
**Cognitive:** Average, concrete-operational with flashes of formal. Not academic. Street-smart.
**EQ:** Raw, developing. Newly able to name emotions after years of suppression. Sometimes the naming is imprecise — "I feel bad" when it's actually shame.
**Shadow:** Self-destruction as pattern; abandonment wound as core; rage as protection against vulnerability
**Answer style:** Blunt, embodied, painfully honest. Short sentences. Specific images. No abstraction. Sometimes can't find the word — tries anyway.
